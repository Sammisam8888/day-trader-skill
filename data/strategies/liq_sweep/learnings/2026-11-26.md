# Stops were too tight to survive the noise, and re-entries on stopped names made it worse: 33 of 49 trades lost

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The session was choppy and two-sided. Early shorts (ONGC, TRENT, TATASTEEL) and early longs (JSWSTEEL, INFY) were both stopped out, so there was no clean trend to lean on. liq_sweep closed 49 trades: 16 won and 33 lost, ending at net -Rs 2,998 against a Rs +1,000 target. The best trade was TCS long at +937 and the worst was HINDUNILVR long at -675. The supplied summary line ("49 won, net Rs 66695, best 4631, worst 163") contradicts the trade log and looks like a reporting bug. This note uses the log.

## Learnings
- **Full-size trades won too rarely for the payoff ratio.**
  - Full-size wins: 7. They averaged about +668 (BAJFINANCE +823, HINDUNILVR +501, TITAN +526, TCS +937, TRENT +908, NIFTYBEES +352, INFY +626).
  - Full-size losses: 16. They averaged about -480.
  - A 30% hit rate at roughly 2R does not cover charges.
- **Stops sat inside normal tick noise and often filled worse than the stop price.**
  - Stop distances were about 0.15-0.22%: ONGC 0.49 on 223, TATASTEEL 0.24 on 162, TRENT 5.9 on 3292.
  - TRENT 10:39 was stopped in under 3 minutes. ADANIENT short 13:46 was stopped in under 1 minute (-540).
  - Several exits filled past the stop, e.g. ONGC 223.76 vs stop 223.73 and TRENT 3298.45 vs 3297.85.
- **Re-entering a name after it stopped out kept losing.**
  - TRENT short was stopped at 10:41 and re-shorted at the same level at 10:55, stopped again at 11:09 (-883 combined).
  - ADANIENT lost three times: 12:20, 13:32 and 13:46, for -1,384 total.
- **Flipping direction right after a winner gave the profit back.**
  - TCS long hit target at 13:37 (+937). The short at 13:39 was stopped at 13:44 (-561).
  - HINDUNILVR short hit target at 13:12 (+501). The long at 13:39 was stopped at 14:06 (-675).
- **Small leftover positions were pure noise.**
  - About 25 trades of 1-5 shares (TECHM, SUNPHARMA, NTPC, COALINDIA, WIPRO and others) netted roughly -50 combined.
  - Charges ate most of their edge: COALINDIA's +1.18 gross target became +0.76 net.
  - They also tied up attention and slots without adding anything.
- **The afternoon was better but not enough to recover.**
  - From 12:57 onwards the 2R targets hit more often: BAJFINANCE, TITAN, TCS, TRENT, NIFTYBEES and INFY.
  - The day bottomed at -3,950 at 14:38 and finished at -2,998.

## Try next
- **Widen stops and demand more confirmation:** sweep_pct 0.15 (stop beyond the sweep extreme plus buffer) and reclaim_bars 3. Lower target_r to 1.5 so the wider stop does not push targets out of reach.
- **Add a per-symbol filter:**
  - No re-entry on a symbol after a stop-out for the rest of the session.
  - No opposite-side entry within 60 minutes of closing a trade on that symbol.
  - On today's log this would have blocked TRENT 10:55, ADANIENT 13:16 and 13:46, TCS 13:39 short and HINDUNILVR 13:39 long, avoiding about -2,786 at the cost of TRENT 13:16 long (+908).
- **Add a minimum-size filter:** skip any entry with notional under Rs 20,000 instead of opening 1-5 share positions when capital is already committed.

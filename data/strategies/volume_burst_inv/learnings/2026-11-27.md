## Learnings
- **Payoff is lopsided.** The average win was about Rs 176 and the average loss about Rs 471, because stops sit roughly twice as far as targets. For example, JSWSTEEL at 09:48 risked 5.45 to make 2.65. At that ratio the break-even win rate is about 73%. Today's 79% cleared it only narrowly, and a single loss wiped out 2-3 wins: BEL at 10:12 (-577) cancelled LT at 10:02 plus NTPC at 10:25.
- **The side split was stark.**
  - Inverted SELLs (the base model's BUY signals) went 19 for 22, netting +1626.
  - Inverted BUYs went 15 for 21 and netted only +129. Six BUY stops (COALINDIA 10:19, JSWSTEEL 10:35, ITC 11:29, ADANIPORTS 11:51, BHARTIARTL 12:16, HINDUNILVR 13:27) cost -2759 and nearly erased +2888 of BUY wins.
- **The opening hour was the weak spot.** Of the first 5 entries (09:45-09:59), 3 hit stops: ITC, JSWSTEEL and BEL, together -1671. This set up the -980 hole that took until 13:49 to climb out of.
- **Losers tended to drift slowly to their stops.** ITC was held 104 minutes, ADANIPORTS 76 and HCLTECH 71. A plain time stop would not have helped, though. Slow winners like POWERGRID (10:16 to 14:38, +208.74) and COALINDIA (10:20 to 13:08, +217.79) would also have been cut.
- **Some trades were too small to matter.** BEL at 10:37 (3 shares, +4.03) and AXISBANK at 11:06 (32 shares, +42.79) carried full execution risk for close to nothing once charges were taken.
- **Re-entering right after a stop worked today.** BEL re-entered at 10:13 for +240.61 and COALINDIA at 10:20 for +217.79. There is no evidence for a cooldown rule.

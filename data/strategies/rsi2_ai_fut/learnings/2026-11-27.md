# Signals and stops are priced on cash but fills are on futures, so the basis ate both stops

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The log has no index or breadth data, so I can't describe the wider market. The strategy took 2 trades and both were stopped out: TATASTEEL short -734.34 and INFY long -1553.74, for a day net of **-Rs 2288** against a Rs 1000 target. The summary line ("2 won, net Rs 1327, best 1162, worst 165") contradicts the trade log and looks wrong. The trade rows show two losses, so the summary generator needs checking. Of 25 candidate signals, 23 were skipped: 18 for margin above the Rs 100000 wallet, 3 for having no futures contract (BANKBEES, NIFTYBEES), and 3 for a broken quote lookup.

## Learnings
- **13:09 TATASTEEL29DEC26F SELL:** the signal fired at 164.14 (cash) but the futures fill was 164.66, 0.52 away. The stop hit at 164.83, only 0.17 above the fill. A stop placed beyond the cash signal price leaves almost no room once the futures premium is added to the entry. The trade lasted 12 minutes. The gross loss was about Rs 467 (0.17 x 2750), and charges plus slippage of about Rs 267 took it to -734.
- **14:29 INFY29DEC26F BUY:** the signal fired at 1162.20 (cash) and the futures fill was 1165.70, a 3.50 premium. The stop filled at 1162.50, above the original cash signal price. The futures price never broke the signal level, yet the trade lost about Rs 1280 gross (3.20 x 400) and -1553.74 net in 11 minutes. This is the same pattern as TATASTEEL: the basis consumed the stop distance.
- **Stop and target levels are not logged** (both columns are empty on every row). Without them I can't confirm the exact stop_atr distance. The engine needs to log both, in futures terms.
- **The Rs 100000 wallet can't hold most stock-futures lots.** BHARTIARTL, KOTAKBANK, ICICIBANK, SBIN, SUNPHARMA, RELIANCE, BAJFINANCE, TITAN, ADANIENT and others needed Rs 112k-225k. Only TATASTEEL (Rs 90563) and INFY (Rs 93256) fit, so the strategy trades whatever is cheap, not its best signals.
- **The quote lookup is flaky.** At 12:44 INFY, 13:49 ADANIENT and 14:14 MARUTI, the system asked for an NFO token and got the NSE equity token back. INFY resolved correctly at 14:29 and ADANIENT at 14:14, so this is intermittent, not a missing contract.
- **ETF signals are wasted.** BANKBEES and NIFTYBEES fired 3 times and can never be traded on this futures variant.

## Try next
- **Compute entry, stop and target on the futures price.** If the engine can't do that, add a filter that skips any trade where the basis (futures minus cash) is more than 25% of the stop distance. If neither change is possible, widen stop_atr to 2.5 so a 0.3% basis doesn't consume the stop.
- **Add a filter: skip if the stop distance x lot size < 4x the round-trip charges** (about Rs 270 per trade today). The TATASTEEL stop risked less than 2x its charges.
- **Set enabled = 0 until the wallet is at least Rs 250000**, or restrict the universe to names whose 1-lot margin fits. Also drop BANKBEES and NIFTYBEES from this variant's universe, and fix the NFO-token fallback before re-enabling.

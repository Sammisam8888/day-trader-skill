# Stops about 2-3x wider than targets meant a 53% win rate lost Rs 3,559

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
Per the log, the strategy closed 32 trades: 17 wins and 15 losses, net -3,559 after charges. The best trade was SBIN at +229 and the worst was TRENT at -649. The "Day numbers" line you were given (32/32 won, +58,844, best 12,730, worst 196) does not match this log and looks like a reporting bug. The market rose into 11:00 and then faded, so the inverted long entries at 10:50-11:05 were stopped out one by one. Shorts in banks and index ETFs worked from 11:25 to 14:00, but the market bounced after 14:00 and late shorts gave back gains until the 14:50 flat.

## Learnings
- **The payoff shape is the core problem.** Average win was about Rs 141 and average loss about Rs 397. At that ratio, breakeven needs roughly a 74% win rate, and 53% falls well short.
- **Stops sit about twice as far as targets.** HDFCBANK at 10:50 had its stop 10.4 below entry and its target 5.1 above, and ONGC at 11:25 was set up the same way. Every full stop-out (BHARTIARTL, TRENT, ULTRACEMCO, ONGC, ADANIENT, BAJFINANCE, HDFCBANK, NIFTYBEES, ICICIBANK) cost Rs 495-650. Each one erased about 3-4 target hits.
- **The 10:50-11:05 long batch caused most of the damage.** Seven longs opened within 15 minutes (HDFCBANK, ADANIENT, BAJFINANCE, RELIANCE, BHARTIARTL, ULTRACEMCO, HINDALCO), all betting on the same continuation. Only RELIANCE hit target. Five were later stopped, for about -2,636 combined.
- **Shorting the midday fade worked.** From 11:25 to 14:00, the short side (ICICIBANK, INFY, TRENT, HCLTECH, POWERGRID, NIFTYBEES, BANKBEES x2, ICICIBANK at 13:52, ADANIENT at 12:51) produced 10 straight target hits, about Rs 1,640.
- **Repeated re-entry on the same idea after 14:00 gave it back.** These were the third BANKBEES short at 13:56, the NIFTYBEES short at 14:00, and the third ICICIBANK short at 14:02. All were taken after the move was mostly done. They lost about Rs 1,600 when the market bounced (NIFTYBEES stopped 14:41, ICICIBANK stopped 14:45, BANKBEES and SBIN flattened at a loss at 14:50).
- **Some trades were too small to matter.** TECHM 1 share, WIPRO 1 share, HCLTECH 4 and SUNPHARMA 4 had near-zero impact apart from charges. This looks like a sizing glitch when capital was tied up.

## Try next
- **Tighten stops:** lower stop_sd to 1.0 (or the lowest value that keeps the stop distance no wider than the target distance). Also add a filter that skips any trade whose stop distance is more than 1.5x its target distance.
- **Limit clustering:** add a filter of at most 1 open position per symbol and at most 3 positions in the same direction opened within any 15-minute window. This would have prevented the 10:50-11:05 batch and the third BANKBEES and ICICIBANK shorts.
- **Stop late entries:** open no new trades after 14:00 and skip any trade sized below Rs 20,000 notional.

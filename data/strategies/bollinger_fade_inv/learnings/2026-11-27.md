# Hit the day target at 14:16 (peak +1500), kept trading, and lost all of it plus 367 in 34 minutes

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The day drifted down through late morning and early afternoon. The inverted fade worked as a momentum-short: SELLs in BAJFINANCE, ADANIENT, ADANIPORTS, ICICIBANK and MARUTI repeatedly hit their targets. From -659 at 10:58 the strategy climbed to +1055 by 13:27 and peaked at +1500 at 14:16 after ONGC, crossing the Rs 1000 target.

It then took four losing stops in 25 minutes and was flattened at 14:50 with net -367. The trades were 35 closed, 23 won and 12 lost, with an average win of about Rs 162 and an average loss of about Rs 341. The supplied "Day numbers" line (35/35 won, net 85633, best 13838) contradicts the log and looks like prices were mixed into the stats. The target banner's "net 1135" also disagrees with the log's +1500. The trade log is the source used here.

## Learnings
- **Ignoring the day target was the whole loss.** After 14:16 the strategy lost about 1867 rupees on the following trades:
  - TATASTEEL -10
  - SUNPHARMA SELL stopped at 14:30, -584
  - ITC SELL stopped at 14:38, -434
  - INFY BUY stopped at 14:40, -556
  - Flat-close losers at 14:50, about -349
  
  The trend signal had faded by then, and every new entry after 14:09 lost.
- **The payoff is lopsided, so a 66% win rate still loses.**
  - On shorts, the stop sat about 2-4x farther than the target. Examples: BAJFINANCE 12:39 had stop 4.6 vs target 1.4, and ITC 11:24 had stop 1.26 vs target 0.52.
  - At an average win of 162 against an average loss of 341, the break-even win rate is about 68%.
  - Five stops (SUNPHARMA 10:58, ITC 12:30, KOTAKBANK 13:45, SUNPHARMA 14:30, INFY 14:40) cost about 2960. That is most of the gross wins of 3727.
- **Stop fills slipped past the stop on every stop exit**, which widened the losses further:
  - ITC: stop 305.25, filled 305.31
  - KOTAKBANK: stop 504.33, filled 504.60
  - SUNPHARMA: stop 1906.16, filled 1906.75
  - SUNPHARMA 10:58: stop 1910.20, filled 1909.55
  - INFY 14:40 was the only exception, filled 0.11 better than its stop.
- **Shorts carried the day, longs did not.**
  - SELL trades won 19 of 26, including all of BAJFINANCE x4, ADANIENT x5 before the flat, and ADANIPORTS x2.
  - BUY trades were mixed: SUNPHARMA 10:49 -659, SBIN 12:44 -232, INFY 14:14 -556, SUNPHARMA 14:34 -231, against wins in INFY, JSWSTEEL and ONGC.
  - On a down-drift day, the inverted long signals were fighting the tape.
- **Flipping the same name was costly.** SUNPHARMA was stopped on a SELL at 14:30, then bought at 14:34 and lost another 231 at the flat. That is 815 lost on one stock in the last hour.
- **Long-lived positions drifted into stops.** KOTAKBANK was opened at 10:54 and stopped at 13:45 for -582. A fade/momentum edge that has not resolved in about 60 minutes has likely expired.

## Try next
- Add a filter that stops opening new trades once the day target is hit, or once day PnL falls 30% from its intraday peak (here, below +1050). Also block new entries after 14:10.
- Tighten stop_sd from its current value toward 1.0, or raise sd toward 2.5 so targets sit farther out. Either way, aim for a stop:target distance of 2:1 or less instead of the 3-4:1 seen on today's shorts.
- Set side to shorts-only when the index is below its opening print. Add a 60-minute time stop and a no-reverse rule on the same symbol within 15 minutes of a stop-out, which would have prevented the SUNPHARMA 14:34 re-entry.

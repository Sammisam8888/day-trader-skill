# Small option targets and wide stops lost Rs 7,613, and the day summary claims a Rs 225 profit

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy opened 7 one-lot long option positions on opening-range breakout signals and closed all 7. Only 3 won: HINDUNILVR C +952, HDFCBANK P +277 and POWERGRID P +528. The other 4 lost: AXISBANK C -4,660, RELIANCE C -2,988, ITC C -1,337 and SUNPHARMA C -385. Net after charges was Rs -7,613, about -7.6% of the Rs 100,000 wallet. The supplied "Day numbers" (7 won, net Rs 225, best 59, worst 7) contradict the trade log, and the "DAY TARGET reached at 09:31: net 1942" banner fired before any trade had closed. The tape appears to have favored downside: both puts won, and 4 of 5 calls lost.

## Learnings
- **Losses were much bigger than wins.** Targets were small in premium terms: HDFCBANK P went 23.30 -> 23.80 (+2%) and HINDUNILVR C went 56.05 -> 59.40 (+6%). The one stop, AXISBANK C at 11:29, was 43.05 -> 35.70 (-17%). That single stop (-4,660) cost more than all three winners combined (+1,757). Stop and target levels from the underlying do not translate into symmetric P&L on option premium.
- **Time exits drove most of the loss.** RELIANCE C, ITC C and SUNPHARMA C all closed at the 14:50 flat exit, for -4,709 in total. RELIANCE was held from 09:30 to 14:50 and lost 12% of premium without hitting either stop or target. These positions decayed and drifted for hours with no exit rule.
- **Calls failed and puts worked.** POWERGRID P (09:32) and HDFCBANK P (10:09) both won. The 09:30 RELIANCE and AXISBANK calls, the 09:45 ITC call and the 12:30 SUNPHARMA call all lost. Upside breakouts did not follow through on this day.
- **The day-target logic is broken.** It reported net 1942 at 09:31 with zero closed trades, so it is probably counting unrealized MTM or using stale data. It then "kept trading for more" with no loss limit, and a phantom +1,942 became a real -7,613.
- **The instrument lookup is broken.** 6 signals were skipped because the NFO token request returned the NSE equity: LT, KOTAKBANK, COALINDIA, BEL, MARUTI and ONGC. Another 7 were skipped for cost (TECHM, SBIN, ICICIBANK, ULTRACEMCO, BHARTIARTL, BAJFINANCE, ADANIENT). This happened because the 09:30 batch had already tied up about Rs 67k of the wallet. The day's results therefore come from a near-random subset of signals.
- **The reported stats are unreliable.** The "7 won, net 225" summary disagrees with the per-trade net column. Today's numbers cannot be compared with other days until the stats are reconciled.

- Add a time-stop filter: exit any option position that has hit neither stop nor target within 90 minutes of entry. RELIANCE C (09:30) and ITC C (09:45) would have been cut long before 14:50.
- Fix the problems before trusting any learning from this strategy:
  - Fix the NFO token resolution.
  - Make the day target count realized P&L only.
  - Add a daily loss limit of about Rs 3,000.
  - Reconcile the summary stats with the trade log.
  - Until these are done, consider enabled=0, or cap the 09:30 batch at 2 positions so capital is left for later signals.

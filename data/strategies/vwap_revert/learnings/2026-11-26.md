# Same-direction re-entries after a stop drove the -554 day; the summary line's "31 won, +26543" is wrong

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The supplied summary does not match the trade log. The log shows 31 closed trades: 10 winners, 21 losers, and a net of Rs -554 after charges. The best trade was HINDUNILVR at +1024.95 and the worst was JSWSTEEL (09:51) at -886.54. From 09:41 to 11:05, the strategy faded stocks that kept trending and hit a low of -2993 by 11:05. Between 13:30 and 14:30, short targets on HINDUNILVR, SUNPHARMA and COALINDIA, plus a COALINDIA long target, recovered most of it. The day finished well short of the Rs 1000 target.

## Learnings
- **Repeated same-direction re-entries after a stop caused most of the damage.**
  - JSWSTEEL was shorted at 09:41 (1306.35), 09:50 (1308.55) and 09:52 (1311.55) while price climbed. The first two were stopped out for -1453 combined.
  - The same pattern hit smaller positions:
    - HDFCBANK was shorted three times (10:17, 10:18, 10:30) as it rose from 859 to 869.
    - ITC was bought five times (11:33 to 13:27) as it slid from 308 to 305.5.
    - NTPC was shorted three times (09:53 to 09:58).
  - Every one of those re-entries was stopped out. The stretch from VWAP was a trend, not an overextension.
- **The tightest stops failed within minutes.**
  - ADANIPORTS (09:41) had a 6.41 stop and was hit in 2 minutes.
  - JSWSTEEL (09:41) had a 1.55 stop, about 0.12%, and was hit in 8 minutes.
  - The second JSWSTEEL short (09:50) was hit in under 2 minutes.
  - In each case the stop sat inside normal opening-hour noise.
- **Wider stops survived and produced the day's winners.**
  - HINDUNILVR at 11:05 had a 9.84 stop and returned +1025.
  - SUNPHARMA at 11:06 had a 6.64 stop and returned +840.
  - SUNPHARMA had been stopped out an hour earlier with a 4.79 stop (-449). The second short was entered higher with more room and worked.
- **Stop fills slipped past the stop price.** The JSWSTEEL stop at 09:51 filled at 1311.35 against a 1310.23 stop, costing 1.12 extra x 268 shares, about Rs 300. Tight stops on large positions get hurt most by this.
- **About 17 trades of 1 to 4 shares were noise.** These included NTPC, HDFCBANK, ITC, NIFTYBEES, TATASTEEL, KOTAKBANK and ONGC. They netted roughly Rs -20 combined and added nothing except churn.
- **The afternoon session suited the strategy.** RELIANCE at 14:33 was the exception: shorted at 1392.75 into a rising move and stopped out for -559.

## Try next
- Raise `stop_sd` to 1.5, or about 1.0 above the current setting. Today, the trades given about 0.35% or more of room won, and the sub-0.15% stops all failed.
- Add a filter that blocks re-entry in the same stock and direction for 60 minutes after a stop. On today's log, this would have skipped JSWSTEEL at 09:50 and 09:52, HDFCBANK at 10:18 and 10:30, and the ITC buys after 11:33.
- Before 10:30, require `band_sd` 2.5 or higher. Also skip any order where the computed quantity is under 10 shares, since those trades cannot clear charges meaningfully.

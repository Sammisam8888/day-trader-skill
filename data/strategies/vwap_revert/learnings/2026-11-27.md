# Buying VWAP dips on a one-way down day, with immediate re-entry after each stop, turned a +497 morning into -7805

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The market fell steadily all session: NIFTYBEES drifted from 293.89 to 292.30 and BANKBEES from 661.0 to 654.4, with no meaningful bounce. vwap_revert closed 47 trades with only 6 winners. It finished at **net -7805 Rs** after charges, against a +1000 day target that it never reached. The best point was +497 at 10:02.

The supplied "Day numbers" line (47 won, net +82682, best 12656, worst 165) contradicts the log. The real figures are:
- Best trade: +1287.27, ICICIBANK short at 10:02.
- Worst trade: -646.91, ULTRACEMCO long at 11:59.

The summary pipeline needs checking.

## Learnings
- **Only shorts made money.** The real winners all faded strength on a down day:
  - ICICIBANK SELL at 10:02: +1287.
  - HDFCBANK third SELL at 10:59: +1284.
  - HINDUNILVR SELL at 14:00: +53.
- **Longs did almost all the damage.** From 10:35 onward nearly every entry was a BUY into a falling tape. Three names repeatedly re-bought after being stopped out lost about -6520 of the -7805:
  - ADANIPORTS lost about -2657 over 6 trades (09:44, 09:55, 11:04, 12:36, 13:26, 13:57).
  - ADANIENT lost about -2001 over 5 trades (11:00, 11:59, 12:38, 13:23, 13:26).
  - BAJFINANCE lost about -1862 over 4 trades (11:00, 12:37, 13:08, 13:11).
- **Re-entry right after a stop is the core failure.** Several re-entries opened in the same second as the stop-out (ADANIENT 12:38:41 and 13:26:43, BAJFINANCE 13:08:01 and 13:11:39, ADANIPORTS 13:26:43 and 13:57:46). Each new entry sat at a lower price with a wider stop, so the strategy was averaging down into a trend.
- **Stops were too tight for the noise.** Some were hit within minutes:
  - HDFCBANK at 10:06 had a stop 1.03 Rs away (about 0.12%) and was hit in 2 minutes. The next entry at 10:09 was stopped in 40 seconds.
  - Combined, those two cost -1126 before the third attempt reached target.
  - Stop fills also slipped past the stop price, for example SBIN at 09:43 (stop 1146.46, filled 1146.55).
- **Many trades were 1-4 shares.** Examples are COALINDIA, KOTAKBANK, NIFTYBEES, AXISBANK, SBIN and ICICIBANK at 11:04/11:31. They contributed almost nothing either way. They look like leftover-capital entries and add trade count without adding edge.
- **The 14:50 forced flat cost -1105 across 10 positions.** Most of these were longs (BAJFINANCE -385, BANKBEES -288, ADANIENT -211) that were still under water in the downtrend.

## Try next
- **Add a per-stock cooldown filter.** After a stop-out, block re-entry in that stock for 30 minutes. After 2 stops in the same stock, block it for the rest of the day. On today's log this would have cut most of the -6520 from ADANIPORTS, ADANIENT and BAJFINANCE.
- **Add a trend filter, or use the side knob.** When NIFTYBEES is below VWAP and making lower lows after 10:30, allow shorts only. If side=2 means short-only in this strategy, set it for that regime; if not, implement the filter directly.
- **Widen the entry and stop settings.** Raise band_sd to 2.5 so the strategy only fades genuinely stretched moves, and raise stop_sd to 1.25. Also skip any entry whose computed qty is below 10 shares, since those trades only add charges and noise.

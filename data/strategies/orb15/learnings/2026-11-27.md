# Long breakouts all failed together on a down day, and only the shorts paid

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The tape was bearish. NIFTYBEES fell from 293.99 at 10:31 to 292.40 by the 14:50 flat. The bank longs were stopped out as a group around 10:30. orb15 closed 14 trades: 3 won and 11 lost, for a net of Rs -651 after charges. Best trade was ULTRACEMCO +1702, worst was HINDUNILVR -592. The Rs 1000 day target was never in reach. The run reached Rs -2911 at 11:20 and only recovered through the ULTRACEMCO target and the 14:50 flat exits.

Note: the supplied summary line ("14 won, net Rs 24233, best 12538, worst 223") is wrong. It appears to read exit prices as P&L. The log itself shows 3 wins, 11 losses and Rs -651 net. That summary calculation should be fixed before it feeds any other review.

## Learnings
- **Longs lost and shorts made money.** The 6 longs netted Rs -1659 and none of them won: POWERGRID -17, SBIN -544, ICICIBANK -110, BANKBEES -422, NTPC -2, HDFCBANK -562. The 8 shorts netted Rs +1008. The strategy took every breakout in both directions with no regard for the index trend, and the long side alone wiped out the day.
- **The bank longs were one bet, not four.** SBIN (09:34), ICICIBANK (09:35), BANKBEES (09:39) and HDFCBANK (10:09) were all long. Three of them stopped between 10:29 and 10:30, and HDFCBANK followed at 11:20, for Rs -1639 combined. At the same time the strategy was short KOTAKBANK and AXISBANK. Holding the same sector on both sides at once wastes risk.
- **Early shorts got whipsawed in the first 25 minutes.** AXISBANK (09:32 to 09:38, -561) and HINDUNILVR (09:33 to 09:56, -592) both reversed through the stop. This was the opening-range noise, and these two were the worst trades of the day.
- **Only 1 of 14 trades reached its target: ULTRACEMCO short, 10:31 to 12:43, +1702.** Targets sat about 3 to 3.7x the risk. On this day the trend moves that worked (NIFTYBEES +352, KOTAKBANK +296) were caught by the 14:50 flat, not the target. A wide target produced one large winner, which only happens when a stock trends hard.
- **Some position sizes were too small to matter.** NTPC was opened with qty 1 at 09:47, and COALINDIA with qty 16 at 10:20, apparently because capital was already used up by open positions. NTPC risked about Rs 2. Trades this small add charges and noise but can never contribute to the target.
- **The stops were tight relative to how much these names move.** ICICIBANK's stop was 4.30 points (0.27%) and ONGC's was 0.67 points (0.30%). Both were hit by ordinary drift, not a real reversal.

## Try next
- **Index-direction filter:** only take a stock's breakout if it agrees with the direction of NIFTYBEES's own 15-minute opening-range break. On this day that would have blocked the long side. This is one day of evidence, so I would prefer this filter over hard-coding a short-only `side` setting.
- **Sector and size filter:** allow at most one open position per sector, so the bank names are not stacked or held on both sides. Also skip any entry where the quantity is below about Rs 20000 notional, which would have removed the NTPC qty-1 trade.
- **Stop width:** raise `stop_frac` from its current level to 0.75 so the stop sits deeper inside the opening range. Keep `target_x` near 3.0, since one 3.7x winner carried the day. Test both changes in paper trading for 5 or more sessions before treating them as settled.

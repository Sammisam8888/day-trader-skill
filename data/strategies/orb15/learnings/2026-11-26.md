# Day summary is wrong; the log shows 4 of 12 trades won for -481, with one long (RELIANCE) offsetting most of the stop-outs

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The "Day numbers" line conflicts with the trade log. It reports 12/12 won, net Rs 27367, best 12730 and worst 267, which look like stop prices rather than P&L. The log itself shows 12 closed trades, 4 winners and 8 losers, net Rs -481 after charges, best +1818.62 (RELIANCE) and worst -638.54 (BANKBEES). The open was choppy. All three shorts opened at 09:36 and the BANKBEES long were stopped within 7-27 minutes, and the day fell to -2371 by 11:37. RELIANCE's +1818.62 target hit at 14:43 pulled it back to near flat, and the 14:50 square-off moved it only slightly.

## Learnings
- The summary line is broken. Treat it as a reporting bug, because any target or loss logic reading "net 27367" would think the day target was hit many times over. Every number here comes from the trade-level log.
- The payoff shape holds and the hit rate does not. Targets sit about 3-3.6x the stop distance (RELIANCE: risk 6.60, target +23.75; NTPC: 0.91 vs 2.79). Eight stops cost about Rs 2,839 in total. One full-size target win (RELIANCE 10:31, +1818.62) covered 64% of that. 4/12 wins is roughly break-even at this ratio once charges are included.
- The early shorts failed together. HINDUNILVR, ADANIENT and POWERGRID were all opened at 09:36 and all stopped by 10:25 (-1013 combined). That points to one market-wide bounce, not three independent breakdowns. Shorts netted about -900 on the day and longs about +418, but this is a single day and too small a sample to pick a side.
- Mid-morning longs also failed: ULTRACEMCO (10:54, -495.50) and JSWSTEEL (09:52, -558.71). Both were stopped while RELIANCE, opened in between, ran to target. The breakouts were not following through consistently on any side.
- BANKBEES (09:33, -638.54) was the worst loss. It is an ETF with a 0.39% stop distance, and it filled 0.42 below the stop (about Rs 82 of slippage on 195 qty). A tight-range index ETF gives a narrow stop that is easily run through.
- Sizing looks capital-limited. Notional on the first five opens is about Rs 5 lakh, so POWERGRID got only 4 shares (09:36, about Rs 1,062 notional). That trade cannot matter either way and only adds charges. Two positions held to the 14:50 flat close, SUNPHARMA (09:31) and COALINDIA (09:32), went nowhere for 5+ hours (-79.77, +38.42).

## Try next
- Filter: exclude ETFs (BANKBEES and similar) from the universe, and skip any entry whose computed qty gives under about Rs 20,000 notional (POWERGRID qty 4).
- Filter: when 3 or more same-side entries trigger in the same minute (09:36 shorts), take at most 1. This limits correlated stop-outs.
- Test stop_frac 0.75 (wider than today's roughly 0.4-0.6%-of-price stops) with target_x 2.5. Then check whether fewer stop-outs like JSWSTEEL/ULTRACEMCO outweigh smaller winners. Judge it over at least 10 days, not on today alone.

# Stacked same-sector shorts stopped out together; one IT bounce at 12:03 erased the morning's day target

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The log shows 39 closed trades: 27 winners and 12 stop-outs. Net is Rs 1935, with the best trade Rs +541 (SUNPHARMA) and the worst Rs -668 (INFY). The summary line ("39 won, net 77152, best 13946, worst 165") contradicts the log and looks like a reporting bug, possibly reading prices instead of P&L. It should be fixed before trusting any automated stats. The strategy reached the Rs 1000 target at 11:21 (Rs 1033), then fell to Rs -328 by 12:03. It recovered to a peak of Rs 2554 at 14:29 and gave back Rs 619 in the final 12 minutes before flattening at 14:50.

## Learnings
- **Correlated shorts were the biggest damage.** At 12:03:33, three IT shorts hit their stops in the same second: INFY (69 @ 1154.95, -288), TCS (68 @ 2574.25, -611) and WIPRO (436 @ 192.94, -247). Together that is Rs -1146. These were one bet on IT weakness, not three independent RSI2 signals.
- **Large positions with tight stops caused the big losses.** COALINDIA 664 qty (11:47, -581), TCS 68 (-611), INFY 187 (14:40, -668) and BEL 214 (11:17, -550) account for Rs -2410 of the Rs -3656 total losses. Stop and target distances are roughly 1:1, so one stop cancels about two average winners (average win is roughly Rs 207).
- **Stops slipped past their levels.** COALINDIA stop 385.82 filled at 385.86, TCS 2581.50 at 2582.15, RELIANCE 1381.23 at 1382.95 and ADANIENT 2641.42 at 2642.45. With stops this tight, slippage is a meaningful share of each loss.
- **Late entries gave back profit.** RELIANCE short (14:34), ADANIENT short (14:14) and INFY long (14:29) all stopped between 14:38 and 14:40, costing Rs -938 combined. They had only 16-36 minutes before the 14:50 flatten. The flatten also forced BAJFINANCE out at 1079.95, just 0.38 short of its 1079.57 target.
- **Many positions were 1-2 shares,** apparently a sizing or capital-leftover bug: WIPRO 1, NTPC 1 (twice), TCS 1, KOTAKBANK 2, BEL 1, TATASTEEL 1, HDFCBANK 4. Seven of those trades netted between Rs -0.64 and Rs +5.46 while using signal slots.
- **What worked:** single-name mean-reversion shorts with no same-sector overlap. Examples are SUNPHARMA (13:24, +541), BHARTIARTL (13:04, +406), BANKBEES (+363) and INFY long (12:44, +376).

## Try next
- **Sector filter:** allow at most one open position per sector per side (IT: INFY/TCS/WIPRO; banks: HDFCBANK/ICICIBANK/KOTAKBANK/SBIN/BANKBEES). Skip the new signal if a same-sector, same-side position is already open.
- **stop_atr:** raise it by about 0.5 from the current value, and size positions down so the rupee risk per trade stays the same. This should cut noise stops like COALINDIA and TCS, where slippage was a large fraction of the stop distance. Keep target_atr unchanged.
- **Time and size filters:** no new entries after 14:15. Skip any signal whose computed quantity is below 10 shares instead of opening a 1-share position.

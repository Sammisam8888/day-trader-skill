# Buying RSI2 dips into a falling morning with a ~1:1 stop/target lost Rs 3279 in 50 minutes, and the day never recovered

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The supplied summary line ("61 won, net Rs 96451, best 12769, worst 162") contradicts the trade log. Its "best" and "worst" match exit prices (ULTRACEMCO 12768.95, TATASTEEL 162.42), so it looks like it was built from the wrong column and should be fixed. The log itself shows 61 closed trades: 22 winners (Rs +3917) and 39 losers (Rs -8507), for a net of **Rs -4590** after charges. The market sold off from about 11:00 to 13:30 (AXISBANK 1428 to 1404, ULTRACEMCO 12811 to 12675, BANKBEES 662 to 657), then bounced from about 14:00. The strategy was long into the decline, did well on shorts at midday, then went short just as the market turned up.

## Learnings
- **The opening cluster of longs was the main damage.** The first 11 closes were all stops: AXISBANK (11:06, -567), KOTAKBANK, TCS (-595), ULTRACEMCO, TECHM (-553), BHARTIARTL, NIFTYBEES (-688), WIPRO, ITC, TRENT (-493) and KOTAKBANK short. That put the day at -3279 by 11:46. The stops came within 6-15 minutes of entry, which means the RSI2 oversold signal fired in the middle of a trending leg, not at an exhaustion point.
- **Stop and target are nearly symmetric, so the strategy needs a win rate above 50% just to cover charges.** For example, AXISBANK had a 5.27 stop against a 5.17 target, and TCS 7.75 against 7.65. Today's win rate was 36%. Stops also filled past the stop level (INFY 13:37 filled 0.93 beyond, SBIN 14:19 0.33 beyond), while targets filled at about the target, which widens the asymmetry further.
- **Shorts placed with the trend worked.** From 11:51 to 13:56, TITAN +211, WIPRO +170, TRENT +268, ICICIBANK +189, AXISBANK 107 qty +352, ULTRACEMCO +282, BHARTIARTL +355 and POWERGRID +219 were all target hits.
- **The 14:07-14:27 short cluster went against the afternoon bounce.** SBIN -599, AXISBANK -390, BANKBEES -684, then BANKBEES again -461 and HDFCBANK -97. At the same time, the longs INFY +515, HINDALCO +226 and TECHM +471 hit their targets. The trigger fired in whichever direction opposed the most recent move, regardless of the session trend.
- **Index ETFs were the worst instruments.** NIFTYBEES and BANKBEES lost Rs -2224 across 4 trades with no winners. BANKBEES was re-shorted at 14:26:33, one minute after being stopped at 14:25:32, and lost again. KOTAKBANK was also re-entered 6 times for small losses.
- **Many 1-5 share positions added nothing.** Examples are POWERGRID 1, WIPRO 1, ITC 1, BAJFINANCE 1 and TCS 1, with results between Rs -10 and +6. These look like residual-capital fills.

## Try next
- **Add a trend filter.** Take longs only when price is above the session VWAP and shorts only when below. This would have blocked the 11:00-11:30 longs and the 14:07-14:22 shorts. Also set low=5.0 and high=95.0 so fewer, more stretched signals fire.
- **Make the payoff asymmetric.** Use stop_atr=1.5 and target_atr=2.5, so the break-even win rate falls to roughly 40% instead of above 50% after charges.
- **Add instrument and sizing filters.** Exclude NIFTYBEES and BANKBEES, apply a 30-minute cooldown on a symbol after it is stopped, and skip any entry with notional below Rs 20000.

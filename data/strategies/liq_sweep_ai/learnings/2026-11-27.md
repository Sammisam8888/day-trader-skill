# Long sweeps failed all morning: 19 of 23 longs were stopped, and stops sat so close that a 0.1-0.3% wick took nearly every one

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy closed 31 trades with 8 winners and 23 losers. It finished at **Rs -3799** net against a Rs +1000 target. The "Day numbers" line (31 won, +49096, best 13953, worst 165) does not match the log. 13953 looks like the MARUTI exit price, not a P&L. From the log, the best trade was TECHM short at +753.68 and the worst was TRENT long at -643.02.

The day peaked at +710 at 10:36, after the ICICIBANK short and BEL long both hit target. Then a run of long stop-outs from 10:56 to 12:29 dragged it to -4310. The long book across banks, metals, IT, autos and FMCG kept failing, which suggests a weak or downward-drifting tape through late morning. I can't confirm the index direction from this log.

## Learnings
- **Direction split:**
  - Longs: 23 trades, 4 wins, about Rs -3596.
  - Shorts: 8 trades, 4 wins, about Rs -203.
  - The two biggest winners were both shorts: TECHM 14:41 (+754) and ICICIBANK 10:31 (+676).
  - The recipe kept buying "sell-side sweeps" in a market that kept making new lows. The low it bought was a step on the way down, not the end of the move.
- **Exits sat right at the stop:**
  - Nearly every stopped trade exited within 0.01-0.6 of the stop price (HDFCBANK 861.8 vs 861.87, MARUTI 13952.95 vs 13953.02, HINDALCO 916.7 vs 916.84).
  - Stops just beyond the sweep wick got re-swept. Typical stop distance was only about 0.1-0.4% (HDFCBANK 1.73 on 863, BAJFINANCE 2.6 on 1095, POWERGRID 0.64 on 268).
  - The only trade with a wide stop, TECHM short (10.38 stop distance, about 0.56%), was the one that paid best.
- **Same-name re-entries doubled losses:**
  - HDFCBANK long stopped at 10:59 (-618), then re-bought at 11:02 and stopped at 11:20 (-590).
  - ITC long stopped at 11:11, re-bought at 11:13 and stopped at 11:25.
  - TRENT short stopped at 11:01 (-565), then flipped long at 11:02 and stopped at 11:33 (-643). That is Rs -1208 on one name whipsawing in both directions.
  - In every case the second "sweep" on a stock that had just stopped us was the same move continuing.
- **Loss clustering with no brake:** 15 trades closed between 10:56 and 12:29 for about Rs -4750. There was no daily loss limit, so the strategy kept firing at full risk (about Rs 500 per trade) after the day was already past -1000.
- **One-share fills are noise:** WIPRO, AXISBANK, POWERGRID, JSWSTEEL x2 and NTPC traded 1-6 shares for P&L of plus or minus Rs 0.8-7. They add nothing but log clutter and charge drag.

## Try next
- **Widen the sweep and confirmation knobs** so wick-and-continue moves are filtered out:
  - Raise sweep_pct from its current value to 0.15.
  - Raise reclaim_bars to 3, so price must hold back inside the level for 3 bars before entry.
  - Keep target_r at about 1.5, since the targets that were reached were reached quickly.
- **Add a filter: no re-entry on the same stock for 30 minutes after a stop, in either direction.** This would have removed the second HDFCBANK, ITC and TRENT trades, about Rs -1282.
- **Add a trend filter: only take long sweeps when price is above the session VWAP, and short sweeps below it.** If the knob is easier, try side restricted to shorts on days when the index is below its opening price by 10:30. Treat this as a one-day observation to verify over more sessions, not a settled rule.

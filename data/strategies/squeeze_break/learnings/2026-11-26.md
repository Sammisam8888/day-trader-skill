# Longs fought a falling tape and lost Rs 2,899, while shorts made +569; squeeze_break needs a direction filter

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The supplied "Day numbers" line (24/24 won, +Rs 42,847, best 12,663, worst 163) does not match the trade log and should not be trusted. The log shows 24 closed trades with 6 winners and 18 losers, net -Rs 2,330 after charges. The best trade was ULTRACEMCO short at +997.88 and the worst was POWERGRID short at -716.44. The market sold off from about 11:00 into roughly 13:50, then bounced into the 14:50 flat. Longs netted -2,899 across 11 trades, and shorts netted +569 across 13 trades. The day never got close to the Rs 1,000 target. Its best point was -877 at 13:55.

## Learnings
- **The opening longs were the whole loss.** TCS, COALINDIA, BHARTIARTL and TRENT were all bought between 10:56 and 11:10, and all four hit their stops by 11:40. Together they lost -2,334. Each breakout was a long into a market that was turning down. Later, LT at 12:00 (-257) and SBIN at 12:25 (-448) repeated the same mistake.
- **Shorts with the trend were the only real edge.** TRENT short at 11:45 made +936 and AXISBANK short at 11:20 made +925, both reaching target. ULTRACEMCO short at 12:51 (+998) and BAJFINANCE short at 12:55 (+152) also hit target. The TRENT flip worked: the long was stopped at 11:20, and the short at 11:45 made back more than that loss.
- **Late shorts gave back the gains when the market bounced.** BHARTIARTL (13:30), KOTAKBANK (13:52) and POWERGRID (13:58) were all stopped between 14:31 and 14:49, for -1,600 combined. That took the day from -877 back to -2,479. The 14:32 COALINDIA long, which caught the bounce, made only +152 before the flat.
- **Stops filled worse than the stop price, and it cost real money.** POWERGRID filled at 267.41 against a 267.21 stop, which on 815 shares was about Rs 160 beyond the plan. KOTAKBANK filled at 508.85 against 508.49, ICICIBANK at 1629.45 against 1629.04, and INFY at 1164.95 against 1164.85. With tight stops (about 0.2-0.4%), this slippage is a large share of the planned risk.
- **Sizing was inconsistent and created noise trades.** Seven trades used 1-6 shares: ONGC 4, TATASTEEL 6 and 2, POWERGRID 3, BANKBEES 1, COALINDIA 1 and HINDALCO 1. They netted about -15 and only added charges and clutter. In contrast, POWERGRID at 815 shares and KOTAKBANK at 242 shares were the two biggest afternoon losses.
- **The trade count was too high for the edge.** With 24 trades, a 25% win rate and roughly 2:1 target-to-stop, the strategy was breakeven at best before charges. Most losers were stopped within 15-45 minutes, which suggests the squeeze threshold accepted weak compressions.

## Try next
- **Add a direction filter.** Only take breakouts in the direction of NIFTY's move since 10:30, or of the stock's own VWAP slope. Today that would have blocked the 10:56-11:10 longs (-2,334) and LT/SBIN (-705), while keeping TRENT, AXISBANK, ULTRACEMCO and BAJFINANCE. If `side` maps 0=both, 1=long-only, 2=short-only, a static side setting cannot do this, so it has to be a filter rather than a knob value.
- **Raise squeeze to 2.0 (from wherever it sits now) and block new entries after 13:30.** The 13:30-13:58 entries netted -1,600 on a trend that was ending. A stricter squeeze should cut the many 15-45 minute stop-outs.
- **Set a minimum position size of Rs 20,000 notional.** Skip any entry below it rather than taking 1-6 share trades. Also cap risk per trade at a fixed rupee amount that includes expected slippage of about 0.05% beyond the stop, so a POWERGRID-style 815-share position cannot lose more than the other trades.

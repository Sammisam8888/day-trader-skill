# Peak of Rs 2470 at 13:58 shrank to Rs 224 because late-afternoon entries after 14:00 were all stopped out

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The trade rows do not match the summary line. The summary says 15 of 15 won, net Rs 19962, worst trade +166. The rows show 15 closes with 5 wins and 10 losses, net Rs +224.21, best +957.65 (BAJFINANCE) and worst -835.88 (BHARTIARTL). This review uses the rows, and the summary generator should be checked.

The late morning favoured shorts. BAJFINANCE, ADANIENT and two ADANIPORTS shorts all hit target, taking the day to Rs +2470 by 13:58. From 14:13 onward every close was a loss, and the day finished at Rs +224, giving back 91% of the peak. The "DAY TARGET reached at 13:08: net 1028" banner also disagrees with the rows: realized P&L was -559 before the 13:09 close and +398 after it.

## Learnings
- **Shorts on squeeze breaks worked from 10:54 to 13:29.**
  - BAJFINANCE 10:54: +957.65
  - ADANIENT 11:24: +746.01
  - ADANIPORTS 13:19: +468.85
  - ADANIPORTS re-entry 13:29: +856.41
  - Together these made Rs 3029. The quick ADANIPORTS re-entry at a larger size (135 vs 63) after a target hit was the best decision of the day.
- **Every entry after 14:00 lost.**
  - BHARTIARTL 14:14: -835.88
  - TCS 14:14: -185.98
  - WIPRO 14:14: -4.03
  - RELIANCE 14:24: -5.44
  - HDFCBANK 14:29: -170.45, flattened at 14:50
  - Together these lost Rs 1202. The three longs opened in the same minute at 14:14 were a correlated bet and were stopped out within 8 to 27 minutes. Late-day breakouts had no time to reach their 2x-stop targets before the 14:50 flat, so they could only end at the stop or as a small flat loss.
- **Old positions held past the morning move turned into stops.**
  - HINDALCO short 11:19 was stopped at 14:13 for -628.70.
  - HINDUNILVR long 11:59 was stopped at 14:43 for -412.45.
  - Both sat for about 3 hours without reaching target, then gave a full loss. A position that has not moved in 90+ minutes is no longer a squeeze break.
- **Several entries were too small to matter.**
  - ONGC qty 1 (+0.47), RELIANCE qty 1, KOTAKBANK qty 2, TATASTEEL qty 10 and WIPRO qty 8 each moved P&L by less than Rs 8.
  - Charges took most of the gross (ONGC gained 0.72 gross for 0.47 net), and each one still used up a slot and an entry signal.
- **Stops filled at the stop price except one.** RELIANCE had a 1.96 slip (stop 1380.99, fill 1382.95). On HCLTECH, a 6.6-point stop on 76 shares cost -551.93, which is close to the size of the average win. With a target about 2x the stop, a 5W/10L day only roughly breaks even, which is what happened.

## Try next
- **Entry cutoff:** no new entries after 14:00. Today that alone would have kept Rs +1404 instead of Rs +224.
- **Time stop:** close any position that has not reached target within 90 minutes of entry (would have exited HINDALCO and HINDUNILVR well before their stops).
- **Lock-in and minimum size:**
  - After day net reaches the Rs 1000 target, stop opening new trades once realized P&L falls 40% below the day's peak.
  - Skip any entry with notional under Rs 20000.
  - Leave squeeze, stop_atr and target_atr unchanged, since today's losses came from entry timing, not the stop/target geometry.

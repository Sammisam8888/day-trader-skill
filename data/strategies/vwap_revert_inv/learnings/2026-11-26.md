# Inverted vwap_revert hit the Rs 1000 target by 11:14, kept trading, and lost Rs 3789 after that

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The inverted copy traded with the morning trend and did well. It went mostly long from 09:50 to 11:05 (NTPC, RELIANCE, ADANIENT, ULTRACEMCO, HINDALCO, SUNPHARMA) and reached the day target of Rs 1001 at 11:14. It peaked at Rs 1396 at 11:56.

The strategy then kept trading. A broad reversal around 13:30 hit its stale longs, and late shorts were caught by an afternoon bounce. The day closed at Rs -2788 net: 41 wins and 22 losses out of 63 closes.

The summary line in the input ("63 won, net 112094, best 12820, worst 163") does not match the log and should be treated as a reporting bug. From the log:
- Best trade: Rs +225.71 (ITC 13:24).
- Worst trade: Rs -640.80 (COALINDIA 14:30).

## Learnings
- **The payoff shape loses money even at a 65% win rate.** Inverting the strategy puts the original's VWAP target behind each trade as a wide stop, and the near band in front as a tight target.
  - Average win was about Rs 95 and average loss about Rs 304, so break-even needs about a 76% hit rate.
  - Example: TECHM 09:42 had a stop 7.95 points away and a target 3.71 points away, and lost Rs 547.74.
- **The 13:30 reversal did the most damage.** HINDUNILVR (13:30, -532), RELIANCE (13:31, -537) and SUNPHARMA (13:32, -555) were all opened 11:04-11:06 and held for about 2.5 hours. Together they lost Rs 1624 in three minutes. Positions opened against a trend that had already run were slow to reach target and fully exposed when the trend turned.
- **Continuing past the day target cost Rs 3789.** Net went from +1001 at 11:14 to -2788 at the close. Every afternoon stop came from positions opened after 11:00 or carried past it, including:
  - TCS 13:16 (-516)
  - COALINDIA 14:30 (-641)
  - LT 14:43 (-531)
- **Late shorts after 13:30 failed into the bounce.** BANKBEES, AXISBANK, ICICIBANK, TRENT and KOTAKBANK lost about Rs 1220 combined between the 14:26 stop and the 14:50 flatten.
  - BANKBEES had won three quick re-entries (13:52-13:59, about +200 total).
  - The fourth re-entry at 13:59 then gave back Rs 339.
- **Sizing was inconsistent.** Many entries used qty 1-3 (HDFCBANK x3, SBIN x1, TATASTEEL x2, ICICIBANK x2), which produced Rs 1-7 wins that barely cover charges. Full-size entries took the large losses.
- **The morning was the real edge.** From 09:50 to 11:05, NTPC re-entries (+140, +216, +144) and the RELIANCE ladder (+129, +119, +157) worked because the market was trending steadily up.

## Try next
- **Lower stop_sd from its current value to 0.75.** This brings the average loss closer to the average win. Today's 13:30 cluster would have stopped out at roughly half the cost.
- **Add a filter: stop opening new entries once the day target is hit,** or at least once net falls back below 50% of peak (here, below about Rs 700, which happened at 13:12). Also block new entries after 13:30.
- **Add a filter: close any position not at target within 60 minutes.** Re-entries on the same stock should also need a fresh signal after a stop, not just a fill on the target.

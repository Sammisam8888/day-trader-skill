# Inverted vwap_revert made money only on the short side; its five losing longs cost ~Rs 2,200 on a falling day

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
Large caps drifted lower all session:
- ICICIBANK fell from 1615 to 1590.
- SBIN fell from 1144 to 1126.
- BANKBEES fell from 661 to 655.
- ULTRACEMCO fell from 12762 to 12516.
- MARUTI fell from 13941 to 13775.

Because this copy inverts the signal, it behaved as a trend follower. It closed 62 trades: 50 winners, 11 losers and 1 flat, for a net of about Rs +2,698 after charges. The best trade was MARUTI +280.19 at 12:36. The worst was BANKBEES -588.67 at 10:25.

The supplied "Day numbers" line (62/62 won, net Rs 206,764, best 13,913, worst 165) does not match the log. It looks like a summary bug, possibly summing prices instead of P&L, and should be fixed before it feeds any tuning.

## Learnings
- **Shorts carried the day.** The 51 short trades netted about +4,300. The 11 long trades netted about -1,600.
  - Repeated short re-entries in falling names kept paying: ADANIENT 7 trades, ULTRACEMCO 6, MARUTI 4, BAJFINANCE 4, ADANIPORTS 5. Each re-entered the same move one leg lower.
- **Five long stop-outs did almost all the damage, about -2,200 combined.** Each was a long into names that kept falling:
  - ICICIBANK -562.74 at 10:02
  - BANKBEES -588.67 at 10:25
  - HDFCBANK -571.54 at 10:59
  - HINDUNILVR -374.85 at 10:18
  - HINDUNILVR -101.84 at 14:43
  - Three of these were bank/bank-ETF longs opened while SBIN, ICICIBANK and BANKBEES were all making lower lows. The HDFCBANK long at 10:10 was a third re-entry after two small target wins of 44.59 and 102.62. One stop erased both wins and more.
- **Reward to risk is upside down, so the high win rate hides fragility.** Typical setup distances were:

  | Trade | Target distance | Stop distance |
  |---|---|---|
  | ICICIBANK long 09:39 | 2.4 pts | 7.1 pts |
  | BANKBEES long 09:44 | 0.9 pts | 2.1 pts |
  | ADANIPORTS short 11:03 | 5.7 pts | 23.8 pts |

  One stop costs 3 to 6 average wins. By 10:59 the day was at -1,352 despite more wins than losses.
- **Shorts got stopped only when the trend reversed.** The 11:42 JSWSTEEL short lost 212.78 at 14:20 as JSWSTEEL rallied to 1321. Re-entering after a target is fine while the move continues; it fails at the turn.
- **Tiny positions were pure noise after charges.**
  - KOTAKBANK 1 share: +0.86
  - BANKBEES 1 share: 0.00
  - TECHM 1 share: +6.26
  - TATASTEEL 7 shares: -2.84
  - KOTAKBANK 2 shares: -3.26

  These used slots and added nothing.
- **Continuing past the day target helped today.** Net went from about 1,063 at 12:56 to 2,698. The 12 forced closes at 14:50 netted about +256. This was a trend-day result and should not be assumed in general.

## Try next

If the side knob maps to 0=both / 1=long-only / 2=short-only, set side=2 on such days, driven by the filter rather than by hindsight. Today this would have skipped all five big long stop-outs.
- **Fix the payoff asymmetry.** Try stop_sd=1.0 (or the current value minus 0.5, staying at least 0.5) and band_sd=2.0. Judge it by average loss divided by average win, not by win rate. Also skip a third re-entry in the same stock after a stop-out on that side, as with HDFCBANK at 10:10.
- **Add a minimum-size filter.** Skip any trade whose qty times target distance is under about Rs 50 of expected gross, such as KOTAKBANK 1-2 shares, BANKBEES 1, TECHM 1 and TATASTEEL 7. Separately, fix the day-summary calculation so it matches the trade log.

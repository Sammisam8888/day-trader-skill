# Two early IT calls (TCS, HCLTECH) made Rs 11,790; the three entries after 13:45 lost Rs 2,614 net

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy started late, with its first entry at 13:20. IT names rallied into the close: the TCS 2560C premium rose 18% (100.70 to 119.10) and the HCLTECH 1420C premium rose 36% (53.55 to 73.05). HINDUNILVR fell, taking its 2160C premium down 17%. The log shows 5 closed trades, 3 won and 2 lost, and net Rs +9,176 after about Rs 320 of charges. The footer line ("5 won, net Rs 286, best 119, worst 8") contradicts the log. Its "best 119" and "worst 8" match option prices (TCS exit 119.10, POWERGRID entry 8.05), not rupee P&L, so the summary generator looks broken. Four signals (INFY, POWERGRID, NTPC twice, TECHM) were skipped because the option quote lookup returned the NSE equity token instead of the NFO contract.

## Learnings
- The day's whole profit came from the two earliest entries. TCS at 13:20 made +4,067 and HCLTECH at 13:22 made +7,723. Both were near-ATM calls on names already trending, held to the 14:50 flat. Neither had a stop or target recorded, so the trend was allowed to run.
- Entries after the 13:37 "day target" made a combined -2,614:
  - HINDUNILVR at 13:46: -2,744
  - POWERGRID at 13:48: -332
  - JSWSTEEL at 13:57: +463

  These were late VWAP-trend signals with about an hour left. HINDUNILVR was bought as an OTM call (spot 2151.60 vs strike 2160) and never recovered.
- Stops behaved inconsistently:
  - POWERGRID was stopped at 13:50, two minutes after entry, on a 0.15 premium move (-1.9%, three ticks). Charges of about Rs 47 made up 14% of that loss. That is noise, not a signal failure.
  - HINDUNILVR lost 9 points of premium (-17%) without any stop firing and was only closed by the 14:50 flat.
  - The stop and target columns are blank for every trade, so the log cannot show which level was active.
- The 13:37 day-target message (net 1202) appeared before any trade had closed, so it was triggered on unrealized P&L. The running "day so far" after the 13:50 POWERGRID close then shows -332, which ignores those open gains. The target logic and the realized tally use different bases.
- Capital caps blocked later signals: RELIANCE at 14:11 (Rs 23,300 per lot) and ULTRACEMCO at 14:28 (Rs 18,340). About Rs 87,400 of the Rs 100,000 wallet was already tied up in four open premiums. JSWSTEEL used Rs 27,337 of that, for +463.
- The NFO token resolution bug cost at least four signals. Their outcome is unknown, but since this was a trend day in large caps, the missed POWERGRID and INFY entries matter.

## Try next
- Filter: no new entries after 13:45. On this day that would have kept +11,790 and avoided HINDUNILVR, POWERGRID and JSWSTEEL, a net improvement of +2,614. Also, only buy calls with strike at or below spot plus half a strike step (no OTM entries like HINDUNILVR 2160 at spot 2151.60).
- Set stop_atr to 3.0, computed on the underlying rather than the premium, and log the stop and target levels on every open. POWERGRID's three-tick stop is too tight for a 1-lot option, and HINDUNILVR's -17% with no stop shows the stop is not being applied the same way on every trade.
- Fix the NFO contract token lookup and the day summary generator (win count, net, best and worst should come from net Rs, not prices), and run the day target on realized P&L, before trusting any further tuning of the confirm or target_atr knobs.

# Net was Rs +3672, not Rs 470: 8 of 12 trades won, and 10 signals were skipped (5 bad option lookups, 5 too costly)

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy closed 12 option trades. Summing the per-trade net column gives Rs +3672 after charges: 8 winners, 4 losers, best Rs +2526 (KOTAKBANK put), worst Rs -2980 (TECHM call). The "Day numbers" footer (12/12 won, net 470, best 106, worst 6) does not match this log and should not be trusted. Put-side signals carried the day: 6 of 8 puts made money. Three of the four losses were fast stop-outs, all within 31 minutes of entry. The other loss, COALINDIA, was held to the 14:50 flat.

## Learnings
- **Option lookup is broken.** Five signals were skipped because a request for an NFO token returned the NSE equity instead. These were HINDUNILVR 09:43, SUNPHARMA 09:44, TCS call 10:19, POWERGRID 10:20 and HDFCBANK call 11:22. This is a data bug, not a strategy decision.
- **Capital ran out by mid-morning.** At 10:25 about Rs 95.4k was tied up in LT, KOTAKBANK, INFY, COALINDIA and BAJFINANCE premium. So ICICIBANK (Rs 28245) and NTPC (Rs 12450) were skipped, followed by HDFCBANK at 10:59 and 11:05. A cheap ONGC put (Rs 9990) was also skipped at 09:58.
- **Slow trades held to 14:50 paid well.** LT put (Rs +1240), INFY put (+749), ONGC call (+1371) and TCS call (+186) never hit stop or target. Together with COALINDIA (-984), the five 14:50 closes netted Rs +2562. LT tied up Rs 17k for 5 hours, which also fed the capital squeeze above.
- **Biggest loss came from the biggest ticket.** The TECHM call at 09:44 cost Rs 39,600, which is 40% of the wallet. It stopped out at 10:02 for Rs -2980 on a 7.3% premium drop. That one trade wiped out more than the NTPC +1231 target hit just before it.
- **IT signals flipped on short timeframes.** TCS put opened 10:04 and stopped at 10:17 (-1123), then a TCS call opened at 10:36 and earned only +186. TECHM and WIPRO calls (10:58 to 11:29, -1111) both stopped out, while the INFY put won. The underlying trend on IT names was not stable enough for the trend signal.
- **TATASTEEL target was too tight.** The 10:52 trade hit target on a 5.65 to 5.77 move (2.1%) and netted only Rs +281. That is a poor payoff next to the 5-7% premium losses on stopped trades. The ATR target on a low-priced underlying translates into very little option movement.

## Try next
- **Fix the token lookup:** if the returned exchange is not NFO, retry or resolve by option symbol. Log the stop and target levels on every open; those columns are currently blank.
- **Cap premium per trade** at about Rs 25k (25% of the wallet), which would have skipped or resized the TECHM 39.6k entry. Also skip any trade whose premium-percent move to target is under 5%, as with TATASTEEL.
- **Raise confirm by 1 (e.g. 2 to 3)** to filter the quick reversals that stopped TECHM, TCS put and WIPRO within 31 minutes. Leave stop_atr and target_atr unchanged until more days confirm this; one day is a thin sample.

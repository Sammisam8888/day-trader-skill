## Learnings
- **One correlated cluster wiped out the morning.**
  - At 11:49 to 11:54 the strategy held three IT shorts at once: TCS (68 shares), INFY (69) and WIPRO (436).
  - All three stopped out together at 12:03:33, for Rs -611, -288 and -247, a total of Rs -1146.
  - This was one sector-wide bounce, not three independent RSI(2) signals.
- **Rs 1754 of the Rs 3656 total losses came from just three trades:** the 12:03 TCS stop, BEL at 11:17 (Rs -550) and COALINDIA at 11:47 (Rs -581).
  - All three were large positions with tight stops.
  - COALINDIA was 664 shares, about Rs 2.56 lakh of exposure on a Rs 1 lakh wallet, with a stop only 0.68 away.
  - Slippage pushed the fills past the stop prices, so the losses ran beyond what was planned.
- **Late entries lost money.** Positions opened after 14:15 netted about Rs -599.
  - INFY long at 14:29 lost Rs -668 (filled at 1159.15 against a 1159.59 stop).
  - RELIANCE short at 14:34 lost Rs -213.
  - ADANIENT short at 14:14 lost Rs -57.
  - HDFCBANK, TITAN and MARUTI made only about Rs 338 between them.
- **INFY flip-flopped all day:** short at 10:54 (won), short at 11:49 (lost), long at 12:44 (won), long at 14:29 (lost). Net Rs -147 on a stock that kept switching between oversold and overbought.
- **Index ETFs and calm large caps reverted cleanly:**
  - NIFTYBEES at 12:19 and 12:34 (Rs +135 and +77).
  - BANKBEES at 12:04 (Rs +363).
  - SUNPHARMA at 13:24 (Rs +541, the best trade).
  - BHARTIARTL twice (Rs +215 and +406).
- **Eight trades were 1 or 2 shares** (WIPRO, NTPC twice, TCS, KOTAKBANK, BEL, TATASTEEL, and similar).
  - They earned Rs -0.64 to +5.46 each.
  - These look like sizing leftovers. They add trade count and charges but no edge.

## Try next
- **Sector cap filter:** allow at most 1 open position per sector (IT, banks, metals). In particular, block a new same-direction entry while a same-sector position is open. This would have avoided the 12:03 triple stop.
- **Time filter:** no new entries after 14:15. Keep the 14:50 flat exit unchanged.
- **Minimum position size:** skip any signal where quantity times price is below Rs 20000.
- **Exposure cap:** limit any single position to about 1.5x wallet exposure. This targets cases like COALINDIA and INFY where tight stops produced oversized positions.
- **Thresholds:** tighten low to 5.0 and high to 95.0 for one day to cut repeat signals in names that keep flipping, like INFY. Leave stop_atr and target_atr unchanged for now.

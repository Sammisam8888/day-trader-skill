# Trading on past the day target turned +2336 into -5703: four late or held losers erased five small option winners

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
Per the trade log, the strategy took 9 option trades. 5 won (+4053) and 4 lost (-9756), for a net of about **Rs -5703** after charges. The supplied "Day numbers" line (9/9 won, net +924, best 440, worst 7) does not match the log and should not be trusted. The day target banner (net 1275 at 10:02) also disagrees with the log's running total of +2336 at 10:00, so the P&L aggregation needs checking. The book peaked at +3430 at 11:20. A single ULTRACEMCO stop and the 14:50 square-off then dragged it deep negative.

## Learnings
- **Early trades worked.** All the winners were opened between 09:32 and 10:09 and closed by 11:20:
  - HINDUNILVR C2120: +1221
  - MARUTI C14000: +1114
  - SBIN P1140: +460
  - HDFCBANK P860: +634
  - POWERGRID P267.5: +623 (held to flat)
- **Late entries all lost.** ULTRACEMCO C12700 (10:31, -4549), RELIANCE C1380 (10:33, -2313) and SUNPHARMA C1900 (12:30, -525) never reached target.
- **Payoff is badly asymmetric on premium.** Targets hit at +1.3% to +8% of premium; SBIN was 29.30 to 30.00. The ULTRACEMCO stop hit at -26% (350.25 to 260.15). That one stop cost more than the four best winners combined.
- **Unstopped trades drift.** KOTAKBANK C505 was opened at 09:31 and drifted from 13.15 to 12.00 for -2369 at flat. Like RELIANCE and SUNPHARMA, it never hit stop or target.
- **The quote resolver is broken.** Five signals were skipped because NFO option tokens returned the NSE equity quote: AXISBANK, ITC, NTPC, COALINDIA and ONGC. Three more were skipped on lot cost (ICICIBANK 23345, BHARTIARTL 25389, BAJFINANCE 30038). The trade sample is therefore biased by plumbing, not by the signal.

## Try next
- **Filter:** no new entries after 10:30, and stop opening trades once the day target is hit. Either rule alone would have kept the day around +2300 to +3400.
- **stop_frac 0.5:** tighten the stop from its current level so a single option stop cannot exceed roughly 2x the typical target win. Optionally pair it with **target_x 2.0** to widen the tiny premium targets.
- **Filter:** close any open position at 12:00 if it has not hit stop or target, instead of holding to 14:50 (KOTAKBANK, RELIANCE, SUNPHARMA). Separately, fix the NFO token lookup and the day-stats aggregation before trusting the next day's numbers.

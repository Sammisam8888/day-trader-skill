# Small option targets and wide premium stops lost Rs 9601 even though the summary reports 8/8 wins

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The day summary says 8 trades, 8 won, net Rs +664, but it contradicts the log. The log shows 8 closes with only 2 winners (HCLTECH +759.73, INFY +444.78). The running total ends at Rs -9601, about 9.6% of the Rs 100000 wallet against a Rs 1000 target. About Rs -428 of that came before 13:44. Losses hit both calls and puts (TECHM P, ICICIBANK P, SUNPHARMA P, WIPRO P, MARUTI C), so the inverted fade lost money in both directions. That points to a structural problem, not a one-sided market move.

## Learnings
- Wins and losses are badly lopsided. The two winners averaged about Rs 600 on a 2.5-4% premium move (HCLTECH 49.70 to 51.75 at 14:00). The two stop-outs averaged about Rs 3100: ICICIBANK P1610 fell 34.85 to 28.90 (-17%, Rs -4226) and TECHM P1800 fell 51.50 to 48.35 (Rs -1971). With this ratio the strategy needs a win rate above 80% just to break even.
- The single worst trade broke the strategy's own cost filter. ICICIBANK was skipped at 13:13 (1 lot cost Rs 23940) and 13:52 (Rs 22645), then opened at 14:02 at a higher cost of Rs 24395. It then lost Rs 4226. Either the cap is not fixed, or it checks free cash instead of a per-trade limit.
- Late entries had no time to work. ICICIBANK, SUNPHARMA and INFY opened between 14:02 and 14:07 and netted Rs -4717 before or at the 14:50 flat. The forced 14:50 exits on MARUTI, WIPRO and SUNPHARMA added Rs -4136 at prices below entry.
- The POWERGRID P267.5 close at 13:58 is logged as "target" but exited at the entry price (7.25 to 7.25, Rs -44.30). The target is being triggered at or below entry, which is a bug or misconfiguration.
- There is a data bug: at 13:13 the RELIANCE P1370 quote request (NFO 132715) returned the NSE equity token 2885, so the signal was skipped. Repeated skips of NIFTYBEES and BANKBEES (no option contracts) are wasted signal checks, and these symbols should be removed from the universe.
- The summary line (8/8 won, +664, worst 6) does not match the log. Until the reporting is fixed, no learning should rely on it.

## Try next
- Set stop_sd to 1.0 (or below whatever value is in use) so option stops are not 6-17% of premium while targets are about 4%. Alternatively, widen the target to at least match the stop distance.
- Add an entry cutoff of 14:00 so no new positions open with less than 50 minutes before the 14:50 flat. Also enforce a fixed per-trade premium cap of Rs 20000 that cannot be bypassed later in the day, as happened with ICICIBANK at 14:02.
- Raise sd to 2.5 so fewer, stronger band breaks qualify. Leave invert unchanged until one more day confirms whether direction or exit sizing is the main problem. Fix the POWERGRID target logic and the RELIANCE token mapping first.

# Reported "10 won, net Rs 708" is wrong: the log shows 3 wins, 7 losses, net Rs -5630, and "target" exits lost money

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy opened 10 option positions. Each was 1 lot, bought as a call or put on an inverted Bollinger signal, with Rs 11.5k-25.5k premium per trade. The ten closes sum to **Rs -5630**: 3 winners (+2134, +836, +678) and 7 losers. The worst was SUNPHARMA at -3543. The summary line ("10 won, net Rs 708, best 399, worst 6") does not match the log. The "DAY TARGET reached at 11:23: net 1552" banner is also suspect, because nothing had closed by 11:23, so that figure was at best unrealized. The strategy kept trading past it and gave back about Rs 7.2k.

## Learnings
- **The exit labels are broken for long options.** Four closes tagged "target" lost money:
  - 12:36 BAJFINANCE P1090: 33.95 -> 33.75, -222
  - 13:07 SUNPHARMA C1920: 45.85 -> 35.85, -3543
  - 13:30 ADANIPORTS P1880: 47.00 -> 43.35, -1795
  - 13:50 ADANIPORTS: the only "target" that actually made money

  The likely cause is that target and stop are evaluated on the underlying in the non-inverted direction, or mapped wrongly to the premium. Until this is fixed, the stop and target behaviour cannot be judged at all.
- **The SUNPHARMA call (10:49-13:07) was the whole problem.** It lost 22% of its premium (Rs 3543) over 2h18m with no stop firing. That single trade is 63% of the day's loss. Option premiums need their own stop. An underlying-SD stop let theta and delta bleed far past the size of the other losers (-400 to -1800).
- **Re-entering the same name right after a stop-out was a coin flip with costs.** ADANIPORTS was stopped at 13:30 for -1795, then re-bought at 13:34 at nearly the same premium (47.10). The re-entry recovered only +836, so the pair netted -959.
- **The only real winners were puts held to the 14:50 flat on a weakening tape:**
  - ICICIBANK P1600: +2134
  - BAJFINANCE P1080: +678

  The inverted SELL signals on banks and financials worked when given time. The fast exits (ITC at 12:30, SBIN at 13:15, both "stop" at -0.45 per share) cost Rs 1212. Charges of about Rs 70 per round trip made these small-tick stops expensive.
- **The quote lookup is broken on many re-entries.** NFO tokens resolved to NSE equity for HDFCBANK, MARUTI, BAJFINANCE (twice), INFY, ITC and SUNPHARMA, so the strategy silently missed trades. There were also 9 budget skips, mostly 1-lot premiums of Rs 17k-32k. The opportunity set is being decided by plumbing, not by the signal.

## Try next
- **Set enabled 0 for the options leg** until exit tagging and the NFO quote lookup are fixed. Then re-check today's 10 trades against correctly computed exits before trusting any P&L summary or day-target banner.
- **Add a premium-based stop filter**: exit if premium falls 12% from entry, regardless of the underlying SD stop. Keep stop_sd at 1.0 for the underlying. At 12%, the SUNPHARMA loss would have been capped at about -1950 instead of -3543.
- **Add a filter blocking re-entry on the same underlying for 20 min after a stop-out** (ADANIPORTS 13:30 -> 13:34). Keep invert 1 and side 0 for now, since the puts that were held (ICICIBANK, BAJFINANCE 1080) were the only clean signal today.

# Rs 280 of charges per futures round trip, against stops and targets of a few ticks, made the day a guaranteed loss (log shows -2718, not +1812)

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The day-numbers line does not match the trade log. It claims 3 wins, net Rs 1812, best 1168 and worst 322. The log actually shows:

- **INFY at 13:08:** lost Rs -1474.97.
- **NTPC at 13:20:** lost Rs -1258.61.
- **NTPC at 14:28:** gained Rs +16.02.

That is 1 win and 2 losses, net Rs -2717.56, best +16 and worst -1475. The summary seems to have reported absolute prices or values instead of signed P&L, so it should be fixed before any later analysis relies on it.

All three entries came after 13:00. Each one used a single lot that tied up 94-97% of the Rs 100000 wallet. That blocked 7 of the 9 skipped signals for lack of margin, plus one TCS skip with Rs 98525 free. The Rs 1000 day target was never close.

## Learnings
- **Charges took about Rs 270-290 per round trip.** INFY lost 3.00 x 400 = 1200 gross and netted -1475. NTPC at 13:20 lost 0.65 x 1500 = 975 and netted -1259. NTPC at 14:28 made 0.20 x 1500 = 300 and netted only +16. Breakeven is about 0.19/share on NTPC and about 0.70/share on INFY.
- **The 14:28 NTPC target sat only 0.20 above entry,** barely more than the breakeven move. The 13:08 INFY stop sat 3.00 (0.26%) below entry and the 13:20 NTPC stop 0.65 (0.20%) below. These are noise-sized levels. Either stop_atr and target_atr are at the low end, or the ATR comes from a quiet intraday window. Neither the stop nor the target column was logged, so I am inferring the levels from the exit prices.
- **Signals are priced on the spot stock but filled on the future.** INFY signalled at 1162.20 and filled at 1171.00 (+8.80). NTPC signalled at 320.30 and filled at 322.35 (+2.05), then at 320.15 and filled at 322.00 (+1.85). If stop and target levels come from spot ATR but are checked against the futures price, the risk geometry is skewed by the basis, so this needs checking in code.
- **The POWERGRID skip at 13:08:03 was a contract-lookup bug:** it asked for NFO 59090 and got NSE 14977 POWERGRID-EQ. By 13:48 the lookup resolved, so the token mapping is intermittent.
- **One lot equal to the whole wallet means one position at a time.** That turns the day into 2-3 coin flips, each paying full charges, with no diversification. It also explains the HCLTECH, HINDUNILVR, POWERGRID and JSWSTEEL skips while NTPC was open.
- **Re-entering NTPC at 14:28,** 27 minutes after its stop at 14:01, on almost the same signal (320.15 vs 320.30) won only because the target was tiny, not because the edge was real.

## Try next
- **Widen the stop and target:** stop_atr 3.0 and target_atr 7.0, so the target distance is at least 4x the per-share charge breakeven. Add a filter that skips any entry where (target - entry) x qty < 4 x estimated round-trip charges, which is about Rs 1100 at today's sizes.
- **Require stronger confirmation:** confirm 4, plus a filter blocking new entries after 14:15 and blocking re-entry on the same symbol within 60 minutes of a stop-out. Today that rule would have blocked the 14:28 NTPC trade.
- **Fix the plumbing before trusting results:**
  - Compute stop and target from the futures price, not spot.
  - Log the stop and target values on every open.
  - Fix the NFO token lookup.
  - Correct the day-summary P&L sign bug.

# Unprotected single trade: NTPC future was held with no stop and lost Rs 4783 (the Rs 321 "win" in the summary is wrong)

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy opened one trade: BUY 1 lot (1500) of NTPC29DEC26F at 09:46, filled at 323.90. That trade used Rs 97170 of the Rs 100000 wallet. It had no stop and no target and was flattened at 14:50 at 320.90, a loss of Rs 4500 gross and Rs 4783.08 net after about Rs 283 of charges. The day summary line ("1 won, net Rs 321") does not match the close row; the close row's numbers (3.00 x 1500 + charges) confirm a loss of Rs -4783. The "DAY TARGET reached at 09:47: net 2113" banner was triggered by unrealized P&L on the open NTPC position, not by booked profit.

## Learnings
- The NTPC entry at 09:46 filled at 323.90 against a signal price of 321.95. That is 1.95 points (0.6%) of slippage, or Rs 2925 on 1500 qty, about 61% of the final loss, paid before the trade could move.
- The NTPC trade had blank stop and target fields. The position drifted under entry and sat for about 5 hours until the 14:50 forced flat. A 2.0 ATR stop would have capped the loss much earlier.
- The day-target check counted open, unrealized P&L (2113 at 09:47) as "reached". The target logic should only count closed trades, or it should lock in profit with a trailing stop when the target is hit.
- One trade using 97% of margin left free cash at Rs 2830. That blocked 10 later margin-limited signals (KOTAKBANK 09:48, INFY 09:58, TCS 10:04 and 10:36, BAJFINANCE 10:20, TATASTEEL 10:52, WIPRO 10:58, HDFCBANK 10:59, ONGC 11:42). Four of these (SUNPHARMA, TECHM, LT, KOTAKBANK) need more than the whole wallet anyway; with a Rs 100000 wallet, this futures universe effectively allows one position per day.
- Contract lookup is broken for several names. HINDUNILVR 09:43, ONGC 09:58, COALINDIA 10:08, TCS 10:19, POWERGRID 10:20, ICICIBANK 10:25 and HDFCBANK 11:05/11:22 asked for NFO tokens and got NSE equity tokens. These 8 skips were infrastructure failures, not strategy decisions.
- The P&L summary generator is unreliable: it reported 1 win / +321 for a -4783 trade. Any tuning based on that summary today would point the wrong way.

## Try next
- Set stop_atr = 2.0 and target_atr = 4.0, and check that the order actually carries a stop. Today's NTPC row shows none, so verify the stop is attached, not just configured.
- Add a fill filter: cancel or skip the entry if the fill differs from the signal price by more than 0.25%. This would have skipped NTPC at 323.90 vs 321.95.
- Add a filter that blocks entries needing more than 60% of wallet margin until the wallet is raised. Separately, fix the NFO token mapping and the day-summary P&L before trusting another day's results; set enabled = 0 if those fixes are not in place.

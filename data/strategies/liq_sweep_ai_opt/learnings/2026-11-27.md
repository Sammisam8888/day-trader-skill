# Net Rs 5,547 on 6 of 9 trades, earned by long holds on two puts; the day-summary line in the log is wrong

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy bought 1-lot options on 9 liquidity-sweep signals. Seven were puts on SELL signals and two were calls on BUY signals. Six trades won and three lost, for a net of Rs 5,547.01 after charges. That is 5.5x the Rs 1,000 day target. The day was carried by TECHM P1840 (+2,931, held 11:26 to 14:41) and WIPRO P192.5 (+1,356). The biggest drag was POWERGRID C267.5 (-902), which was held to the 14:50 flat. The footer "9 won, net 701, best 434, worst 6" does not match the trade rows. Neither does the banner "DAY TARGET reached at 10:51: net 1079", since the running total was 208 at 10:47 and 385 at 11:02. The real day target was crossed at 11:20 with the MARUTI close (1,501).

## Learnings
- **Patience paid on puts.** TECHM P1840 needed 3h15m to reach target and made Rs 2,931, which is 53% of the day. MARUTI P14000 (+1,115 in 48 min) and WIPRO P192.5 (+1,356 in 56 min) also ran. These were among the higher-premium contracts held (Rs 34k, 20.5k and 16.7k).
- **Exit labels on the underlying did not match option P&L.** BAJFINANCE closed on "target" at 10:47 but lost Rs 186, because the premium went from 34.80 to 34.65. TRENT closed on "stop" at 11:02 but made Rs 177. NTPC stopped at 12:07 for -115 on a 0.05 premium move. These targets and stops were too close to entry for the option's delta, spread and charges to turn an underlying move into money. ITC (+394 on a 0.25 move) only worked because the lot size was large (1,725).
- **Calls were net negative (-126 over 2 trades).** HINDALCO C920 (+775) and POWERGRID C267.5 (-902) both sat until the 14:50 flat without reaching target or stop. Puts made +5,673 over 7 trades. Two calls is too small a sample to switch the side off, but nothing today argued for them.
- **Signals were lost to plumbing, not the market.** ICICIBANK (10:18) and TITAN (11:10) were skipped because the NFO token lookup returned the NSE equity instead (99172 became 4963 ICICIBANK-EQ, 147846 became 3506 TITAN-EQ). BANKBEES (12:07) was a signal on an instrument that has no options.
- **Capital capped the strategy before signal quality did.** Up to five lots were open at once; at 11:26 about Rs 100k of premium was deployed. Because of that, TATASTEEL (Rs 18,865), KOTAKBANK (Rs 28,600) and BAJFINANCE C1100 (Rs 27,038) were skipped. A single TECHM lot alone took Rs 34k.

## Try next
- **Raise target_r by 0.5 from today's value (to at least 2.0).** This lets the stop and target distances on the underlying produce a premium move that clears spread and charges. Today's BAJFINANCE and TRENT trades had exit labels opposite in sign to their P&L.
- **Filter on contract resolution.** Skip any signal whose resolved instrument is not on NFO, and fix the symbol-to-token map behind the ICICIBANK and TITAN misses. Drop non-optionable names such as BANKBEES from the scan universe.
- **Filter on position size.** Cap premium per trade at about Rs 20,000, or 20% of the wallet. Today that would have skipped TECHM, the best trade, so treat it as a trade-off between more concurrent signals and fewer large winners. Leave side and time-stop unchanged: TECHM's 14:41 target argues against an early time stop.

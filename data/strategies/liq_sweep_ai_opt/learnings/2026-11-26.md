# Five calls bought in 7 minutes were one correlated long bet with no stops; HCLTECH made 64% of the profit

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
Between 13:14 and 13:21 the strategy fired BUY signals on NTPC, TATASTEEL, INFY, BEL and HCLTECH. It bought one lot of the Dec-29 call on each. Together these used Rs 91,116 of the Rs 100,000 wallet, so COALINDIA was skipped at 13:22 because its lot cost Rs 15,188. The day target was flagged at 13:39 on a net of Rs 1,160, which must have been unrealized because nothing had closed yet. All five positions were then held to the 14:50 flat and every one closed green, for a log total of Rs 12,452 net. The summary line ("net Rs 157, best 73, worst 7") contradicts the log. 73 and 7 match the HCLTECH and TATASTEEL exit prices, not P&L, so the day-stats calculation looks broken and needs fixing before these numbers feed any tuning.

## Learnings
- **One trade drove the day.** HCLTECH (13:21, 1420C bought at 52.85, sold at 73.05) made Rs 8,003, a 38% premium gain and 64% of the day's net. INFY (13:17, 46.90 to 54.00, +15%) added Rs 2,779. The other three together made only Rs 1,670.
- **The low-premium-move legs barely cleared costs.** BEL (+2.3% on premium) netted Rs 371 after about Rs 56 of charges. TATASTEEL (+3.0%) netted Rs 519 after about Rs 58. A sweep signal that only moves spot a fraction of a percent pays very little through a near-ATM monthly option.
- **This was not five independent wins.** All five signals came in a 7-minute window in the same direction, so it was one bet on a broad afternoon up-move. On a day where that move fails, all five would lose together on about 91% of capital. Nothing in the log shows a stop or target on any leg.
- **Exits were purely time-based.** The stop and target columns are empty for every trade, so target_r never acted. Holding to 14:50 happened to capture the full HCLTECH run, but that was luck of the tape, not a rule working.
- **The capital-first sizing was arbitrary.** COALINDIA was skipped only because it came 6th, not because its setup was weaker.
- **Continuing past the day target was harmless today, but only because no new trades opened.** The net went from Rs 1,160 (MTM) at 13:39 to Rs 12,452 by simply holding. That cannot be credited to the "keeps trading" setting.

## Try next
- **Put a real exit on every option leg.** Map the sweep extreme on the underlying as the stop, and use target_r = 2.0 on the underlying as the target, instead of relying only on the 14:50 flat. Then compare against today's time-exit result to see whether target_r would have cut HCLTECH short. If it would have, test target_r = 3.0.
- **Add a correlation filter.** Allow at most 3 concurrent same-direction positions, opened within any 15-minute window, and cap them at 60% of the wallet. When signals compete, rank them by sweep depth (sweep_pct) rather than arrival order.
- **Fix the day-summary calculation.** Best and worst should be trade P&L, and net should match the sum of the closed trades. Until that is fixed, do not change lookback, touches, tol_pct or sweep_pct based on this day: 5/5 wins on one correlated move is a single data point.

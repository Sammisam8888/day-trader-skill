# Margin blocked 6 of 7 signals; the one trade that filled paid more in entry slippage than it kept as net profit

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy produced 8 signals across 7 names, but 7 of them (on 6 stocks) were skipped because one futures lot needed Rs 116878-201840 of margin against Rs 100000 free. The only trade was INFY29DEC26F long, 400 qty. It was signalled at 1159.40, filled at 1162.80 at 13:34, and closed at target at 1166.70 at 14:09, booking Rs 1285.44 net on the trade line. The day summary reports Rs 1167 net, and the log disagrees with itself on this (see below). With one trade, the day says almost nothing about the squeeze_break edge itself.

## Learnings
- Capital, not the signal, decided the day. BAJFINANCE (10:54), HINDALCO (11:19), HINDUNILVR (11:59), KOTAKBANK (12:34), ADANIPORTS (13:19) and TCS (14:14, 14:29) were all skipped because their lot margin was 1.17x to 2.02x the wallet. INFY (Rs 93024) was the only lot that fit, so its trade was a margin accident, not a selection.
- Even after the INFY win, the wallet (Rs 101285) was still about Rs 15600 short for TCS at 14:14 and 14:29. Winning will not grow the account into these lots on any useful timescale.
- The INFY entry filled 3.40 points (about 0.29%) above the 1159.40 signal price. That cost about Rs 1360 on 400 qty, more than the trade's net profit.
- The INFY exit at 1166.70 was only 3.90 points above the fill but 7.30 above the signal. The target appears anchored to the signal price, so fill slippage directly ate half the planned move.
- The bookkeeping is inconsistent and should be checked before trusting any P&L:
  - Gross on the trade is (1166.70 - 1162.80) x 400 = Rs 1560.
  - The trade line implies about Rs 275 in charges, while the day summary (Rs 1167) implies about Rs 393.
  - "DAY TARGET reached at 14:08: net 1006" is stamped before the 14:09:39 close, so it was likely an unrealised mark.
- The stop and target columns are blank on the open row, so the log cannot verify the risk that was actually taken on INFY.

## Try next
- Add a universe filter: only scan futures whose 1-lot margin is at most 0.9 x free capital. On today's prices that leaves roughly INFY-class names only. Alternatively, run this recipe on cash equity so the 6 skipped names become tradable. Either way, stop counting margin skips as missed signals.
- Add a fill-quality filter: skip the entry if the fill is more than 0.15% away from the signal price. Also anchor the target and stop to the actual fill price, not the signal price.
- Keep squeeze, stop_atr, target_atr, side and invert unchanged for now (leave enabled = 1). One winning trade is no evidence to tune them. Revisit after about 20 filled trades.

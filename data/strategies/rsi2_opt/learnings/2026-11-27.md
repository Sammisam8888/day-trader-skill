## Learnings
- **"target" exits that lost money.** Three closes were tagged "target" but lost:
  - TECHM P1860 at 11:15 (-797)
  - KOTAKBANK P500 at 12:09 (-766)
  - BEL P410 at 12:46 (-2255, premium 11.50 to 9.95)
  
  The target appears to be checked on the underlying's ATR move, not on the option premium. The option can lose value through time decay, an IV drop, or the bid-ask spread even when the stock reaches its level. This single defect cost Rs 3818.
- **Holding paid.** The only large wins came from positions that ran to the 14:50 flat: BAJFINANCE (+4309, held 3h46m) and ICICIBANK (+2414, held 2h46m).
- **Quick exits were too small.** Most target hits were 3-8 minute scalps of Rs 300-1200, such as HINDALCO at 10:57 (+400) and MARUTI at 14:24 (+326). The current target_atr is too tight for 1-lot options, and charges (about Rs 50-60 per round trip) take a meaningful share.
- **Cheap premiums with big lots hurt.** WIPRO P192.5 at 5.97 with 3000 qty lost Rs 1162 on a 0.37 premium move (stopped at 12:03). The underlying barely moved; spread and tick size dominated.
- **BUY-side calls did poorly.** The three calls netted -1631: INFY C1154 (+345), HINDUNILVR C2140 (-1263, stop at 14:00) and INFY C1160 (-713, stop at 14:40). On a day of financial weakness, the long-call side fought the tape. The INFY C1154 strike also looks non-standard and is worth checking against the contract master.
- **Plumbing problems:**
  - SBIN and HDFCBANK option quotes resolved to the equity token (for example, "asked for NFO 137221, got NSE 3045"). This is a token-mapping bug that skipped 3 signals.
  - BANKBEES and NIFTYBEES have no options, yet were evaluated 4 times.
  - Six signals were skipped for capital because the 10:54 batch (TECHM alone Rs 40920) used 94% of the wallet.

## Try next
- **Widen the target, keep the stop.** Set target_atr to 3.0 with stop_atr at 1.5, so winners can run toward the BAJFINANCE/ICICIBANK pattern instead of closing at +1 to +2 premium points. Separately, require option premium above entry plus charges before an exit can be tagged "target"; otherwise route it as a stop or keep holding.
- **Add filters.**
  - Skip contracts with premium below Rs 15, which would have excluded WIPRO at 5.97.
  - Cap premium per position at 25% of the wallet, which would have excluded TECHM at Rs 40920.
  - Drop ETFs (BANKBEES, NIFTYBEES) from the options universe.
  - Fix the NFO token lookup for SBIN and HDFCBANK.
- **Test puts only.** Run side=1 for a few sessions to see whether the call side is consistently the drag, as it was today (-1631).

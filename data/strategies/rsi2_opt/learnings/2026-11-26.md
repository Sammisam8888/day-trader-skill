# Log shows about Rs -3,300 with 6 wins and 6 losses, not "12 won, Rs +467"; put-side (SELL-signal) trades caused the loss

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy traded only after 13:08 and closed 12 option trades: 6 winners (Rs +3,831) and 6 losers (Rs -7,103). Summed per-trade net is Rs -3,272, but the log's own running "day so far" ends at Rs -3,548. That Rs 275 gap starts at the first close: ADANIENT netted +498.62 while "day so far" shows 223. So the day missed the Rs 1,000 target by about Rs 4,300. The summary line ("12 won, net Rs 467, best 106, worst 6") matches none of the trade rows and should not be trusted. Five more signals (JSWSTEEL, RELIANCE, BAJFINANCE, TECHM call, KOTAKBANK, HDFCBANK) were lost to a quote bug where NFO tokens came back as NSE cash quotes.

## Learnings
- **Puts were the problem.** The 8 SELL-signal puts netted Rs -4,661: 3 wins (ADANIENT 13:14, ITC 13:48, BHARTIARTL 13:47) against 5 losses (TECHM 13:28 -1,656, WIPRO 13:29 -713, AXISBANK 14:07 -1,091, POWERGRID 14:28 -614, SBIN 14:07 -2,237). The 4 BUY-signal calls netted Rs +1,389: HINDALCO +1,265, INFY +859, TRENT +56, NTPC -792. Overbought fades did not revert this afternoon; oversold bounces mostly did.
- **Losses were about twice the size of wins** (average -1,184 vs +638). Targets were hit at small premium gains (ADANIENT +2.2%, ITC +4.4%), while stops cost 3-5% on larger premium (TECHM Rs 36,840 at risk, the biggest single ticket). The ATR stop and target translate unevenly into option premium.
- **SBIN P1140 (14:07) looks like a stop failure.** It never hit a stop and was flattened at 14:50 for -9.4% of premium (Rs -2,237). That is more than double any stopped loss on a similar ticket. Either the stop was not monitored for this position or the underlying stop was too wide. This one trade alone wiped out HINDALCO plus INFY.
- **The quote resolver is broken.** It is returning NSE equity tokens for NFO option tokens. This cost 6 skipped signals (13:08 JSWSTEEL, 13:42 RELIANCE, 13:47 BAJFINANCE, 14:21 TECHM, 14:22 KOTAKBANK, 14:27 HDFCBANK) and makes today's sample unrepresentative. NIFTYBEES and BANKBEES should simply be filtered out, since they have no options.
- **Stacking entries clustered the losses.** At 13:28-13:29 and again at 14:07 the strategy opened two puts at once (TECHM+WIPRO, SBIN+AXISBANK). Each pair lost together (Rs -2,369 and Rs -3,328), which suggests they were one correlated bet, not two independent ones.

## Try next
- Make SELL entries much stricter: high 92.0 (up from the default range middle), or set side to long-only for the next sessions until puts show an edge.
- Tighten and verify the option stop: stop_atr 1.0, and add a hard premium stop of -6% so a position like SBIN cannot reach -9% before the 14:50 flat. Keep target_atr at 2.0 or higher so wins are not smaller than stops.
- Filter: allow at most one open put at a time, and skip any symbol with no F&O contract (NIFTYBEES, BANKBEES). Also fix the NFO-to-NSE token mismatch and the day-summary aggregator before judging this strategy's results again.

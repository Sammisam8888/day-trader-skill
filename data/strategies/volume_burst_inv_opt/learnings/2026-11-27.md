# No premium stop: MARUTI 14000CE was held 5 hours to a -24% premium loss and erased the day

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy bought 12 single-name options, all Dec-26 expiry. It booked Rs 3607 by 11:15 from quick target exits, led by NTPC 322.5PE (+2338) and LT 4100CE (+532). It then gave all of that back, plus more. ITC 305CE was stopped out at -1767. At the 14:50 flatten MARUTI 14000CE (-5096), INFY 1154CE (-1094) and KOTAKBANK 500PE (-564) were closed at a loss, partly offset by RELIANCE 1390PE (+2716). Per the trade rows the day ended at Rs -2175 net, with 7 winners and 5 losers.

The footer ("12 won, net Rs 1055, best 320, worst 7") and the "DAY TARGET reached at 10:08: net 2350" banner are both wrong:
- Summing the net column gives -2174.87.
- At 10:08 only the LT trade (+532) had closed; NTPC did not close until 10:30.
- The reporting code needs fixing before these summaries are trusted.

## Learnings
- **Exits are asymmetric, and that decided the day.** Winners were cut at small premium gains: ULTRACEMCO +2.0%, HINDALCO +3.2%, LT 4100CE +2.8%. Losers had no premium stop and rode to 14:50.
  - MARUTI 14000CE (open 09:43, 417.90 -> 317.00) lost more than the seven winners made combined.
  - INFY 1154CE (open 10:03) and KOTAKBANK 500PE (open 11:18) also drifted to the flatten.
- **Some "target" exits did not cover charges.** HCLTECH 1440CE (10:38-10:44) exited at 48.95 against a 48.90 entry and netted -38. LT 4150PE (10:50-10:51) moved 0.35 and netted +9.64. On options, the target distance derived from the underlying's ATR is too small once it is translated to premium.
- **Stale losers blocked capital.** From 11:18 the open MARUTI, INFY, KOTAK, BEL and RELIANCE positions held about Rs 94k of the Rs 100k wallet. That is why 16 later signals were skipped for cost, including NTPC 322.5PE (13:46), the same contract that earned +2338 in the morning. Some of those skipped signals might have been winners.
- **Only ITC (11:47) hit a stop**, at -12% premium. MARUTI sat past that level for hours, so either stops are not applied consistently or they are measured on the underlying rather than the premium. The stop and target columns are blank on every row, so this cannot be checked from the log.
- **Option symbol lookup failed five times.** COALINDIA, BAJFINANCE, AXISBANK, TRENT and TATASTEEL each resolved to the NSE equity instead of the NFO contract.
  - COALINDIA C389.75 is not a listed strike, so the strike-rounding step is producing invalid strikes.
  - These are data bugs, not strategy decisions.
- **Morning signals at 09:43-09:53 were mixed.** LT and NTPC worked, while MARUTI and ITC were the two biggest losers. Both were deep-premium or near-ATM calls entered on the first volume burst of the day.

## Try next
- **Add a hard premium stop of -15% and a 60-minute time stop.** Under that rule MARUTI would have lost about Rs 3100 instead of Rs 5096, and INFY and KOTAK would have been cut earlier, freeing capital for the afternoon signals.
- **Raise target_x to 2.0, with a floor of +3% premium after charges.** This stops target exits like HCLTECH's that lose money net.
- **Skip entries before 10:00 or require vol_x >= 3.5**, and fix strike rounding to listed strikes so that no-quote skips stop happening.

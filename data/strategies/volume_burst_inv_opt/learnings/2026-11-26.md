# Positions without a stop bled to the 14:50 flatten and caused most of the loss, and the day summary line is wrong

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy made 10 option buys between 13:13 and 14:31 and closed all of them by 14:50:53. It won 3 and lost 7, for a net of **Rs -6119.02** after charges. That contradicts the supplied summary, which says 10 won, net +475, best 104, worst 6. The best trade was HINDUNILVR C2140 at +575.17 and the worst was BEL P405 at -1900.08.

The log's "day so far" column also starts at 1953 after a -767.67 close. That implies about Rs 2720.67 of earlier P&L that does not appear in this log, and it ends the day at -3399. Either way, the day was well short of the Rs 1000 target.

By side, the six puts lost Rs 5513.07 and the four calls lost Rs 605.95.

## Learnings
- **The 14:50 flatten did most of the damage, not the stops.** Four positions never hit a stop or target and were closed at the flatten:
  - TITAN P4650, opened 13:14, lost 8.6% of premium (-1770.56).
  - TATASTEEL P162.5, opened 13:56, lost 5.1% (-873.69).
  - POWERGRID P267.5, opened 14:31, lost 4.8% (-708.02).
  - BEL P405, opened 14:31, lost 11.1% (-1900.08).
  - Together that is Rs -5252.35, about 86% of the day's loss. Each of the three largest was bigger than any stop-out (the largest stop was -767.67 on RELIANCE).
  - This suggests the stop is set on the underlying and was wider than the option premium actually lost. The log does not show stop levels or underlying prices at exit to confirm this.
- **Entries at 14:31 had no room to work.** POWERGRID and BEL opened together 19 minutes before the flatten and lost Rs -2608.10 combined. TITAN was held 96 minutes without resolving. A volume-burst signal that has not paid within about 20-30 minutes is not paying today.
- **Targets are set too close relative to charges.**
  - ADANIENT P2700 hit "target" on a 0.30 premium move. Gross was about Rs 93, net was Rs 5.97.
  - ADANIPORTS C1920 hit target on a 0.70 move. Gross was Rs 332, net was Rs 255.
  - Wins averaged Rs 279 and losses averaged Rs 994, so a 30% hit rate cannot work at this payoff ratio.
- **Fast stop-outs on calls point to entering at the top of the burst.** HDFCBANK C860 was stopped 32 seconds after entry (14:10:13 to 14:10:45, -668.75). RELIANCE C1370 was stopped after 18 minutes (-767.67). The fill came at the spike, and the inverted signal did not get follow-through.
- **There are plumbing problems, separate from the strategy:**
  - At 13:38, the TCS P2580 lookup asked for NFO token 151308 but got NSE 11536 TCS-EQ, a token-mapping bug.
  - At 13:54, BANKBEES was scanned even though it has no option contracts.
  - The day-summary line disagrees with the trade rows.

## Try next
- **Add a time filter.** Allow no new opens after 14:15, and close any position at 25 minutes if neither stop nor target has hit. Today that rule would have cut TITAN and TATASTEEL early and skipped POWERGRID and BEL, removing most of the Rs -5252 from the flatten.
- **Raise target_x by about 1.0, toward 2.5-3.0.** Also require the target to be at least 3x the round-trip charges in premium terms, so trades like the ADANIENT +5.97 "win" are not taken.
- **Fix the data issues before trusting another day's numbers:**
  - Restrict the scan universe to F&O names, which drops BANKBEES.
  - Fix the NFO token lookup.
  - Recompute the summary from the closed rows.
  - Only after that, consider testing side restricted to bullish signals. One day of puts losing Rs 5513 is not enough evidence on its own.

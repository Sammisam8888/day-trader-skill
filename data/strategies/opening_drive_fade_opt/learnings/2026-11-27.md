# Net day was -1803, not +242; the reported summary and the 12:45 "day target reached" banner are both wrong, and one AXISBANK stop caused most of the loss

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy opened 4 option buys at 09:46 (AXISBANK C1400, ADANIPORTS P1920, HINDALCO P920, TRENT P3300) and 2 more at 10:31-10:32 (POWERGRID P267.5, NTPC P322.5). The six closes sum to -21.88 - 2386.00 + 934.44 + 53.21 + 19.88 - 402.25 = Rs -1802.60. That is 3 winners and 3 losers, best +934 (NTPC) and worst -2386 (AXISBANK). The footer's "6 won, net 242, best 110, worst 7" and the 12:45 banner "DAY TARGET reached, net 1247" do not match the trade rows: running P&L at 12:25 was -1473 and never went positive. Five entries were also skipped: 4 because of an option-token lookup bug and 1 because capital was used up.

## Learnings
- AXISBANK29DEC26C1400 (09:46 buy at 44.85, stopped 09:57 at 41.15, -2386) was the whole day. The premium lost 8.3% in 11 minutes. The opening drive kept going after the "retrace" and the fade was simply early. One stop-out wiped out more than all three winners combined (+1008).
- ADANIPORTS29DEC26P1920 (09:46 -> 09:54) was closed as "target" at 51.40 against an entry of 51.30. That is +47.50 gross and -21.88 net. The target sits inside the round-trip charge band, so a "target" exit can still lose money. Targets need to clear charges on the premium, not just be above entry.
- NTPC29DEC26P322.5 (10:32 -> 12:25, 7.60 -> 8.25, +934) was the only real fade that worked. It was a later entry on a low-priced premium with a large lot (1500). The 10:30-window entries did better than the 09:46 cluster.
- TRENT (109.55 -> 109.95) and POWERGRID (6.65 -> 6.70) went about 5 hours with premiums essentially flat and were closed at 14:50 for +20 and +53. HINDALCO went from 24.90 to 24.40 over the same period (-402). None of the three ever hit stop or target, so they tied up about Rs 54.7k of capital for no edge. The stop and target columns are blank for every trade, so the levels used aren't visible in the log.
- Firing 4 entries in the same minute at 09:46 used Rs 94.5k of the Rs 100k wallet. That is why TITAN at 09:50 (Rs 19,705/lot) was skipped. The 09:46 signals were correlated and dominated the book.
- Infrastructure bug: BHARTIARTL, ADANIENT, SBIN and JSWSTEEL all failed with "asked for NFO <token>, got NSE <token> <SYM>-EQ". The NFO instrument lookup is falling back to the cash-segment symbol. Today's sample is therefore incomplete, and the summary/banner P&L code is also untrustworthy until it is fixed.

## Try next
- Raise `retrace` to 0.6 (from whatever is set now) so the fade only triggers after the opening drive has clearly given back more than half. AXISBANK at 09:46 is the case this should filter out.
- Add a filter: target premium must be at least entry + 2x round-trip charges per share. Also add a time stop that exits any position whose premium has moved less than 3% by 12:30. This would have avoided the ADANIPORTS "target" loss and freed the TRENT/POWERGRID/HINDALCO capital.
- Add a filter: no more than 2 new positions in any 5-minute window, and no more than 30% of wallet in premium per trade. Separately, fix the NFO token resolution and the day-summary/target-banner calculation before trusting any further day numbers.

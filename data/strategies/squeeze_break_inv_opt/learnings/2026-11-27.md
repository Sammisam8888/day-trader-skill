# Real day was -4,235, not +449; a "target" exit lost money, and one premium stop erased all five wins

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The summary line says 9 trades, 9 wins and net Rs +449. The trade rows say otherwise: 9 closes, 5 winners and 4 losers, for a net of Rs -4,235 after charges. The 10:54 "DAY TARGET reached, net 2650" banner fired before any trade had closed, so it was also false. The strategy bought 1-lot options on squeeze-break signals: calls on BUY signals and puts on SELL signals. The 5 winners made Rs 2,622 in total (largest TRENT +785). The 4 losers cost Rs 6,857 in total (BAJFINANCE -4,087 and HCLTECH -2,129).

## Learnings
- **The reporting is broken.** The day summary, win count and day-target banner do not match the trade log. Until these agree with the summed net, they cannot drive keep, kill or scale decisions. Today's true result is -4,235, more than 4x the Rs 1,000 target in the wrong direction.
- **Exit labels contradict the fills.**
  - HCLTECH (11:04 to 11:13) closed as "target" at 59.90 against a 65.05 entry, for -2,129.
  - A target exit that loses money suggests the target is calculated on the underlying in the opposite direction to the option held. That could be an `invert` / side-mapping bug in the `_inv_opt` path, but this log alone cannot prove it.
  - Stop and target columns are empty on every row, so none of the levels can be checked.
- **Losses are much larger than wins.**
  - BAJFINANCE C1090 was held 2h15m (10:54 to 13:09) and stopped at 34.70 from 40.05. That is -13% on premium and Rs -4,087, more than all five winners combined.
  - Winners were small: TCS +245, HDFCBANK +50, KOTAKBANK +137.
  - About Rs 50-70 of charges per round trip took half of the HDFCBANK gross gain (Rs 97 gross, Rs 50 net).
- **Too much capital was tied up at once.** At 12:44, open premium was BAJFINANCE 30,038 + HINDALCO 21,140 + KOTAKBANK 21,700 + TRENT 23,861 = about Rs 96.7k of a Rs 100k wallet. That is why ICICIBANK was skipped at 12:59 (a lot cost Rs 30,625). The book was effectively all-in on four correlated directional bets.
- **Instrument lookup is failing.** HINDUNILVR (11:59) and BHARTIARTL (14:14) were skipped because an NFO token request returned the NSE equity quote. That is a data bug, not a signal decision.
- **One thing behaved well.** The ADANIPORTS re-entry on a different strike (C1880) was correctly blocked right after the 13:29 stop.

## Try next
- **Fix exits before changing any tuning.**
  - Log the stop and target on every open.
  - Assert that a "target" close has a premium above entry for a long option, and flag any violation.
  - Recompute the day summary from the closed-trade net values.
  - If the target direction turns out to be flipped, run with invert=0 until the `_inv_opt` mapping is verified.
- **Tighten stops relative to targets.** Set stop_atr 1.5 (down from the current value, which let BAJFINANCE fall 13%) and target_atr 3.0, so one loser cannot exceed several winners. Add a hard premium stop at -8% of entry, because ATR on the underlying does not track option premium decay.
- **Add an exposure filter.**
  - Cap total open premium at Rs 60k, with at most 2 concurrent positions.
  - Skip entries where the expected target move in premium is under about Rs 300 net of charges.
  - Skip any symbol whose option token returns an NSE-EQ quote, and alert on it.

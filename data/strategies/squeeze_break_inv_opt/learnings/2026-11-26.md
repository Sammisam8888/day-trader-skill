# Underlying-ATR targets can lose money on options: TATASTEEL 13:15 hit "target" but closed Rs -111

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy traded 1-lot monthly options on NSE large-caps from 13:04 to 14:50. It closed 9 trades: 6 won, 3 lost, net Rs +3,959 after charges. The best trade was KOTAKBANK C505 (+2,115) and the worst was HINDALCO P920 at 14:50 (-509). The supplied summary line is wrong and should not be trusted: it says 9/9 won, net Rs 160, best 54, worst 6. The "DAY TARGET reached" banner also says net 1196, but the log shows 1,009 at that time. The underlyings mostly drifted up in the afternoon: all 5 call trades closed by target, and 2 of the 3 put trades lost.

## Learnings
- **Calls carried the day.** BHARTIARTL C2120 (13:28 to 14:42, +902) and KOTAKBANK C505 (13:52 to 14:49, +2,115) made 76% of net profit. Both were held about 60-75 minutes, so premium had time to expand beyond the underlying move.
- **The underlying target and the option P&L can disagree.** TATASTEEL C162.5 at 13:15 closed as "target" with premium 6.86 to 6.84, a gross loss of Rs 55 plus about Rs 56 in charges, for -111. NTPC C320 at 14:19 hit target for only +0.10 premium (+108 net). A small ATR target on the stock, held only 2-15 minutes, does not reliably clear the bid/ask spread plus round-trip charges (about Rs 55-90 per lot here).
- **Late put entries failed.** TATASTEEL P162.5 (14:21) and HINDALCO P920 (14:22) both ran out of time and were force-flattened at 14:50 for a combined -613. This gave back 30% of the BHARTI gain.
  - The TATASTEEL put fired at 163.34, just 2 minutes after the same stock's call hit target on an up-move. That was a direct reversal against the prevailing drift.
- **The earlier HINDALCO put (13:47, +713) worked, but the re-entry did not.** The re-entry at 14:22 came at almost the same underlying level (919.85 vs 919.80) and lost -509. A second signal at the same level later in the session did not repeat.
- **Two signals were lost to a quote-mapping bug.** RELIANCE (13:13) and SUNPHARMA (13:37) were requested from NFO but returned NSE equity tokens. This is an infrastructure bug, not a strategy decision. Two more signals were skipped for lack of capital: ADANIENT at 14:03 (Rs 35,380 needed, about Rs 33.6k free) and BEL at 14:33 (Rs 15,390 needed, about Rs 12.4k free).
- **Hitting the day target did not stop trading, and that helped.** Trading after the 13:52 target added about Rs 2,950 net. However, every loss after that point came from entries made after 14:15.

## Try next
- **Add an entry cutoff at 14:15.** No new opens after 14:15, since today's only post-target losses (14:21 and 14:22) were entries with under 30 minutes to the 14:50 flat.
- **Raise target_atr by about 1.0 within the 1.5..6.0 range.** Also add a filter that only exits on target if the option premium gain covers charges (at least about Rs 0.05/unit on 1500-2750 lot sizes). This avoids "target" exits like TATASTEEL 13:15.
- **Fix the NFO token lookup.** Also add a filter that blocks a put signal on a stock within 10 minutes of a call target exit on the same stock, which would have skipped TATASTEEL P at 14:21. Keep side=0..2 unchanged until there are more days of data; one afternoon is not enough to drop puts.

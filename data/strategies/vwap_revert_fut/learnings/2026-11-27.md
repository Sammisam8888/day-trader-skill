# Rs 100k cannot fund this futures strategy: 1 of ~45 signals was tradable, and that trade had no stop or target

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy fired about 45 signals but opened only one position: 1 lot (2750) of TATASTEEL29DEC26F, bought at 165.12 at 12:22:45 on a spot signal at 164.50. It was closed at the 14:50 flatten at 165.22, for Rs 275 gross. The log does not agree on the net result: the close row says Rs 7.32 ("day so far 7"), while the day summary says Rs 165. The note at 13:28 shows a peak of Rs 1134, which was almost certainly unrealized mark-to-market, so the position gave back roughly Rs 970-1130 before the time exit. Every other signal was skipped, mostly because 1 lot needed Rs 101k-227k of margin against Rs 100k free.

## Learnings
- **Capital sizing is the binding constraint.** These signals were skipped for margin alone:
  - ICICIBANK 227k, TECHM 221k, ADANIPORTS ~181k, JSWSTEEL 178k, AXISBANK 174k, SBIN 172k, ADANIENT ~165k, BAJFINANCE ~163k, HINDUNILVR 129k, ULTRACEMCO 127k.
  - COALINDIA (104.5k) and ONGC (101.1k) missed by under 5%.
  - At this wallet size the futures universe is effectively TATASTEEL alone.
- **The TATASTEEL trade (12:22) had no stop or target.** Both columns are empty, so it ran unmanaged for 2.5 hours. The 13:28 note implies MTM of about Rs 1134 that was not banked. It reverted to near entry and exited at 14:50 on the time rule. A revert-to-VWAP trade should exit at VWAP, not ride through it.
- **One lot uses 91% of margin (Rs 90,816 of 100k).** Free margin was then Rs 9,184. That made all 15 later signals from 12:36 to 14:16 untradable, even ONGC, which would otherwise have been close to affordable.
- **The contract lookup is broken for several names.** The system asked for the NFO futures token and received the NSE equity quote for:
  - JSWSTEEL at 10:26 and 11:53
  - ULTRACEMCO at 11:30
  - LT at 13:27
  - ADANIPORTS at 13:57

  This is intermittent: JSWSTEEL resolved correctly at 11:11. These are data bugs, not market skips.
- **ETFs are in the signal universe but have no futures.** BANKBEES was skipped at 09:44, 10:54 and 11:22, and NIFTYBEES at 10:31 and 11:21, wasting signal slots.
- **The Rs 0.62 gap between signal (164.50) and fill (165.12) is mostly futures basis, not slippage.** Stop and target levels computed on spot need to be shifted by the basis before they are applied to the future.
- **The net P&L reporting is inconsistent.** The close row says 7.32, the summary says 165, and gross was 275. Charges cannot be verified until this is reconciled.

## Try next
- **Add a pre-signal filter.** Drop any symbol without a futures contract (the ETFs), and any whose 1-lot margin exceeds free margin.
  - On Rs 100k this leaves TATASTEEL, plus COALINDIA and ONGC only on days their margin dips under 100k.
  - Otherwise keep enabled=1 only if the wallet is raised to at least Rs 200k.
- **Make stop and target mandatory on every fill, or reject the order.** Use stop_sd=1.0 (spot-derived and basis-adjusted) and a take-profit at VWAP touch. Leave band_sd at the current value: one trade is no evidence to retune it.
- **Fix the NFO token resolution** for JSWSTEEL, ULTRACEMCO, LT and ADANIPORTS (it currently returns the -EQ token). Also reconcile the close-row net against the day summary before trusting any P&L-based tuning.

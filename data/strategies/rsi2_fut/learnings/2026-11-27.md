# One INFY stop at 14:40 (-1674) erased two target wins (+1252) because the stop sat further away than the targets and the strategy kept trading past its day target

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy traded only INFY futures today, because Rs 100000 covers just one ~Rs 93k INFY lot. Every other signal was skipped for margin, missing contracts or bad quotes. It went short at 10:54 and took a +766 target, then went long at 12:44 for a +486 target. A third trade, long at 14:29, hit its stop for -1674. The true day result is 2 wins and 1 loss, net -422 after charges. The footer line "3 won, net Rs 3489, best 1164, worst 1163" is wrong: the log's own per-trade nets are 765.55 + 486.28 - 1673.83 = -422.

## Learnings
- **Losses were bigger than wins.** The INFY trades moved 2.6 and 1.9 points to target (10:54 short, 12:44 long) but 3.5 points to the stop (14:29 long, 1166.40 to 1162.90). With the loss bigger than either win, one stop-out wiped out a 2-for-2 morning.
- **Charges took a big share of each trade.** Round-trip cost on 1 INFY lot was about Rs 275. That consumed about 26% of the 10:54 gross win (1040) and about 36% of the 12:44 gross win (760), while adding to the 14:29 loss (1400 gross became 1674 net). Small 2-point targets on one lot barely clear costs.
- **Trading past the target hurt.** At 13:39 the day stood at +1252, above the Rs 1000 target. The 14:29 INFY re-entry was optional risk and turned a winning day into -422.
- **The 14:29 long bought high.** The signal came at spot 1162.20, about 5 points above the 12:44 buy (1157.00), so it bought a "dip" in a stock that had already bounced. It was stopped out 11 minutes later.
- **The wallet is too small for the universe.** About 25 signals were skipped (HCLTECH, BEL, WIPRO, SBIN, KOTAKBANK, ICICIBANK and others) because 1 lot needs Rs 112k to Rs 225k against Rs 100k free. The day's result is therefore really one INFY trade sequence, not a test of the recipe.
- **There are data and plumbing bugs:**
  - At 10:54 (HINDALCO) and 12:29 (SBIN), requests for the futures contract came back with equity quotes.
  - BANKBEES and NIFTYBEES (ETFs with no futures) keep producing signals.
  - The day-target banner says "12:49 net 1252", but 1252 was only realized at 13:39.

## Try next
- **Make targets at least as wide as stops:** stop_atr 1.5 and target_atr 2.5, so one stop no longer outweighs two wins.
- **Stop trading for the day once realized PnL reaches the Rs 1000 day target.** Today this would have ended the day at +1252 instead of -422.
- **Filter the universe before signalling:** drop symbols whose 1-lot margin exceeds free capital (keep INFY and TATASTEEL at this wallet size) and drop ETFs without futures (BANKBEES, NIFTYBEES). Also fix the NFO-to-NSE quote fallback, and fix the footer so it is computed from the actual closes.

# Every trade lost: rsi2_fut lost Rs 3456 net, and the summary line claiming +648 is wrong

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The trade log shows 3 round trips, and all 3 lost money:
- TATASTEEL fut, closed 13:12: -1310
- NTPC fut, 13:53-14:02: -1633
- TATASTEEL fut, 14:31-14:50: -513

That is Rs -3456 net on a Rs 100000 wallet, against a Rs 1000 target. The "Day numbers" line says 3 won, net +648, best 321, worst 163. That line contradicts the log. Its "best/worst" look like fill prices (NTPC about 321, TATASTEEL about 163), not rupee P&L, so the summary generator has a bug.

Only 2 new entries happened after 13:08. Of the 21 skips, 15 were margin rejects (1 lot cost Rs 93920 to 220488 against about Rs 97000-98690 free), 5 were quote failures and 3 were ETFs with no futures contract.

## Learnings
- **Signals are priced on spot, but fills happen on futures at a premium.** NTPC signalled at 320.20 and filled at 322.15, a 1.95 (0.6%) gap. TATASTEEL at 14:31 signalled at 163.18 and filled at 164.09, a 0.91 (0.56%) gap. RSI2 is a mean-reversion entry, so paying about 0.6% above the price that triggered it gives away much of the expected bounce before the trade starts.
- **Stops are too tight for the position size.** NTPC was stopped 0.90 below entry (0.28%). TATASTEEL at 13:12 was stopped 0.38 below entry (0.23%). With 1 lot worth about Rs 450000-480000, a 0.25% move costs about Rs 1100-1350 gross, more than the whole day target. Both stops look like ordinary noise, not a failed setup. The stop and target columns are blank in the log, so I cannot check the actual levels.
- **Charges take a large, fixed share.** Gross losses were about 1045, 1350 and 248. Net losses were 1310, 1633 and 513. So about Rs 265-285 per round trip went to charges, about Rs 810 in total, or 23% of the day's loss. The 14:31 TATASTEEL trade was nearly flat on price (-0.09) but still lost 513.
- **The last entry had too little time.** TATASTEEL opened at 14:31 and was force-closed at the 14:50 flat after 19 minutes. A 2-period RSI reversal needs room to play out, and this one was closed for time, not by the market.
- **Plumbing bug in quote lookup.** JSWSTEEL, WIPRO (13:29), TECHM (13:36) and HDFCBANK requested an NFO token but got the NSE equity token back. That symbol-to-exchange lookup is broken and is silently dropping signals.
- **Capital limits the universe to about 3 names.** With about Rs 98000, only NTPC, TATASTEEL and sometimes INFY fit in 1 lot. The strategy is not choosing its best setups, only the cheapest ones. The free cash also fell after each loss (98690 to 97057), so this will get worse.

## Try next
- Widen stops: set stop_atr to 2.5 and target_atr to 2.0, so a stop needs more than about 0.5% to trigger. If the wider risk per lot is too large for the wallet, set enabled = 0 for futures until margin is above about Rs 300000.
- Add a basis filter: skip an entry if the futures fill is more than 0.25% from the spot signal price. Better still, compute RSI2 on the futures series itself.
- Add a time filter: no new entries after 14:15, so each trade has at least 35 minutes before the 14:50 flat. Separately, fix the NFO token lookup and the summary line (it should count wins by net Rs and report best/worst in Rs, not price).

# Fixed ~Rs 270 round-trip charges eat tiny targets: TATASTEEL made Rs 275 gross and kept Rs 6.58

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
All 4 trades closed at target and none lost. Per the log, the trades netted Rs 1739.03 (NTPC short), Rs 126.58 (INFY long), Rs 465.87 (NTPC short) and Rs 6.58 (TATASTEEL short), for Rs 2338. The "Day numbers" line says something different: net Rs 1972, best 1161, worst 166. Neither the total nor the best/worst figures match the trade rows, so the summary is wrong or built from different data. The Rs 100000 wallet was the main constraint: about 35 signals were skipped because 1 lot needed more margin than was free, and only NTPC, INFY and TATASTEEL fit. The day target was flagged at 10:23 with net 1139, before the first close at 10:30, so the banner is using mark-to-market rather than realized P&L.

## Learnings
- Charges per round trip were nearly flat at about Rs 270-285, whatever the size of the move:
  - NTPC 09:47: gross 2025, net 1739
  - INFY 12:22: gross 400, net 127
  - NTPC 13:46: gross 750, net 466
  - TATASTEEL 14:21: gross 275, net 7
- Any target worth less than about 3x charges is near-breakeven risk for no reward. TATASTEEL at 14:21 risked a full lot on a 0.10-point target. INFY at 12:22 had a 1.00-point target on a 400-qty lot.
- The signal is computed on the spot price, but fills happen on the future, and the basis was large:
  - NTPC: signal 323.45, fill 324.65 (+1.20)
  - INFY: signal 1155.30, fill 1160.40 (+5.10)
  - TATASTEEL: signal 165.42, fill 165.70
  
  The NTPC shorts gained from selling at the premium. The INFY long paid it, and its 1-point target captured less than the basis it paid.
- The only meaningful money came from the 09:47 NTPC short: 1.35 points times 1500 qty, held 43 minutes. That was one trade out of four. The two short scalps after 13:46 added Rs 472 combined.
- The margin wall decided which trades happened, not signal quality:
  - Holding NTPC at 09:47 left Rs 2605 free, which blocked LT, BEL, INFY, COALINDIA and SBIN until 10:30.
  - ONGC at 13:46 and JSWSTEEL at 14:21 were also blocked by an open position.
- NFO quote lookup failed intermittently. HINDALCO at 11:04, TATASTEEL at 11:14 and JSWSTEEL at 13:33 all returned the NSE equity token instead of the future. HINDALCO and TATASTEEL resolved correctly later in the day, so these trades were lost to a lookup bug, not to filtering.

- Compute stop and target from the futures fill price, not the spot signal price. Also skip BUY entries when the futures premium over spot exceeds half the target distance. Today's INFY 12:22 trade paid 5.1 points of basis to chase a 1-point target.
- Keep invert=1 and side=2. Fix the day-summary aggregation (1972 vs 2338) and the NFO token fallback before judging this recipe across days; otherwise the reported P&L and the opportunity set are both unreliable.

# One unstopped 1-lot TATASTEEL short lost Rs 1805, nine times the day's only win

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy closed two TATASTEEL29DEC26F trades. A long carried in from before 13:13 closed at target at 13:46 for +Rs 202.19. A short opened at 13:56 and was force-closed flat at 14:50 for -Rs 1805.20, so the day ended at **-Rs 1603**. The day summary ("2 won, net Rs 328, best 164, worst 164") is wrong and contradicts the log's own running total of -1603. Eleven other signals were skipped:
- 6 because 1 lot needs Rs 112k-221k of margin against a Rs 100k wallet.
- 4 because of a quote-lookup bug.
- 1 (BANKBEES) because it has no futures contract.

## Learnings
- **The win was too small to cover charges well.** On the 13:46 TATASTEEL close, the target was only 0.17 points above entry (163.53 to 163.70), about 0.1%. Gross was about Rs 467 and round-trip charges were about Rs 265, so charges took 57% of the gross.
- **The 13:56 short had no stop and no target.** Both columns are blank on the open, and it only ended at the 14:50 flat time. A 0.56-point move against it (163.57 to 164.13, 0.34%) times 2750 qty gave -1540 gross and -1805 net. With 1 lot of 2750 shares, every 0.1 point is Rs 275, so an open-ended position can't be survived on a Rs 1000 day target.
- **The signal price and the fill price come from different instruments.** The signal fired on the TATASTEEL equity at 162.90, but the futures fill was 163.57. That 0.67 gap is futures basis, not slippage. Any stop or target computed from the equity price will be misplaced on the futures contract by about that much.
- **One lot ties up nearly the whole wallet.** TATASTEEL took Rs 89,963 of margin, leaving Rs 10k free. TECHM, HDFCBANK, BEL, HINDUNILVR and TCS were all skipped for margin. Even with the full wallet free, ADANIENT at 13:48 (Rs 166k) and TECHM (Rs 221k) were never affordable. In practice the universe is TATASTEEL plus a few cheap lots.
- **The NFO quote lookup is broken.** RELIANCE, TITAN, ADANIPORTS and POWERGRID asked for an NFO token and got the NSE-EQ quote back. This is a data bug, not a market condition, and it silently shrinks the universe further.
- **The day summary can't be trusted.** It reported +328 with 2 wins, but the actual result was -1603 with 1 win and 1 loss. Any knob tuning based on these summary stats will be wrong until this is fixed.

## Try next
- **Add a hard stop on every futures open.** Set it at 1x the target distance, computed on the futures price rather than the equity signal price. Raise `target_x` to 2.5 so that a target hit clears about Rs 265 of charges with room to spare.
- **Filter the universe before signalling.** Allow only symbols where 1 lot's margin is at most Rs 90k and the NFO token resolves to an NFO quote. Drop ETFs such as BANKBEES. Until the token mapping is fixed, set `enabled` = 0 for RELIANCE, TITAN, ADANIPORTS and POWERGRID.
- **Keep `invert` = 1 for now.** The losing short would have won un-inverted, but one trade is not evidence. Revisit after 10 or more trades with a corrected PnL summary.

# One runner paid for the day: MARUTI put +4643 covered seven tight stops worth -4284

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The tape leaned bearish: 8 of 11 entries were puts, and MARUTI fell hard enough to lift its 14000 put from 413.60 to 507.85 (+23%). The strategy closed 10 trades with 3 winners and 7 losers, for a net of Rs +1845 after charges according to the log's running total. That total breaks down as BEL +1436, ITC +49 and MARUTI +4643, against TECHM -1201, TATASTEEL -571, POWERGRID -327, ONGC -193, COALINDIA P -594, TRENT -1089 and COALINDIA C -308. The summary line above is wrong and does not match the log: it says "10 won, net 716, best 508, worst 4". Best 508 looks like MARUTI's exit price, not its P&L. The bookkeeping that produces that summary needs fixing.

## Learnings
- Nearly all the day's profit came from one trade. The MARUTI put opened at 09:43 never hit a stop and was held to the 14:50 flat for +4643. Without it the day would have finished at -2798. The only real edge today was letting a correct directional call run.
- Stops were set too tight for option premiums. Each loser was stopped out at 1.5-4.3% of the premium paid:
  - ONGC 4.52 to 4.45 at 12:49
  - POWERGRID 6.80 to 6.65 at 12:35
  - TATASTEEL 5.86 to 5.67 at 11:38
  - TECHM 57.45 to 55.60 at 10:50
  - TRENT 104.85 to 100.30 at 13:29
  
  Moves that small are mostly spread and noise on these premiums, not proof that the signal was wrong. Charges also took roughly Rs 35-40 per round trip.
- Some targets were too small to be worth taking. ITC's target was hit at 12:02 after more than 2 hours, at 6.10 to 6.15, for +49 net. That ties up Rs 10.5k of capital for a payoff smaller than one stop-out.
- Trading on after the day target cost money. The target was reached by 10:20, with the log showing +1436 at 10:13. The next six new entries (TECHM, TATASTEEL, POWERGRID, ONGC, TRENT, COALINDIA C) all lost, a combined -3,882. By 13:29 the day stood at -2490.
- Flipping direction on the same stock lost on both legs. COALINDIA's put was stopped at 13:08 (-594), and a call on the same stock opened 51 seconds later at 13:09 (-308 at flat). The burst signal on COALINDIA was chop, not direction.

## Try next
- Raise target_x to 3.0. Today's winners that mattered were runners, so the strategy should stop taking +0.05-premium exits like ITC's.
- Raise body_atr to 1.5 to cut the count of marginal bursts. Six of today's seven losers were small-body midday entries (10:36-12:53) that reversed within 15 minutes. Add one filter alongside it: no opposite-side entry on a stock within 30 minutes of being stopped out on it, which would have blocked the COALINDIA flip.
- Add a filter to stop new entries once the day target is hit, or after 3 consecutive stop-outs. Positions already open would keep running to flat, so MARUTI would still have been held. On today's tape either rule would have saved roughly Rs 1,400-3,900.

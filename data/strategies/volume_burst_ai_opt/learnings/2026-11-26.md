# IT call buys paid; the two losers were cheap-premium, high-qty contracts, and the day summary line is wrong

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy bought 1-lot calls on volume bursts from 13:17 onward and closed 7 trades: 5 winners, 2 losers, net Rs +8,172 after charges. It passed the Rs 1,000 day target at 13:43 and kept trading. Most of the profit came from the IT names: TCS +2,831, TECHM +2,103, HCLTECH +2,052 and +1,572. All four IT trades hit their targets as the sector moved up in the afternoon. The auto-generated summary line ("7 won, net Rs 333, best 115, worst 7") does not match the log. The trade rows add up to +8,171.90 with 2 losses, and the "best/worst" values look like exit and entry premiums, not Rs P&L. The reporting code needs fixing before any of these summary numbers are trusted.

## Learnings
- The IT calls worked. TCS C2560 (13:17, 102.50 to 115.40), HCLTECH C1420 (13:38, 55.70 to 61.00), TECHM C1820 (14:05, 71.20 to 74.90) and HCLTECH C1440 (14:07, 56.30 to 60.40) all reached target. These are premiums of Rs 55-115, where a 5-10% premium move covers charges easily.
- Both losses were low-premium, large-quantity contracts. ITC C307.5 (13:38, 1725 qty at 7.70) stopped out at 7.25 for Rs -818. TATASTEEL C162.5 (14:20, 2750 qty at 7.36) was force-flattened at 7.19 for Rs -526. On a Rs 7 premium, one tick of noise is a large share of the trade, and the high quantity makes charges heavier.
- The 14:20:16 TATASTEEL re-entry was a mistake. It came 32 seconds after the 14:19:44 target exit and bought at 7.36, above the 7.34 exit price. That chased the move the previous trade had just captured. With only 30 minutes left before the 14:50 flat, there was not enough time for a new target, so it ended in a forced-exit loss.
- Capital ran nearly full. Around 14:07, open premium was TCS 23,063 + TECHM 42,720 + HCLTECH 22,520 = Rs 88,303 of the Rs 100,000 wallet. One more signal would likely have been skipped or under-funded. All open positions were also IT calls, so the strategy was in effect making one sector bet.
- The 13:25 NTPC skip was a data bug, not a strategy decision. The system asked for NFO token 139977 and got NSE 11630 (NTPC-EQ). The option-to-token lookup needs fixing, or valid signals will keep being lost.
- The log shows blank stop and target columns on every open, so stop distances cannot be checked after the fact. The levels should be logged at entry.

## Try next
- Add a minimum-premium filter: skip any contract with premium below Rs 15. This would have removed the ITC and TATASTEEL #2 losses (Rs -1,344) and the TATASTEEL #1 win (Rs +959). The net effect on today would have been about +385, with less exposure to tick noise.
- Add a re-entry cooldown: no new entry on the same underlying within 10 minutes of a close, and never above the last exit price. Also block new entries after 14:20, since the 14:50 flat leaves too little time.
- Keep vol_x, body_atr, target_x and side at their current values for now. Today's targets were reached within 31 seconds to 70 minutes and gave no evidence they need changing. Revisit them after the summary bug is fixed and there are multi-day stats.

# Calls on breaks that held paid (3 of 3); puts mostly got stopped (1 of 5), and the losers tied up the capital

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy opened 8 option trades between 13:09 and 14:32. It closed 4 for losses totalling Rs -5209 and 4 for gains totalling Rs +7075, so the trade-by-trade net is Rs +1866. Upside breaks were the ones that held. The TCS call made +3954 and the INFY call made +2098, while puts on WIPRO, TECHM, TITAN and COALINDIA were all stopped out. The header figures contradict the log and should not be trusted until fixed:
- "8 won, net Rs 401, best 119, worst 6" does not match the log.
- "DAY TARGET reached at 14:03: net 2496" was printed when the running P&L was actually Rs -2418.

## Learnings
- **Calls worked, puts did not.**
  - Calls on BUY signals went 3/3: TCS 13:18 (+3954), INFY 13:39 (+2098, hit target), POWERGRID 14:32 (+618).
  - Puts on SELL signals went 1/5: WIPRO 13:09 (-803), TECHM 13:12 (-1615), TITAN 13:17 (-1116), COALINDIA 13:29 (-1675). Only HINDUNILVR 13:24 made money (+404, at the 14:50 flat).
  - The downside "break and hold" mostly did not hold today.
- **IT signals contradicted each other.** The system bought puts on WIPRO and TECHM and calls on TCS and INFY within 30 minutes of each other. The calls won and the puts lost. Downside breaks against a sector moving up were the losing setups.
- **The premium stops were tight but not the main problem.** The losers were stopped at -4% to -8% of premium (TECHM 53.65 to 51.10, WIPRO 6.06 to 5.81). That is consistent with the break simply failing, not with noise.
- **The losing puts used up the capital.** By about 13:30 roughly Rs 95k of the Rs 100k wallet was in open premium. That forced 10 "1 lot costs Rs X" skips after 13:44, including ITC at Rs 10,091. Several of those skipped trades were calls (TRENT, LT, HCLTECH, WIPRO, RELIANCE, HINDALCO, BHARTIARTL) in the direction that was working.
- **Option token lookup bug.** Four signals were lost because the NFO contract resolved to the NSE equity instead: BEL 13:04, HINDALCO 13:18, HCLTECH 13:22 and BHARTIARTL 13:57.
- **The 14:50 forced close made money.** TCS, HINDUNILVR and POWERGRID were all closed in profit at the flat. TCS, at +17.7% on premium, was the day's best trade.

## Try next
- **Restrict direction.** Set `side` to the buy-signals-only value for a few sessions. As an alternative, add a filter that skips a put when 2 or more same-sector names have fired BUY signals in the last 30 minutes.
- **Demand a firmer hold before entering.** Raise `hold_bars` (for example from the current value to 3) and set `break_pct` to at least 0.15, so a break has to hold longer before entry.
- **Fix the plumbing.**
  - Repair the NFO token mapping so the option contract is quoted, not the equity.
  - Recompute the day summary and the day-target check from the closed-trade P&L.
  - Cap open premium at about Rs 60k so later signals can still be taken.

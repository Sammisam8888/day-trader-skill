# Realized day was Rs -2528, not +793: winners closed early, losers rode to the 14:50 flat

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The tape leaned bearish. 11 of 13 entries were puts on SELL signals, and the four that worked were all downside: AXISBANK, TITAN, BAJFINANCE and TATASTEEL. The 13 closes were 5 wins (+5955) and 8 losses (-8482), for a net of Rs -2528 by the log's own running "day so far". The summary line (13 won, net 793, best 296, worst 6) does not match the log. The 10:59 "DAY TARGET reached, net 1002" banner is also wrong: at 10:59 the only realized trade was TCS at -784, so it was probably counting unrealized MTM. Five positions still open at 14:50 were flattened for a combined -4011.

## Learnings
- **The flat clock did the most damage.** Four positions were force-closed at a loss, each having reached neither target nor stop:
  - HINDALCO P920, held 10:21 to 14:50, lost 1313.
  - HINDUNILVR C2140, held 11:15 to 14:50, lost 1127.
  - BAJFINANCE C1080 lost 1315.
  - ULTRACEMCO P12500 lost 499.
  
  When the "hold beyond the pool" thesis has not paid within an hour or so, it is not paying. Holding a long option for 4 hours only adds premium decay.
- **Late entries had no room.** BAJFINANCE C1080 (14:19) and ULTRACEMCO P12500 (14:23) opened 27-31 minutes before the flat and together lost 1815. Neither could plausibly reach target_r in that window.
- **BAJFINANCE flipped at the low and gave back half the day's best trade.** The P1090 trailed out at 14:19 for +2473 with the stock down from 1092.95 to 1082. A BUY signal then fired on the same stock in the same second, and the C1080 bled -1315. A break right after an extended move is more likely exhaustion than a fresh run.
- **Several breaks did not hold even briefly.** NTPC P320 stopped out 1m44s after entry (-935), and TCS C2580 stopped in 5.5 min (-784). COALINDIA P385 lost 1451 on a 14% premium drop. The low-premium contracts carry very large lot quantities: COALINDIA 1350, NTPC 1500, POWERGRID 1900, TATASTEEL 2750. A few paise of spread or noise is therefore Rs 500-1000. These four nets to -1285.
- **Capital, not signal quality, chose the trades.** About 7 concurrent positions tied up nearly the whole Rs 100000. That forced ~30 "costs Rs X" skips, including AXISBANK again at 13:22 after it had just been the day's cleanest target hit. Entries were first-come, not best-first.
- **Plumbing issues:**
  - Token lookups returned the NSE equity instead of the NFO option three times: WIPRO, KOTAKBANK, TATASTEEL.
  - The day summary and day-target figures are not computed from realized closes.
  
  Today's review would be misleading without reading the raw rows.

## Try next
- **hold_bars = 3**, so a break must hold for three bars before entry. This targets the NTPC and TCS style instant stop-outs.
- **Time filter:** no new entries after 14:00. Add a time-stop that closes any position not at target within 60 min of entry, instead of carrying it to 14:50.
- **Filter:** no opposite-side re-entry on the same underlying within 30 min of a profitable exit (the BAJFINANCE flip). Also skip contracts with premium under Rs 10, or size them by premium risk rather than one fixed lot. Separately, fix the day P&L and target accounting to use realized net.

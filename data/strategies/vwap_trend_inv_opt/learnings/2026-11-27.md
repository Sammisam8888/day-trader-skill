# Day target hit by 10:31 and peak of +2807 at 11:45 were both given back; ended at -4529 because nothing stopped trading after the target

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The market was choppy and two-way. The strategy took both puts and calls on the same names within minutes: NTPC put then call, HDFCBANK put then call, TRENT call then put. It hit the Rs 1000 day target at 10:31 and peaked at +2807 at 11:45. It then lost on almost every trade after noon and closed at **-4529 net**: 19 closed trades, 8 won and 11 lost, best +1504 (HINDUNILVR 10:09), worst -2137 (NTPC call, flat 14:50). The "Day numbers" footer (19 won, net +889, best 110, worst 6) does not match the trade log and should be treated as a reporting bug. The log's running total reconciles to -4528.77.

## Learnings
- **No profit protection.** The system logged "Keeps trading for more" at 10:31 with +1006. After the 11:45 high of +2807, every entry lost:
  - TATASTEEL C 11:43: -1593
  - LT C 12:26: -1685
  - ITC C 13:22: -302
  - JSWSTEEL C 13:46: -477
  - HINDALCO P 14:16: -892
  
  The morning edge did not carry into the afternoon chop.
- **Cheap, high-lot-size contracts caused most of the damage.** These four trades together lost -5658, more than the whole day's loss:
  - NTPC P322.5 at Rs 7.00 x 1500: -1234
  - NTPC C322.5 at Rs 8.25 x 1500: -2137
  - TATASTEEL C165 at Rs 6.83 x 2750: -1593
  - WIPRO C192.5 at Rs 7.48 x 3000: -694
  
  On these contracts a 0.5-1.4 rupee premium move is a 10-17% hit. The NTPC call fell from 8.25 to 6.85 and never triggered its underlying-based stop before the 14:50 flat.
- **"Target" exits on the underlying do not mean profit on the option.** Three exits labelled "target" lost money:
  - HINDUNILVR 10:40: exited at entry 59.50, -54 after charges
  - TRENT C 11:02: -98
  - WIPRO C 14:13: premium 7.48 to 7.27 after 4h16m of theta, -694
  
  The underlying reached its target, but the premium move was too small to beat decay and charges.
- **What worked: early, directional entries in mid-priced premiums (Rs 38-64).** Examples are TECHM P 09:43 (+1305), HINDUNILVR C 09:42 (+1504), INFY P 09:46 (+750) and LT P 10:11 (+438). The INFY winner needed nearly 4 hours to pay.
- **Contract lookup bug.** LT, MARUTI, RELIANCE and ICICIBANK signals were skipped because the NFO token request returned the NSE equity quote instead. Signals on liquid names were lost for infrastructure reasons, not strategy reasons.

## Try next
- **Lock in gains after the day target.**
  - Once the day target is hit, stop new entries if day P&L falls below 50% of the running peak.
  - Make no new entries after 12:00.
  - Either rule alone would have ended today between +1000 and +2800 instead of -4529.
- **Filter out cheap options.** Skip contracts with premium below Rs 15 or lot size above 1500 (today: NTPC, TATASTEEL, WIPRO). Alternatively, add an option-premium stop of -8% from entry, independent of stop_atr on the underlying.
- **Raise confirm by 1-2, capped at 5.** This should cut the same-name call/put flip-flops on NTPC, HDFCBANK and TRENT. Also raise target_atr by about 2, so an underlying "target" exit implies a premium gain that covers charges (roughly Rs 50-100 per round trip here).

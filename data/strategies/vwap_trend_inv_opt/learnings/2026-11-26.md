# One carried HCLTECH call made the whole day (+7926); the other 8 closes lost Rs 775 net, and the summary line misreports the day

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
- **Result:** The log ends the day at **+Rs 7152**. That is 9 closes: 5 winners and 4 losers.
- **Where the money came from:** The day hinged on one trade, the HCLTECH 1420 CE. It was bought at 49.30 and force-closed at the 14:50 flatten at 69.30, for +7926. Without it, the day was **-775**. The big losses were the HUL stop (-1222), POWERGRID (-522), LT (-371) and NTPC PE (-335).
- **Summary line is wrong:** It says "9 won, net Rs 340, best 106, worst 6". None of that matches the trade rows, so it should not be trusted or used for tuning.
- **Day-target banner looks wrong:** It reports net 1445 at 13:32. Realized P&L was only 363 at that point, so the banner seems to count unrealized P&L.

## Learnings
- **The big win came from holding, not the target.** HCLTECH (and JSWSTEEL, +632) were opened before 13:13 but have no "open" rows in the log. HCLTECH never hit its target and was closed by the 14:50 flatten, up 40% on premium. The day's edge came from letting one position run.
- **NTPC whipsawed around the 320 strike.** The strategy went BUY at 13:13 (319.75), SELL at 13:27 (320.45), then BUY again at 13:58 (320.00). Three trades on a stock moving less than Rs 1 netted only +104 (406 - 335 + 32). That is mostly churn and charges, with no trend to follow.
- **Small wins, large losses.** Targets were tiny in premium terms: NTPC CE won +0.30 on 8.70 and +0.05 on 8.75. Meanwhile the HUL 2140 CE (14:03) was stopped out in about 3.5 minutes for -3.90 premium, costing -1222. That one stop erased about three typical wins.
- **Exit reasons are mislabeled.** NTPC PE (13:46, 6.70 to 6.50) and POWERGRID CE (13:50, 7.95 to 7.70) are tagged "target" but closed at a loss. Either the target is computed from the underlying while the premium moved against us, or the label is a bug. Either way, target_atr cannot be judged from these rows.
- **Instrument lookup bug.** BHARTIARTL (13:35), POWERGRID (13:54) and JSWSTEEL (14:34) were skipped because the system asked for an NFO option token but got the NSE equity quote back. Those were 3 lost signals caused by data plumbing, not by the strategy.
- **Per-trade cost cap blocks liquid names.** SBIN (26700), KOTAKBANK (25200) and WIPRO (23220) were skipped as too expensive, while HUL at 18135 was allowed. The cap appears to be about Rs 20k, which excludes a chunk of the universe on a Rs 1L wallet.

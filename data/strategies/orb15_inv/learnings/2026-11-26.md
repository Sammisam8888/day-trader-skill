# Day target was hit at 10:25 (+1018) but the strategy kept trading and ended at -80

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The faded ORB signals paid steadily in the first hour. Nine targets hit between 09:43 and 10:25, taking the day to +1018, past the Rs 1000 day target. After that, two wide stops (NTPC 11:28 -530.95, RELIANCE 14:43 -523.59) and six flat-at-14:50 losses (-396.14 combined) erased it. The trade log shows 12 wins and 8 losses: +1370.43 gross from wins, -1450.68 from losses, net -80.25. The supplied "Day numbers" line is wrong and should not be trusted. It claims 20/20 won, net Rs 41278, best 12730 and worst 267, but those best/worst figures are actually prices (ULTRACEMCO exit 12730.05, POWERGRID exit 267.14).

## Learnings
- Reaching the day target did not stop the strategy. New entries came after the 1018 mark: RELIANCE 10:31, ULTRACEMCO 10:54, ITC 11:44 and TRENT 12:01. Together they netted -555.07 (+62.32, -523.59, -76.82, -16.98). Positions already open at 10:25 then bled out as well.
- Reward to risk is badly lopsided in this inverted copy:
  - Targets sit about 0.3x the stop distance. NTPC: target 0.89 away, stop 2.81 away. RELIANCE: target 6.50 away, stop 23.85 away. SUNPHARMA: target 10.55 away, stop 37.65 away.
  - So one stop costs about 4-5 typical wins (wins averaged about Rs 114). Two stops took back 1054.54, roughly 77% of all winning P&L.
- The fade edge was concentrated early. Every one of the 9 targets hit by 10:25 came from entries made between 09:31 and 09:43, and most filled within 10-30 minutes (KOTAKBANK 9 min, BANKBEES 10 min, LT 20 min).
- Positions that missed their target in the first hour mostly ended as small losers at the 14:50 flatten:
  - HDFCBANK, open from 09:37: -171.50
  - TITAN, open from 09:40: -68.16
  - COALINDIA, open from 09:32: -49.86
  - SUNPHARMA, open from 09:31: -12.82
  - Total: -302.34. Not hitting the tight target early was itself a sign the fade had failed.
- Stop fills were close to plan (NTPC 321.41 vs 321.40, RELIANCE 1399.55 vs 1398.30). The losses came from where the stops were placed, not from slippage.

## Try next
- Filter: once day net reaches the Rs 1000 target, block new entries and flatten open positions. Today that would have stopped trading at 10:25 with about +1018 booked, before the open positions were marked. Also block new entries after 10:30, since all of today's post-10:30 entries netted -555.
- Shrink the inverted stop distance. In this copy the stop appears to come from the original signal's target. Test target_x 1.5 (down from the current roughly 3.5x geometry) so a stop costs about 1.5 wins instead of 4-5, and confirm the mapping holds under invert 1.
- Filter: time-stop any position that has not hit target within 60 minutes of entry. HDFCBANK, TITAN, COALINDIA and SUNPHARMA all sat for 5+ hours and still lost.

# Booked +1300 by 11:20, then gave back Rs 1821 by trading past the day target into a falling afternoon

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The provided "Day numbers" line does not match the trade log. The log shows 20 closed trades with 11 wins and 9 losses, net about Rs -521. The best trade was LT at +173.26 and the worst was ULTRACEMCO at -410.91. The summary's figures (47212, 14020, 223) look like prices misread as P&L.

The inverted ORB book hit the Rs 1000 target at 10:31 (+1068 after NTPC) and peaked at +1300 at 11:20. It then bled to -521. The market drifted lower after midday, and 8 positions were still open at the 14:50 flat. All 8 closed red, including 7 of the 8 longs.

## Learnings
- **Early entries were profitable; late entries lost money.**
  - Trades opened by 10:30 netted about +916: 11 targets worth +1449, less KOTAKBANK -137.66, ITC -363.32 and POWERGRID -32.25.
  - The six entries from 10:31 onward lost about -1437 combined: ULTRACEMCO, NIFTYBEES, RELIANCE, BHARTIARTL, BAJFINANCE and SUNPHARMA.
- **Reaching the target did not stop new entries.** NTPC took the day to +1068 at 10:31:00. In the same minute ULTRACEMCO (10:31:00) opened, followed by NIFTYBEES (10:31:03), RELIANCE (10:33), BHARTIARTL (10:39), BAJFINANCE (10:53) and SUNPHARMA (12:30). Every one of them lost.
- **Payoff is lopsided against the strategy.**
  - Targets sit about 0.3x the stop distance. For example, KOTAKBANK at 09:31 had its target +3.32 away and its stop -10.55 away. AXISBANK had +5.95 vs -20.45.
  - Average win was about +132 and average loss about -224, so breakeven needs roughly a 63% win rate. The day's 55% was not enough.
- **Stops almost never fired, so losers ran to the 14:50 flat.** Only ULTRACEMCO (12:43, -410.91) hit its stop. ITC (-363.32) and NIFTYBEES (-374.15) ended just above their stops and were closed by the time rule instead. The wide stops added risk without ever cutting a loss early.
- **The open book was heavily long into a down afternoon.** Seven of the 8 open positions were longs. KOTAKBANK, ITC and NIFTYBEES were effectively overlapping long-bank and index exposure. On days like this, the inversion behaves like one correlated long bet.

## Try next
- **Filter: lock the day once the target is reached.** Block all new entries once realized P&L is at or above Rs 1000. Today that would have frozen the book near +1068, with only the already-open KOTAKBANK, POWERGRID, ITC, HDFCBANK, COALINDIA and ONGC still running.
- **Filter: no new entries after 10:30.** The orb signal's edge today was confined to the first hour after the 15-minute range formed.
- **Knob: stop_frac 0.5 (from the current wide setting).** This brings risk closer to the small target. Test whether the 09:31-10:30 winners survive a tighter stop before adopting it.

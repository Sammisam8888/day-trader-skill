# Margin, not signals, was the binding constraint: 1 of 14 signals was tradable, and that trade had no stop

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy first logged activity at 13:13 and generated 14 signals by 14:11. Only one became a trade: BUY 400 INFY29DEC26F at 14:07:23, filled at 1178.30. It closed at the 14:50 flat time at 1180.90, a gain of +2.60 per share, Rs 1040 gross and Rs 762.22 net in the trade log. The day summary reports net Rs 1181, which does not match the Rs 762.22 trade-level figure. One of the two numbers is wrong, and the Rs 1000 day target was not reached on the trade-log figure.

## Learnings
- **Margin skips:** 6 signals (ICICIBANK x3, HCLTECH, SBIN, SUNPHARMA) were skipped because 1 lot needed Rs 113584 to Rs 228060 against Rs 100000 free. HCLTECH at Rs 113584 was the closest miss. On this wallet, most liquid stock futures are structurally unreachable.
- **No-contract skips:** 5 signals were wasted on NIFTYBEES and BANKBEES (13:32, 13:36, 13:51, 13:56, 14:00), which have no futures. These should be filtered out before signal generation, not after.
- **RELIANCE quote bug (13:13:09):** the system requested NFO token 59097 but received the NSE cash quote for RELIANCE-EQ (2885). This is a broker/token mapping bug, not a market issue. It must be fixed, because pricing a futures order off a cash quote would be dangerous.
- **INFY 14:07 signal-to-fill gap:** the signal was computed on the cash price 1169.90 but filled on futures at 1178.30, an 8.40 gap. That is about 3x the 2.60 the trade eventually made. The bands are computed on the underlying, but P&L runs on the futures price, so entry levels are effectively shifted by the basis.
- **No stop or target:** the INFY trade has blank stop and target columns. It ran unprotected for 43 minutes with Rs 94264 margin on 400 shares, where a 1% adverse move would have cost about Rs 4700. The win came from a small drift into the flat time, not from the bollinger exit logic.
- **Capital locked by one position:** after the INFY entry, free cash fell to Rs 5736, so TECHM at 14:11 was impossible. Any single position consumes the whole wallet.

## Try next
- **Universe filter:** before evaluating signals, keep only symbols that have a live futures contract and whose 1-lot margin is at most free cash. On this wallet that is roughly INFY-class names under Rs 100000. This drops the ETFs, ICICIBANK, SBIN, TECHM, SUNPHARMA, and HCLTECH unless margin is raised.
- **Stop:** set stop_sd = 1.0, and verify that the stop is actually written to the order and shows in the log before the next session. Keep invert = 1, sd and side unchanged, since one +2.60 trade is not evidence to retune them.
- **Basis-aware levels:** compute the bands on the futures price itself, or offset them by the live basis, so that signal levels and fill prices are on the same series. Separately, reconcile the Rs 1181 summary against the Rs 762.22 trade net.

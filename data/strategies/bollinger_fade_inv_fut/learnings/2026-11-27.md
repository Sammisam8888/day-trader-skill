# Unrealised +1025 on TATASTEEL futures counted as "day target reached", then became a -2550 stop-out, and the day summary still reports +166

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy took one trade. At 12:29 it shorted 1 lot (2750) of TATASTEEL29DEC26F at 165.12, on a SELL signal from the cash price of 164.40. That one position used Rs 90,816 of the Rs 100,000 wallet. By 12:58 the open position was up Rs 1025 and the day target was flagged as reached, but the position stayed open. It was stopped out at 14:20:52 at 165.95, a net loss of Rs -2550.13, or 2.55% of the wallet on a 0.83-point (0.5%) adverse move. The day summary says "1 won, net 166". That contradicts the log, which shows a single losing close of -2550.13. The summary is wrong, or it is reading some other P&L.

## Learnings
- **12:58 target flag**: the "day target reached, net 1025" check fired on unrealised P&L from the open TATASTEEL short. Nothing was locked in. The profit turned into a full stop loss within 82 minutes. A target based on open P&L means nothing unless reaching it also closes or protects the position.
- **Position size vs stop**: one TATASTEEL lot is about Rs 4.5 lakh notional on a Rs 1 lakh wallet. That made a 0.5% move cost 2.5% of capital. At this lot size the stop distance is too tight for the risk, or the lot is too big for the stop. There is no smaller unit to trade in futures.
- **Signal vs fill**: the signal was on cash at 164.40, but the fill was on the future at 165.12, a basis of 0.72. The stop and target columns are blank, so it is unclear whether the stop was set from cash-price Bollinger levels or adjusted for basis. If it was set in cash terms, the true stop distance on the future differs from what the model intended.
- **Margin locks out the universe**: 25+ setups were skipped. Most failed on margin: ICICIBANK needed 225k, ADANIPORTS 180k, SBIN 171k, all against 100k free. INFY at 93k and TATASTEEL at 91k were the only ones that fit. After the 12:29 entry, free margin was 9184, so every later signal was blocked. In practice this is a single-position, single-name strategy.
- **Data bug**: HDFCBANK at 11:24, ITC at 11:29, SUNPHARMA at 13:09, ADANIENT at 13:24, ADANIPORTS at 13:29 and INFY at 14:14 all returned "asked for NFO <token>, got NSE <token> -EQ". The quote lookup is falling back to the cash token. These are lost signals, and the bug is also a risk if a wrong-instrument price ever gets used for a fill or a stop.
- **One trade is not evidence**: a single trade says nothing about whether invert=1 (trading with the band break) has an edge. Do not change sd or invert on the strength of today.

## Try next
- **Filter: lock in the target.** When the day target is hit, either close all open positions or move the stop to breakeven plus charges. Today that would have banked about Rs 1025 instead of losing Rs 2550.
- **stop_sd: raise from the current value to 1.5.** Pair this with a hard rule that skips any entry where 1 lot times the stop distance exceeds Rs 1500 (1.5% of the wallet). That keeps futures losses bounded given the fixed lot sizes.
- **Filter: tighten the symbol list.** Pre-filter to symbols whose 1-lot margin is at most 95% of the wallet; today that means INFY and TATASTEEL. Block any signal whose quote comes back with an NSE -EQ token instead of the NFO token. Also fix the day-summary P&L, which reported +166 / 1 won against an actual -2550.13 loss.

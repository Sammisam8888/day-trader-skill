# Only one of 9 signals was tradable, and it lost Rs 2107 because a 1-lot TATASTEEL future put 90% of the wallet behind a 0.67-point stop

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy produced 9 signals between 13:12 and 14:22 but opened only one position. That was a SELL of 2750 TATASTEEL29DEC26F at 163.18 at 14:06, using Rs 89749 margin. It was stopped out at 163.85 at 14:18:
- Gross loss: 0.67 x 2750 = Rs 1842.50
- Charges: about Rs 265
- Net: Rs -2107.10, which is 2.1% of the Rs 100000 wallet and twice the Rs 1000 day target.

The day summary says "1 won, net Rs 164". That contradicts the trade log, which shows a single losing close of Rs -2107.10. The summary appears to be wrong and should not be trusted for this day.

## Learnings
- **The TATASTEEL stop (14:06 to 14:18) was too tight for the position size.** The stop sat 0.67 points (0.41%) from entry. With 2750 qty, that normal 12-minute wiggle cost Rs 1842. The stop/target columns are blank in the log, so the actual stop level the exit used is not recorded. That needs fixing before stop_atr can be tuned with confidence.
- **The signal and the fill came from different instruments.** The signal fired on spot TATASTEEL at 162.55, but the futures fill was 163.18. That 0.63 gap is roughly equal to the stop distance, so the trade started with almost no room relative to where the cross actually happened.
- **Margin blocks most of the universe.** On a Rs 100000 wallet, 1 lot needs more than the free cash for:
  - TCS (116320)
  - HDFCBANK (112892)
  - SUNPHARMA (134799)
  - TRENT (148684)
  - WIPRO (118164)
  - JSWSTEEL (177633)

  6 of 9 signals died this way. The strategy is effectively trading only whatever single name happens to fit.
- **One lot consumed nearly the whole wallet.** TATASTEEL used 89.7% of capital, so WIPRO at 14:08 was skipped with only Rs 10251 free. There is no sizing control: every trade is all-in on one lot.
- **Two signals were lost to data issues, not strategy logic.**
  - TECHM at 13:51: the quote lookup asked for NFO token 59133 and got NSE 13538 TECHM-EQ back. That is a symbol-mapping bug.
  - NIFTYBEES at 13:12: this is an ETF with no future. It should be filtered out of this strategy's universe up front.
- **All signals arrived after 13:12.** The only trade was opened at 14:06, late in the session, where a stopped trade leaves little time to recover.

## Try next
- **Widen the stop.** Set stop_atr = 2.5 (from whatever produced a 0.67-point stop). Add a filter to skip any futures entry where (stop distance x lot qty) exceeds Rs 1000, the day target. At 2750 qty, that requires the stop to be under about 0.36 points or the trade is skipped, which would have blocked this one.
- **Account for the spot/futures basis.** Anchor the stop to the futures fill price, not the spot signal price. Skip the entry if futures-minus-spot slippage at fill is more than 30% of the stop distance.
- **Disable or fix the futures variant.** Set enabled = 0 for this strategy until the wallet is at least about Rs 200000, or restrict the universe to contracts needing under Rs 50000 margin per lot. Also fix the TECHM NFO token mapping and the day-summary P&L reporting, which showed +164 against an actual -2107.

# Futures fills sit 0.2-0.4% above the cash signal price, and tiny R cannot cover Rs ~280 of charges per trade

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy took 3 futures trades, one lot at a time, and lost all three on net. TATASTEEL lost Rs -901, NTPC made Rs +166 and INFY lost Rs -633, so the day closed at Rs -1368 against a Rs 1000 target. The summary line ("3 won, net Rs 1648, best 1161, worst 166") contradicts the trade log and looks like a sign or abs() bug in the day-stats code. The log itself shows 1 winner and 2 losers. Thirteen other signals were skipped: the Rs 100000 wallet funds only one lot (about Rs 91-97k margin) at a time, and several contracts need more than the whole wallet. This log says nothing about the broader market direction.

## Learnings
- **Futures basis versus cash signal.** Every signal was priced on the cash stock but filled on the future, which carries a premium:
  - TATASTEEL: 165.59 vs 166.17 (+0.35%)
  - NTPC: 321.40 vs 321.95 (+0.17%)
  - INFY: 1155.30 vs 1160.00 (+0.41%)
  
  The sweep, stop and target levels come from cash structure. If they are applied to futures prices without adjusting for basis, the risk is distorted. On the 10:48 TATASTEEL BUY, the "stop" fired at 165.94, which is still above the cash signal price of 165.59. The trade was stopped out by the basis gap, not by the setup failing.
- **R is too small for the charges.** The 12:10 NTPC "target" exit captured only 0.30 points (0.09%), or Rs 450 gross. About Rs 284 of that went to charges, leaving Rs 166. Implied round-trip charges on all three trades were about Rs 270-285. With R this small, a 1R win barely clears costs and a 1R loss is roughly doubled by them.
- **TATASTEEL lost more to charges than to price.** The 11:19 loss of Rs -901 was only 0.23 points x 2750 = Rs 632 of price move; the remaining Rs ~269 was charges.
- **INFY never resolved.** The 12:22 SELL sat for about 2.5 hours without reaching its stop or target and was force-closed at 14:50 for Rs -633 (Rs 360 price, Rs ~273 charges). While it was open, it blocked 7 other signals for lack of margin, including NTPC three times.
- **Data bugs:**
  - At 11:34 the TATASTEEL quote lookup asked for NFO token 59131 and received the NSE equity token 3499. This is a contract-mapping bug.
  - At 12:07 BANKBEES was scanned even though it has no futures contract. It belongs out of the fut universe.

## Try next
- Set `target_r` to 2.5 (from whatever produced a 0.30-point NTPC target). Add a filter that skips any trade where the gross target, (target - entry) x qty, is less than 3x the estimated round-trip charges (about Rs 850).
- Compute sweep, stop and target on the futures chart itself, or shift the cash levels by the live basis (fut LTP - cash LTP) before placing the order. Also skip any trade where that basis exceeds 0.5x the risk distance.
- Add a time-stop that exits any position open more than 45 minutes without reaching 0.5R, which would have freed margin during the INFY stall. Separately, fix the NFO token mapping, remove ETFs such as BANKBEES from the fut universe, and correct the day-summary win/net calculation.

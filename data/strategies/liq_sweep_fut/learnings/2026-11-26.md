# One INFY lot made the day, but 12 of 15 signals never executed and the P&L figures contradict each other

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
liq_sweep_fut generated 15 signals between 13:11 and 14:30 but executed only one: a BUY of 1 lot (400) INFY29DEC26F at 13:17. The fill was 1169.80, while the signal fired on the cash price of 1161.80. The trade hit its target at 1180.80 at 14:49. The other 14 signals were skipped:
- 4 because of a quote-resolution bug.
- 9 for margin. 7 of these needed more than the entire Rs 100000 wallet.
- 1 (NIFTYBEES) because it has no futures contract.

The day's net figure is not reliable. The close row says net Rs 4122.38, which matches 11 points x 400 = Rs 4400 gross less about Rs 278 charges. The summary says Rs 1181, and the "DAY TARGET reached at 13:39: net 1124" banner fired while the INFY trade was still open.

## Learnings
- **The one trade worked as designed.** The INFY sweep-and-reclaim long from 13:17 ran 11 futures points to target in about 1.5 hours. With 1 lot that is roughly Rs 4400 gross. It is one sample, though, and says nothing about the recipe's edge.
- **Signal and fill are on different instruments.** The signal was on INFY cash at 1161.80 and the fill was on the future at 1169.80, an 8-point (0.69%) basis gap. Stop and target are blank in the log, so it is unclear whether they were set from the spot reference or the futures fill. If they used spot, the real R multiple was distorted by the basis.
- **The symbol-resolution bug cost a third of the tradeable signals.**
  - SBIN (13:11), SUNPHARMA (13:13), AXISBANK (13:32) and HINDUNILVR (13:39) all asked for an NFO token and got the NSE -EQ quote back. They were skipped as "no quote."
  - This is a data bug, not a strategy decision. It means today's signal-to-trade conversion says nothing about the recipe.
- **The wallet size makes most of the universe untradeable.**
  - ADANIENT (about Rs 166k), TCS (116k), BHARTIARTL (202k), LT (145k), JSWSTEEL (177k), HINDALCO (130k) and ICICIBANK (227k) each need more than Rs 100000 for 1 lot, even with no position open.
  - INFY used 94% of the wallet (Rs 93584), so NTPC at 14:20 (Rs 96570, which would otherwise fit) was also blocked.
  - In practice this is a one-position-per-day strategy.
- **The accounting cannot be trusted yet.**
  - Three different numbers appear for one trade: 1124 at 13:39, 4122.38 on the close row, and 1181 in the summary.
  - The day-target banner fired on unrealized MTM before any close.
  - Until this is reconciled, "day target reached" and win/loss stats for this strategy are not meaningful.

## Try next
- **Fix the NFO token lookup.** Hard-fail and log the bug instead of silently returning the -EQ quote. Then re-run 2026-11-26 to see how SBIN, SUNPHARMA, AXISBANK and HINDUNILVR would have done.
- **Add a pre-signal filter on margin.** Skip any stock whose 1-lot futures margin exceeds 60% of the wallet (Rs 60000 at the current size). This removes dead signals from the log. Alternatively, run this recipe on cash equity (where qty can scale) and keep futures only for names like INFY and NTPC that fit.
- **Compute stop and target from the futures fill price, not the spot signal price.** Log both so the basis effect is visible. Keep the knobs unchanged until the P&L figures reconcile. One winning trade is not enough to justify moving target_r or sweep_pct.

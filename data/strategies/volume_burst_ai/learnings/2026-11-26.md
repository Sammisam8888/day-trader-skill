# A 2:1 payoff, not the hit rate, made the day: 10 wins and 8 losses still netted Rs 4731

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy went long only and closed 18 trades. The log's own closes show **10 won and 8 lost, net Rs 4731 after charges**.

The "Day numbers" line is wrong and should be fixed upstream. It claims 18/18 won, Rs 18432 net, best 2690 and worst 163. The real best was HINDUNILVR at +1011.08 and the real worst was ITC at -649.38. The 10:00 day-target banner ("net 1003") also conflicts with the logged running total: realized was 973 at 09:58 and 423 at 10:05.

The average win was about Rs 794 and the average loss about Rs 401, so the target/stop geometry carried a 56% hit rate. The day split into three phases:
- **Morning:** good, reaching 3332 by 11:05.
- **Midday:** bad, sliding to 1683 by 13:28.
- **Afternoon:** strong in IT names, peaking at 5083 by 14:49, then dropping to 4731 at the 14:50 flatten.

## Learnings
- **Payoff carried the day.** Every target hit paid roughly 2x a typical stop: HINDUNILVR +1011, HCLTECH 14:07 +1005, BEL 09:56 +973, SUNPHARMA +942, TCS +940, TECHM +936. Losses stayed capped at about Rs 350-650. The current target_x setting is doing its job.
- **Midday entries failed.** Stops hit on BEL 11:54 (-601) and SBIN 11:57 (-88). POWERGRID, opened at 10:32, drifted for 103 minutes before stopping at 12:15 (-605). ITC 13:38 (-649) also stopped. From 11:05 to 13:53, realized P&L fell from 3332 to 1778. Volume bursts in this window did not follow through.
- **Re-entering a stock that failed earlier lost twice more.** WIPRO stopped at 10:05 (-550), and re-entering at 11:02 stopped again (-356). BEL won at 09:58, but the 11:54 re-entry lost (-601).
- **Two trades were too small to matter.** ONGC at 10:39 was 7 shares, about Rs 1.6k notional, and lost -4.95. ADANIENT at 10:34 was 3 shares and made +48. Capital was mostly tied up in open positions, so these were charge-dominated noise.
- **The late TATASTEEL re-entry ran out of time.** It reopened at 14:20:16, 32 seconds after the 14:19 target exit. It needed +0.36% with 30 minutes left and was flattened at 14:50 for -352. The 14:07 HCLTECH trade needed 39 minutes to reach target, so entries after about 14:15 have little runway.
- **The afternoon IT cluster was the day's real signal.** HCLTECH (twice), TECHM and TCS all hit target between 13:50 and 14:49, adding +3624 in total.

## Try next
- **Leave the knobs unchanged.** Keep vol_x, body_atr and target_x where they are. One day gives no per-trade volume or body data to justify moving them, and the payoff ratio is working.
- **Add a time filter:** no new entries from 11:45 to 13:15, and none after 14:15.
  - Today the window filter would have skipped BEL 11:54 and SBIN 11:57 (-689).
  - The late cutoff would have skipped TATASTEEL 14:20 (-352).
  - Treat this as one-day evidence to test, not a proven rule.
- **Add a per-symbol and minimum-size filter.**
  - Skip a symbol for the rest of the day after it hits a stop. This would have saved WIPRO 11:02, -356.
  - Skip any entry with notional under Rs 20000, which would have dropped the ONGC and ADANIENT trades.
  - Separately, fix the day-summary calculation so it matches the closes.

# Every entry after noon lost, turning a near-flat day into Rs -1560

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The market chopped in both directions, and longs and shorts were stopped out alike. The strategy closed 14 trades: 5 wins and 9 losses (36% win rate), for a net of Rs -1560.19 after charges. Best was ITC short at +914.72, worst was LT short at -676.56, and the Rs 1000 day target was never reached. Peak equity was only +42, at 12:57.

The pre-computed "Day numbers" line (14 won, net Rs 18344, best 4695, worst 223) does not match the trade log and should not be trusted. Every figure here comes from the log rows.

## Learnings
- **Afternoon entries lost every time.** By 12:02 the strategy had recovered to Rs -103, helped by the ITC long target. After that, every new entry lost:
  - POWERGRID short at 12:22: -497.91
  - ONGC short at 12:36: -256.87
  - TRENT long at 12:53: -493.48
  - COALINDIA long at 13:09: -529.82
  - Together that is Rs -1778. Trades opened before 12:00 netted about Rs +218.
  - Volume bursts in the lunch and early-afternoon lull did not follow through.
- **Instant reversal into a stopped name doubled a loss.**
  - COALINDIA short was stopped at 388.01 at 13:08:54. A long opened 51 seconds later at the same 388.01 and was stopped at 387.49, for a combined Rs -1108 on one stock.
  - The ITC flip did work: short target at 11:29, then long at 11:54 for +453.10. The difference was a 25-minute gap, not an immediate re-entry.
- **Some stops were too tight for the charges.**
  - ONGC risked 0.13 per share (223.21 to 223.34) on 878 shares. The fill at 223.41 lost Rs 175 gross but Rs 257 net, so about a third of the loss was charges and slippage.
  - NIFTYBEES had a target of only 0.59 away and netted just Rs 187 on 418 shares.
- **Stop fills slipped past the stop on fast moves.**
  - LT stop 4126.5 filled at 4128.95, costing about Rs 105 extra on 43 shares.
  - BEL stop 411.2 filled at 410.99.
  - Real risk per trade was larger than the stop distance implies.
- **The targets themselves were fine.** All 5 winners hit full target: ITC 11:29, ADANIPORTS 11:51, ITC 12:02, HCLTECH 12:57, NIFTYBEES 10:59.
  - The problem was signal quality, at 9 stops, not the target_x setting.
  - At roughly 2:1 reward-to-risk, a 36% hit rate is break-even before charges and negative after.
- **Longs were worse than shorts, but both lost.**
  - Longs: 2 wins, 5 losses, net Rs -1092.
  - Shorts: 3 wins, 4 losses, net Rs -468.
  - That is too small a sample to justify switching side today.

## Try next
- **Time filter:** no new entries after 12:15. Entries after 12:00 today were 0 for 4 for Rs -1778.
- **Re-entry cooldown:** after a stop on a stock, block entries in the opposite direction on that stock for 20 minutes (the COALINDIA 13:09 case).
- **Tighter signal plus minimum stop:**
  - Raise vol_x to 3.5 and body_atr to 1.5 to cut marginal bursts. The 9 stop-outs say entries are firing on weak bursts.
  - Skip any trade where the stop distance times qty is under Rs 250, or the stop is under 0.1% of price. This targets ONGC-style trades where charges eat most of the edge.
  - Keep target_x unchanged.

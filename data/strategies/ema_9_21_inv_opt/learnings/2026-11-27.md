# Gave back Rs 2371 of a Rs 2865 peak because two stops of about Rs 1950 each erased five small target wins

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy bought 1-lot calls on BUY signals (SUNPHARMA, HDFCBANK, BEL, TRENT) late in the morning, then rotated into puts after midday as SELL signals hit LT, RELIANCE, WIPRO, POWERGRID, JSWSTEEL and SUNPHARMA. Realized P&L peaked at Rs 2865 at 12:38 after the RELIANCE put hit its target. It ended the day at Rs 494 net over 10 closed trades: 8 winners and 2 stop-outs. Best trade was RELIANCE P1390 at +1617.53, worst was JSWSTEEL P1320 at -1959.57.

The summary line ("10 won, net 444, best 124, worst 6") does not match the trade log and looks like a reporting bug. The log sums to Rs 493.74. The "DAY TARGET reached at 11:49: net 1225" banner also fired before any trade had closed, so it must have counted unrealized marks.

## Learnings
- **Payoff was badly lopsided in premium terms.**
  - Stops cost 7-10% of premium: SUNPHARMA C1900 went 53.85 to 48.50 at 12:59 (-1925), and JSWSTEEL P1320 went 38.55 to 35.75 at 14:20 (-1960).
  - Targets paid 0.5-3%: BEL went 13.20 to 13.60, POWERGRID 8.05 to 8.25, WIPRO 5.76 to 5.88, and SUNPHARMA P 40.65 to 40.85.
  - At this ratio a single stop wipes out three to five wins. An 80% win rate still netted only Rs 494.
- **Some targets were too small to beat charges.** The SUNPHARMA P1900 target at 14:45 grossed Rs 70 and netted Rs 24.50. The WIPRO target lost about Rs 53 of its Rs 360 gross to charges. Targets under about 1% of premium are not worth taking.
- **Continuing past the day target cost money.** The account was at +2865 at 12:38, nearly 3x the Rs 1000 target. New entries after that (POWERGRID, JSWSTEEL, SUNPHARMA P) netted -1604. The JSWSTEEL entry at 13:19 alone took the day from +1271 to -255.
- **SUNPHARMA whipsawed both ways.** The call was opened at 1909 at 11:24 and stopped at 12:59. A put was then opened at 1905.90 at 14:34, with the underlying barely moved, and closed for +24.50. The 9/21 cross on SUNPHARMA was signalling chop, not trend.
- **Capital tie-up caused skips.** HCLTECH at 12:24 (Rs 20260) and SBIN at 13:04 (Rs 26512) were skipped because four or five open lots had absorbed about Rs 80k. Slow-moving positions like TRENT (held 11:34 to 14:50 for +418) and HDFCBANK (held 11:29 to 14:13 for +433) blocked fresh signals.
- **The KOTAKBANK skip at 14:14 was a data bug, not a strategy decision.** The quote request for NFO token 134955 returned NSE 1922 KOTAKBANK-EQ, so the option-token lookup is mapping to the equity.

## Try next
- **Rebalance the exits:** set stop_atr to 1.5 and target_atr to 3.0, so stops are smaller and targets clear charges by a wide margin. Add a filter that rejects any entry whose target is less than 1.5% of the option premium.
- **Lock in the day target:** add a filter that blocks new entries once realized net is at or above the Rs 1000 target. If that is too strict, block them once realized net drops 30% from its intraday peak. Today either rule would have stopped trading around 12:38-12:59 with roughly Rs 940-2865 banked.
- **Slow the crossover to cut chop:** try fast 9, slow 26 to reduce re-crosses like SUNPHARMA's. Separately, fix the NFO token resolution for KOTAKBANK before the next session.

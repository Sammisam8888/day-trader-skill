# Logged option buys lost Rs 11,992, yet the summary line reports +Rs 358 with 9/9 winners, so the scoring is broken

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The log covers 13:28 to 14:50. In that window 9 positions closed: 1 winner (POWERGRID C267.5, +47.83) and 8 losers, for a combined -11,992. The "day so far" column finishes at -13,014, which means about -1,022 came from closes before 13:28 that the log does not show. The supplied "Day numbers" (9 won, net +358, best 111, worst 6) contradict the log on every figure and should not be trusted. Nearly every trade bought puts on SELL signals, and those puts lost premium, which suggests the underlyings rose or held up through the afternoon.

## Learnings
- The trade labels are wrong. INFY P1160 closed at 13:37 marked "target" for -187, and TRENT P3300 closed at 13:49 marked "target" for -1,264 (116.00 to 110.70 in 2 minutes). Either the target is checked on the underlying while the premium falls, or the label logic is buggy. Until this is fixed, win/loss stats for this strategy mean nothing.
- The puts went against the signals. TRENT (SELL at 3290), TECHM (SELL at 1822.60, -2,995 stop at 14:07), NTPC (-859) and WIPRO (-321) all lost premium after entry. The SELL calls from the inverted EMA cross were on the wrong side this afternoon.
- The biggest single loss came from a carried position. TCS P2560 was opened before the log window and closed at the 14:50 flat at 63.70 against 79.00 entry, a 19% premium loss (-3,490). The stop either did not exist or never triggered on this position.
- Stops sit too far out for option premium. BAJFINANCE lost 2,249 (-7.7% premium) and TECHM lost 2,995 (-8.7%). On 1-lot sizes of Rs 26k-34k, each stop gives back 2-3x the Rs 1,000 day target.
- Late entries had no time to work. WIPRO (14:08) and NTPC (14:33) were forced out at the 14:50 flat for a combined -1,180, before any move could develop.
- The affordability skips are uneven. HDFCBANK (16,575), TATASTEEL (19,085) and JSWSTEEL (23,558) were skipped, but TECHM at 33,600 was taken. Whether a trade gets in depends on how much capital is already tied up, not on signal quality.

## Try next
- Set `invert 0` for one session, or run it side by side with the inverted version, to test whether the un-inverted cross would have caught today's afternoon direction.
- Tighten the stop to `stop_atr 1.0` and set `target_atr 2.0`, so a single loser cannot exceed roughly the day target. Add a filter that blocks new entries after 14:00, given the forced flat at 14:50.
- Fix the bookkeeping before trusting any stats:
  - "target" closes must be checked against premium P&L.
  - The day summary must be built from the logged closes.
  - The log must include the pre-13:28 trades (the TCS, BHARTIARTL and POWERGRID entries, plus the -1,022 of earlier closes).

# Shorts paid on a down-drifting day, but three longs against the tape and four tight stops lost more, so orb5 finished Rs -311

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The tape drifted lower all day: NIFTYBEES slid from 293.99 at 10:31 to 292.40 at 14:50, and every short still open at the flat close was in profit except SUNPHARMA. orb5 made 13 trades: 5 won and 8 lost, for a net of Rs -310.51 after charges. The best trade was ADANIENT at +819.76 and the worst was COALINDIA at -559.88. Only one trade reached its target (AXISBANK, 11:23). The header's "13 won, net Rs 17760, best 4114, worst 223" does not match this log and should be treated as a reporting bug, not as the result.

## Learnings
- **Shorts made money and longs lost it.** The 10 shorts netted about +510. The 3 longs netted about -821: NTPC 09:47 lost -302.05, HDFCBANK 10:09 lost -518.44, and POWERGRID lost -0.21. On a day where the index breakout was clearly down, taking upside breakouts in NTPC and HDFCBANK was the whole difference between a small green day and a red one.
- **Stops were hit by a few paise at the range edge, then price did not keep going.**
  - TECHM closed at 1858.85 against a 1858.75 stop.
  - HINDUNILVR closed at 2131.25 against 2131.00.
  - COALINDIA closed at 389.11 against 389.07.
  - NTPC closed at 321.59 against 321.62.
  
  These four account for -1943. COALINDIA is the worst case: it survived from 09:30 to 14:38, then was stopped 12 minutes before the flat close. The stop sits exactly on the opposite range boundary, which is where noise probes.
- **Targets are too far for this kind of day.**
  - RELIANCE captured 7.20 of a 17.95 target.
  - LT captured 13.30 of 41.95.
  - ADANIENT captured 24.70 of 55.35.
  - NIFTYBEES captured 1.59 of 2.37.
  
  All four were closed by the 14:50 flat rule, not by their targets. Winners were capped by time, while losers took the full stop.
- **Early entries did worse than late ones.** The six 09:30 shorts plus the 09:32 and 09:47 longs netted about -802. The later breakouts (HDFCBANK, ONGC, NIFTYBEES, ADANIENT, SUNPHARMA) netted about +492. ADANIENT at 11:22 was the day's best trade, so a late breakout was not a problem in itself.
- **Two trades were sized at quantity 1.** POWERGRID (09:32) and ONGC (10:30) were each opened with qty 1 because margin was already used by six 09:30 shorts worth about Rs 5 lakh notional. They added charges and noise and no real exposure. Opening six same-direction entries in the same second is also a single correlated bet, not six independent ones.

## Try next
- **Restrict to the index direction.** Set side to short-only when NIFTYBEES has broken its own opening range downward, and long-only when it has broken upward. Check which value of side (0..2) maps to short-only before applying this. On today's log this alone would have removed -821.
- **Widen the stop beyond the range edge.** stop_frac is already at its 1.0 maximum, so the stop can only go further out as a buffer past the boundary, for example 0.1 x range width. Set target_x to 1.5 so time-capped winners like RELIANCE and NIFTYBEES are banked before 14:50.
- **Skip undersized trades.** Add a filter to skip any entry where computed qty x price is under Rs 5000 (that would have removed POWERGRID and ONGC). Cap simultaneous 09:30 entries at 3 so margin is left for later breakouts like ADANIENT. This is one day of evidence, so re-check these changes over at least 10 sessions before locking them in.

# One RELIANCE target (+1697) rescued a -1098 morning, and the day's summary line is wrong

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy opened six ORB positions at 09:30 and lost Rs 1098 by 10:20. Most of that came from two stops: TATASTEEL at -528 and LT at -551. RELIANCE was entered long at 10:31 and hit its target at 14:43 for +1696.60, and the 14:50 flat sweep added a net +655. Per the trade log, the day closed **13 trades, 8 won, 5 lost, net Rs +1263**. Best was RELIANCE at +1696.60 and worst was LT at -551.41.

The two summary sources disagree with this log:
- The "Day numbers" line says 13/13 won, net 34136, best 14064, worst 160. That is not consistent with the log.
- The "DAY TARGET reached at 11:20: net 1011" banner is also wrong. The running total was -1098 at 10:20 and -1095 at 11:28. The target was actually crossed only at 14:50 (TRENT flat close, 1263).

Both summary paths need fixing before their numbers can be trusted.

## Learnings
- **TATASTEEL (09:30 BUY, stopped 09:34)** carried a 1.23% stop distance (162.24 to 160.25). It reversed through the whole range in under 5 minutes. A range that wide on a Rs 160 stock gives no edge and forces a large qty (251), which turned one bad breakout into -528.
- **LT (09:30 SELL, stopped 10:20)** lost -551 on a 0.52% stop. Sizing is risk-normalised to about Rs 500 per trade for the 09:30 batch, so each early stop costs about Rs 500. Two of six failing put the day at about -2R before 10:30.
- **RELIANCE (10:31 BUY)** was the only full-size entry after the open. It made about 3.6R (risk 6.6, reward 24.9) and alone produced 134% of the day's net. Without it the day is -434.
- **Late entries were sized at qty 1** because capital was already deployed: HINDUNILVR, HDFCBANK, KOTAKBANK, NTPC and ITC. Combined they netted about -9 after charges. They add trade count and charges but contribute nothing to P&L.
- **Only 2 of 13 trades reached target** (NTPC, RELIANCE). The rest of the profit came from holding to the 14:50 flat close: TRENT +426 (about 1R), COALINDIA +166, TITAN +130. MARUTI gave back -84. Targets at about 3.4-3.6x are rarely hit, so the strategy's P&L depends on unstopped trades drifting favourably until flat.

## Try next
- Set `max_width_pct` to 1.0 so wide-range names like today's TATASTEEL (1.23% stop) are skipped. The goal is to remove the fast whipsaw losers that cost about Rs 500 each.
- Add a filter: skip any entry whose computed qty gives a risk budget below about 50% of the normal Rs 500 (today's qty-1 trades). Keeping that capital free would let a full-size entry like RELIANCE be taken when it appears.
- Keep `target_x` unchanged for now; RELIANCE shows the 3.5x tail is what makes the day. But log max favourable excursion per trade so the next review can test `target_x` 2.5 against today's flat-closed winners (TRENT, TITAN, COALINDIA).

# Long fades of down-drives lost Rs 1091 in two stops, more than the shorts' entire Rs 874 profit

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The trade log shows 12 closed trades: 6 wins and 6 losses, net Rs -217 after charges. The day target of Rs 1000 was never reached. The best trade was SBIN at +344.27 and the worst was AXISBANK at -551.47. The "Day numbers" summary line (12 won, Rs 20159, best 4709, worst 165) is wrong and should not be used. Its "best" and "worst" match the TITAN and TATASTEEL entry prices, so it looks like it summed prices instead of P&L. The tape split by direction:
- **Fading up-drives (SELL):** 10 trades, net +874.33.
- **Fading down-drives (BUY):** 2 trades, both stopped out, net -1091.55.

## Learnings
- **The two longs were the whole loss.** Both kept falling after the fade entry.
  - AXISBANK BUY at 09:46:03 (entry 1403.55) was stopped at 09:56:57 for -551.47.
  - BHARTIARTL BUY at 09:46:03 (entry 2093.35) was stopped at 10:34:55 for -540.08.
  - The tape gave back opening gains (6 of 10 shorts reached target) but did not bounce from opening losses. Fading the down-drive was the wrong side today.
- **Short fades worked when entered early.** ADANIPORTS, SBIN, ADANIENT, HINDALCO and NTPC all hit target, for +1123.72 combined. NTPC (10:32) and SBIN (09:55) were the best at +318.70 and +344.27.
- **Some setups risked far more than they could make.**
  - ADANIPORTS at 09:46:00: target 3.25 points away, stop 30.45 points away (about 1:9). It won only +33.34 after charges, while risking roughly Rs 490.
  - AXISBANK (target 4.45 vs stop 6.85) and BHARTIARTL (target 9.15 vs stop 14.65) also had targets well short of their stops. When those stops hit, one loss erased two or three wins.
- **Five positions drifted to the 14:50 flatten for -249.39 combined.** These were TRENT -100.85, POWERGRID -147.64, JSWSTEEL -62.36, TITAN -14.16 and TATASTEEL +75.62.
  - TRENT (09:46) and TITAN (09:50) sat for 5 hours without reaching stop or target, so the fade signal had no follow-through.
  - The late entries JSWSTEEL (10:24) and POWERGRID (10:26) never moved toward target. The late NTPC entry (10:32) did, so entry time alone is not a clean filter on this sample.
- **Charges matter on the small-target trades.** TITAN moved +1.20 x 5 shares for a gross of about Rs 6, but closed at -14.16 net.

## Try next
- **Run short fades only.** Set `side` to the short-only value (confirm the 0/1/2 mapping in the recipe first). Today this would have turned -217 into about +874. Re-check after a few up-trend days, since one day is not enough to drop longs permanently.
- **Add a reward-to-risk filter.** Skip any entry where the target distance is less than 0.5x the stop distance. This would have removed ADANIPORTS (0.11x), and on the long side AXISBANK (0.65x) would sit near the line. Alternatively, cap the stop at about 1.5x the target distance.
- **Add a time stop.** If a position has not moved 50% of the way to target within 90 minutes, close it. On today's trades this would have exited TRENT, TITAN, POWERGRID and JSWSTEEL early instead of holding them to 14:50.

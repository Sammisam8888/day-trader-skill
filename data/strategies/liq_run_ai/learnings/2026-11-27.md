# Margin was used up in 12 minutes and a metals cluster lost Rs 960; a Rs 2399 day was smaller than it could have been

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy shorted every signal, opening 15 trades all on the SELL side. That suggests a broadly weak tape where breakdowns below liquidity pools mostly held. The trade log nets **Rs 2399 after charges**, from 10 wins and 5 losses. The best trade was RELIANCE at +967.70 and the worst was COALINDIA at -552.38.

The "Day numbers" line (15/15 won, net 13738, best 2131, worst 165) does not match the log and should not be trusted. The "DAY TARGET reached at 11:00: net 1019" banner was also unrealized P&L, because no trade had closed by then. The first realized result was HINDALCO's stop at 11:06, putting the day at -295.

## Learnings
- **The 2R targets carried the whole day.** Three trades hit their targets:
  - NTPC at 12:10: +948.89
  - ADANIPORTS at 13:29: +875.84
  - RELIANCE at 14:45: +967.70

  Together they made Rs 2792, more than the day's net. All three were sized at 72-424 shares, meaning full risk.
- **Position size collapsed after 10:28.** The first five shorts carried about Rs 4.99L of notional on a Rs 1L wallet: RELIANCE 1.43L, NTPC 1.37L, JSWSTEEL 0.97L, COALINDIA 0.67L, HINDALCO 0.55L. Every later signal except AXISBANK, ADANIPORTS, SBIN and HINDUNILVR was sized at 1-2 shares. Those were KOTAKBANK, TATASTEEL, BEL x2, NTPC re-entry and BAJFINANCE, and they netted Rs 2.05 combined. BAJFINANCE fell 9.55 points from entry, which would have paid well at normal size.
- **The metals shorts were one correlated bet, and it lost.** COALINDIA, JSWSTEEL, HINDALCO and TATASTEEL netted about -960. HINDALCO stopped at 11:06, COALINDIA stopped at 13:42, and JSWSTEEL ended slightly underwater at the 14:50 flat. This cluster used about 2.2L of the early margin that later starved the bank and FMCG signals.
- **Stop fills slipped past the stop level.**
  - HINDALCO: stop 922.11, filled 923.00, costing about Rs 53 extra on 60 shares.
  - COALINDIA: stop 388.36, filled 388.46.

  Losses therefore ran slightly beyond 1R.
- **The 14:50 flat was mildly positive.** AXISBANK, SBIN and HINDUNILVR closed +565 combined. All were in profit but short of target, so later entries such as the 13:30 SBIN short had little time to reach 2R.
- **Quick re-entries in the same names failed.** The NTPC re-short at 12:24 came 14 minutes after its target was hit, and the BEL short at 11:20 was the same kind of trade. Both stopped together at 12:35. A pool that has just paid out did not offer a fresh trap.

## Try next
- **Add a margin-aware filter.**
  - Cap concurrent exposure at about 3x wallet so later signals keep full size.
  - Skip any entry whose risk in rupees is below Rs 150, since the 1-2 share trades add charges and noise but no edge.
- **Add a sector filter.** Allow at most 2 open shorts per sector (metals: TATASTEEL, JSWSTEEL, HINDALCO, COALINDIA) and block re-entry in a stock for 60 minutes after it closes at target.
- **Keep target_r at 2.0, since it produced every large winner.** Raise hold_bars by 1 (for example 1 to 2) so a break must hold one more bar before entry. This should filter breaks like HINDALCO and COALINDIA, which reversed through the stop.

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
All four entries were SELL signals on the underlying, expressed as long Dec-29 puts at about Rs 11-16.5k premium each. Up to three were open at once (about Rs 49k deployed between 11:44 and 12:00). From the log, the closed trades were BEL -1828.13, SUNPHARMA +1538.68, ITC -38.45 and HDFCBANK -1088.37. That is net Rs -1416.27, with 1 win and 3 losses. The "Day numbers" summary (4 won, net 92, best 52, worst 7) does not match this log and looks like a reporting bug. Two more entries, RELIANCE at 12:44 and LT at 12:59, were skipped because the NFO token lookup returned the NSE equity quote instead of the option quote.

## Learnings
- **BEL put (11:39:45 to 12:00:06):** the stop hit in 20 minutes. Premium fell from 11.45 to 10.20, a -10.9% move. The underlying reversed upward almost immediately after the 409.15 signal. One stopped trade lost Rs 1828, which is 1.8x the Rs 1000 day target. The sizing is roughly a flat Rs 16k premium per trade, so it does not cap the rupee loss at the stop.
- **SUNPHARMA put (11:44:39 to 13:00:04):** the only clean follow-through. Premium went from 47.20 to 51.75 (+9.6%) and hit the target in 75 minutes. Its +1538.68 was smaller than the BEL loss, so the effective reward:risk on premium was below 1:1 today.
- **HDFCBANK put (11:29:44 to 14:50:28):** held 3h20m with no stop or target hit. It bled from 25.15 to 23.55 and was flattened for -1088.37. The signal never developed, and there is no time-based exit, so a stale trade drifted into a larger loss than a scratch.
- **ITC put (13:34:34 to 14:50:11):** entered with only 76 minutes left before the forced 14:50 flat. Exit price equalled entry, so the -38.45 is pure charges. Late entries have too little time to reach an ATR-based target.
- **Logging gap:** the stop and target columns are blank on every open. I cannot verify from the log whether BEL was stopped at the intended level, or whether stop_atr and target_atr map sensibly onto option premium.
- **Infra bug:** the RELIANCE and LT skips happened because the NFO instrument token resolved to the -EQ quote. Today's results are not representative of the full signal set until this is fixed.

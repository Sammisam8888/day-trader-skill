# Day net was Rs -723 (not +808): ~Rs 280 round-trip charges overwhelmed 0.04-0.30 point moves, and a "target" exit closed at a loss

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy took 3 trades after 13:00: two NTPC futures longs and one TATASTEEL futures short. Per-trade net was -733.75, +166.49 and -155.93, so the day closed at Rs -723. The day summary is wrong on three counts: it reports 3 won and Rs +808, and its "best 322, worst 164" are entry prices, not P&L. Ten candidate signals were skipped. Nine failed margin checks against the Rs 100000 wallet, and one (RELIANCE) failed because of a quote-mapping bug.

## Learnings
- **Charges dominated every trade.** Implied cost was about Rs 280 per round trip: gross 0.30 x 1500 = 450 against net 166 at 14:19, and gross 0.04 x 2750 = 110 against net -156 at 14:50. The trades captured 0.04-0.30 points, so a target smaller than about 0.2 pts on NTPC (1500 qty) or 0.1 pts on TATASTEEL (2750 qty) cannot pay for itself.
- **The 13:24 NTPC exit is labeled "target" but lost money.** Entry was 322.20 and exit 321.90, for -733.75. Signals are priced on spot (319.65) while fills are on the future (322.20), about a 2.5 point basis. The target and stop appear to be checked against spot levels, so the exit fired while the futures leg was underwater. The same spot/future mismatch makes the "target" label at 14:19 unreliable too.
- **The 13:24 loss is larger than its price move explains.** A 0.30 point move on 1500 qty is only Rs 450 gross, so the -733.75 implies roughly Rs 284 of charges or extra slippage on that fill. The 13:04 fill also went 2.55 above the signal price, which is basis, not edge.
- **The stop and target columns are blank on every open.** The log cannot show where exits were supposed to be, so the risk per trade cannot be audited.
- **A Rs 100000 wallet only fits NTPC (~96k) and TATASTEEL (~90k).** BHARTIARTL, SUNPHARMA, HINDALCO, KOTAKBANK, ADANIENT and BEL (116k-203k margin) were skipped every time. In effect the strategy is trading a 2-stock universe.
- **The RELIANCE skip at 13:13 is a bug, not a market condition.** It requested NFO token 59097 but received the NSE equity quote (2885 RELIANCE-EQ).
- **TATASTEEL 14:21 SELL went flat at 14:50 after moving 0.04.** A squeeze entry after 14:15 has too little time to develop before the 14:50 flatten.

## Try next
- **Widen the target and stop.** Raise target_atr to 4.0 and set stop_atr to 1.5. Also add a filter: skip any entry where (target distance in futures points x qty) is below 3x estimated round-trip charges, which is about Rs 850.
- **Evaluate stop and target on the futures price, not spot.** Alternatively, shift spot levels by the live basis at entry, and log the stop and target values on every open. Fix the RELIANCE NFO token lookup.
- **Pre-filter the universe and set a time cutoff.** Drop symbols whose 1-lot margin exceeds the free wallet so they are not scanned at all. Block new entries after 14:15 so trades are not force-flattened at 14:50 before they can develop.

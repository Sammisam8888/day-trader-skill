Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The trade log does not match the supplied summary line ("12 won, net Rs 477, best 89, worst 7"). The log shows 12 closed trades: 4 won and 8 lost, for a net of Rs -2765 after charges. The wins totalled Rs +5476 and the losses Rs -8241. Best trade was BAJFINANCE P1090 at +2623, worst was HINDALCO P920 at -1593. ## Learnings
- **The midday window worked.** BAJFINANCE P1090 (10:54 to 13:09, +2623), ADANIPORTS P1900 (13:19 to 13:29, +994), ONGC C222.5 (12:59 to 13:45, +809) and ADANIPORTS P1880 (13:29 to 13:58, +1050) all hit target. The two ADANIPORTS re-entries on the same move each finished within about 30 minutes.
- **Late entries lost as a group.** BHARTIARTL C2060, TCS C2600, WIPRO C192.5, RELIANCE P1380 and HDFCBANK C860 were opened between 14:14 and 14:29. They lost -1355, -667, -1023, -1177 and -476, for -4697 in total, and none reached target. Squeeze breaks after 14:10 had about 35 minutes before the 14:50 flat, which was too little room, and they failed on both calls and puts (chop, not trend).
- **Same-second clustering.** At 14:14:42 the strategy bought three IT/telecom calls at once (BHARTIARTL, TCS, WIPRO). That is one correlated bet sized as three; all three were stopped within 26 minutes. Deployed premium at that moment was about Rs 103.6k, nearly the whole wallet.
- **Morning holdovers decayed into stops.** HINDALCO P920 (opened 11:19) was stopped at 14:13 for -1593. HINDUNILVR C2140 (opened 11:59) was stopped at 14:43 for -1518. HCLTECH P1420 (opened 11:04) was flattened at 14:50 for -432. Positions held 2.5 to 3.5 hours without reaching target mostly ended as losers, and paid theta along the way.
- **No protection on the day's P&L.** After the 1000 target was hit, there was no rule to lock in gains. The day fell from +5476 at 13:58 to -2765, a swing of Rs 8241, which is more than 8x the daily target.

## Try next
- **Entry cutoff (filter):** no new entries after 14:00, since a squeeze break needs more than about 50 minutes of option life before the 14:50 flat. Today this alone would have avoided -4697.
- **Giveback lock (filter):** once realized P&L passes the day target, stop new entries if the day drops 40% from its realized peak. Also cap entries at 1 per sector per bar to stop the 14:14:42 IT/telecom triple. Separately, fix the day-target check so it counts only realized P&L.
- **Time stop (filter, since there is no knob for it):** exit any option position held 120 minutes without reaching target. This would have closed HINDALCO, HINDUNILVR and HCLTECH well before their stops or the flat. Keep squeeze, stop_atr and target_atr unchanged, because the target hits today came from timing, not from bad level settings.

# Squeeze breaks whipsawed all afternoon, the day ended near Rs -8055, and the summary line (11 won, +564) contradicts the log

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The log covers only the afternoon (13:04 to 14:50). It closed 11 trades: 2 winners (BAJFINANCE P +331, SUNPHARMA P +125) and 9 losers, together worth about Rs -6944. Including Rs -1111 from earlier trades not shown, the running total ended at Rs -8055 against a Rs 1000 target. The "Day numbers" line (11 closed, 11 won, net +564, worst 7) does not match the log and looks like a reporting bug that needs fixing. The tape was choppy and range-bound: breakouts reversed within minutes, and late entries had no time to work before the 14:50 flatten.

## Learnings
- Direction flipped on the same stock within an hour. The COALINDIA put opened at 13:29 on a SELL signal at 386.70 and was stopped at 14:12 for -237. The COALINDIA call opened at 14:32 on a BUY signal at 387.75 and was flattened for -176. A squeeze break that fires both ways in a range only a rupee wide is noise.
- Several stops sat inside normal premium noise, so charges took a large share of the loss:
  - COALINDIA P: 7.25 to 7.10 gives Rs -202 gross, -237 net.
  - POWERGRID P: 7.25 to 7.15 gives Rs -190 gross, -234 net.
  - TATASTEEL C: 7.26 to 7.19 gives Rs -192 gross, -251 net.
  - A stop measured in underlying ATR turns into about 1-2% of premium on these cheap OTM contracts.
- The big losers were full-size premium bets that went wrong fast:
  - SBIN C: -1905, 33.70 to 31.25.
  - HINDALCO C: -1288, stopped 5 minutes after the 13:47 entry, 32.20 to 30.45.
  - TITAN P: -1762, decayed from 113.85 to 104.10 and was never stopped, only flattened at 14:50.
  - Three trades lost more than the whole day target.
- Trades entered after 14:00 had no runway. TATASTEEL C (14:21) and COALINDIA C (14:32) were both flattened at 14:50 for small losses plus charges. ULTRACEMCO P was also flattened at a loss, 291.50 to 275.65 for -837.
- With no daily loss limit, the strategy kept opening positions after it was already Rs -3727 at 13:28. By 13:47 about Rs 96k of the Rs 100k wallet was tied up in premium. That is why KOTAKBANK was skipped at 13:52 for lack of cash.

## Try next
- squeeze 2.0 (stricter; fewer breaks fired in a flat range), plus a filter: no opposite-side entry on the same stock within 60 minutes of a stopped trade.
- stop_atr 2.0 and target_atr 3.0, plus a filter to skip contracts whose implied premium stop is under 3% of entry. This stops charges from eating noise-level stops.
- Filter: no new entries after 14:00, and halt new entries for the day once net is at or below Rs -3000.

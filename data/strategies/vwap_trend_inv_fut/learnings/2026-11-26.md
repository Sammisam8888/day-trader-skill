# Three NTPC futures trades that went nowhere lost Rs 1527, mostly to charges, and the day summary reports the opposite

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
NTPC futures were flat all afternoon. All six fills fell between 322.10 and 322.60. The strategy flipped BUY, SELL, BUY on NTPC29DEC26F, one lot of 1500 each time. All three trades lost: -733.75, -359.29 and -433.91, for a day net of Rs -1527. That contradicts the "Day numbers" line, which says 3 won and net +967. That line looks broken and should not be trusted. NTPC was the only contract the Rs 100000 wallet could margin, so 12 other signals were skipped.

## Learnings
- **Charges did most of the damage.** The gross price losses were -450 (13:13 buy, 322.20 to 321.90), -75 (13:27 short, 322.55 to 322.60) and -150 (13:58 buy, 322.20 to 322.10), totalling -675. Charges therefore came to about Rs 850, roughly Rs 284 per round trip. That is about 0.19 per share on a 1500 lot, so NTPC must move about 0.19 just to break even. It never moved more than 0.30 in our favour.
- **Exits labelled "target" were losers.** Every close says "target", but all three lost money, and the stop and target columns are blank. The likely cause is that signals and levels are computed on the cash price but filled on the future. Examples are signal 319.75 vs fill 322.20 at 13:13, and 320.45 vs 322.55 at 13:27. That is a 2.1-2.45 basis gap, so "target hit" on the cash price does not mean profit on the future. This needs checking in code before any knob tuning means anything.
- **The strategy whipsawed in chop.** It went long at 13:13, short at 13:27 and long again at 13:58 within a 0.5 range. The VWAP trend signal had no real trend to follow, and the inverted logic did not help either.
- **It was effectively a one-stock strategy.** Every other contract needed Rs 93816 to Rs 203860 of margin, against at most Rs 98907 free. POWERGRID at 13:47 and 13:54 missed by only about Rs 3000. INFY at 13:39 missed only because NTPC already had the margin tied up.
- **Instrument mapping is broken for JSWSTEEL.** At 13:09 and 14:34, a request for NFO token 59009 returned the NSE cash token 11723 (JSWSTEEL-EQ), so the quote was rejected. This is a data bug, not a strategy signal.

## Try next
- Raise target_atr to 8.0 and confirm to 4. This cuts flip-flopping in flat tape and only takes moves big enough to beat about 0.19 per share in charges.
- Add a filter: skip any entry where the expected target distance, measured on the future's own price, is less than 2x the round-trip charges per share. For NTPC lot 1500 that means at least 0.40.
- Compute stop and target on the futures price rather than the cash price, and fix the summary line and the "target" exit label so the day numbers match the trade log. Until then, set enabled=0 on days when NTPC is the only contract that can be margined.

# Summary says +647 and 2 wins, but the log shows 2 NTPC futures losses totalling Rs -7019, and one lot used all the margin

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
NTPC futures chopped in a narrow band of about 320.90 to 325.80, and the strategy traded only NTPC because one 1500-qty lot took 97% of the Rs 100000 wallet. It went short at 09:45 and was stopped within 3 minutes for Rs -2760.74. It then went long at 10:25 and was flattened at 14:50 for Rs -4258.06. Day net was Rs -7018.80, seven times the Rs 1000 target in the wrong direction. The "Day numbers" line (2 won, net Rs +647, best 326, worst 321) contradicts the trade log. The best/worst values look like prices, not P&L, so the summary generator is broken and should not be used to score this day.

## Learnings
- **Size was the main problem.** With a lot of 1500, each Rs 1 move in NTPC is Rs 1500. A 1.65-point stop on the 09:45 short cost Rs 2475 gross, about 2.5% of the wallet, on a single tick-level whipsaw. No position should risk that much on a Rs 1000 day target.
- **Signal and fill prices differ.** Signals use the spot price, but fills use the futures price, which runs about Rs 1.5-2.0 higher:
  - 09:45 SELL: signal 322.20, filled 324.15.
  - 10:25 BUY: signal 322.05, filled 323.55.
- **The stop may be using the wrong price series.** The 09:45 short was stopped at 325.80, only 1.65 points from its fill. If the stop is computed off spot (about 322.20 + 3.6), the premium eats roughly half the stop distance on shorts and widens it on longs. That would explain why the 10:25 long sat 2.65 points underwater for 4.5 hours without being stopped. This is a hypothesis that needs checking in code.
- **The strategy flipped on chop.** It went from SELL to BUY on the same stock 40 minutes apart, at nearly the same spot price (322.20 vs 322.05). That shows a VWAP-hugging, trendless tape where the inverted vwap_trend signal had no edge. Both directions lost.
- **The margin lock cost the rest of the day.** From 10:25 to 14:50, free margin was Rs 174, and about 25 later signals were skipped. Most other names were unaffordable anyway: 1 lot needs Rs 91k-178k against a Rs 100k wallet.
- **There is a contract mapping bug.** Requests for NFO tokens (LT, TECHM, RELIANCE, BHARTIARTL, ICICIBANK, HDFCBANK, HINDUNILVR, ITC) returned NSE equity quotes, so valid futures signals were dropped at random times.
- **Charges were about Rs 284 per round trip**: Rs 285.74 on the 09:45 trade and Rs 283.06 on the 10:25 trade. That alone is 57% of the day target for two trades.

## Try next
- **Set enabled=0 for vwap_trend_inv_fut** until the wallet can carry a lot with a stop risking 1% or less (Rs 1000) of capital. Alternatively, add a filter: skip any entry where lot_size x stop distance exceeds Rs 1000. That filter would have blocked the 09:45 NTPC short (1500 x 1.65 = Rs 2475).
- **Compute signal, stop and target on the futures price, not spot.** Also fix the NFO token lookup so futures quotes are not silently replaced by -EQ quotes. Re-test before trusting any stop_atr value.
- **If re-enabled, set confirm=4 and add a no-reversal filter:** no opposite-side entry on the same stock within 60 minutes of a stop-out. This targets the 09:45 short / 10:25 long flip-flop on NTPC.

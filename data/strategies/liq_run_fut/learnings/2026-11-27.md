# A short TATASTEEL fut caught a move worth Rs 1876, but it tied up 91% of margin for over four hours and the summary line misreports the net

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy took one trade. It shorted 1 lot (2750) of TATASTEEL29DEC26F at 10:25:21, filled at 166.20 on a spot signal of 165.66, and a trailing stop covered it at 165.42 at 14:40:49. The close row shows net Rs 1875.88. That checks out: 0.78 x 2750 = Rs 2145 gross, minus roughly Rs 270 of futures charges. The "Day numbers" footer says net Rs 165, which contradicts the trade log and looks like a reporting bug. The day target of Rs 1000 was hit at 11:20 on an open position marked at Rs 1133. About 55 other signals were skipped, mostly because 1 lot needed more than the Rs 8590 of free margin.

## Learnings
- The 10:25 TATASTEEL short worked because the break below the pool held. The trade went from about Rs 1133 open profit at 11:20 to Rs 1876 realized at 14:40, so letting it run past the day target paid off. The exit is labelled "stop" but closed in profit, so the stop had trailed. Peak open profit is not logged, so how much was given back is unknown.
- Capital, not signal quality, was the binding limit. With a Rs 100000 wallet, only sub-Rs 100k lots (TATASTEEL, NTPC, INFY) are even possible. One lot of TATASTEEL locked Rs 91410 from 10:25 to 14:40. That blocked around 45 signals, including NTPC at about Rs 96k, which fired 7 times, and INFY at Rs 93032 at 13:37.
- The contract resolver is broken for at least 10 names: TITAN, POWERGRID, ADANIENT, WIPRO, HINDUNILVR, LT, NTPC, RELIANCE, HCLTECH and ICICIBANK. Each "asked for NFO <token>, got NSE <token> -EQ", meaning the futures token lookup returned the cash-market symbol. This happened intermittently: NTPC worked at 11:19 but failed at 12:11, and TITAN failed at 10:18 but quoted at 11:02. It looks like an exchange-prefix or token-cache bug, not missing contracts.
- Some names in the universe cannot trade this instrument at all. BANKBEES and NIFTYBEES have no futures and were scanned anyway at 10:25, 11:00 and 13:48.
- The log records no stop or target for the open at 10:25. The trade's risk (R) cannot be audited, so target_r and the trail cannot be judged from this log.
- One winning trade is not evidence that the edge works. It only shows the plumbing can complete a round trip.

## Try next
- Add a filter that drops non-F&O symbols such as BANKBEES and NIFTYBEES before signal generation. Fix the NFO token lookup so a reply from the NSE equity exchange is treated as a hard error and retried, not skipped silently. Fix the summary line so it matches the realized Rs 1875.88.
- Pre-filter the universe to contracts whose 1-lot margin is at most about 95% of the wallet (currently TATASTEEL, NTPC, INFY). Rank simultaneous signals so the scarce margin goes to the best setup instead of the first one.
- Log the stop and target prices at open. Keep target_r unchanged (for example 2.0 if that is the current value) until there is a sample of 20 or more trades with known R. Do not tune it on this single trade.

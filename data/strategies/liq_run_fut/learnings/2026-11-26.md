# One trade fit the wallet, and it won, but the books disagree on how much

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The log shows no market-wide data, so today can only be judged by this strategy's own entries. liq_run_fut produced 27 signal events from 13:04 to 14:32 and took exactly one: INFY29DEC26F BUY 400 at 13:39 (fill 1173.50, signal 1164.00 on spot, margin Rs 93,880). It closed on target at 14:49 at 1180.80, a gross gain of Rs 2,920 (7.30 pts x 400). The reported net figures conflict: the close row says Rs 2,642.32, the day summary says Rs 1,181, and a "day target reached, net 1363" banner fired at 14:06 while the trade was still open.

## Learnings
- **Most signals could not be traded.** 26 of 27 signals were skipped. 20 of them were margin skips, because one lot of BEL, WIPRO, TCS, COALINDIA, HINDALCO, ITC and others needs Rs 104k to 218k against a Rs 100k wallet. INFY at Rs 93,880 was the only F&O name that fit, so today's result says almost nothing about the liq_run edge.
- **The INFY trade used 94% of the wallet.** Free margin fell to Rs 6,120 from 13:39 to 14:49, so every later signal (TRENT, LT, ITC, BHARTIARTL, HCLTECH, WIPRO, RELIANCE, HINDALCO) was blocked. That is about 4.7x notional leverage on one name, and the log shows no stop or target price.
- **The signal ran on spot but the order filled on futures.** The signal was at 1164.00 and the fill was at 1173.50, a 9.5 pt (0.8%) gap, which is consistent with futures basis. If the stop and target are computed from spot levels and applied to the future, the R-multiple is off by roughly the basis. The blank stop and target columns mean this cannot be checked.
- **The P&L accounting is broken.** Shoonya charges on about Rs 9.4 lakh of futures turnover should be roughly Rs 120 to 150, so net should be about Rs 2,780. The log instead reports Rs 2,642 (Rs 278 charges) in one place and Rs 1,181 (Rs 1,739 charges) in another. The 14:06 "target reached" counted unrealized mark-to-market as banked P&L.
- **Contract lookup fails for some names.** HCLTECH (13:22), SBIN (13:30) and POWERGRID (14:12, 14:32) asked for an NFO token and got the NSE equity token back. HCLTECH resolved at 14:02, so the bug is intermittent rather than a missing contract.
- **ETF signals are wasted.** BANKBEES (twice) and NIFTYBEES have no futures contract, so these signals can never trade here.

## Try next
- **Add a universe filter.** Generate signals only for symbols whose 1-lot futures margin is at or below free margin, and drop the *BEES ETFs. Keep recipe knobs unchanged (no lookback, touches, break_pct or target_r change) until there are more than one or two fills to judge them on.
- **Fix the trade mechanics.** Compute stop and target on the futures price at fill (or shift spot levels by the observed basis), and log both on every open row. Leave target_r where it is until R can actually be measured.
- **Fix the accounting and lookups.** Reconcile net P&L to one source, and fire the day-target check only on realized P&L. Retry the NFO token lookup when the exchange comes back as NSE, instead of skipping the signal.

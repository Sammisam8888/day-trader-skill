# Today's only profit came from a suspicious entry fill, not from the signal

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy opened one trade, a SELL of 1 lot (2750) TATASTEEL29DEC26F at 11:14:40. The signal fired at 165.37 but the fill was logged at 165.89. It closed flat at 14:50:28 at 165.31 for a logged net of Rs 1326.34, and the day target was flagged at 12:18 with net 1079. The summary line reports net Rs 165 for the same single trade. The log contradicts itself, so the day's P&L cannot be trusted until that is reconciled.

Nine other signals were skipped:
- 2 because the NFO quote lookups returned NSE equity tokens.
- 7 because 1 lot needed more margin than was free, either Rs 100000 or Rs 8760 while TATASTEEL was open.

## Learnings
- **The fill drove the result, not the signal.** At 11:14 TATASTEEL the signal price was 165.37 and the SELL was filled at 165.89. That is 0.52 of favourable slippage, about Rs 1430 on 2750 qty. Measured from the signal price, the move to the 165.31 exit was only 0.06, about Rs 165 gross, which is less than the roughly Rs 270 in charges. Judged at signal price, the trade was roughly break-even to a small loss. The simulator should not fill a SELL 0.3% above the signal price.
- **The P&L figures disagree.** The close row shows 1326.34 net, while "Day numbers" shows net 165 with best and worst both 165. One of them is wrong. The 165 may come from a price field leaking into the P&L field. Until this is fixed, the "DAY TARGET reached" flag at 12:18 is not reliable either.
- **The trade had no risk controls.** The stop and target columns are empty for the TATASTEEL open, and the position ran 3.5 hours to the 14:50 square-off. The volume_burst exit logic (target_x, stop) either did not attach or did not log.
- **The quote mapping is broken.** At 09:43 MARUTI and 09:45 ITC the system requested NFO tokens 59036 and 59005 and got NSE-EQ 10999 and 1660. These were the first two signals of the day and both were lost to a data bug, not a strategy decision.
- **The wallet is too small for the universe.** BEL, COALINDIA (twice), TECHM, POWERGRID, ONGC and TRENT all need Rs 100k to 222k per lot. With Rs 100000, only sub-Rs 100k-margin names like TATASTEEL can ever trade, and one open position locks out everything else, leaving Rs 8760 free from 12:22 to 13:09.

## Try next
- **Add a pre-trade filter on margin.** Drop any symbol whose 1-lot margin exceeds the free margin before it can generate a signal, and log those as filtered rather than skipped.
- **Fix the fill model and quote mapping before changing any knobs.** Fill a SELL at or below the signal price (bid side), reject any NFO-to-EQ token mismatch, and keep vol_x and body_atr at their current values. Today gives zero valid evidence about signal quality.
- **Force a target and stop on every entry.** Set target_x to 1.5 so a position like the 11:14 TATASTEEL trade exits on the move rather than drifting to 14:50, and refuse to open if the stop or target fields are empty.

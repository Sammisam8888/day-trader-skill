# One lot of NTPC used 97% of the wallet, so the day rode one signal that drifted to a small win

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy took one trade. It bought 1 lot (1500 qty) of NTPC29DEC26F at 13:25:11 for 322.65, then closed it at the 14:50 flat time at 323.25. That is +0.60 per share, or Rs 900 gross. The log does not agree with itself about the result:
- The close row says net Rs 615.16.
- The day summary says net Rs 323.
- A banner says the day target was reached at 14:49 with net Rs 1215, while the only position was still open.

Margin of Rs 96,795 left Rs 3,205 free, so the strategy skipped 6 later signals across 5 stocks: ITC, HCLTECH twice, TECHM, and TATASTEEL twice.

## Learnings
- **Bad entry fill.** The 13:25 NTPC signal fired at 320.65, but the simulated fill was 322.65, 2.00 (0.62%) worse. On 1500 qty that costs Rs 3,000, which is more than three times the trade's gross profit. Either the fill model takes a stale or wide ask, or the entry chases far past the burst bar. The real edge on this signal from the signal price was +2.60, not +0.60.
- **Margin decides which signals get traded.** NTPC's lot needed 96.8% of the wallet, so this was a one-trade-per-day system. The first signal that fit the wallet won by default, not because it was the best signal. The 13:17 TCS signal was skipped even with the full wallet free: TCS margin is Rs 116,433, and the same goes for ITC, HCLTECH and TECHM. Those names can never trade at this wallet size and only add noise to the log.
- **No stop or target on the trade.** The NTPC entry shows an empty stop and target, so target_x had no effect and the trade lived only until the 14:50 flat time. A +0.19% move over 85 minutes held to the time exit is not evidence that the burst signal worked. It drifted.
- **The P&L numbers can't be trusted yet.** There are three different net figures (615, 323, 1215), and the "target reached" event fired before any trade had closed. The day-target logic seems to count unrealized or wrong P&L, and charges are recorded inconsistently: Rs 285 versus Rs 577 deducted from Rs 900 gross. Today cannot count as a target day until the accounting is reconciled.
- **TATASTEEL fit the wallet but lost the race.** TATASTEEL fired twice (14:19 and 14:20) and needed about Rs 90,200, which fits the wallet. It was blocked only because NTPC was already holding the capital.

## Try next
- **Add a margin filter.** Drop from the universe any symbol whose 1-lot margin is above 95% of the wallet (TCS, ITC, HCLTECH, TECHM at Rs 100k). This stops wasted signals and skip noise.
- **Raise vol_x to 3.5 and body_atr to 1.5.** With only one slot per day, the single entry should be the strongest burst, not the first one. Also add a filter that cancels the entry if the fill is more than 0.25% away from the signal price. NTPC's 0.62% gap would have been rejected.
- **Make sure stops and targets are set, with target_x = 2.0.** Every entry should log a stop and target so the trade exits on its own logic instead of the 14:50 flat time. Fix the day-target check to use realized net after charges only, before trusting any future "target reached" flag.

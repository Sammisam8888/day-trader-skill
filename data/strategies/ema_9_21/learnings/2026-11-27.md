# Hitting the day target on paper did not lock it in: +1060 at 12:54 became -651 by 14:50

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The day's numbers line does not match the log. It claims 8 of 8 won, net Rs 7256, best 3281 and worst 164. The log actually shows 8 closed trades: 2 won and 6 lost, net Rs -651 after charges. The best trade was +1075 (TATASTEEL) and the worst was -621 (HDFCBANK). The day target was flagged at 12:54 with net 1060, but realized P&L was only +560, so the rest was unrealized gains on HDFCBANK, HCLTECH and WIPRO. Prices then chopped in both directions in the afternoon. Shorts (HDFCBANK) were stopped on a bounce and longs (HCLTECH, WIPRO) were stopped on a dip within about 30 minutes, which gave back the whole day.

## Learnings
- The give-back came from positions already open, not from new trades. HDFCBANK (open 11:29, stopped 14:13, -621), HCLTECH (open 12:24, stopped 14:45, -593) and WIPRO (open 12:34, stopped 14:43, -56) together lost Rs 1270. "Keeps trading for more" left all of that exposure live after the target was reached.
- TATASTEEL SELL at 11:19 was the one clean trend trade. It reached the 164.43 target and filled at 164.26 for +1075, which by itself was more than the Rs 1000 day target.
- Stops are tight, roughly 0.3% of price (TRENT 9.27 points, HCLTECH 4.28, HDFCBANK 2.63), so ordinary midday noise kills them. Stop fills also slipped past the stop level: TRENT filled at 3280.75 against a 3280.32 stop, and HDFCBANK at 863.75 against 863.48. 4 of 6 losers were stop-outs at this tight distance.
- BEL whipsawed. It went SELL at 11:34, was stopped at 11:50, flipped to BUY at 11:54, and was flattened at 14:50 for -17.53. A 9/21 cross on 1-minute-scale moves keeps generating opposite signals on the same stock within 20 minutes.
- The BEL SELL at 11:34:02 was sized at only 2 shares, against 368 for the later BEL BUY. That points to a capital or sizing cap being hit with 3 positions already open. The trade was pointless after charges (-4.21 on a 2-share trade) and should either be sized properly or skipped.
- The reporting bug is itself a learning. The summary line (8/8 won, +7256) is wrong and would mislead any tuning that uses it, so the summary should be recomputed from the close rows.

## Try next
- Filter: when the day target is reached on a mark-to-market basis, close all open positions at market and stop new entries, or at minimum move every open stop to breakeven. Today this would have banked about Rs 1060 instead of -651.
- Slow the cross to fast 13, slow 34 to cut same-stock flips like BEL's, and widen stop_atr to 2.0 while keeping target_atr at 4.0. Today's stops sat inside normal noise and still slipped on fills.
- Filter: skip any entry whose computed qty is below 25% of normal size (like BEL qty 2), and block new entries after 13:30. Afternoon chop produced none of today's profit.

# Choppy two-way tape: tight stops on 9/21 crosses lost Rs 2297 while 14 of 18 trades hit stop

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The midday session (11:00-14:50) was choppy with no follow-through. Shorts opened at 11:25-11:31 were stopped within 15-40 minutes. Longs opened after 11:55 were also stopped, and the 13:14-13:25 shorts in NIFTYBEES and MARUTI were stopped together at 14:42:29. Per the trade log, ema_9_21 closed 18 trades with 4 winners and 14 losers, for a net of Rs -2297 after charges. The best trade was ITC at +1028 and the worst was BANKBEES at -731. The day never got near the Rs 1000 target and was already at -2052 by 13:13. NOTE: the supplied "Day numbers" line (18 won, net 29690, best 14060, worst 163) does not match the log. It appears to report prices, such as the MARUTI and TATASTEEL fills, instead of P&L. The pipeline that builds that line should be fixed.

## Learnings
- **Both sides lost, so direction bias was not the problem. Chop was.** Shorts netted about -1221 and longs about -1076. Disabling one side would not have saved the day.
- **Whipsaw on the same name.** JSWSTEEL was shorted at 11:25 and stopped at 12:03 (-637). It was then bought at 12:05, just 63 seconds later, and stopped at 12:58 (-22). It was bought again at 14:22. Each new 9/21 cross reversed the last one, which signals a flat EMA regime rather than a trend.
- **The win rate is too low for the payoff.** Stops were about 1x the risk unit and targets about 2x (ITC: stop 1.09 away, target 2.15 away). That needs roughly a 33%+ hit rate after charges, but the day delivered 22%. Several stops filled worse than the stop level: KOTAKBANK 509.35 vs 509.26, JSWSTEEL 1309.95 vs 1309.41, MARUTI 14060.05 vs 14053.45. That slippage made real risk larger than planned. TRENT (13:47) was stopped in 2 minutes for -496, which means the stop sat inside normal noise.
- **Correlated entries doubled the drawdowns.** KOTAKBANK and BEL were both shorted at 11:31 and both stopped. NIFTYBEES and MARUTI were both stopped at 14:42:29 for -961 combined, on one index up-move.
- **Tiny-quantity trades only paid charges.** The 1-4 share entries made no meaningful money: WIPRO 1 share +1.06, NTPC 1 share -1.12, NTPC 4 shares -4.43, TATASTEEL 9 shares -5.78, JSWSTEEL 1 share -1.28. They added noise and charges with no edge.
- **What worked:** ITC short (11:01 to 13:24, +1028) and TECHM long (13:51 to 14:07, +881) were the only clean trends. Both came on names that moved steadily away from entry and were held long enough to reach target.

## Try next
- Widen the stop and the target together: `stop_atr` 1.5 (from about 1.0) and `target_atr` 3.0. This keeps the 1:2 ratio but moves stops outside the 2-10 minute noise that killed TRENT, BEL and NTPC.
- Slow the crossover to cut whipsaw: `fast` 9 and `slow` 30. Add a filter that blocks a re-entry on the same stock in the opposite direction within 60 minutes of a stop, which would have avoided the JSWSTEEL flip at 12:05.
- Add a filter to skip any entry with notional under Rs 20,000. Also cap simultaneous same-direction positions in index-heavy names (NIFTYBEES, BANKBEES, large banks) at 1, to stop correlated double stops like 11:31 and 14:42.

# Chop killed it: 30 of 43 trades hit their stops and nothing halted the drawdown

Date: 2026-11-27

Note: the "Day numbers" line (43 won, net Rs 57434, best 4706, worst 165) contradicts the trade log. It looks like prices were read in place of P&L. The figures below are recomputed from the log.

## What happened
- The tape was choppy and two-way. HDFCBANK, LT, COALINDIA and BHARTIARTL each stopped the strategy out on both sides or on re-entry.
- The strategy closed 43 trades: 13 won and 30 lost (30% hit rate), for a net of about Rs -3712 after charges.
- The best trade was ITC (short 09:45, closed 11:29) at Rs +914.72. The worst was NTPC (long 13:46) at Rs -754.41.
- The day peaked at Rs +274 at 10:35 after the JSWSTEEL target. With no loss limit, it then bled to a low of Rs -4124 at 14:33.

## Learnings
- **The payoff was too thin for a 30% hit rate.**
  - Winners averaged about Rs 415 (Rs 5390 total).
  - Losers averaged about Rs 303 (about Rs 9100 total).
  - At roughly 1.4:1 realized payoff, the strategy needs about 42% winners just to break even after charges.
  - Charges and slippage widened the losses. POWERGRID 12:46 risked about Rs 337 by stop distance (1249 x 0.26) but lost Rs 467.76. LT 10:02 filled at 4128.95 against a 4126.5 stop.
- **The only real wins were the first entries.**
  - ITC (+914.72) and JSWSTEEL (+862.86) were opened at 09:45-09:48 and given time to work.
  - From 10:36 onward, entries that sized up to around Rs 1-4 lakh notional mostly lost.
- **The 10:36-11:44 cluster cost about Rs -4200.**
  - Losers included TECHM, TATASTEEL, LT, TRENT, KOTAKBANK, HDFCBANK x2, TCS and others.
  - Volume bursts in a range-bound market were noise, not breakouts.
  - HDFCBANK shows it most clearly. The long at 11:02 was stopped at 11:20 (-683.87). The short at 11:21 was then stopped at 11:39 (-595.86). That is Rs -1280 on one stock in 37 minutes.
- **Re-entering the same stock after a stop mostly lost.**
  - Losers: BEL 10:13, COALINDIA 10:20, BEL 10:37, HDFCBANK 11:21, BHARTIARTL 12:36, COALINDIA 13:09 (a flip right after its stop), TATASTEEL 11:14.
  - The one exception was HINDUNILVR 13:25 (+540.76).
  - Net on these re-entries was about Rs -850.
- **The afternoon stops were inside normal noise.**
  - Examples: POWERGRID 268.41 with a 268.15 stop, NTPC 321.51/321.05, ONGC 224.15/223.91, and AXISBANK 14:03, all stopped within 2-5 minutes.
  - Big quantities on these tight stops made the charges large relative to the edge.
- **Several fills were too small to matter but still paid charges.** These were LT x2, BEL x9 and x4, AXISBANK x1, ADANIPORTS x1, INFY x1 and JSWSTEEL x1. The sizing logic is producing junk orders.

## Try next
- **Filter for chop.** Raise `vol_x` from its current value to 4.0 and `body_atr` to 1.5, so only bursts with real range expansion trigger. Today's mid-morning entries were triggered by ordinary chop.
- **Add a halt filter.** Stop opening new trades after 5 consecutive stops or a day P&L of Rs -1500, whichever comes first. Today that would have stopped trading around 11:20 at about Rs -2300, instead of -4124.
- **Add entry filters.**
  - Skip a stock for 30 minutes after it hits a stop, in either direction.
  - Require the stop distance to be at least 0.15% of price.
  - Skip any order with notional under Rs 10000.

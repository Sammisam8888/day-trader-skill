# Tight stops in a choppy tape plus no loss cap turned +390 at 09:58 into -6043 at the close

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The tape was choppy and two-way. The same names reversed within minutes: TECHM was traded 6 times on both sides, and ICICIBANK, TATASTEEL and POWERGRID were each re-entered after being stopped. volume_burst closed 44 trades, won 15 and lost 29 (34% win rate), for a net of Rs -6043 after charges. The best trade was +1011 (HINDUNILVR) and the worst was -1064 (ADANIPORTS). The supplied summary line ("44 won, net Rs 63950, best 12724, worst 162") does not match the trade log and should be treated as a reporting bug. The day peaked at +390 after BEL at 09:58, never came near the Rs 1000 target, and bottomed at -6883 at 14:26.

## Learnings
- Stops were far too tight for the day's noise, so the nominal ~2:1 reward:risk never showed up in results. The average win was about +420 and the average loss about -425, which at a 34% hit rate is a guaranteed bleed. Several stops were hit almost immediately:
  - TCS long at 13:38:11 stopped in 5 seconds (-773).
  - ADANIENT at 13:48:04 stopped in about 1 minute (-598).
  - HCLTECH at 09:54 stopped in 2 minutes (-584).
  - TECHM short at 12:54 stopped in 2 minutes (-316).
- Stop fills slipped past the stop level and widened losses:
  - ADANIPORTS: stop 1913.9, filled 1915.55, about Rs 425 extra on 258 shares.
  - HINDUNILVR short: stop 2145.8, filled 2147.35, about Rs 200 extra.
  - TCS: stop 2570.6, filled 2568.95.
  - BANKBEES: stop 658.0, filled 658.32.
- The morning was the worst stretch. Between 09:52 and 12:35 only 5 of 21 closes won. Eight straight losers from 10:01 to 10:21 (NTPC, WIPRO, TECHM, ICICIBANK, ULTRACEMCO, SUNPHARMA, TATASTEEL, ONGC) took the day to -2357. Afternoon short-side bursts did better: TECHM +350 at 12:53, KOTAKBANK +589, AXISBANK +343, BAJFINANCE +452, HDFCBANK +255 and TECHM +307 between 12:53 and 14:14.
- Re-entering the same name right after a stop or a win usually failed:
  - POWERGRID 10:32 re-entry 17 seconds after a target: -567.
  - TECHM 12:54 short right after a target: -316.
  - ICICIBANK was entered 3 times and lost 3 times, for -954.
- The strategy took several near-zero-quantity trades, apparently because capital was tied up in other open positions. Examples: ONGC 3 and 1 shares, ITC 5 and 3, INFY 1, TATASTEEL 1, WIPRO 2. They added nothing; WIPRO hit its target and made Rs 1.18. They only add noise and charges.
- With no daily loss limit, the strategy kept firing at full size through a regime it was clearly losing in. ADANIPORTS (-1064) and BANKBEES (-848) came after the day was already about -5800.

## Try next
- Tighten the burst filter so only cleaner bursts trigger: vol_x 3.5 (or one step higher than the current value if it is already there) and body_atr 1.5. Both aim to cut the morning chop entries that died within 1-2 minutes.
- Add a daily loss cap of Rs -2000 (halt new entries once reached). Today that would have stopped trading at 10:20 near -2151 instead of -6043. Also add a per-symbol cooldown: no re-entry in the same stock within 10 minutes of a close.
- Add a minimum-size filter: skip any entry where the planned risk ((entry - stop) x qty) is below Rs 150. Separately, fix the day-summary aggregation, which is reporting fictional numbers.

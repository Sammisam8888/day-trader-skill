# Four of five opening drives kept going, and all five fades went on at 09:46 with 94% of the wallet, losing Rs 8,549

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The supplied day summary does not match the trade log. The summary says "5 won, net Rs 462, best 334, worst 4". The log shows 1 win, 4 losses, and a net of Rs -8,549.26. The running "day so far" column and the price math both confirm the log figure. At 09:46:00 the strategy bought five 29DEC26 options in the same bar: calls on AXISBANK, BHARTIARTL and SUNPHARMA, and puts on ULTRACEMCO and ONGC. Together they cost Rs 94,258.75 in premium, against a Rs 100,000 wallet. Only ULTRACEMCO hit its target (+1,104.33). AXISBANK (-2,354.74) and BHARTIARTL (-4,431.98) were stopped out by 10:34. SUNPHARMA (-2,023.00) and ONGC (-843.87) drifted lower until the 14:50 flat. The day lost 8.5% of the wallet against a Rs +1,000 target.

## Learnings
- The fades were mostly wrong on direction. Four of five opening drives continued rather than reversing:
  - The three BUY fades of down-drives all lost (AXISBANK, BHARTIARTL, SUNPHARMA calls), so those stocks kept falling.
  - The ONGC put lost, so its up-drive kept rising.
  - Only ULTRACEMCO reversed. This looks like a trend day where the drive was real momentum, not overextension.
- All five signals fired on the first eligible bar (09:46, right after the 30-min window). That suggests the retrace condition was already met by small intra-window wiggles, not by a genuine give-back after the drive. The trigger is too loose to separate a real reversal from noise.
- Five same-bar entries are effectively one correlated bet. Three long-call positions on index-heavy names lost together, and position sizing put about 94% of capital at risk at once with no daily loss limit to cap it.
- The payoff was lopsided on the option leg:
  - Winner: ULTRACEMCO made +7.4% on premium (310.75 -> 333.85).
  - Stops: AXISBANK lost -8.1% (44.80 -> 41.15, in 11 minutes) and BHARTIARTL lost -17.6% (52.25 -> 43.05).
  - The stop and target are evidently set on the underlying, so option losses on stops ran larger than option gains on targets.
- Trades that went nowhere bled out. SUNPHARMA (-12.4% premium) and ONGC (-8.1%) were held about 5 hours without touching stop or target, losing a combined Rs 2,867. With no time stop, a failed fade simply decays until the 14:50 flat.

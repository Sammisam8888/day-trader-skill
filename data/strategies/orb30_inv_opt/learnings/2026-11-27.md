# Gave back a +Rs 4,019 morning to finish at -Rs 8,920 by stacking five long calls worth ~90% of the wallet

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy was up Rs 4,019 by 10:31 after the LT and NTPC exits. It kept trading past the day target and, between 10:20 and 10:34, opened four more long calls (COALINDIA, ULTRACEMCO, RELIANCE, MARUTI). Together with ITC from 09:47, about Rs 90,365 of the Rs 100,000 wallet sat in same-direction call premium. The underlyings faded through midday and afternoon, and the trade log shows the day closed at -Rs 8,920: 3 winners, 4 losers, best +2,936 (NTPC) and worst -5,404 (MARUTI). The summary line ("7 won, net Rs 777, best 316, worst 8") contradicts the log. The 10:08 "DAY TARGET reached, net 1550" banner is also wrong, because no trade had closed by 10:08 and the first close was at 10:09. Both reporting bugs need fixing before any day stats are trusted.

## Learnings
- The early ORB entries worked. LT C4100 (09:53, +5.4% on premium) and NTPC P322.5 (09:47, +15% on premium) were the only meaningful wins (+1,082 and +2,936), and both were taken in the first 45 minutes after the 30-min range.
- Wins and losses on premium were badly lopsided. Targets paid +1.4% (COALINDIA, +157), +5.4% (LT) and +15% (NTPC), but stops cost about -25% (MARUTI 422.70 -> 315.65, -5,404) and -24% (ULTRACEMCO 349.00 -> 265.60, -4,215). Stop and target seem to be set on the underlying, so option delta and decay make premium losses on stops far larger than premium gains on targets. MARUTI was also bought out of the money (C14000 with spot at 13,965), which adds to that.
- Late entries cost the day. Every position opened after 10:20 lost or roughly broke even: COALINDIA +157, ULTRACEMCO -4,215, RELIANCE -2,313, MARUTI -5,404. The ORB edge was gone by then.
- All five open positions were long calls from 10:34 onward, so one market direction decided the whole book. When it turned, MARUTI, ULTRACEMCO, RELIANCE and ITC all lost together (-13,095 combined).
- Continuing after the target, with no loss limit, turned +4,019 into -8,920.
- Plumbing bug: 5 signals (AXISBANK, HDFCBANK, ONGC, BHARTIARTL, ICICIBANK) were skipped because the NFO token request came back with the NSE equity quote. The skip list also included BAJFINANCE, which was really skipped for lack of free cash, not because of a cost filter.

## Try next
- Stop opening new positions once realized P&L is at or above the day target (stop at +4,019 here), and only allow entries before 10:30 when minutes=30.
- Set stop_frac 0.5 (down from whatever produced roughly -25% premium stops) and target_x 2.0, so the premium risk/reward is at least even. Also require an at-the-money or in-the-money strike, never out of the money like MARUTI C14000.
- Filter: cap total open premium at 50% of the wallet and allow at most 3 open positions on the same side (calls or puts).

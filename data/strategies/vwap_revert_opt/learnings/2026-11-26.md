# One ITC call held to the 14:50 flat gave back Rs 1084 of a Rs 1388 day, and the summary line misreports the result

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy took three single-lot option buys, all in the afternoon. It entered ONGC C222 at 13:13, ITC C305 at 13:28 and RELIANCE P1390 at 14:33, and closed all three between 14:44 and 14:50. Summing the log's net figures gives +1166.05, +222.10 and -1083.88, so the day ended at Rs +304 net: 2 winners, 1 loser, about 0.3% on the Rs 100000 wallet. That contradicts the "Day numbers" line (3 won, net Rs 51, best 35, worst 7), which matches nothing in the trade log and should be treated as a reporting bug. The log has no market-wide data, so I can't say what the overall tape did.

## Learnings
- **ONGC (13:13 to 14:44): the only real edge of the day.** The premium went from 6.56 to 7.10 (+0.54) on a 2250-qty lot, and the target exit netted Rs 1166. That trade alone beat the Rs 1000 day target. A large lot on a cheap premium means a small move pays well.
- **ITC (13:28 to 14:50): the trade that did the damage.** The premium slid from 9.60 to 9.00 and never hit a stop or target, so it rode to the 14:50 flat for -1083.88. The stop/target columns are blank for every trade, so I can't tell what stop was active. Either way, it allowed a 6.25% premium loss on a 1725-qty lot, which is almost as large as the best winner. The losing exit was nearly the size of the winning one, so the stop width is too loose for the hold time available.
- **ITC contract resolution is broken.** At 13:17 the signal asked for NFO ITC29DEC26C307.5 (token 104379) and got the quote for NSE ITC-EQ (token 1660), so the trade was skipped. Eleven minutes later it entered C305 on a fresh signal. That is a token-mapping bug in the option lookup, not a market condition. It also means the 13:28 entry was a later, different setup from the original signal.
- **RELIANCE (14:33 to 14:45): a "stop" exit that made money.** The exit at 34.95 was above the 34.40 entry and netted +222.10. The stop is likely trailing or measured on the underlying, so the exit was fine. But the label hides how much of the move was given back. Entering at 14:33, only 17 minutes before the forced flat, left almost no room for a VWAP reversion to play out.
- **BANKBEES is wasted watchlist space.** It was skipped twice (13:31 and 13:58) with "no opt contract". It is an ETF without stock options, so it can never trade in this _opt variant.
- **Charges are noticeable but not the problem.** Gross was about Rs 455 against Rs 304 net, so roughly Rs 150 in charges over 3 round trips. The P&L swing came from ITC's stop, not from costs.

## Try next
- **Tighten the stop:** set stop_sd to 1.0 (or 0.5 below whatever ITC ran with today), and record the actual stop/target level in the log on every open, so the next review can check it.
- **Add an entry cutoff filter:** no new opens after 14:15, since the flat is at 14:50. Today this would have blocked RELIANCE at 14:33, a small winner but structurally a coin-flip with 17 minutes left.
- **Fix the data and universe issues, which are not knobs:** drop BANKBEES (and any non-F&O symbols) from the _opt universe, fix the NFO token lookup that returned ITC-EQ for ITC29DEC26C307.5, and fix the day-summary calculation so it reports 2W/1L, net Rs 304.

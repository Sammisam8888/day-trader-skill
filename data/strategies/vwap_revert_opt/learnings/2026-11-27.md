# vwap_revert_opt lost Rs 14,329 because it kept buying calls into a falling tape, and the day summary falsely reports a winning day

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
- The log shows a broadly weak session. The underlyings behind most BUY signals kept slipping after entry: ADANIPORTS went 1911 to 1903, ULTRACEMCO 12664 to 12641, and ADANIENT and TECHM premiums bled all day.
- The strategy closed 14 option trades: 4 won and 10 lost, for net Rs -14,329 after charges.
  - The 8 call trades (BUY signals) netted Rs -14,630.
  - The 6 put trades (SELL signals) netted Rs +301.
- The supplied "Day numbers" (14 won, net +1131, best 324, worst 5) do not match this log. The banner "DAY TARGET reached at 09:59: net 1230" is also wrong: the running total was -697 at 09:59.

## Learnings
- **Long calls drove the loss.** Every BUY-side reversion call except JSWSTEEL C1310 lost:
  - ADANIPORTS C1920 -2731
  - JSWSTEEL C1320 -613
  - ULTRACEMCO C12700 -1135
  - ADANIPORTS C1900 -2072
  - ADANIENT C2650 -2686
  - ULTRACEMCO C12600 -1721
  - TECHM C1840 -4505

  Buying "below-VWAP" dips on a down day was fading the trend, not reverting to it.
- **Re-entering the same name right after a stop doubled the damage.**
  - ADANIPORTS: stopped 10:38:58, re-entered 10:39:35, stopped again for -2072.
  - ULTRACEMCO: stopped and re-entered at the same second, 11:30:40, then -1721.
  - SBIN: stopped 09:41:29, re-entered 09:42:00, then -2209.
  - ONGC: stopped and re-entered at 14:16:39. This one was saved only by the flat close (+187).

  In every case the first stop was the correct signal that the move was not reverting.
- **The stop does not control premium risk.** TECHM C1840 fell from 64.80 to 57.45 (-11%, -4505) without triggering a stop and was only cut at the 14:50 flat. The stop seems to watch the underlying in SD units, so a slow grind lets the option lose more than any stopped trade.
- **The exit labels are unreliable.**
  - SBIN P1150 at 10:17:44 is tagged "target" but exited at 32.95 against a 35.80 entry, for -2209.
  - Any learning that counts "target" exits as wins is being fed bad data.
- **The SELL side (puts) was roughly break-even and carried the only real winners.** ICICIBANK P1610 made +2206 and HINDUNILVR P2140 made +1212, both held about 45-100 minutes. Reversion from above VWAP worked; reversion from below did not.
- **Plumbing errors caused missed or mis-sized trades.**
  - Option tokens repeatedly resolved to the equity: ADANIENT, COALINDIA, ADANIPORTS and BAJFINANCE logged "asked for NFO ..., got NSE ...-EQ".
  - BANKBEES and NIFTYBEES were scanned even though they have no option contracts.

## Try next
- **Cut the call side.**
  - Set side to puts/SELL-signals only (side=2, if 2 = short-only in this recipe), or set invert=1 for BUY signals.
  - Or require the underlying to be above its day open before taking any call.
- **Add a same-symbol cooldown after a stop.** Allow no re-entry on that name for 30 minutes, or for the rest of the day after 2 stops. Tighten stop_sd from its current value to 0.75 and add a hard premium stop at -8% of entry, so a TECHM-style grind cannot exceed about Rs 3,100.
- **Widen band_sd to 2.5** so entries need a more stretched move from VWAP. Also fix three things before trusting any stats:
  - the NFO token lookup
  - the day-summary and day-target bookkeeping
  - the "target" exit label
  
  Drop the ETFs from the options universe.

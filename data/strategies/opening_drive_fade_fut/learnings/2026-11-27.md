# Capital, not the signal, limited today: 10 of 11 fade signals were skipped for margin, and the one win's numbers don't reconcile

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The opening drive fired fade signals across 11 large-cap futures between 09:46 and 10:31. Ten were skipped because 1 lot needed Rs 102334 to Rs 200013 of margin against Rs 100000 free. The only fill was NTPC29DEC26F, short 1500 at 322.75 (10:32:30), covered at 321.20 on target at 12:25:26. The close row shows net Rs 2040.64, but the day summary says net Rs 321. The gross move of 1.55 x 1500 is Rs 2325, which supports the ~Rs 2040 figure.

## Learnings
- Margin, not signal quality, was the bottleneck. AXISBANK, BHARTIARTL, ADANIENT, ADANIPORTS, HINDALCO, TRENT (09:46), TITAN (09:50), SBIN (09:55), POWERGRID and JSWSTEEL (10:31) all signaled but could not be traded. Today's results say nothing about whether those fades would have worked.
- NTPC was taken only because its margin (Rs 96825) fit, using 97% of the wallet on one lot. The trade was chosen by margin, not by signal strength.
- Most of the NTPC profit came from the fill, not the fade. The signal price was 321.40 but the simulated sell filled at 322.75, 1.35 better. The exit at 321.20 was only 0.20 below the signal price. A fill 0.4% better than the signal on a sell is suspect and should be audited before trusting this P&L.
- The log has two bookkeeping bugs:
  - The summary net (Rs 321) contradicts the close row (Rs 2040.64). Rs 321 looks like a price value, close to 321.20, written into the money field.
  - "DAY TARGET reached at 11:24: net 1216" was declared while NTPC was still open, so unrealized P&L was counted as banked.
- The NTPC trade shows no stop. A full-wallet single-lot short with no stop is uncapped risk, and there is no daily loss limit as a backstop.

## Try next
- Add a pre-signal filter that keeps only symbols whose 1-lot futures margin is no more than about 90% of free capital (today that leaves NTPC and few others). Alternatively, set enabled=0 for the futures variant until the wallet can hold at least 2 lots. That stops ten dead signals a day from crowding the log.
- Fix and verify the fill model: require a SELL fill to be no better than the signal price minus normal slippage. Re-run 2026-11-27 NTPC with the fill at 321.40; the net should drop to roughly Rs 0 to 50.
- Keep move_pct and retrace unchanged. One trade with a questionable fill is not evidence for tuning them. Do enforce a non-empty stop on every futures open.

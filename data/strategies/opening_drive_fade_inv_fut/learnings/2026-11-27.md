# Entry slippage of Rs 1.65 on a one-lot NTPC future cost Rs 3,208, over 3x the day target

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy got 8 signals. Seven were skipped because one futures lot needed Rs 102,201 to Rs 199,918 margin against Rs 100,000 free. The only fill was NTPC29DEC26F, a BUY of 1500 at 323.05 at 10:32:30. It was stopped out at 321.10 at 12:25:26 for net Rs -3,208.18, which is 3.2% of the wallet. The log's summary line ("1 won, net Rs 321") contradicts the trade row. The row is internally consistent: 1.95 x 1500 = Rs 2,925 gross loss plus about Rs 283 charges. So the summary is wrong and needs checking. The log does not show the day's market direction or the size of the opening drives, so I cannot say whether the setups themselves were good.

## Learnings
- **NTPC 10:32:30: the slippage did the damage.** The signal was at 321.40 but the fill was at 323.05, 1.65 points (0.51%) worse. The stop filled at 321.10, only 0.30 below the signal price. Had the order filled near the signal, this would have been a small loss, not Rs 3,208.
- **The stop and target columns are blank on the open row.** I cannot verify where the stop was placed relative to the fill. It looks like the stop was not recalculated after the bad fill.
- **Position size is set by lot size, not by risk.** One NTPC lot used Rs 96,915 of the Rs 100,000 wallet. A 0.6% adverse move therefore wiped out 3.2x the day's target. The Rs 100k wallet cannot size this futures variant sensibly.
- **The trade was picked by affordability, not quality.** Seven of eight signals (AXISBANK, BHARTIARTL, ADANIENT, ADANIPORTS, SBIN, POWERGRID, JSWSTEEL) were rejected on margin. NTPC was traded only because it was the one that fit, possibly the weakest of the day.
- **The fill came late.** The entry at 10:32 is about 47 minutes after the 30-minute drive window closed. A retrace that takes that long to trigger may no longer reflect the opening drive.
- **Reporting is unreliable.** The day summary shows +321 and "1 won" for a trade that lost 3,208. Any aggregate stats built on these summaries are suspect until this is fixed.

## Try next
- **Set enabled=0 for the _fut variant while the wallet is Rs 100k.** Alternatively, run the same signals on cash equity with risk-based quantity. One lot per trade means about 97% of capital on a single position.
- **Add a fill filter:** cancel the entry if the fill differs from the signal price by more than 0.15%, or re-anchor the stop to the actual fill. NTPC slipped 0.51%.
- **Raise move_pct from the current value to 1.2 and add a time filter.** No entries after 10:15, so only strong, fresh drives get traded rather than late retraces at 10:30 and after.

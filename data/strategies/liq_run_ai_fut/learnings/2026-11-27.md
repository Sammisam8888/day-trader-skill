# One TATASTEEL short made the day, but the wallet fits only one futures lot and the log's numbers disagree

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy took a single trade: a short of 1 lot (2750) of TATASTEEL29DEC26F at 166.24 at 10:28. It covered at 165.32 at the 14:50 flatten. That is 0.92 x 2750 = Rs 2530 gross, and the close row shows net Rs 2260.84. The summary line, however, says net Rs 165, which cannot be squared with that price move, so one of the two figures is wrong. Twelve other signals were skipped: nine for margin (needing Rs 96k-178k against Rs 100k free, or Rs 8568 after the TATASTEEL entry) and three because the quote lookup returned a cash-market quote instead of the futures quote.

## Learnings
- The 10:28 TATASTEEL short worked. The break of the pool held to the downside, and the day target was hit by 11:20 (Rs 1243). Holding to 14:50 roughly doubled that.
- The trade carried no stop or target in the log. It was closed by the time flatten, not by a target_r exit. The profit came from holding a trend, not from the recipe's exit logic, and the downside was unbounded for 4h20m.
- The TATASTEEL fill (166.24) was 0.62 above the signal price (165.62). That favoured this sell by Rs 1705, about two thirds of the gross. Without that gap the trade nets roughly Rs 600, so today overstates the edge.
- Capital, not signal quality, set the trade count:
  - Before the entry, RELIANCE, POWERGRID, JSWSTEEL and SBIN (10:20-10:27) were all unaffordable even with the full Rs 100k.
  - The entry then locked Rs 91432, so ITC, NTPC, SBIN, HINDUNILVR and BEL could not be taken afterwards.
- The skips for COALINDIA (10:24), ADANIPORTS (12:12) and BAJFINANCE (12:39) are a token-mapping bug. NFO token requests came back as NSE -EQ quotes, so those signals were lost through system error.
- P&L reporting is inconsistent: Rs 2260.84 on the close row versus Rs 165 in the day summary. Today's result cannot be trusted for tuning until this is reconciled.

## Try next
- Add a pre-signal filter that drops any symbol whose 1-lot margin is more than 0.9 x free cash. This stops the scanner from logging unaffordable signals. Also consider limiting the universe to lots under about Rs 95k so the wallet is not monopolised by one position.
- Make every open write an explicit stop and a target at target_r 2.0, and verify the exit fires. Today's trade showed no stop and no target, so the recipe's own exit was never tested.
- Fix the NFO token resolution (COALINDIA 58937, ADANIPORTS 58884, BAJFINANCE 58916) and the net-P&L summary bug before changing lookback, touches or break_pct based on today.

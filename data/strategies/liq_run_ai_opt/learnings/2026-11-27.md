# Early put entries made Rs 5,839, but the day summary and day-target line are wrong and two put tokens resolved to equity

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The market leaned bearish. Every signal was a SELL, so the strategy bought puts. Puts on RELIANCE, SBIN, TATASTEEL and JSWSTEEL gained premium, while COALINDIA moved against the trade. Summing the log's net column gives 7 closed trades, 5 winners and 2 losers, for Rs +5,839 net. That is about 5.8x the Rs 1,000 day target.

The "Day numbers" footer does not match the log. It reports 7 won, Rs 140 net, best 41 and worst 6, which is a reporting bug. The "DAY TARGET reached at 10:30: net 1872" line is also suspect. No position had closed by 10:30, so it must have used unrealized mark-to-market.

## Learnings
- **The 10:20-10:28 entry cluster made the day.**
  - SBIN P1140 (10:27) was the only trade to hit its target, at 11:22: 29.85 to 35.10, +Rs 3,865.
  - RELIANCE P1390 (10:20): +Rs 2,165.
  - TATASTEEL P165 (10:28): +Rs 1,736.
  - JSWSTEEL P1320 (10:24): +Rs 266.
  - The breaks that held through the first hour carried the trend into the afternoon.
- **COALINDIA P385 (10:24) was the one real loser.** Premium fell from 7.85 to 6.25, -20% or Rs -2,193, and the trade sat until the 14:50 flat. No stop appears in the log for any option leg, so a failed run was simply held to the time exit. That one trade wiped out nearly all of RELIANCE's gain.
- **Late entries added nothing.** ITC P305 (11:22) closed at -Rs 38 and NTPC P320 (12:24) at +Rs 38. By late morning the runs had already played out. Two entries returned net zero after charges.
- **Only 1 of 7 trades reached its target.** RELIANCE (+12% on premium) and TATASTEEL (+11.5%) were decent movers but still ended on the 14:50 flat instead of a target. The target looks too far for how far these runs actually extended.
- **Two signals were lost to an instrument-lookup bug.** POWERGRID (10:20) and HINDUNILVR (13:51) asked for an NFO token and got the NSE equity back. These were system faults, not strategy decisions.
- **Four signals were skipped for cash.** ADANIPORTS (12:12), BAJFINANCE (12:39), SBIN (13:30) and BEL (13:52) were skipped because about Rs 92k of premium was committed by 10:28. Given how flat the late entries were, these skips probably cost little today.

## Try next

- **Add an entry-time filter: no new opens after 11:30.** Today ITC and NTPC added Rs 0 net while tying up Rs 21.8k of premium.
- **Add a premium stop and fix the reporting and lookup bugs.**
  - Add a stop at about -15% of premium, or on the underlying's break level, so a failed hold like COALINDIA exits early.
  - Fix the NFO token lookup for POWERGRID and HINDUNILVR.
  - Fix the day-summary calculation.
  - Make the day-target check use realized P&L only.

# Buying the opening-drive continuation failed: 7 of 9 trades lost, mostly long stop-outs, for Rs -2799

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The supplied summary line (9 won, net Rs 10772, best 2660, worst 165) does not match the trade log, so this review uses the log. The log shows 9 closed trades with 2 wins and 7 losses, for a net of Rs -2799 after charges. The best trade was BHARTIARTL at +196.08 and the worst was ADANIPORTS at -664.06. Eight of nine entries were BUYs, which means the opening drives were mostly up and this inverted copy bought continuation. Six of those longs hit their stops before 12:30. No daily loss limit was set, so the day ran to almost 2.8x the Rs 1000 target in the wrong direction.

## Learnings
- **Long continuation was the wrong side all day.**
  - ADANIPORTS (09:46), SBIN (09:55), ADANIENT (09:46), HINDALCO (09:46) and NTPC (10:32) were all stopped out, each costing Rs -535 to -664.
  - These stops mean the drives did retrace, which is the move the non-inverted fade bets on. The original strategy was likely on the right side today.
- **The only clean win was the one short.** BHARTIARTL SELL at 09:46:03 reached its target at 10:34:55 for +196.08. When the inverted copy faded against a down drive, it worked.
- **The stops were too tight compared with the targets.**
  - ADANIPORTS risked 3.35 points (stop 1911.3) against a 30.35-point target.
  - HINDALCO risked 5.15 points against a 13.65-point target.
  - None of the far targets was reached. These stops sit inside normal post-drive noise, so they get hit long before the target is in play.
- **Stop fills slipped by about 0.05 per share, and that adds up.**
  - Five of the six stop-outs filled 0.05 below the stop: ADANIPORTS at 1910.95 vs 1911.3 (0.35), SBIN 1136.45 vs 1136.65 (0.20), ADANIENT 2659.95 vs 2660.0, HINDALCO 915.45 vs 915.5, NTPC 320.19 vs 320.25.
  - On large quantities such as NTPC (431 shares) and ADANIPORTS (149 shares), this pushes each loss to roughly Rs 550-660.
- **The 09:46 entries were one correlated bet, not four separate trades.**
  - Three longs opened at the same second: ADANIPORTS, ADANIENT and HINDALCO. Later metals longs followed: JSWSTEEL (10:24) and TATASTEEL (11:22).
  - The two Adani names and HINDALCO all stopped out.
- **The trades that survived to 14:50 went nowhere.** TATASTEEL -151.45, POWERGRID +65.90 and JSWSTEEL -2.93 netted Rs -88.48. Holding to the flat time added charges without adding any edge.

## Try next
- Set `invert=0` for sessions like this one, or set `side` to short-only. Check which of the values 0/1/2 means short-only, since today's only winner was the SELL.
- Raise `move_pct` to at least 1.0 so the inverted copy only takes very strong drives. Today's long continuations came from moves that faded right away.
- Add a filter: stop new entries once the day is at Rs -1500 (1.5x the target). Also allow at most 2 open positions in the same sector or group (Adani, metals) at once.

# Inverted fade made Rs 712, but a 0.15% stop on AXISBANK erased almost a whole target win

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The log has no index data. The trades show an upward opening drive in ONGC, AXISBANK and TATASTEEL, which this inverted copy bought at 09:46. Later downward setups in BAJFINANCE, TRENT and MARUTI were shorted.

From the log, the day was 6 closed trades, 5 winners and 1 loser, for a net of about Rs +712 after charges. The best trade was TATASTEEL at +691.56 and the worst was AXISBANK at -662.04. That misses the Rs 1000 day target.

The "Day numbers" line (6 won, net Rs 20257, best 14064, worst 163) does not match the log and looks broken. 14064 is MARUTI's exit price, not a P&L. The "DAY TARGET reached at 09:51: net 1023" note fired before any trade had closed, so it must be counting open positions. Realized P&L never reached Rs 1000.

## Learnings
- **TATASTEEL (09:46 to 10:29, +691.56):** Following the opening drive worked cleanly. The stop was 1.21 below entry (0.75%) and the target 2.06 above, and price reached the target within 45 minutes. This supports running with invert=1 when the drive is strong.
- **AXISBANK (09:46 to 11:16, -662.04):** This trade had a 2.15-point stop (0.15%) against a 24.75-point target. That stop is inside normal AXISBANK noise. A 232-share position (about Rs 3.3 lakh notional, roughly 3.3x the wallet) turned a 2.3-point dip into Rs -662. The trade did not fail because the idea was wrong. It failed because the stop sat in the noise while the position was large.
- **TRENT (11:10 to 12:00, +104.78):** This target hit, but 6 shares for a 20.5-point move gives a tiny payoff. When the per-share risk is large, sizing makes these trades nearly irrelevant to the day.
- **14:50 exits:** BAJFINANCE (+329.92), MARUTI (+95.82) and ONGC (+151.83) were all in profit but never reached their targets. ONGC needed 224.32 and peaked short of it at least through the 223.38 exit. Midday targets are too far for afternoon drift.
- **Reporting:** The day-target check and the summary line are unreliable. The 09:51 "target reached" note, built on unrealized P&L, should not be trusted for deciding whether to keep trading.

## Try next
- Add a minimum stop-distance filter of max(0.4% of price, the stop distance implied by retrace). Alternatively, skip any signal whose stop is under 0.3% of entry; that rule would have skipped or resized AXISBANK at 09:46.
- Keep invert=1 and move_pct around 0.8 for the 09:46 batch. Make retrace stricter, 0.5 rather than shallower, so entries need a real give-back and stops sit beyond the noise.
- Fix the reporting before trusting it. Compute day P&L only from closed trades, and recompute the summary line, before letting "day target reached" influence anything.

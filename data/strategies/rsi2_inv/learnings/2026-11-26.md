# Inverted RSI2 worked as trend-following: shorts made money in the morning slide, longs fading that slide lost, and the Rs 2324 peak shrank to Rs 596

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The market drifted down from about 10:55 to 14:05 and then bounced into the 14:50 flat. The strategy took only SELLs until 11:31 and reached the Rs 1000 day target at 11:08. It peaked at about Rs 2324 around 11:48. It kept trading, and the BUY entries fading the decline gave most of that back. It ended at about Rs 596 net over 64 closed trades.

The "Day numbers" summary is wrong and should not be trusted. It reports 64 won, net Rs 93464, best 12769 and worst 162, which look like prices, not P&L. The log itself shows:
- 42 wins and 22 losses.
- Best trade: SBIN BUY 14:19, +453.53.
- Worst trade: INFY SELL 14:06, -689.98.

## Learnings
- **Morning shorts worked.** Inverting oversold-buy signals meant shorting into a falling tape. Every early SELL hit its target:
  - AXISBANK 11:06, +429
  - TCS 11:08, +442
  - TECHM 11:16, +426
  - NIFTYBEES 11:26, +418
  - TRENT 11:43, +373
  - ADANIENT 12:34, +441
- **Midday BUYs fought the downtrend and nearly all stopped out:**
  - TITAN 11:51, -301
  - WIPRO 12:34, -252
  - TRENT 12:38, -378
  - ICICIBANK 12:52, -286
  - AXISBANK 13:03, -504
  - ULTRACEMCO 13:52, -398
  - BHARTIARTL 13:55, -497
  - POWERGRID 13:56, -352
  
  Those eight losses total about -2968, more than the entire final net.
- **After about 14:05 the tape turned up and the pattern flipped.** BUYs won: SBIN 14:19 +454, AXISBANK 14:20 +407, BANKBEES 14:25 +422. SELLs lost: INFY 14:06 -690, TECHM 14:41 -555, INFY 14:49 -253. The inverted signal only pays when its side matches the current intraday trend.
- **Some sells lost even while the market fell.** HINDUNILVR 12:13 (-550) and TATASTEEL 11:55 (-283) were big-quantity shorts in names that were not falling with the index. Single-stock strength overrode the index move.
- **The bracket is roughly 1:1, so one stop erases one target.** For example, AXISBANK has a 5.27 stop distance against a 5.17 target. Stops also filled slightly past the stop level (INFY stop 1169.14, filled at 1169.95). With 66% wins, giving back Rs 1728 from the peak came from continuing to trade after the target, not from bad luck.
- **Many trades were 1 to 5 shares and moved nothing.** Examples are POWERGRID x1 (+0.44), NTPC x1 (+0.43) and BAJFINANCE x1 (-5.02). About 25 of the 64 trades had near-zero P&L; they only add charges and noise.

## Try next
- **Add a trend filter.** Allow only SELLs when NIFTYBEES is below its 30-minute VWAP or EMA, and only BUYs when it is above. Today this would have blocked the 11:31-13:55 BUY losers (about -2968) and the post-14:05 SELL losers (about -1498).
- **Protect profit after the day target.** Once day net reaches 1000, stop opening new trades if day net falls 40% below its peak. Today that would have stopped at about 1394 around 12:13 instead of ending at 596.
- **Widen the reward and drop tiny trades.** Change target_atr from about 1.0 to 1.5 and keep stop_atr at 1.0, so winners outpay the slippage on stops. Also skip any entry whose position value is below Rs 20000.

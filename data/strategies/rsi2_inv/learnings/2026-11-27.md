# Inverted RSI2 lost Rs 6,209 by repeatedly buying breakouts in a choppy tape; 1:1 stops/targets need a win rate it never reached

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The reported day numbers are wrong and do not match the log. They claim 38 wins, net +76,650, best 13,946 and worst 165. Recomputed from the trade log: 38 closed trades, 12 won and 26 lost, net -6,209 after charges. Best was BEL short +455.61 at 11:17 and worst was SUNPHARMA long -745.61 at 13:30. The market was a choppy, slightly heavy range: NIFTYBEES longs were stopped twice within 20 minutes (12:25 and 12:37), and most momentum-style longs reversed within 3-10 minutes. Gross wins were about Rs 2,452 against about Rs 8,661 of losses. The 1,000 day target was never close, and with no loss limit the drawdown reached 6x the target.

## Learnings
- **Payoff math.** Stop and target sit at roughly equal ATR distances, so breakeven needs about 50%+ wins after charges. The strategy hit 32%. The average loser (about -333) was bigger than the average winner (about +204) because stops filled past the stop price. Examples: SUNPHARMA stop 1897.62, filled 1894.65; BHARTIARTL stop 2059.31, filled 2058.25.
- **Inversion was wrong for this tape.** Taking the opposite of RSI2 meant buying strength and shorting weakness in a mean-reverting range. Nine longs were stopped quickly: INFY 11:00, HINDALCO 11:01, MARUTI 11:18, ADANIPORTS 11:22, BHARTIARTL 11:50 and 13:21, ICICIBANK 12:16, BANKBEES 13:01, SUNPHARMA 13:30. Together they cost about Rs 4,600.
- **Re-entering after a stop compounded losses.**
  - NIFTYBEES long was stopped at 12:25, re-bought at 12:34 and stopped again (-464 combined).
  - HDFCBANK short was stopped at 14:13, re-shorted at 14:24 with 3x the size (258 qty) and stopped again (-452).
  - INFY was traded 4 times, both directions, for a net loss.
- **Winners clustered in one window.** At 12:03 INFY, TCS and WIPRO hit target together (+790), as did COALINDIA at 11:47 and INFY short at 14:40. The edge appeared only in brief directional bursts and was not sustained.
- **Sizing bug.** Seven qty-1 to qty-4 trades opened (WIPRO, NTPC x2, TCS, BEL, TATASTEEL, HDFCBANK 4). They carried zero economic value and only paid charges. The sizer is leaving residual cash in tiny orders.
- **Late-day flat cost money.** BAJFINANCE was held from 13:39 to the 14:50 flat and closed at -529.61, which is effectively a stop-size loss after 70 minutes of drift.

## Try next
- **Set invert 0 for the next comparable session.** Choppy, range-bound intraday action favours RSI2's native mean-reversion side, and 26 of 38 inverted trades lost. If the strategy stays enabled as the inverse, set enabled 0 on days when the first hour's NIFTY range is narrow (under 0.4%).
- **Widen the stop and shorten the target.** Set stop_atr 2.0 and target_atr 1.5, and tighten the entry trigger to low 5.0 and high 95.0. That gives entries more room against noise and makes targets hit more often, so fewer marginal signals fire.
- **Add filters:**
  - a daily loss stop at -2,000 (2x the target);
  - no re-entry in the same symbol for 30 minutes after a stop;
  - a minimum order value of Rs 20,000 to kill the qty-1 trades.

# Without a stop, one stale futures short gave back the day's target and left net Rs 81

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The trade log shows two closed trades with a day net of Rs 81. NTPC29DEC26F was a long from 09:47 to 09:48 that made Rs +1063.03 and hit the day target. TATASTEEL29DEC26F was a short from 12:22 to 14:50 that lost Rs -982.50 and closed at the 14:50 flat time. The summary line ("2 won, net Rs 491, best 326, worst 165") does not match the trade log, which shows 1 win and 1 loss, so the summary generator needs checking. Of the roughly 60 other signals, about 46 were skipped for margin and about 12 were skipped for wrong-exchange quotes. The log gives no index-level picture of the market.

## Learnings
- **TATASTEEL short (12:22:45, sell 165.08):** The stop and target columns are blank. The position sat for 2.5 hours and was closed only by the 14:50 time exit at 165.34. A move of just 0.26 points on 2750 qty cost about Rs 715 gross, plus about Rs 267 in charges. This looks like a missing stop, not a strategy idea that failed.
- **NTPC long (09:47:06):** Fast and clean, with entry at 324.90 and exit at 325.80 about 1.5 minutes later. But it is one trade, so it proves nothing about the edge.
- **Signal price versus fill price:** Signals come from the cash price and fills come from the futures price. NTPC signalled at 323.45 and filled at 324.90, a 1.45 gap. TATASTEEL signalled at 164.50 and filled at 165.08, a 0.58 gap. So VWAP bands are measured on one instrument and traded on another, and the basis quietly moves the entry.
- **Charges are heavy:** Each 1-lot round trip cost about Rs 270-290, which is close to 30% of the Rs 1000 day target per trade.
- **Data bug:** About 12 skips (ADANIENT, NTPC, LT, HDFCBANK, COALINDIA, SBIN, ADANIPORTS, TECHM, ONGC) asked for an NFO token and got the NSE equity quote instead. Those signals were lost to a bug, not to market conditions.
- **Margin wall:** With Rs 100000, only a few contracts fit in one lot: NTPC, TATASTEEL, and on a good day COALINDIA or ONGC. Most of the universe (ICICIBANK, SBIN, KOTAKBANK, TECHM and others) can never be traded. NIFTYBEES and BANKBEES have no futures contract at all. In addition, once TATASTEEL was open, free margin dropped to Rs 10269 and blocked every other signal for the afternoon.

## Try next
- **Set stop_sd = 1.0 and confirm a stop price is actually written on every open.** Today's stop column was empty on both trades, so the stop logic itself needs checking.
- **Add a filter: if the day target is already hit, take no new trade unless a stop is set and the worst case at that stop keeps the day net at or above Rs 500.** Today, net went from Rs 1063 to Rs 81 on one unprotected short.
- **Fix the inputs and the universe:**
  - Fix the NFO token lookup.
  - Measure VWAP and bands on the futures series, not the cash series.
  - Remove from the universe any symbol whose 1-lot margin is above wallet size (or that has no futures contract), so signal slots are not wasted.
  - Keep band_sd, invert and side unchanged until there are more than two trades of evidence.

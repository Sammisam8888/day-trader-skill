# Early 09:30 put breakdowns carried the day; entries after 10:00 added nothing, and broken plumbing hid both the opportunity set and the real score

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
Four of the five 09:30 ORB signals were SELL breakdowns and three of them paid: AXISBANK P1400 hit target at 11:29 for +3896, RELIANCE P1380 made +1671 at the 14:50 flatten, and KOTAKBANK P505 made +2126. HINDUNILVR P2120 lost -1453, and the two later entries were weak: HDFCBANK C860 at 10:09 lost -1387 and SUNPHARMA P1900 at 12:30 made +322. Per the trade log the day was 6 closed trades, 4 won and 2 lost, net Rs +5175 after charges (about Rs 410 total). The "Day numbers" footer says 6/6 won, net 205, best 43, worst 13. It contradicts the log and should not be trusted. Three signals (LT, COALINDIA, ADANIENT) were never traded because of a quote-lookup bug, and ONGC was skipped for lack of cash.

## Learnings
- The 09:30 bearish breakdowns were the edge today. AXISBANK, RELIANCE and KOTAKBANK together made +7693, which is more than the whole day's net. Signals that came later in the session were worse: HDFCBANK (10:09, a call against the day's put-heavy tape) lost -1387, and SUNPHARMA (12:30) earned only +322 over 2h20m of holding.
- Only one trade (AXISBANK, +17.5% on premium) reached its target. The other five drifted to the 14:50 flatten. RELIANCE peaked at +10% and KOTAKBANK at +9% on premium, well short of target, so target_x looks too far for these option premiums on an ordinary day.
- HINDUNILVR (09:30) lost 9.8% of premium and HDFCBANK lost 7%, yet neither closed on a stop. Both were held to 14:50. The stop and target columns are blank for every row, so I cannot tell whether a stop existed or was simply never touched. Either way, losers were allowed to run all day.
- Plumbing bug: LT, COALINDIA and ADANIENT were skipped with "asked for NFO <token>, got NSE <equity>". The resolver returned the cash-market instrument instead of the option. That is 3 of the 10 signals lost, and today the put side was working.
- Capital was exhausted. By 10:09 the five open positions had used Rs 97,210 of the Rs 100,000 wallet, so ONGC at 10:30 (Rs 10,508 per lot) could not be funded. With no per-position cap, one large lot such as KOTAKBANK at Rs 24,400 can crowd out later signals.
- The "DAY TARGET reached at 10:31: net 1639" message fired with zero closed trades. It must be counting unrealized MTM, which is not the same thing as a banked target.

## Try next

- Filter: no new entries after 10:15 for minutes=5. Today that would have removed HDFCBANK (-1387) and SUNPHARMA (+322) for a net gain of about +1065. Also cap premium at Rs 20,000 per position so that late-morning signals like ONGC can still be funded.
- Lower target_x by 0.5 from its current value so moves like RELIANCE and KOTAKBANK can bank before the flatten. Set stop_frac to 0.5 so a loser like HINDUNILVR is cut early instead of bleeding to 14:50. Compare both changes against today's AXISBANK result before keeping them.

## Learnings
- **The target was reached on paper only.** The +1423 at 12:42 was entirely unrealized, and "keeps trading" meant holding all 4 legs into the afternoon. About Rs 3075 swung from peak to close. Nothing in the strategy converts a target hit into a realized gain.
- **The losses came from one broad mid-afternoon reversal.** In 32 minutes, the HDFCBANK put (14:13, -6.2% premium) and both IT calls (WIPRO 14:43, -5.8%; HCLTECH 14:45, -3.5%) stopped out. The stops sat only 3.5-6.2% below entry premium, so a normal afternoon wobble was enough to trigger them.
- **Size per lot was uneven, and the biggest lot lost the most.** Premium per trade ranged from Rs 13230 (SUNPHARMA) to Rs 21630 (WIPRO). WIPRO's 3000-qty lot turned a 0.42 premium move into the day's worst loss, -1320. Using "1 lot" as the unit gives very different risk per trade.
- **The late KOTAKBANK entry at 14:14 had little upside.** It was opened 36 minutes before the 14:50 flat, so it had almost no room to reach target. It made +337 on a 0.20 move, which was luck rather than edge.
- **SUNPHARMA P1900 (11:24 to 14:50, +1145) was the only trade that reached the signal's intent.** It was the oldest position, with the smallest premium outlay, and it was held to the flat.
- **The BEL skip at 11:34 was a system bug, not a market decision.** The quote lookup asked for NFO token 88697 and got back NSE 383 BEL-EQ. The option-to-token mapping is returning the equity token.

## Try next
- **Lock the day once the target is reached:** when net P&L (realized + open) reaches the day target, either close everything or move every stop to breakeven. Under the first option, 12:42 today would have banked about +1423 less exit charges.
- **target_atr 2.0** (lower than the current value), so winners get realized before the afternoon fade instead of sitting open. Pair it with **no new entries after 14:00**, which would have skipped KOTAKBANK.
- **Normalize risk per trade:** cap premium per position at about Rs 15000 (skip or reduce if one lot exceeds it), or size stop_atr so the rupee loss at the stop is about equal across positions. Separately, fix the NFO token mapping (BEL) and the day-summary stats bug.

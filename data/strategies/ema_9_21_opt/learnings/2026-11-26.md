# Early entries held to the 14:50 flat made the day; tight stops and entries after 14:00 gave most of it back

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy started late (first signal 13:12) and closed 7 option trades. By my count from the closes, it had 3 wins and 4 losses for net Rs +2,036 after charges. The best trade was TCS +3,746.56 and the worst was TRENT -1,114.30. That does not match the summary line ("7 won, net Rs 381, best 108, worst 6"), and the "day so far" column carries an unexplained +1,112 offset (it reads -2 right after a -1,114 first close), so the reporting pipeline needs checking. Every rupee of profit came from the two earliest positions, TCS call and SUNPHARMA put, which hit neither stop nor target and rode to the 14:50 flat. Without them the day was Rs -2,763.

## Learnings
- Time in the trade was the edge. TCS C2580 (13:19) went from 90.60 to 107.55 over 91 minutes, +18.7% on premium and Rs +3,746.56. SUNPHARMA P1920 (13:28) went from 43.00 to 46.15, Rs +1,053.09. Both crosses had room to develop before the forced flat.
- Entries after 14:00 lost. WIPRO C195 (14:08) and JSWSTEEL C1310 (14:22) had under 45 minutes before the flat. Both drifted down slightly and were cut at 14:50 for Rs -215.23 and Rs -786.63. On a 9/21 cross, that is not enough time for a move to show up in the premium.
- Stops sized off the underlying were too tight on premium. TRENT C3300 was stopped 2 minutes after entry (13:47 to 13:49) on a 4.2% premium dip, Rs -1,114.30. TATASTEEL P162.5 was stopped after 12 minutes on a 0.32 move in a 6.06 premium, Rs -929.43. Bid/ask noise plus delta below 1 turns a small wiggle in the underlying into a stop-out.
- Reward-to-risk was inverted. The TECHM C1820 target fired at +0.65 on a 67.15 premium (+1.0%), netting Rs 282.41 on Rs 40,290 deployed. The stops that fired cost 4-5% of premium. One target hit cannot pay for one stop hit.
- Capital was nearly maxed out. At 14:22 the open premium was about Rs 86,800 of the Rs 100,000 wallet (TCS, SUNPHARMA, WIPRO, JSWSTEEL). TECHM alone took 40% of the wallet for a 1% target.
- There are two data and plumbing faults, not strategy faults. HDFCBANK P870 was skipped because the quote lookup returned the NSE equity token 1333 instead of NFO 96370, a token-mapping bug. NIFTYBEES has no option chain and should not be in this strategy's universe.

## Try next
- Widen both exits: stop_atr 2.0 and target_atr 4.0. This should stop 2-minute shakeouts like TRENT and stop capping winners at 1% like TECHM.
- Add a filter: no new entries after 14:00, since the 14:50 flat leaves too little runway (WIPRO, JSWSTEEL). Also skip any trade where premium paid is more than 30% of the wallet (TECHM).
- Fix the NFO token lookup (HDFCBANK) and the day-summary math, and drop NIFTYBEES from the opt universe. Do not tune further until the summary numbers reconcile with the trade log.

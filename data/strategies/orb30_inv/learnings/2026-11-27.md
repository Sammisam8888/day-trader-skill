# Fading breakdowns on a down-trending day lost: 8 inverted longs opened 10:30-10:53 bled into the 14:50 flat, and 0.25R targets could not cover full-range stops

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The tape drifted lower all day. NIFTYBEES went from 294.01 at 10:31 to 292.38 at 14:50, and banks (ICICIBANK, AXISBANK, BAJFINANCE) closed well below their entries. The strategy closed 17 trades: 7 won and 10 lost, for a net of Rs -1798 after charges. The best trade was LT at +173.26 and the worst was ULTRACEMCO at -410.91. The "Day numbers" line (17/17 won, +45024, best 13775, worst 223) does not match the log and looks like a reporting bug. This review uses the log.

The day peaked at +676 at 11:20 after the HDFCBANK target. It then gave everything back through two stop-outs and a 14:50 flatten in which 8 of 9 open positions were losers (-1870 combined at the flat).

## Learnings
- The side split decided the day. The 4 inverted SELLs all won: BANKBEES 10:29 +114, NTPC 10:31 +156, HDFCBANK 11:20 +125 and HINDUNILVR at the flat +68, for +463 together. The 13 inverted BUYs (fades of ORB breakdowns) netted -2261. On a down day, the original breakdown signals were correct, and inverting them put us on the wrong side.
- Reward-to-risk is badly lopsided. ITC risked 4.51 to make 1.17, and MARUTI risked 178 to make 46. Six target hits averaged +137, while two stops (MARUTI 12:36 -410, ULTRACEMCO 12:43 -411) erased three of those wins. The flat-time losers were also large: NIFTYBEES -374 and AXISBANK -353.
- Entries clustered into one correlated bet. Between 10:30 and 10:53 we opened 8 longs: ONGC, ULTRACEMCO, NIFTYBEES, RELIANCE, MARUTI, ICICIBANK, BHARTIARTL and BAJFINANCE. In effect this was one leveraged bet on a market bounce. Only ONGC (11:04, +107) paid, and the rest accounted for about -2093.
- Most trades that worked did so quickly: LT in 16 min, BANKBEES in 19 min, ONGC in 34 min and HDFCBANK in 71 min. The exception was COALINDIA, which took over 4 hours. Positions still open after about 11:30 mostly drifted against us until the 14:50 flat. The inverted edge on these names is a quick snap-back, not a hold.
- Nothing locked in gains. At +676 by 11:20, with the 1000 target never reached, the remaining open longs were left to run into a -1798 close.

## Try next
- Set stop_frac 0.5, down from what looks like a full-range stop (1.0). At the current tiny target_x, a fade that fails should exit early rather than ride the whole range.
- Add a trend filter: skip new inverted BUYs when NIFTYBEES is below its 30-minute opening-range low (invert=0 for that signal, or use side to allow only inverted SELLs). Also cap concurrent same-direction positions at 3.
- Add a time exit: close any inverted position that has not reached target within 60 minutes, instead of holding to the 14:50 flat.

# Real result was -12,295 net with 1 win in 6; the summary and day-target lines are wrong, and a missing stop on ULTRACEMCO caused most of the loss

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The trade log does not match the summary lines. Adding up the six closes gives net Rs -12,295: 1 winner (KOTAKBANK put, +1,526) and 5 losers (worst: ULTRACEMCO call, -7,178). The summary says "6 won, net Rs 358, best 242, worst 8", and that is wrong. The "DAY TARGET reached at 09:45: net 1077" banner is also wrong: at 09:45 the only closed trade was AXISBANK at -1,782. Price action was mixed and leaned weak. Puts did slightly better (one win, one loss), and all four call entries lost (POWERGRID, SBIN, HDFCBANK, ULTRACEMCO).

## Learnings
- **Reporting bug.** The day-stats and day-target code are reading the wrong values. Possible causes are option premiums or the best/worst price fields instead of net Rs, or a sign error. A false "target reached" at 09:45 told the system it was +1,077 when it was -1,782. That needs fixing before any day-target or loss logic can be trusted.
- **ULTRACEMCO C12700 (10:31 to 14:50, -7,178).** This trade ran from 384.40 to 241.70, a 37% premium loss, with no stop exit. AXIS, SBIN and HDFC were stopped out after 6-8% premium drops, so stops were not applied the same way to every position. The stop and target columns are blank on every open, so the levels were never logged.
- **POWERGRID C267.5 (09:32 to 14:51, -1,662).** This also had no stop exit and bled about 10% of premium into the 14:50 flat. KOTAKBANK P505 is the mirror case: it was held to flat with no target hit and finished as the only winner (+1,526). Holding to the end of the day with no working stop or target is just a coin flip on the full premium.
- **Capital crowding.** Four entries between 09:31 and 09:33 tied up about Rs 91k of the 100k wallet in correlated names: three banks plus POWERGRID. The bank signals also contradicted each other. There were SELLs on KOTAKBANK and AXISBANK at the same time as BUYs on SBIN (09:33) and HDFCBANK (10:09). Two of those four bank trades had to lose.
- **Late breakouts did worst.** The 10:09 HDFCBANK and 10:31 ULTRACEMCO entries came well after the opening range and lost Rs 8,468 combined. ULTRACEMCO's single lot carried the largest premium at risk per point.
- **Contract lookup bug.** HINDUNILVR, ITC and COALINDIA were skipped because the NFO token lookup returned the NSE equity instrument instead of the option. BANKBEES and NIFTYBEES should be filtered out before signal generation, since they have no options.

## Try next
- Fix the stop and stats code paths first. Log the stop and target levels on every open, and assert that every open position has a live stop. Compute the day net from the summed close net Rs so that today's -12,295 shows as -12,295.
- Set stop_frac=0.5 and target_x=2.0, and add a filter: no new entries after 10:00. That would have blocked both HDFCBANK and ULTRACEMCO. Also cap open premium at Rs 50k, and don't open opposite-direction trades in the same sector at the same time.
- Do not change side or invert based on one day. Today's pattern (calls 0/4, puts 1/2) is too small a sample. Track call and put results separately over the next 10 sessions first.

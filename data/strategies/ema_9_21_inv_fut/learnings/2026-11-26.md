# Kept trading after the day target and gave it all back on a late NTPC short that the 14:50 flatten closed at a loss

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy took no trades until 14:06 because 5 of the first 6 signals failed the margin check against the Rs 100000 wallet. Its first trade was a TATASTEEL long that closed at target for +1329.47 by 14:18, which cleared the Rs 1000 day target. It then opened an NTPC short at 14:33 using nearly the whole wallet. The 14:50 flatten closed that short at a loss of -2384.08, so the log ends the day at Rs -1055.

The summary line does not match the log. It says "2 won, net Rs 487, best 324, worst 164", but the log shows 1 win, 1 loss and net -1055. The banner's "net 1137 at 14:18" also differs from the 1329 close. The stats or banner code is computing something different from the trade rows, and that needs fixing before these numbers are trusted.

## Learnings
- **14:18 TATASTEEL29DEC26F BUY 163.29 -> 163.87 (+1329.47):** this was the only trade that fit the account. The lot was 2750 shares on Rs 89810 of margin. The gross move was 0.58 x 2750 = Rs 1595, and about Rs 265 of that went to charges. The target exit worked as designed.
- **14:33 NTPC29DEC26F SELL 322.20 -> 323.60 (-2384.08):** the entry came 17 minutes before the forced flatten, and the log shows no stop. The trade had almost no time to work and was closed at whatever price 14:50 gave. The gross loss was 1.40 x 1500 = Rs 2100, plus about Rs 284 of charges.
- **Loss size versus win size:** the NTPC loss was 1.8 times the TATASTEEL win. With one lot taking 90-97% of the wallet, a single trade decides the whole day. After the target was hit, there was more to lose than to gain from another trade.
- **Signal price versus fill price:** signals are priced on the cash stock but filled on the future.
  - TATASTEEL signalled at 162.55 and filled at 163.29, a gap of 0.74.
  - NTPC signalled at 320.70 and filled at 322.20, a gap of 1.50.
  - The futures basis therefore moves the entry by about 0.5% of price. The NTPC short started 1.50 worse than its signal level.
- **Margin limits the universe:**
  - TCS, HDFCBANK, TRENT (twice), TECHM, WIPRO and JSWSTEEL could never be traded at a Rs 100000 wallet.
  - SUNPHARMA failed because of an instrument token bug: it asked for NFO 59116 and got NSE 3351 SUNPHARMA-EQ.
  - In effect, the strategy trades only low-margin names such as TATASTEEL and NTPC.
- **Sample size:** two trades say nothing about whether the inverted EMA cross has an edge. Today's result came from timing and sizing, not from the signal.

## Try next
- **Filter: stop new entries once the day target is hit, or after 14:15, whichever comes first.** This would have skipped the 14:33 NTPC short and kept the +1329.
- **stop_atr 1.0 (tighter), and make sure the stop is actually placed.** Both stop columns in today's log are blank. With a full-wallet single lot, an unprotected position is not survivable.
- **Fix the data issues, which are separate from the strategy knobs:**
  - Correct the SUNPHARMA NFO token mapping.
  - Restrict the universe to contracts whose 1-lot margin is at most Rs 100000, so signals are not wasted on skips.
  - Reconcile the day-summary stats with the trade rows.

# Margin, not the signal, decided today: 1 of 14 setups was tradable, and its profit rests on a suspicious fill

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy took one trade. It shorted 1 lot (1500) of NTPC29DEC26F at 09:47 at 324.65 and covered at 323.25 at 10:31 on target, for Rs 1814.03 net. That beat the Rs 1000 day target. The other 13 setups were skipped: 10 because one lot needed more than the Rs 100000 wallet, 2 because the futures quote mapped to the equity token, and 2 because ETFs have no futures contract. The summary line says net Rs 323 while the trade row says Rs 1814.03, so the day's real result is unclear.

## Learnings
- The NTPC profit depends on the entry fill. The signal fired at 323.45, but the SELL filled at 324.65, Rs 1.20 better than the signal. Measured from the signal price, the move to the 323.25 exit was only 0.20, about Rs 300 gross before roughly Rs 286 of charges. Almost all of the Rs 1814 comes from that fill gap, which is unlikely in live trading.
- The two P&L figures disagree: Rs 1814.03 on the close row and Rs 323 in the day summary. The 10:23 "day target reached, net 1139" also appeared before any close, so it was mark-to-market. Until this is reconciled, today's P&L shouldn't feed any tuning.
- The wallet is too small for most of the universe. KOTAKBANK, AXISBANK, SBIN, ICICIBANK, BHARTIARTL, BAJFINANCE and others need Rs 112k-226k per lot. POWERGRID (102448), ONGC (100764) and ITC (106036) missed by under 7%.
- Holding one position blocked everything else. NTPC used Rs 97395 of margin, leaving Rs 2605 free from 09:47 to 10:31, so LT (09:53), HDFCBANK (10:09) and ONGC (10:30) could not be taken.
- There are two quote-mapping bugs. COALINDIA (10:20) and ULTRACEMCO (10:31) requested the NFO futures token and got the NSE equity token back. These are lost trades caused by data plumbing, not by the strategy.
- BANKBEES and NIFTYBEES are still in the futures universe even though they have no futures contract. This wastes scan slots.

## Try next
- Fix the fill model so entry fills at or near the signal price (323.45 here), not a better price. Then rerun 2026-11-27 and reconcile the Rs 1814 vs Rs 323 discrepancy before changing any knobs.
- Add a pre-filter: only consider symbols whose 1-lot margin is at most the free margin. Drop BANKBEES and NIFTYBEES from the futures universe, and fix the NFO token lookup for COALINDIA and ULTRACEMCO.
- Keep the knobs unchanged (invert=1, current minutes, target_x and stop_frac). One trade with a questionable fill is not evidence for retuning.

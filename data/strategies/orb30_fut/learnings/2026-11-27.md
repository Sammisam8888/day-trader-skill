# Day summary is wrong: the only trade lost Rs 3585, not +323, and one lot used 97% of margin

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy made one trade: it bought 1 lot (1500) of NTPC29DEC26F at 324.90 at 09:47 and was stopped out at 322.70 at 10:31, for net Rs -3584.55. The footer says "1 won, net Rs 323", which contradicts the log, so the real day result is about Rs -3585, or -3.6% of the wallet. The 09:47 "DAY TARGET reached, net 1063" banner fired before any trade had closed, so it is also wrong. The log has no index or breadth data, so I cannot judge the overall market. NTPC fell about 0.7% from entry within 45 minutes of the breakout.

## Learnings
- The reporting is broken in two places. The footer (1 win, +323) does not match the only close (-3584.55), and the day-target banner fired at 09:47 with nothing realised. Do not use today's stats for tuning until the PnL aggregation and target check are fixed.
- One NTPC lot needed Rs 97,470 of the Rs 100,000 wallet, leaving Rs 2,530 free. This caused 3 margin skips while NTPC was open (LT 09:53, AXISBANK 10:00, COALINDIA 10:20). At 1500 x Rs 324.90 (about Rs 4.9 lakh notional), a 2.20 move cost 3.3% of the wallet before charges. Sizing, not the signal, decided the size of the loss.
- The signal fired on the cash price (NTPC 323.45) but the order filled on the future at 324.90. That 1.45 gap is roughly the December basis, not slippage. If the ORB stop and target were computed on cash levels and checked against futures prices, the stop sits about 0.45% closer than intended. The stop column is blank for this trade, so I cannot verify this, and it needs checking.
- Instrument lookup failed 4 times: ITC 09:47, HDFCBANK 10:09, ONGC 10:30 and HINDUNILVR 12:00 each requested an NFO token but got the NSE equity quote. That is 4 of the 10 signals lost to a symbol-mapping bug. BANKBEES (10:10) was also signalled even though it has no future and should be filtered out upstream.
- Even with the full Rs 96,415 free after 10:31, RELIANCE (Rs 139,420) and SUNPHARMA (Rs 133,770) could not be traded. With this wallet, most of the universe can never be traded as futures.

## Try next
- Add a filter: only signal symbols whose 1-lot margin is at most 60% of the wallet. Today that leaves roughly NTPC-sized contracts, which avoids starving later signals and avoids pointless skips. Also drop non-F&O symbols like BANKBEES before signalling.
- Set stop_frac 0.5 (down from the current value) together with max_width_pct 0.8. Also convert the ORB stop and target to futures prices by adding the live basis (fut - spot) at entry. The goal is to keep the per-lot loss near Rs 1,500 instead of Rs 3,500.
- Set enabled 0 until the NFO token mapping (ITC, HDFCBANK, ONGC, HINDUNILVR) and the day-PnL/target aggregation are fixed. Today's numbers cannot be trusted for any knob tuning.

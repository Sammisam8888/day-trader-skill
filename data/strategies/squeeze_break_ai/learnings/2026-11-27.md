# Day target was hit at 13:29, the strategy kept trading, and it gave back Rs 2434 of a Rs 2623 peak in the last hour

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy closed 10 trades: 5 won and 5 lost, for a net of Rs +189 after charges. The printed summary line is wrong and contradicts the log. It says 10/10 won, net 16954, best 2644 and worst 504. The log actually shows best +1018 (ADANIPORTS) and worst -725 (TCS long). The "DAY TARGET reached at 13:08: net 1031" banner is also wrong. Running net at 13:08 was 268, and the 1000 target was first crossed at 13:29 (1915). Late morning to early afternoon trended cleanly in the short breakouts. After 14:00 the market turned choppy, and every position still open or opened late was stopped out.

## Learnings
- **Short squeeze breakouts paid in the midday trend.** BAJFINANCE (10:54 short, +820), ADANIENT (11:24 short, +629) and ADANIPORTS (13:19 short, +1018 in 10 minutes) all ran to target. The short side carried the day's gross profit.
- **After 14:00 every outcome was a loss.** TCS long at 14:14 was stopped at 14:24 (-725). The strategy then flipped TCS short at 14:29, which was stopped at 14:34 (-668). That is -1393 in 20 minutes on one name that was ranging, not breaking out. A squeeze "break" in both directions within 15 minutes is a sign of a failed squeeze, not a signal.
- **Old positions left open into the afternoon turned into losers.** HINDALCO (short from 11:19, -629 at 14:13) and HINDUNILVR (long from 11:59, -412 at 14:43) had sat for 3 hours without reaching target, then hit their stops in the late chop. Neither had any time-based exit.
- **Stops filled with slippage while targets filled at or near the level.** Slippage on stops was: HINDALCO 0.50 past the 920.65 stop, TCS long 0.78 past 2592.33, and HCLTECH 0.05 past its stop. Because the stops were tight, around 0.3-0.4% of price, this slippage plus charges made the losses about as large as the wins. The result was 5 wins and 5 losses netting nearly zero.
- **KOTAKBANK at 12:34 was opened with only 2 shares.** It made +2.64 net. Five positions were open at that point, with combined notional well over the wallet, so sizing was capital-starved. A trade that can only make Rs 3 is not worth taking. The log also shows a close at 504.25, marked as "target", against a 504.30 target, which looks like a fill-logic inconsistency.
- **The summary and target-banner generator is reporting bad numbers.** Until it is fixed, day-level stats for this strategy cannot be trusted.

## Try next
- **Add an end-of-day filter.** Open no new entries after 14:00. Force-close any position that has been open more than 120 minutes without reaching target.
- **Add a give-back lock.** Once day net is at or above the 1000 target, stop opening new trades if day net falls below 60% of its peak. Today that would have stopped trading at about 1574, before the TCS round trip.
- **Add a whipsaw filter.** After a stop-out on a stock, block entries in the opposite direction on that same stock for 30 minutes. Skip any entry where sized quantity puts risk at stop below Rs 100, which would have skipped KOTAKBANK at 2 shares.

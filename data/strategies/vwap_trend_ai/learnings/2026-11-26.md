# Morning edge, afternoon bleed: up Rs 4591 by 10:43, flat by 14:50 because entries never stopped

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy went long only. It closed all 15 of its first trades (09:56-10:43) at target, reaching Rs +4591 by the SBIN exit at 10:43. After that, entries kept coming as the long VWAP-trend moves stopped following through: 19 of the next 29 closes were stops. The day ended at Rs -266 after the 14:50 flatten. The log shows 45 closes, 23 wins and 22 losses; the best trade was BEL at +795 (09:56) and the worst was TECHM at -639 (14:16). The supplied summary line ("45 won, net 81494, best 13988, worst 162") does not match the trade log and looks like a reporting bug. The 09:56 "DAY TARGET reached, net 1181" banner also disagrees with the 795 realized at that moment.

## Learnings
- **The open was the edge.** Every full-size entry from 09:47 to 10:03 hit its target within 10-40 minutes: BEL +795, WIPRO +604, ULTRACEMCO +604, HDFCBANK +600, SUNPHARMA +484, SBIN +516. Trends off the open carried at least 1.3x the stop distance.
- **The regime flipped around 10:45 and the strategy did not notice.** Five stops landed in 30 minutes: ITC 10:48 (-255), ICICIBANK 11:00 (-574), TCS 11:08 (-562), BEL 11:10 (-227) and BHARTIARTL 11:17 (-475). New longs still went on at 11:18-11:23 (JSWSTEEL, MARUTI, ITC). From the 10:43 peak the day gave back about Rs 4850.
- **Re-entering a name that just stopped out was the worst habit.**
  - POWERGRID was bought four times (12:26, 12:43, 13:03, 13:48) and stopped every time, for -1300 total.
  - KOTAKBANK at 12:34 (410 qty, -598) came after one stop and one target in that name.
  - ITC was entered three times, for a net of about -273.
- **The payoff was too thin for a coin-flip afternoon.** A full-size win was about +450 to +680 and a full-size loss about -380 to -640. Stop fills also came in 0.05-0.15 below the stop level (e.g. TECHM 1829.35 vs 1829.96, POWERGRID 267.09 vs 267.13). After 11:00 the strategy won roughly 1 in 3 full-size trades, and at about 1:1 payoff that is a guaranteed bleed.
- **Qty-1 trades are noise.** TATASTEEL, NTPC, ITC, BANKBEES and BAJFINANCE netted Rs 1-4 each, apparently squeezed by capital in use. They add charges and log clutter but no information about the edge.

## Try next
- **Add a time and give-back filter.** Take no new entries after 11:00. Also stop new entries once realized P&L falls 30% below the intraday peak; today that would have halted at about Rs 3200 near 11:00, instead of finishing at -266.
- **Add a per-symbol cooldown.** After a stop, block that stock for 60 minutes, or for the rest of the day once it has 2 stops. That would have removed POWERGRID entries 2-4, the later ITC entries and the 12:34 KOTAKBANK.
- **Demand a better payoff and stronger confirmation.** Use confirm 4 (up from the current value if it is lower) and widen target_atr relative to stop_atr to at least 2:1, for example stop_atr 2.0 and target_atr 4.5. This should cut the 1:1 trades that failed after 10:45. Also add a minimum position value of about Rs 20000 to skip qty-1 entries.

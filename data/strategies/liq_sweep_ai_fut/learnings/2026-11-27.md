# Futures entries were priced on spot signals, so ~0.4-0.5% basis sat between signal and fill, and the day ended -318, not +488

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy took 2 of 16 signals and lost Rs 318 net after charges. The table rows sum to -318.17 (-1533.61 + 1215.44). The "Day numbers" summary line, which claims 2 won and net Rs 488, is wrong.

- **TATASTEEL long:** 10:48, 2750 qty at 166.24. Stopped at 11:19 at 165.78 for -1533.61.
- **NTPC short:** 11:22, 1500 qty at 322.95. Closed at 12:07 at 321.95 for +1215.44.

The other 14 signals were skipped:
- 12 for margin, because one futures lot needs Rs 91k-226k against a Rs 100k wallet.
- 1 because the KOTAKBANK quote returned the cash instrument instead of the futures contract.
- 1 because BANKBEES has no futures contract.

## Learnings
- **Signal and fill are on different instruments.** TATASTEEL signalled at 165.59 (cash) and filled at 166.24 (Dec future), +0.39%. NTPC signalled at 321.40 and filled at 322.95, +0.48%. Both gaps match roughly one month of futures premium, so they are basis, not slippage. The sweep levels come from cash prices, but entries and exits happen on futures prices.
- **TATASTEEL (10:48) lost to the basis gap.** The exit at 165.78 was still above the 165.59 cash signal price. The stop distance on the futures fill was only 0.46 (0.28%), smaller than the 0.65 basis gap. That 0.46 times 2750 qty is Rs 1265 of gross risk, more than the whole Rs 1000 day target, on one trade.
- **The NTPC win (11:22) was partly luck.** As a short, it got the favourable side of the premium. Its exit is labelled "stop" yet booked +1.00/share. Either the stop was trailed, or the stop check compares a cash-based level against a futures price. Neither the stop nor the target is logged (both columns are empty), so today's exits cannot be audited.
- **Charges are a big share of the result.** They came to about Rs 550 across the 2 trades (~268 on TATASTEEL, ~285 on NTPC). That is over half the day target. At these lot sizes, a trade that does not move at least ~0.2% in favour is a net loser.
- **One lot ties up the whole wallet.** Each position used 91-97% of capital, so after 10:48 every other signal was skipped for margin (HINDALCO, TITAN, POWERGRID, TECHM, BAJFINANCE). The strategy is effectively running one trade at a time, chosen by whichever eligible signal comes first rather than by quality.
- **Two data issues to fix:**
  - KOTAKBANK29DEC26F returned a cash quote, a token mapping error (NFO 59015 resolved to NSE 1922).
  - BANKBEES is in the universe but is an ETF with no futures contract.

## Try next
- **Translate levels to the future before trading.** Add the live basis (fut LTP minus spot LTP at signal time) to the sweep level, stop and target before placing the order. Alternatively, compute the whole recipe on futures candles. Also log the stop and target on every open.
- **Add a risk-cap filter.** Skip a signal if (stop distance on the futures price) x lot qty + about Rs 280 of charges is more than Rs 750. Today this would have blocked TATASTEEL's 2750-qty lot as sized.
- **Fix the plumbing before tuning any knobs.** Drop BANKBEES from the universe, fix the KOTAKBANK token mapping, and correct the day summary calculation, which disagrees with its own trade rows. With only 2 trades, today gives no basis for changing lookback, touches, sweep_pct, reclaim_bars or target_r; keep them as they are.

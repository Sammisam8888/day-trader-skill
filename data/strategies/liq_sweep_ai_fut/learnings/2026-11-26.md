# One NTPC future won Rs 1065 net, but it used 97% of margin, had no stop or target logged, and the day summary misreports the result

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy took one trade. At 13:14 it bought 1 lot (1500) of NTPC29DEC26F at 322.45 on a BUY signal priced at 320.20. It held until the 14:50 flat-time exit at 323.35.

That 0.90 move gives Rs 1350 gross and Rs 1065.10 net per the close row, which beat the Rs 1000 day target at 14:42. The "Day numbers" footer says net Rs 323, best/worst 323, which conflicts with the close row. Rs 1065 looks right from the prices, so the footer is likely a reporting bug.

Five later signals were lost: three for lack of margin and two because the quote lookup failed.

## Learnings
- **The NTPC entry tied up the whole wallet.** At 13:14 the NTPC entry took Rs 96,735 of the Rs 100,000 wallet, leaving Rs 3,265 free. TATASTEEL (13:16), HCLTECH (13:21) and COALINDIA (13:22) were all skipped for margin. We cannot tell whether those three would have done better than NTPC, because the first signal simply wins by arriving first.
- **INFY and BEL were lost to a data bug, not the strategy.** At 13:17 and 13:18 the request was for NFO tokens 59001 and 58921 but came back as NSE cash tokens (INFY-EQ, BEL-EQ). The contract-to-token mapping for these two names is broken.
- **The entry price ran 2.25 above the signal (0.7%).** The signal was 320.20, apparently the cash price, and the fill was 322.45 on the future. That gap is mostly futures basis. Any stop or target computed from cash levels would sit in the wrong place on the future.
- **The NTPC trade logged no stop and no target.** Both fields are blank on the 13:14 open. The trade only closed because of the 14:50 time exit. The setup's actual edge (sweep_pct, reclaim_bars, target_r) went untested today, and the one win carried unbounded risk while it was open.
- **The win was small and partly luck.** A 0.28% favorable drift on a full-wallet position produced the whole day's result. Charges took about Rs 285 of Rs 1350 gross (21%), so on 1-lot futures this size, moves under about 0.2% would barely cover costs.

## Try next
- **Add a margin filter.** Skip any entry where 1-lot margin is more than 50% of free capital. If several signals fire within 10 minutes, rank them and take the best instead of the first.
- **Fix how stops and targets are calculated.** Before placing them, convert the cash-price levels to the future by adding (future LTP - cash LTP). Refuse any entry whose stop field is empty.
- **Fix the logs before tuning.** Repair the NFO token mapping for INFY (59001) and BEL (58921), and fix the day-summary net calculation (323 vs 1065.10). Keep target_r at 2.0 and the other knobs unchanged until a day logs real stops and targets.

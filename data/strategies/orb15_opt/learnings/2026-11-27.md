# Puts won and calls lost on a down day for +Rs 6754, but the day summary and target banner are wrong

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The day was broadly bearish. Every put the strategy held to the end of the day closed green: KOTAKBANK, ITC, RELIANCE and SUNPHARMA were flattened at 14:50, and ULTRACEMCO hit its target. Both calls (SBIN, HDFCBANK) were stopped out, as was one early put (AXISBANK).

From the trade log there were 8 closed trades: 5 won and 3 lost. Wins totalled Rs 11768 and losses Rs -5013, for a net of about Rs +6754 after charges. The best trade was ULTRACEMCO at +4884 and the worst was SBIN at -1909.

The reported day numbers are wrong on every field: "8 won", "net 583", "best 390" and "worst 6". The 09:45 banner "DAY TARGET reached, net 1783" is also wrong, because the day was actually at -1782 at that point.

## Learnings
- **Bookkeeping is broken.**
  - The 09:45 banner flipped the sign of the running P&L.
  - The summary looks like it is reading prices, not P&L: 390 is the ULTRACEMCO exit price. Its win count also ignores losses.
  - If any logic keys off "target reached", it fired on a -1782 day. This needs fixing before any of today's numbers are used for tuning.
- **Direction mattered more than entry timing.**
  - SELL-signal puts went 5/6. Only AXISBANK at 09:32 failed, stopped at 09:38 after 6 minutes for -7.3% on premium.
  - BUY-signal calls went 0/2: SBIN 09:33 to 10:30 for -1909, and HDFCBANK 10:09 to 11:20 for -1322.
  - On a trending down day, upside breakouts in heavyweight banks failed.
- **Profit came from holding, not from targets.**
  - Only ULTRACEMCO (10:31 to 12:43, 291.25 to 390.05, +34% premium) reached target.
  - The four 14:50 flat exits added Rs 6883, or 58% of gross wins. Their premium gains were only +3.5% to +16%, so target_x was not reachable for them within the session.
- **Stops lose more than the day target.**
  - The three stops cost Rs 1322 to 1909 each, at about 6.5-7.5% of premium.
  - A single stop costs 1.3-1.9x the Rs 1000 day target. By 11:20 the day was at -5013, and it was rescued by one target hit.
- **Capital, not signals, limited participation.**
  - Each 1-lot premium cost Rs 14-27k, so 4-5 positions filled the Rs 100k wallet.
  - COALINDIA (Rs 9788, 10:20) and BAJFINANCE (Rs 25500, 10:54) were skipped for lack of cash. Both were put signals on a day when puts paid.
- **Two kinds of skip are system or universe issues, not market issues.**
  - POWERGRID and HINDUNILVR at 09:32-09:33: an NFO token request returned the NSE equity quote. This is an instrument-lookup bug.
  - BANKBEES and NIFTYBEES at 09:39 and 10:31: these ETFs have no options. They should never reach the opt router.

## Try next
- **Fix the accounting before using today for tuning.**
  - Make the running day P&L sign and the summary (wins/losses, net, best/worst in Rs) come from the per-trade net Rs column.
  - Add a filter that drops ETF symbols (BANKBEES, NIFTYBEES) from the orb15_opt universe.
  - Fix the NFO token mapping so POWERGRID and HINDUNILVR options can be quoted.
- **Paper-test a lower target_x (current value -> 1.5) alongside the current setting.**
  - Four of five winners never reached target and depended on the 14:50 flat.
  - A closer target would bank gains intraday instead of relying on end-of-day drift.
  - Re-check that ULTRACEMCO-type runs are not cut too much.
- **Test a side filter in parallel: side set to puts-only on days when the index is below its first-15-minute range.**
  - Keep the baseline on side=0/both.
  - Today's 0/2 call record is one day of evidence, not a rule. Compare over at least 10 sessions before switching.

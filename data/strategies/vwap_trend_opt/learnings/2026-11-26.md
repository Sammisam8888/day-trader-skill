# One HCLTECH call (+8003) carried the day, and the reporting is broken: the summary line and exit labels contradict the log

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy opened 8 single-lot option trades between 13:06 and 14:16 and closed all of them by 14:50. Four trades won and four lost. Summing the net column gives **+Rs 5155**. The footer ("8 won, net Rs 230, best 73, worst 6") contradicts this and cannot be trusted. The day was carried by one trade: HCLTECH29DEC26C1420 (13:21 to 14:50, +8003). Without it the day would have been -2848. The "DAY TARGET reached at 13:13, net 1571" banner also fired when no trade had closed yet, so it was counting unrealized mark-to-market.

## Learnings
- **The HCLTECH flip was the whole day.** The put was closed at 13:20 for +662, and the call was opened 60 seconds later on a fresh BUY signal at 1418.20. That call ran from 52.85 to 73.05 (+38% premium). Fast reversal on a stock moving decisively through VWAP was the edge today.
- **Losers that ran to the 14:50 flat were not cut by any stop.** RELIANCE P1370 drifted from 32.75 to 26.10 (-20% premium, -3369) over 97 minutes. That single trade erased BAJFINANCE plus the HCLTECH put plus NTPC combined. The stop appears to be tied to the underlying, and it never fired while theta and delta bled the premium.
- **Exit labels are unreliable.** POWERGRID C267.5 at 14:02 is tagged "target" but lost 1280 (7.95 to 7.30). HCLTECH P at 13:20 and NTPC C at 13:46 are tagged "stop" but closed in profit. Either a trailing stop is mislabeled or the stop and target levels are swapped for some option legs. Any stop_atr or target_atr tuning is meaningless until this is checked.
- **Late entries lost.** HDFCBANK P860 (14:12, -440) and WIPRO P195 (14:16, -291) both lost with under 40 minutes to the forced flat. Neither had time to work, and both paid round-trip charges.
- **Instrument lookup bug cost 5 signals.** HINDUNILVR (twice), JSWSTEEL, BHARTIARTL and SBIN each requested an NFO token and got the NSE equity back. These are lost opportunities of unknown sign, but they are a system fault, not a strategy result.
- **Capital was exhausted by 14:26.** About Rs 95.9k of premium was locked in 5 open legs, so ULTRACEMCO (18452) and NTPC (13200) were skipped. TRENT (24930) was skipped at 13:50 even though BAJFINANCE (24825) was allowed at 13:06. The per-lot cost cap sits right around Rs 24.9k.

## Try next
- **Fix the plumbing before tuning.** First, the NFO token resolution, so it never falls back to an -EQ quote. Second, the footer stats, so they are recomputed from closed-trade net Rs. Third, the stop/target labeling on option exits. Fourth, the day-target check, so it uses realized P&L only.
- **Add a premium-based hard stop of -12% from entry**, alongside the underlying stop_atr. Today it would have cut RELIANCE near 28.8 (about -2000 instead of -3369) and POWERGRID near 7.0. It would not have touched the HCLTECH call.
- **Add a filter: no new entries after 14:10** (40 minutes before the 14:50 flat). This would have skipped HDFCBANK and WIPRO (-731 combined) and kept capital free.

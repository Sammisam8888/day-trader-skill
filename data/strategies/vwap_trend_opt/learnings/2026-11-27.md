# Choppy VWAP whipsaw: 17 of 22 option buys stopped out for Rs -12,938, and the summary line wrongly reports a +718 win day

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The trade log does not match the "Day numbers" line. The log has 22 closes: 5 winners (+3,318) and 17 losers (-16,256), for a net of **Rs -12,938** against a Rs 1,000 target. The summary line says "22 won, net Rs 718", which is wrong. So is the 09:44 "DAY TARGET reached, net 1265": no trade had closed by then, so it was unrealized open premium.

The market chopped around VWAP all day. LT, POWERGRID, KOTAKBANK and BEL all gave both BUY and SELL signals within hours. On top of that, 10 entries were skipped because the NFO token lookup returned the cash equity instead of the option.

## Learnings
- **Same-day re-entries after a stop all lost.**
  - SUNPHARMA C1920 was stopped at 09:56 and re-entered at 10:40 for another -799.
  - WIPRO P192.5 was stopped at 10:53 and re-entered at 10:58 for -801.
  - HCLTECH C1440 was stopped at 13:23 and re-entered at 13:41 for -1,696.
  - POWERGRID was stopped on a put, re-bought the put (-327), then flipped to a call (-516).
  - Together these re-entries cost about Rs -4,140.
- **Stops are too tight for option premium noise.**
  - KOTAKBANK C505 was stopped in under 3 minutes on a 0.35 drop (-769).
  - POWERGRID P267.5 was stopped on a 0.15-0.20 drop, twice.
  - These are bid/ask-sized moves, not trend failure.
- **Targets are tiny, so winners do not pay for the losers.**
  - ITC P305 hit "target" at +0.05, netting +47 after charges.
  - NTPC P320 hit target at +0.10, netting +113.
  - The two real winners came from letting moves run: TATASTEEL P165 was held to the 14:50 flat for +1,818, and LT P4100 made +1,049.
  - Average loser was about Rs 956 versus an average winner of about Rs 664.
- **Position size is unconstrained by premium.**
  - TECHM C1860 used Rs 38,640 of premium (600 qty) and gave the worst loss, -3,756 at 10:35.
  - That is about 4x a typical 1-lot ticket.
- **Signal direction was noise on whipsaw names.** The LT call was stopped at 09:52 (-770), then an LT put at 10:56 made +1,049. One name flipping sides is a chop signature, not a trend.
- **The skips are an infrastructure bug, not a filter.** NTPC, ONGC, WIPRO, KOTAKBANK, ICICIBANK, POWERGRID, ITC, HDFCBANK, HCLTECH and BEL were skipped with "asked for NFO X, got NSE Y -EQ". The quote request is going to the wrong exchange segment.

## Try next
- **Slow entries and give trades more room:** raise confirm from current to 4, stop_atr to 3.0, and target_atr to 8.0. This cuts chop entries, stops trades exiting on premium noise like KOTAKBANK and POWERGRID, and stops +0.05 "targets" like ITC.
- **Add filters:**
  - Block any re-entry on a symbol for the rest of the day after it hits a stop.
  - Block an opposite-side entry on a symbol that already flipped.
  - Cap premium per trade at Rs 20,000. That would have skipped TECHM C1860.
- **Fix the reporting and data bugs:**
  - Make the day summary and day-target check use realized net from closes only.
  - Fix NFO token resolution so option quotes are not answered from NSE equity.
  - Until both are fixed, treat this strategy's reported stats as unreliable.

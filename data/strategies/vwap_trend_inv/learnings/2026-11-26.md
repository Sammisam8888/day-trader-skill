## Learnings
- Stops sit about 2x further away than targets. For example, RELIANCE at 09:44 had a stop 12.65 below entry and a target 6.25 above. As a result, full-size wins paid only Rs 120-218 while full-size losses cost Rs 450-570. At that payoff the strategy needs roughly a 70% win rate to break even, and it hit 54%.

- Inverted BUYs in the first hour all reached target: HDFCBANK 09:48 (+209), RELIANCE 09:44 (+211) and BAJFINANCE 09:48 (+218). Fading only one side was not the problem. Fading the uptrend side was.
- The inversion did work in the 11:15-14:45 range-bound tape. KOTAKBANK traded back and forth for +198, +189 and +213. - With no daily loss limit, the strategy kept trading after reaching -2100 by 10:43 and -3500 by 11:13. It never got back near flat.

## Try next
- Fix the payoff shape by setting stop_atr = 2.0 and target_atr = 4.0, so a target is at least the size of a stop. Re-check the win rate on the next run. If it falls below 45%, the inversion has no edge at a symmetric payoff.
- Add a time filter: no inverted entries before 10:30 (or set invert = 0 for the first hour), because the opening move was trend-following today.
- Add a minimum-size filter that skips any entry under Rs 20000 notional, so the 1-5 share trades stop appearing. Also flag the day-summary calculation bug so the next review gets correct numbers.

# Small wins, large stops: up Rs 1,473 by 10:53, then gave it all back to close at Rs -2,220

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy closed 53 trades: 37 winners and 16 losers, finishing at net Rs -2,220. The supplied summary line (53 of 53 won, net Rs 118,826, best 14,047, worst 164) does not match the trade log. The log's actual figures are: best trade Rs +319.97 (COALINDIA, 10:19), worst Rs -680.30 (HINDUNILVR, 12:02), wins about Rs +4,310, and losses Rs -6,530. In the morning the inverted signals hit target after target, and the day peaked at Rs +1,473 at 10:53. The day target banner at 11:07 says 1,017, but the running total was already 1,392 by then. After 11:20 the stops started landing, and the last 40 minutes (14:13 to 14:50) took the day from Rs -6 to Rs -2,220.

## Learnings
- The payoff is badly lopsided, so a 70% win rate still loses money. Targets sit at roughly half the stop distance. For example, the 09:45 NTPC trade had its stop 1.72 away and its target 0.85 away. The average win was about Rs 116 and the average loss about Rs 408, so breakeven needs about a 78% hit rate. Today delivered 70%.
- The 09:42 to 10:53 window worked: 18 of 20 closes there were winners. Examples are COALINDIA +320, ULTRACEMCO +242, HINDUNILVR +232, LT +223 and SUNPHARMA +223. Fading early VWAP-trend signals paid while prices were rotating around VWAP.
- After 14:00, the trend signals were real and fading them failed:
  - ITC BUY 676 at 14:14: -605
  - HINDALCO SELL at 14:36: -574
  - TECHM BUY at 14:40: -537
  - TATASTEEL BUY 784 at 14:44: -528
  - HINDUNILVR at the 14:50 flat close: -211

  That is about Rs -2,450 in under 40 minutes. Large quantities with tight stops meant each stop cost Rs 500 to 600.
- Flipping repeatedly in the same name hurt:
  - ITC: SOLD at 13:01, then BOUGHT 676 shares at 13:22 the moment the first trade closed. The second trade was stopped for -605.
  - HINDUNILVR: won long at 10:09, lost short at 12:02 (-680), lost long again at 14:50.
  - TATASTEEL: four long entries; the two morning ones won about 342 in total, the two afternoon ones lost 1,148.
- Stop fills can slip. The HINDUNILVR stop at 2140.77 filled at 2143.85, which added about Rs 140 to that loss.
- Single-share entries were pure noise. WIPRO 1, NTPC 1, BAJFINANCE 1 and 2, POWERGRID 1 and BEL 1 each netted between Rs 0.5 and Rs 2.5. Sizing appears to collapse to qty 1 when capital is tied up, and those fills add nothing.

## Try next
- Fix the payoff ratio: set stop_atr 2.0 and target_atr 4.0, or at least make sure target_atr is no smaller than stop_atr. Re-check the win rate against the new breakeven, which is about 50%.
- Add a time filter: no new entries after 13:30. As a separate test, stop opening new trades once the day target is hit. Today that would have locked in about Rs +1,400 instead of Rs -2,220.
- Add two filters: no opposite-side re-entry in the same stock within 30 minutes of a close, and skip any order with qty below 10 or notional below Rs 10,000.

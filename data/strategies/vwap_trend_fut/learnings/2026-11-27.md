# Banking the day target at 09:48, then re-arming into an ONGC short with no live stop, turned +2188 into +47

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
- The 09:45 NTPC long (1500 @ 323.80) hit target at 09:48:11 (325.45) for +2188.37 net, so the Rs 1000 day target was cleared in 3 minutes.
- In the same second the strategy shorted ONGC (2250 @ 224.40), which used Rs 100980 margin and left Rs 1208 free.
- ONGC drifted against the short and was force-flattened at 14:50:28 (225.22) for -2141.23, leaving the day at +47 net.
- The footer's "2 won, net Rs 551, best 325, worst 225" is wrong. Actual: 1 win, 1 loss, net +47. The "best/worst" values are exit prices, not P&L, so the summary generator needs fixing.

## Learnings
- **The NTPC entry worked.** The 09:45 long on a VWAP-trend signal paid within 3 minutes. The early-session trend read was right, and the target was close enough to be hit quickly.
- **The ONGC short had no protective exit.** Its stop and target columns are blank, and it sat in a losing position for about 5 hours until the 14:50 flat rule closed it. The adverse move was only 0.82 pts (0.37%), but on 2250 qty that cost Rs 1845 gross plus about Rs 296 in charges. Any ATR stop would likely have cut it earlier, or at least capped it.
- **Continuing after the day target is what gave the day back.** "Keeps trading for more" put a full-wallet position at risk right after the target was banked. Nothing protected the realized gain.
- **One lot ties up the whole account.** With Rs 100000, one futures lot consumes about 97-101% of capital. The ONGC trade blocked about 40 later signals, including LT, INFY, TATASTEEL and POWERGRID. The blocked list is mostly margin-unaffordable anyway; INFY (93k) and TATASTEEL (91k) were the only ones that fit. So the real issue is that a stuck position blocks everything, not missed upside.
- **Signal price and fill price differ by the futures basis.** NTPC was signalled at 322.20 but filled at 323.80 (+1.60, about 0.5%). ONGC was signalled at 223.30 but filled at 224.40. Signals are computed on spot, while stops and targets act on futures. Any ATR levels must be computed on the futures price, or the basis will shift them.
- **Quote lookups are broken for some symbols.** Six skips (SBIN 10:20, COALINDIA 10:22, BEL 12:18, KOTAKBANK 12:56, HCLTECH 13:24, NTPC 13:50) came from asking for an NFO token and getting the NSE-EQ token back. This is an instrument-mapping bug, not a strategy issue.

## Try next
- **Stop after the target.** After the day target is hit, take no new entries, or allow one more trade only if its stop risk is no more than 50% of the banked P&L. Today that would have locked +2188.
- **Enforce a live stop on every open.** Use stop_atr = 1.5 computed on the futures price, and reject the order if the stop or target field would be empty. Pair it with target_atr = 3.0, since quick targets are what paid today (NTPC).
- **Add a time stop.** Close any position not at target within 60 minutes, so one trade cannot hold all the margin for the whole session.

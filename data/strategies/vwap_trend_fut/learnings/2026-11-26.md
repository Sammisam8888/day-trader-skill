# Rs 1 lakh only fits one NTPC lot, and Rs 285 of charges per round trip means a scratch exit loses money

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy traded only in the afternoon, from 13:06 on. It took two long NTPC29DEC26F trades of 1500 qty each.
- **13:08 trade:** bought at 322.30, stopped at 322.45. Gross was +225, net was -59.19.
- **14:26 trade:** bought at 322.15, closed flat at 14:50 at 323.30. Gross was +1725, net was +1440.15.

The trade rows add up to a real day net of +1381, which beats the Rs 1000 target. The printed summary does not match the log: it says net Rs 646 and "2 won", but the first trade lost after charges. "best 323 / worst 322" are prices, not rupees. Seventeen other signals were skipped: 13 because one lot needed more margin than the wallet had, 4 because of quote and contract errors.

## Learnings
- **Charges set the minimum move.** Each NTPC round trip cost about Rs 285, which is gross minus net on both trades. That is roughly 0.19 points on 1500 qty. The 13:08 trade moved +0.15 and still lost Rs 59. A stop that sits at or near entry is a guaranteed small loss, not a breakeven exit.
- **The 14:26 trade worked because it was given room.** It held 24 minutes and went from 322.15 to 323.30 (+1.15). It only closed at the 14:50 flat time. That one trade made the whole day; the first trade's tight stop gave nothing.
- **Capital limits the universe to NTPC.** With Rs 100000, every other F&O name was skipped on margin: BAJFINANCE 168k, SBIN 172k, JSWSTEEL 177k, HCLTECH 114k, and others. POWERGRID missed twice, at 13:47 and 13:54, by about Rs 2000 (102011 vs 99941 free). The live strategy is really "NTPC vwap_trend", and it will be judged on very few trades.
- **Signal price and fill price differ by about 2 points.** The NTPC signal fired at 320.20 and 320.30, but the futures fills were 322.30 and 322.15. That gap is the futures basis. If stops and targets are computed from the spot signal price, they are measured on a different series than the fills. The stop/target columns are blank in the log, so this cannot be checked from the log.
- **Instrument lookup is broken for some names.** RELIANCE (13:13), BHARTIARTL (13:35) and HINDUNILVR (14:03) asked for an NFO token and got the NSE -EQ quote. RELIANCE resolved correctly later, at 14:09. The resolver is unreliable, and right now margin is hiding the bug.
- **The day-target banner is mark-to-market.** It said "net 1231 at 14:40" while the second trade was still open. Also, two trades are far too few to tell signal edge from luck.

## Try next
- **Add a filter so trades can clear charges:** take a trade only if the expected target move times qty is at least 3x the round-trip charges (about Rs 850 for NTPC, so a target of 0.6 points or more). Raise target_atr to at least 4.0 to match.
- **Widen the stop to stop_atr 2.5,** computed on the futures price rather than the spot signal price, so stops like the one at 13:46 do not fire inside the charge band.
- **Fix the tooling:**
  - Recompute the day summary from the closed trades, using net Rs rather than prices.
  - Fix the NFO token resolution.
  - Pre-filter the universe by one-lot margin against the free balance, so scans stop logging unaffordable skips. Otherwise, either accept that the strategy trades NTPC only, or keep enabled 1 only once the wallet is at least Rs 1.2 lakh.

# Counter-trend BHARTIARTL short and 4R targets cost the Rs 1,000 day; both longs hit about 2R and gave back 37%

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The tape turned UP after a MIXED open. The AI opened a short in BHARTIARTL and longs in HDFCBANK and RELIANCE, all as ORB trades. It never reached the day target. All three positions closed together at 14:50:07 for a net of about Rs +613.64:
- HDFCBANK +457.11
- RELIANCE +323.97
- BHARTIARTL -167.44

The two longs peaked at a combined Rs 1,144 open profit (625 + 519) by about 11:22. They then stalled for 3.5 hours and gave back Rs 363. Note: the summary line "no closed trades" contradicts the log, which shows three closes, so that summary feed looks broken.

## Learnings
- **HDFCBANK long (09:30, 856.05):** It trended cleanly to best net 625. The trail ratcheted 13 times and locked 469 at stop 866.75. It closed at 457, about Rs 12 below the lock's own estimate, so the lock's net figure slightly understates charges or slippage.
- **RELIANCE long (10:30, 1375.40):** This was a second ORB entry taken once the market had confirmed UP, and it worked. Best net was 519 and it locked 311 at stop 1384.60. It closed at 324, right at the locked level. Both longs exited within a few paise of their trailing stops, so the late session simply drifted back to the stops.
- **Targets were too far:**
  - HDFCBANK's target of 879.65 was about 4R (risk 5.90), and RELIANCE's 1393.50 was about 2.6R (risk 6.90). Neither was touched.
  - Both stocks did trade through roughly 2R: about 867.85 for HDFCBANK (stop raised to 867.75 at 10:47) and about 1389.20 for RELIANCE (stop at 1384.60 implies price near 1389.6).
  - Fixed 2R exits would have banked roughly Rs 510 + 503 net (about 1,013) against the 781 actually realized.
- **BHARTIARTL short (09:30, 2103.70) was a counter-trend bet:** The pick was "weakest stock" on a MIXED day. By 10:30 the AI itself was calling the market UP and buying RELIANCE, yet the short stayed open.
  - It never reached its stop (2118.50, risk about Rs 266), and its stop never moved.
  - It sat as a loser for 5h20m and closed around 2111, costing Rs 167.
  - Pairing one short against two longs at the open hedged away part of the day's edge.
- **No time-based tightening:** After 11:22 no new highs printed on either long, but the trail stayed at about 40-60% of best profit. A 3.5-hour stall turned 1,144 of open profit into 781 realized on the longs.

## Try next

- **Direction filter:** Once the scan tags the market UP (or DOWN), close or block any open or new trade against that direction. Concretely, exit an open counter-direction position at the scan that flips direction (BHARTIARTL at 10:30). Also skip opposite-side ORB entries when `direction` = MIXED at 09:30, or halve `size` for them.
- **Time stop and stall lock:**
  - Exit any trade not at +0.5R within 60 minutes of entry (BHARTIARTL by 10:30).
  - If a profit-locked trade makes no new best for 45 minutes, raise the lock to 75% of best net. For HDFCBANK that is about Rs 469 locked by about 12:00, and for RELIANCE about Rs 389 instead of 311.

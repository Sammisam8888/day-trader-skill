# BHARTIARTL short gave back a Rs 115 open profit to close at Rs -117 despite a profit lock at 2074.30

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The market was MIXED. IT was the weak spot (IT sector about -1.7%, INFY -2.15% by 11:40), and PSU Banks were firm early (+0.88%). Four positions filled and all four closed as losers at 14:50:06:
- SBIN long: -218.06
- RELIANCE short: -140.33
- BHARTIARTL short: -116.84
- ICICIBANK short: -1.35

The net was Rs -476.58, with 0 wins in 4 trades. The "Day numbers: no closed trades" summary contradicts these four close rows, so the summary feed is broken. Seven other entry orders were cancelled before filling, either by the decider or because price crossed the planned stop.

## Learnings
- **The profit lock failed on BHARTIARTL.** The 10:31 short (17 @ 2078.50) was +115 at 11:50, and the lock moved its stop to 2074.30 to keep net 46. It still closed at -116.84, which implies an exit near 2085, about 10 points through the locked stop. Either the trailed stop was never enforced, or the close was only a 14:50 sweep. This single failure cost about Rs 163 against the locked amount, and it is the biggest fixable leak of the day.
- **SBIN 09:35 long (31 @ 1144.10) was a chase against the decider's own evidence.** The note said the 09:30 bar poked 1144 and closed back inside the 1141.35 range high, but it still bought the poke. It lost -218.06, about the full 6.3-point stop plus charges, the largest loss of the day. It was also the only long on a day when the strongest signals pointed down.
- **The losing ORB stop-entries were triggers at the day extremes.** Of those that filled, SBIN lost close to its full stop and RELIANCE lost -140.33. Several others never filled and were cancelled because price reversed through the stop before triggering:
  - INFY at 11:46: price reached 1155.70, through the 1154.60 stop.
  - BHARTIARTL at 10:11: price reached 2089.60, below the 2090.50 stop.
  - ICICIBANK at 12:00: price reached 1599.50, through the 1599.40 stop.
  
  Stop-entries placed right at the high or low kept getting faded in this mixed tape.
- **Sizing was inconsistent and the decider had to fix it after the fact.** INFY was first sent at qty 87 (about Rs 409-461 risk), then cut to 37. RELIANCE was cancelled at 10:11 for breaking risk caps and re-sent at 28. The sizer is not applying the cap before the order goes out.
- **A second BHARTIARTL short was sent at 13:20 (26 @ 2059.00) while the 10:31 short was still open.** That stacks risk on one name. There is no close row for it, so its status is unclear.
- **The 13:05 ICICIBANK short (24 @ 1591.40) went nowhere,** closing at -1.35. Late-session day_break entries had no time to work before the 14:50 exit.

## Try next
- **Enforce the profit lock:** once armed, if last price is at or beyond the locked stop, exit at market immediately. Log a "lock breached" event, and alert if any position closes below its locked net.
- **Hard cap risk at entry:** qty = floor(Rs 200 / abs(entry - stop)), computed before sending. Allow only one open-or-pending order per stock and direction.
- **Restrict orb stop-entries:** skip them when the trigger is more than 0.4% from VWAP or when the trigger bar closed back inside the range (as SBIN did). No new day_break entries after 13:00.

# Kept trading after the day target and gave back 76% of a Rs 3243 peak on three late losers

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy opened 5 option trades and closed all of them: 2 won and 3 lost. By the log it ended at about Rs +792 net (2248.59 + 994.06 - 789.90 - 869.24 - 791.05). The supplied summary line says "5 won, net Rs 250, best 89, worst 26", which contradicts the trade log and should be treated as a reporting bug. Realized P&L peaked at Rs 3243 at 13:29 after two put winners, well above the Rs 1000 target. All three trades still open or opened after that point lost, so the day finished below target. Three more signals (HINDUNILVR, KOTAKBANK, TCS put) were skipped because the NFO quote lookup returned the NSE equity quote instead.

## Learnings
- **The two winners were both bearish puts that hit target.** BAJFINANCE P1090 (10:54 to 13:08) returned 34.00 to 37.10 for +2249, and ADANIPORTS P1900 (13:19 to 13:29) returned 54.30 to 56.55 for +994. Both followed SELL signals and both reached target rather than drifting.
- **Both calls lost.** TCS C2600 opened at 14:14 and was stopped in 10 minutes for -869. INFY C1160 opened at 13:34 and was force-flattened at 14:50 for -791 without reaching stop or target. Both were BUY signals on IT names that never followed through.
- **The TCS signal reversed within 15 minutes.** A BUY at 14:14 was followed by a SELL at 14:29 (the put was skipped). A squeeze break that flips direction that fast was noise, and the call entry paid for it.
- **HINDALCO P920 sat open for nearly 3 hours.** It opened at 11:19 and was stopped at 14:13 for -790 on only a 1.05 premium move. A slow, low-momentum hold mostly lost to time decay and drift, not a real break.
- **The losses outweigh the wins per trade.** Losers lost Rs 790-870 each on 3.5-4% premium moves, while the ADANIPORTS winner made only Rs 994. With charges of roughly Rs 55-75 per round trip, the stop-to-target ratio leaves little edge once win rate drops below 50%.
- **There is a data-plumbing bug.** "Asked for NFO token, got NSE -EQ" occurred 3 times. This silently cut the trade sample, and it would have taken the TCS put that matched the reversal.

## Try next
- **Add a profit-protection filter.** Once realized day P&L exceeds the Rs 1000 target, block new entries if P&L falls more than 40% from its intraday peak. Today that would have blocked TCS at 14:14 (P&L 2453 vs peak 3243 is a 24% drawdown, so it is borderline). Tighter alternative: no new entries at all after target is hit and it is past 13:30. Either version avoids the INFY and TCS entries.
- **Add a time cutoff of no new entries after 14:00.** That leaves at least 50 minutes before the 14:50 flatten. TCS (14:14) would not have been opened, and INFY-style holds force-flattened at a loss become less likely.
- **Test puts-only on these large-caps for the next 3 sessions.** Set side to whichever value maps to SELL-only, and keep stop_atr unchanged so the comparison is clean. Separately, fix the NFO/NSE quote-resolution bug before trusting any side-specific win rate, since the skipped trades bias the sample.

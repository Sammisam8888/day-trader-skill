# One inverted-ORB short on NTPC futures made about Rs 5189, but it locked up 97% of margin and the log's summary line disagrees with the trade

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
At 09:47 the strategy shorted 1 lot (1500) of NTPC29DEC26F at 324.65 on a SELL signal from NTPC cash at 323.45. It held the position until the 14:50 flat and covered at 321.00. The close row shows net Rs 5189.17: the gross is 3.65 x 1500 = Rs 5475, so about Rs 286 went to charges. The "Day numbers" line says net Rs 321, which matches the exit price and not any P&L, so the summary is probably a reporting bug. The 10:23 "DAY TARGET reached, net 1139" message must have come from open mark-to-market, because nothing had closed by then. That one position used Rs 97,395 of the Rs 100,000 wallet, and the 16 later events were all skips.

## Learnings
- The 09:47 NTPC SELL worked. Price drifted about 1.1% in the short's favour into the afternoon, and holding to the 14:50 flat captured all of it. This is one trade, though, not evidence of an edge.
- The NTPC trade shows no stop and no target in the log. Either it ran unprotected for 5 hours on 1500 qty or the log does not record exits. A 1% move against the position would have cost about Rs 4870, close to the whole day's gain.
- Margin, not signal quality, decided the day:
  - ITC at 09:47 was skipped because 1 lot needs Rs 106,415 against a Rs 100,000 wallet.
  - After NTPC opened, LT, HDFCBANK, RELIANCE, BHARTIARTL, MARUTI, ICICIBANK, BAJFINANCE and SUNPHARMA were all skipped with Rs 2605 free.
  - Most of these need more than the full wallet anyway, so they can never trade at this size.
- Contract lookup is broken for some names. AXISBANK (10:00), COALINDIA (10:20), ONGC (10:30), ULTRACEMCO (10:31) and HINDUNILVR (12:00) requested NFO tokens but got NSE equity quotes, so 5 signals were lost to a data bug.
- BANKBEES (10:10) and NIFTYBEES (10:31) produced signals even though ETFs have no futures. They should not be in this strategy's universe.
- The 1.20 gap between the signal (323.45) and the fill (324.65) on a SELL is the futures basis, not slippage. Stop and target prices computed on cash levels will be misplaced on the future unless they are adjusted for it.

## Try next
- Keep minutes=30 and invert=1. Make sure exits are actually set and logged, starting with stop_frac=0.5 and target_x=2.0, and offset the levels by the cash-to-futures basis at entry.
- Add a universe filter: drop symbols whose 1-lot margin exceeds the wallet (ITC, LT, HDFCBANK, RELIANCE, BHARTIARTL, MARUTI, ICICIBANK, BAJFINANCE, SUNPHARMA) and drop the ETFs. Then fix the NFO token mapping before judging signal counts.
- Fix the day-summary line so it reports realized net (5189.17, not 321), and stop reporting "DAY TARGET reached" from unrealized mark-to-market.

# Day was actually -2183, not +547: put-side fades lost Rs 4509, and the summary and target banner misreported the day

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy started late (first entry 13:09) and traded until the 14:50 flat. Summing the per-trade net figures gives 9 closed trades, 4 wins and 5 losses, net Rs -2183 after charges, with a best of +2887 (SBIN) and a worst of -3892 (HCLTECH). That contradicts the day summary ("9 won, net 547, best 342, worst 7"). It also contradicts the "DAY TARGET reached at 13:13: net 1129" banner, which fired when the running total was -862. The tape favored calls in the afternoon: the 7 call trades netted +2325 and the 2 put trades netted -4509.

## Learnings
- The reporting is broken and the target logic can't be trusted. The 13:13 "target reached" fired at -862, and the summary line matches none of the trades. Until this is fixed, any "keeps trading after target" behavior is based on false data.
- SELL-signal fades (buying puts) were the whole loss:
  - HCLTECH P1440 (13:46) went from 50.65 to 41.05, -19% of premium, and ran to the 14:50 flat without a stop firing. That one trade was -3892.
  - BEL P405 (14:33) lost -617 the same way.
  - Call stops fired at -3% to -6% of premium, but no put stop fired at -19%. The stop handling for puts looks inconsistent and needs checking.
- Early call stops were too tight for option noise:
  - ULTRACEMCO C12700 was stopped in 3.5 minutes (357.70 to 341.50, -862).
  - RELIANCE C1370 was stopped at -2.6% of premium (-693).
  - ICICIBANK C1620 was stopped at -5.8% (-1794).
  - The calls held longer paid well: KOTAKBANK C505 +2416 at target, SBIN C1140 +2887 at the flat. A fade on the underlying needs room beyond premium spread and decay.
- Clustered entries locked up the capital:
  - 13:13:09 opened 3 calls at once (Rs 72795 premium).
  - 13:57:10 opened 2 more (Rs 53050).
  - As a result, NTPC (12675), INFY (16460) and HDFCBANK (17062) were skipped for cash between 14:03 and 14:12. Those skips were caused by position stacking, not by the signals.
- Contract mapping bugs cost signals. TATASTEEL P162.5 and POWERGRID C267.5 returned NSE-EQ tokens instead of NFO. NIFTYBEES and BANKBEES fired 5 times despite having no option contracts.

## Try next
- Set side to BUY signals only (calls; the knob value depends on how 0..2 is encoded) until put-side stops are verified. Alternatively, keep both sides and add a hard premium stop of -10% on puts.
- Raise stop_sd to 1.5 (from current) so premium stops don't trigger on 3-minute noise like ULTRACEMCO at 13:12.
- Add a filter of at most 2 concurrent positions and at most 1 new entry per minute. Also exclude NIFTYBEES and BANKBEES from the universe, and fix the NFO token lookup before the next session.

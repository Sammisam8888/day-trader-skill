# Two unstopped morning calls (SBIN, RELIANCE) lost Rs 3445 and turned the day into a Rs 3500 loss

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy bought 1-month CE/PE premium on Bollinger-band fade signals. The log shows 10 closed trades, 4 won and 6 lost, for a net of Rs -3500. That matches the running "day so far" column (wins +1646, losses -5147). The "Day numbers" line (10 won, net Rs 180, best 42, worst 5) does not match the log and should not be trusted. The "DAY TARGET reached at 11:38: net 1111" banner fired before any trade had closed, so it counted unrealized open P&L, which later reversed.

## Learnings
- The two morning calls did the damage.
  - SBIN29DEC26C1130 (opened 10:59, 35.85 -> 32.80) lost Rs 2358.
  - RELIANCE29DEC26C1380 (opened 11:24, 44.30 -> 42.25) lost Rs 1087.
  - Both rode to the 14:50 flat with no stop exit, while COALINDIA and ONGC were stopped out at 3-4% premium drops. Either stops were not attached to the 10:59/11:24 entries, or they were set far wider. The stop/target columns are blank for every trade.
- ONGC29DEC26P224.5 was bought three times (13:44, 13:49, 14:14) at the same strike. Each was stopped within 2-25 minutes for -354, -488 and -488 (Rs -1330 total). Re-entering the same fade right after a stop just paid charges plus spread three times.
- The stops that did fire were noise-sized on cheap premium. COALINDIA P385 went 7.15 -> 6.90 and ONGC went 5.18 -> 5.04. On 1350-2250 lot sizes, a 0.14-0.25 premium tick is Rs 350-490, so the option stop was much tighter than the underlying band logic implies.
- Winners were small and slow:
  - TATASTEEL C165 hit its target (+0.30 premium, +769) after 3 hours.
  - ITC C305 hit its target (+0.10, +130).
  - SUNPHARMA P1900 (+217) and HDFCBANK C860 (+531) only profited at the 14:50 flat.
  - Targets of a few paise on premium do not pay for the stop sizes in use.
- Cash was tied up by 11:24. Four opens used about Rs 84k of the Rs 100k wallet, which caused 15+ "costs Rs X" skips in the afternoon (BAJFINANCE, ADANIENT, ADANIPORTS, INFY, HINDALCO).
- There is a contract-resolution bug. SUNPHARMA, ICICIBANK, ADANIPORTS and POWERGRID requested NFO tokens but got NSE equity quotes back, which lost 4 signals.

## Try next
- Make stop_sd 1.5 apply to every open position, verified at entry time. Also add a hard premium stop of 10% so no single 1-lot position can lose more than about Rs 2500, as SBIN did.
- Add a filter: after a stop-out, block the same underlying for 60 minutes or until price re-tags the band. This would have saved 2 of the 3 ONGC losses.
- Raise sd from the current value to 2.5 to cut marginal fades. Also cap concurrent positions at 3 so wallet cash is still available for later signals.

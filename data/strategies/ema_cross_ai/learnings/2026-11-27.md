# Two quick stop-outs on the first entries nearly erased the day; the "6 won, Rs 11389" summary is wrong

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
Every trade was a SELL, which suggests a weak tape or a short-only side setting. This log alone cannot tell which. The strategy closed 6 trades: 4 winners and 2 stop-losses. Net was Rs +857.36, short of the Rs 1000 day target.

The supplied day numbers do not match the log. They claim 6 won, net Rs 11389, best 4114 and worst 304. The log actually shows a best of +783.13 (RELIANCE) and a worst of -572.33 (BEL). The 4114 and 304 figures look like the LT and ITC exit prices, so the stats generator is probably reading the wrong column.

The day ran -1137 by 12:16. It then recovered through four straight profitable shorts.

## Learnings
- **The first cluster of entries was early and crowded.** TRENT opened at 11:34, BEL at 11:39 and SUNPHARMA at 11:49, all within 15 minutes. BEL stopped out at 12:00 after 21 minutes and TRENT at 12:16 after 42 minutes, for -1136.77 combined. Both stops were hit on the same counter-move around 12:00-12:16. That looks like one correlated bounce taking out two positions, not two independent bad signals.
- **Stops filled slightly worse than set.** BEL filled at 411.16 against a 411.07 stop, and TRENT at 3282.45 against 3282.17. The slippage is small, but with tight stops each loss (about Rs 565-572) was about 75% of the size of the best winners (717, 783).
- **Target/stop distance ratio was only about 1.5-1.65 on every trade.** Examples: BEL 3.11/1.88, RELIANCE 6.34/3.88, ITC 0.85/0.58. With a ratio that low, two stops need roughly 1.5 targets to recover. That is exactly what happened: SUNPHARMA at 13:00 (+717.12) and RELIANCE at 14:44 (+783.13) were needed just to get back to +363.
- **Later entries all worked.** RELIANCE at 12:44 hit its target, with the fill at 1376.15 beating the 1376.51 target. LT at 12:59 and ITC at 13:34 were both in profit at the 14:50 flatten, adding +107.88 and +386.00. Every entry after the 12:00-12:16 bounce finished green.
- **The time flatten cost the day target.** ITC closed at 304.11, only 0.22 from its 303.89 target, and LT was about halfway to target. The day ended Rs 143 short of 1000 with ITC nearly done.

## Try next
- **Raise stop_atr by 0.5 (staying within 1.0..3.0) and raise target_atr to keep the ratio at 2.0 or more.** For example, use stop_atr 2.0 with target_atr 4.0. The aim is to survive the kind of noon bounce that took out BEL and TRENT, while making each target worth more than one stop.
- **Add a filter allowing at most 2 open positions in the same direction within a 30-minute window.** This would have kept TRENT, BEL and SUNPHARMA from all being exposed to the same reversal.
- **Fix the day-stats generator before trusting any summary.** It should compute wins from net Rs > 0 and best/worst from the net Rs column, not the price column. The correct values today are 4/6 wins, net +857.36, best +783.13 and worst -572.33.

# Premium stops are too tight: 6 of 10 trades died in minutes, and only positions held to the 14:50 flatten made money

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The session ran from 13:14 to the 14:50 flatten. The strategy bought 1-lot options on 13 volume-burst signals, took 10 of them and skipped 4. From the per-trade net figures, 4 trades won and 6 lost: wins Rs +2335, losses Rs -3207, net about Rs -872. The log's running total ends at -887.

The summary line ("10 won, net Rs 552, best 140, worst 7") contradicts the trade rows and should not be trusted. The day target of Rs 1000 was missed by roughly Rs 1900. The three biggest winners were all calls flattened at 14:50: TITAN +1104, NTPC +555 and BEL +371. Every stop-out came within 15 minutes of entry.

## Learnings
- **Stops sit inside normal premium noise.**
  - TCS29DEC26C2580 (13:38:11) was stopped 5 seconds after entry on a 0.50 move (0.55% of premium), losing Rs -172.
  - ADANIENT29DEC26C2700 (13:48:04) was stopped after 64 seconds on a 3.2% premium dip, losing Rs -1072.
  - HINDUNILVR P2140 (8 min, -825) and ITC C307.5 (15 min, -818) were also cut quickly.
  - The stop looks like a fraction of underlying ATR applied to a premium that has bid/ask spread and theta on top. Bursts need time to follow through.
- **Patience paid.** The three trades that were never stopped (TITAN 13:14, NTPC 13:25, BEL 14:31) were held 19 to 96 minutes and produced all of the day's real profit. TITAN premium went from 133.00 to 139.70 against a 4637 to ~4650+ underlying move.
- **The "target" exit is broken for options.**
  - TECHM29DEC26C1820 closed on "target" at 71.05 against a 71.20 entry, for Rs -202.
  - HDFCBANK P860 hit "target" for only +0.55 premium (Rs +306).
  - The target appears to fire on the underlying's price while the premium lags or decays. A target that can book a loss is a bug.
- **Contract resolution failed 3 times.** ITC at 13:16, TATASTEEL at 13:56 and POWERGRID at 14:31 were each skipped with "asked for NFO token, got NSE -EQ quote".
  - ITC traded fine at 13:38, so the failure is intermittent rather than a bad symbol map.
  - INFY29DEC26C1174 was skipped at 14:07 at Rs 18000 per lot, which is under budget. Both the skip reason and the 1174 strike, which is off the normal strike grid, need checking.
- **Charges were not the problem.** Round-trip charges were about Rs 60 (TCS: gross -112.5, net -172). The losses came from exits, not costs.
- **Logging is incomplete.** The stop and target columns are empty on every open row, so stop placement cannot be audited from this log.

## Try next
- **Widen the effective stop on premium.**
  - Raise body_atr from the current value to 1.5 so only stronger burst candles qualify.
  - As a filter, evaluate stops on the underlying's price rather than option premium, with no stop check in the first 2 minutes after entry.
- **Fix the target.**
  - Set target_x to 2.5.
  - Add a guard so that a "target" close only fires when premium is at least entry + 1.5x round-trip charges (about Rs 90 / qty). Otherwise keep holding to 14:50.
- **Fix infra before tuning further.**
  - Make the NFO quote lookup retry or fail loudly when it receives an NSE equity quote.
  - Fix the day summary aggregation, which reported +552 and 10/10 wins on a -872 day.
  - Keep vol_x unchanged until these are fixed, so the next day's sample measures the strategy rather than plumbing errors.

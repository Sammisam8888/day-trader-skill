# One stop of Rs 2.15 on Rs 3.3 lakh of AXISBANK gave back almost all of the best trade

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The three 09:46 BUY fades (ONGC, AXISBANK, TATASTEEL) show those stocks drove down in the first 30 minutes. Later SELL fades in BAJFINANCE, TRENT and MARUTI show other names drove up. The strategy closed 6 trades: 5 won, 1 lost, net Rs +711.87 after charges. Best was TATASTEEL at +691.56 and worst was AXISBANK at -662.04, so the day finished below the Rs 1000 target.

The 09:51 "DAY TARGET reached, net 1023" line could only have been unrealized mark-to-market, because nothing had closed by then. The summary line is wrong: it reports 6 won, Rs 20257, best 14064 and worst 163, which look like prices, not P&L. The trade log is the correct source.

## Learnings
- **AXISBANK 09:46 BUY, stopped 11:16 at -662.04.**
  - The stop was 1418.1, only 2.15 below a 1420.25 entry (0.15%), while the target was 24.75 above. That is an 11:1 reward-to-risk ratio that normal noise was always likely to break.
  - It was also the largest position by far: 232 x 1420 = Rs 3.29 lakh notional, against Rs 0.57 lakh for TATASTEEL.
  - The fill at 1417.95 was below the stop. Slippage plus about Rs 128 of charges on that turnover turned a 2.3-point move into a Rs 662 loss.
- **TATASTEEL 09:46 BUY worked as designed.** The stop was 1.21 below (0.75%) and the target 2.06 above. It hit target at 10:29 for +691.56. A sensibly placed stop on the same fade signal gave the cleanest trade of the day.
- **ONGC 09:46 BUY.** The target at 224.32 (+0.6%) was never reached in five hours, and the trade was closed flat at 14:50 for +151.83. The fade bounce ran out of energy at about 0.4%, so the target was too ambitious for this name.
- **The late SELL fades all made money but were small.**
  - BAJFINANCE (entered 10:44) made +329.92 and MARUTI (12:14) made +95.82, both on the 14:50 flat close.
  - TRENT (11:10) made +104.78 at target, but only 6 shares.
  - Entries 75 to 165 minutes after the drive window still worked, but small notional (Rs 20k to 56k) kept them minor.
- **The day was decided by position sizing, not by signal quality.** Position sizes varied about 16x in notional across trades. The single oversized position with a too-tight stop decided the day's P&L more than the fade signals did.

## Try next
- Add a minimum-stop filter: skip the trade, or widen the stop, if the stop distance is under 0.4% of entry or under 0.5x the 15-minute ATR. This would have rejected or resized the AXISBANK 1418.1 stop.
- Size by rupee risk instead of letting notional float. Fix risk at about Rs 300 per trade and cap notional at Rs 1.5 lakh. With that rule, AXISBANK at a 0.4% stop would have been about 52 shares, not 232.
- Keep retrace at its current setting (TATASTEEL and TRENT both reached target after the retrace entry). Cap the target at the size of the retraced move, so ONGC-style targets of 0.6% become about 0.4% and actually fill before 14:50.

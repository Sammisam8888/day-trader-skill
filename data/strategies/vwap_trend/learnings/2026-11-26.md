# Morning trend entries banked +2733 by 11:25, but midday re-entries into chop on the same names turned it into -2795

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy reached the Rs 1000 day target at 10:02 and peaked at +2733 at 11:25:39 (TCS target), then lost money steadily into the 14:50 flatten. It ended at **-2795 net**: 55 closed trades, 16 winners and 39 losers. The best trade was WIPRO BUY at 10:02, +973.68. The worst was BHARTIARTL SELL at 14:38, -598.70. The supplied "Day numbers" line (55 won, net +86906, best 14060, worst 163) is wrong and should not be trusted. Its "best" and "worst" values are the MARUTI and TATASTEEL exit prices, not P&L. These figures come from the trade log itself. The market trended cleanly in the first 90 minutes. After about 11:30 it chopped around VWAP, and the strategy kept flipping long and short on the same symbols.

## Learnings
- **Early trend entries worked.** Six full-size trades hit target at roughly a 2:1 reward-to-risk:
  - BEL 09:45 BUY, +970
  - WIPRO 09:46 BUY, +974
  - HCLTECH 09:52 BUY, +505
  - ADANIENT 09:57 BUY, +946
  - HINDUNILVR 10:04 BUY, +901
  - TCS 11:08 SELL, +926
- **Repeated stop-outs on one symbol cost the day.**
  - BHARTIARTL was stopped four times for -1923 in total: SELL 10:37, BUY 10:43, SELL 11:27, SELL 13:35. It flipped direction within six minutes of the first stop.
  - NTPC was traded six times between 12:06 and 14:26 for -1357 net, flipping BUY, SELL, BUY, SELL, SELL.
  - POWERGRID was traded four times for about -100.
  - Together these three names account for about -3380, more than the whole day's loss.
- **Stops were too tight for low-priced, low-volatility names at midday.** NTPC stops were Rs 0.41-0.95 away and POWERGRID stops Rs 0.39-0.52, both well inside normal VWAP noise. Each stop also filled a few paise beyond its level (for example, NTPC 13:24 had stop 320.52 and filled at 320.66). That slippage makes a 1-tick stop structurally negative.
- **No giveback rule let the profit drain.** After the 11:25 peak of +2733, the strategy kept opening full-size trades. These included INFY 11:26, NTPC 12:06, SUNPHARMA 12:48, HINDUNILVR 13:22, MARUTI 13:25 and JSWSTEEL 13:25. Almost all of them stopped out; from 12:00 to the close the strategy lost about 4976.
- **Capital exhaustion produced trades that cannot pay.** With many positions open, new entries were sized at 1-14 shares, such as ONGC, TATASTEEL, NIFTYBEES, HDFCBANK, SBIN and INFY at 1 share. Charges ate their targets: winners like ONGC 12:20 (+0.39) and TATASTEEL (+1.72) were near zero, and stops still cost a full charge.

## Try next
- **Per-symbol cooldown:** after a stop-out, block re-entry on that symbol (either side) for 30 minutes, and allow at most 2 stops per symbol per day. Today this would have prevented the BHARTIARTL entries at 10:43, 11:27 and 13:35, and the NTPC entries after 12:52.
- **Giveback lock:** once the day target is hit, stop opening new trades if realized P&L falls 40% below the day's peak. Today that means halting near +1640, which happened around 12:01, instead of ending at -2795. Also skip any entry whose computed qty is under 20% of normal size.
- **Knobs:** raise confirm by one step (to 4 if it is currently 3) to filter midday VWAP crosses, and widen stop_atr to at least 2.5, with target_atr scaled to keep about 2:1. Alternatively, add a filter that skips any trade whose stop distance is under 0.3% of price. That would have blocked the NTPC, POWERGRID and BANKBEES entries.

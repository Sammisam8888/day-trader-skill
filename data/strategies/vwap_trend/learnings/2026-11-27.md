  - Normal post-open swings were enough to hit them.
- **The win rate was below break-even.**
  - Target distance was about 2x stop distance, so break-even needs roughly 33% winners. The day delivered 9/46 (20%).
  - The real target hits that paid were INFY short (10:38, +725.51) and SBIN short (10:54, +929.76).
  - Two later shorts held to the 14:50 flat also paid: LT (+329.38) and HINDUNILVR (+293.46).
  - These four trades were all shorts, which points to a mild down-bias that the longs kept fighting.
- **Flipping direction on the same stock after a stop bled steadily.**
  - KOTAKBANK went SELL 12:56, BUY 13:05, SELL 13:15, and all three stopped out (-52, -60, -67).
  - HCLTECH long stopped at 13:23 (-290), then the immediate short stopped at 13:32 (-205).

  - TRENT and TECHM did the same.
  - In each case the price was oscillating around VWAP, which is not a trend.

  - Only HINDUNILVR (+11.64) and HCLTECH (+27.61, 18 qty) won on the long side.

  - Each lost a few rupees, mostly to charges, and no outcome on them could matter.

## Try next

- Add a filter: no re-entry in the opposite direction on a stock within 30 minutes of a stop on that stock. Also skip any order where qty x price is under Rs 20000, which removes the charge-only trades.

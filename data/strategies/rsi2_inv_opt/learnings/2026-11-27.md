# Unenforced stop on BAJFINANCE turned a -5k day into -11.7k, and the day summary misreports it as a win

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The log disagrees with the summary line. The summary claims 14 wins and net Rs +543. The log shows 14 closed trades: 5 winners totalling Rs +2245 and 9 losers totalling Rs -13972, for a net of about **Rs -11727** against a Rs 1000 target. Call buys on RSI2 BUY signals mostly bled. 7 of 9 calls lost, and 4 of them stopped out within 3-17 minutes of entry. Puts on SELL signals did better: 3 of 4 won, for Rs +1878. About 7 more signals were skipped because the system pulled an equity quote instead of the option quote, or because there wasn't enough free capital.

## Learnings
- **BAJFINANCE stop never fired.** BAJFINANCE29DEC26C1090 was opened at 11:04 at 41.05 and held until the 14:50 flat at 32.25, a 21% premium loss. It lost Rs 6672, which is 57% of the day's net loss. Every other trade hit a stop after a 1-4 point premium move, so this position was apparently not being monitored. The stop/target columns are blank for every row, so stop levels can't be checked from the log.
- **"target" exits can close at a loss.** INFY29DEC26C1160 closed at 12:03 at 43.80 against an entry of 45.35 (Rs -673), labelled "target". The target is probably checked on the underlying, and by the time it was hit, theta had eaten the premium. The trade waited 69 minutes for a move worth less than the decay.
- **Call entries were repeatedly wrong.** HINDALCO (10:57, -798), HCLTECH (10:59, -1158), ADANIPORTS (11:21, -1907) and BHARTIARTL (11:41, -1352) were all stopped within minutes. BHARTIARTL was then re-bought at 13:04 on a lower strike and stopped again at 13:21 (-381). Stops sized at about 1 ATR on the underlying cost Rs 800-1900 each in premium, while targets paid Rs 200-500. Example: SBIN +226 against ADANIPORTS -1907.
- **Puts did the work.** BEL P410 (+522), HINDUNILVR P2140 (+506) and INFY P1160 (+849 at flat) won. HDFCBANK P860 was the only put loss (-763).
- **Infrastructure losses.** Six skips were "asked for NFO X, got NSE Y -EQ": TECHM, WIPRO, INFY P1154, RELIANCE, ADANIENT and TITAN. The INFY P1154 request also used an invalid strike (1154), so strike rounding is broken for some names. BANKBEES and NIFTYBEES signals are wasted because these ETFs have no options.
- **Capital crowding.** By 11:04, four positions held about Rs 93k of the Rs 100k wallet. KOTAKBANK, ICICIBANK, ADANIENT, MARUTI, TATASTEEL and RELIANCE were then skipped on cost, while bad positions like BAJFINANCE kept the capital tied up.

## Try next
- **Filter (fix first):** enforce the stop on every open option every tick, and log the stop and target premium on each open. Also add a hard premium stop of 25% of the entry premium as a backstop. BAJFINANCE would have been cut near 30.8 instead of 32.25, and much earlier in time.
- **Exits:** set stop_atr = 1.5 (from about 1.0) and target_atr = 1.5. Make the target close only when the premium is above the entry price plus charges, so a "target" can never book a loss like INFY at 12:03.
- **Direction:** set side = 2 (SELL signals / puts only) for the next few sessions, assuming 2 means the short side. Also exclude BANKBEES and NIFTYBEES from the signal universe, and re-test once the NFO token lookup and strike rounding are fixed.

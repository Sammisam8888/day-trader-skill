# Underlying-based exits ignore option premium: two "target" exits lost money, and one Rs 1673 stop wiped out the day's first four trades

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy bought 1-lot calls on rsi2 BUY signals from 13:08 onward and made 8 round trips. It finished at Rs +257 net, not the Rs 349 in the summary line. By the log it went 4 wins and 4 losses, not 8 for 8. The best trade was SBIN at 14:19 for Rs +976.39 and the worst was ADANIENT at 13:32 for Rs -1672.96. The day was -1975 by 14:06 and recovered only because SBIN, COALINDIA, the second SBIN and BHARTIARTL all ran in the last 45 minutes. The day target of Rs 1000 was not reached.

## Learnings
- **Two "target" exits lost money.** Stop and target are evidently set on the underlying; the stop and target columns are blank in the log.
  - RELIANCE hit "target" at 14:06, but the call sold at 47.70 against a 48.10 entry, for Rs -268.15.
  - WIPRO hit "target" at 13:39, sold at 7.32 against 7.31: Rs +30 gross, Rs -33.59 net.
  - The cause is that the underlying target_atr distance is too small to beat theta, the spread and about Rs 65 of charges on a 1-lot option.
- **ADANIENT 13:14 lost about 6.5x the median winner.** Premium fell 121.10 to 116.00 in 18 minutes for Rs -1672.96. On a Rs 37k premium (the largest ticket of the day), an underlying-ATR stop produces an outsized rupee loss.
- **The late-session long-call trades in liquid names worked.**
  - SBIN 14:07 rose 33.80 to 35.20 for Rs +976.
  - COALINDIA 13:42 rose 10.85 to 11.55 for Rs +897.
  - SBIN re-entry 14:22 rose 35.55 to 36.55 at the flat for Rs +674.
  - Premium moves of 2-6% were enough when they were not cut off early.
- **JSWSTEEL 13:08 was held 100 minutes and never hit stop or target.** It was flattened at 14:50 for Rs -719 (42.25 to 41.30). This is pure theta and drift bleed on a signal that never followed through.
- **Capital lock caused 10+ skips.** By 13:47, four open premiums (about Rs 92k) used nearly all of the Rs 100k wallet. That is why cheap tickets like ITC (Rs 13455), INFY (Rs 14620) and POWERGRID (Rs 14345) were skipped while JSWSTEEL sat idle.
- **Infrastructure faults:**
  - The contract lookup returned the cash token instead of the option token for NTPC (13:53), AXISBANK (14:07) and TATASTEEL (14:31): asked for NFO, got NSE EQ.
  - The day summary line is wrong. It should be computed from the closed-trade net column.

## Try next
- **Widen the targets and cut the risk-to-reward gap.** Raise target_atr to 3.0 so the underlying target clears the option spread plus charges. Tighten stop_atr to 1.0.
- **Add a premium-aware exit filter.** Do not take a "target" exit unless the option bid is at least entry + Rs 100/lot equivalent of charges. Cap the premium per trade at Rs 25,000 so that ADANIENT-size tickets (Rs 37k) are skipped and capital stays free for more names.
- **Add a time stop and fix the data bugs.** Close any position not at +1% premium after 45 minutes (this would have cut JSWSTEEL around 13:53). Separately, fix the NFO token mapping and the day-summary aggregation before trusting the next day's stats.

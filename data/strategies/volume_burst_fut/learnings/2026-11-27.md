# Entry fills drifted 0.23-0.45% from signal price, eating most of the stop room, and all 4 trades stopped out for Rs -5775

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy took 4 single-lot futures trades (NTPC long, TATASTEEL short, INFY short, TATASTEEL long), and every one hit its stop. The day closed at Rs -5775 net, against a Rs 1000 target on a Rs 100000 wallet. The footer saying "4 won, net Rs 1817, best 1162, worst 166" contradicts the log line by line, and the "DAY TARGET reached at 09:47: net 1063" banner fired while NTPC was only just opened. Both reporting paths look broken and should not be trusted. 25 of the day's 29 signals were skipped: 20 for margin above free cash, 4 for wrong contract tokens and 1 for having no futures contract.

## Learnings
- **Fills were far from the signal price on every trade.**
  - NTPC 09:47 filled at 324.90 against a 323.45 signal (+0.45%).
  - TATASTEEL 11:14 filled at 165.91 against 165.37 (+0.33%).
  - INFY 12:22 filled at 1160.00 against 1155.30 (+0.41%).
  - TATASTEEL 14:21 filled at 165.80 against 165.42 (+0.23%).
  - The distance from fill to stop was only 0.13-0.54% (0.28, 1.70 and 0.22 points on the last three), so the drift was as large as the risk budget. Each trade started already most of the way to its stop.
- **On both shorts, price had already reversed by fill time.** TATASTEEL 11:14 and INFY 12:22 sold 0.54 and 4.70 points above the signal price. The bar that triggered the signal had already retraced against the trade before entry. Both trades stopped within 7-24 minutes (INFY lasted 6 minutes).
- **Charges are a large share of each loss.** Gross losses were about 2625, 770, 680 and 605. Net losses were 2910, 1039, 953 and 873. That is roughly Rs 270-285 per round trip, or about 27% of the day target per trade. With stops this tight, a lot sizes risk mostly in charges plus slippage.
- **One lot uses almost the whole wallet, so the strategy trades strictly one position at a time.** While NTPC was open (09:47-10:30), five signals were skipped with only Rs 2530 free: LT, BEL, INFY, SBIN and COALINDIA. Twenty contracts need more than Rs 100000 margin per lot and can never trade at this wallet size, and NTPC at 13:46 missed by just Rs 1502.
- **The instrument map is broken for at least four names.** ITC, ULTRACEMCO, POWERGRID (twice) and BAJFINANCE asked for an NFO token but got the NSE equity token back. BANKBEES has no futures contract and should not be in this universe.

## Try next
- **Add a slippage filter.** Reject the entry if the fill price differs from the signal price by more than 0.15%, or by more than one third of the planned stop distance. Under this rule all 4 trades today would have been rejected.
- **Raise vol_x to 4.0 and body_atr to 1.5.** Today's bursts did not follow through: two shorts reversed before fill, and no trade got near its target. Leave target_x unchanged, since nothing tested it. Four trades is a thin sample, so do not set invert=1 on this evidence.
- **Fix the plumbing before judging the edge.**
  - Correct the NFO token lookup for ITC, ULTRACEMCO, POWERGRID and BAJFINANCE.
  - Pre-filter the universe to contracts whose 1-lot margin is at most 95% of free capital.
  - Drop BANKBEES.
  - Fix the day-target check and the day-summary aggregation, which reported +1063 and +1817 on a day that lost Rs 5775.

# Rs 1 lakh can hold one futures lot, so one NTPC fill tied up 98% of margin and blocked 13 later signals

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy took a single trade. It bought 1 lot (1500) of NTPC29DEC26F at 13:25:11, fill 322.65, on a signal at 320.65. It sold flat at 14:50:22 at 323.25 for a logged net of Rs 615.16. The day summary says net Rs 323 (1 trade, 1 win), short of the Rs 1000 target, and the close note says "day so far -344". These three figures do not reconcile. The log gives no broader market data, so I cannot judge the day's market beyond these fills. Of 15 skipped signals, 11 were margin rejections, 2 were contract-lookup failures and 1 had no futures contract.

## Learnings
- **Margin is the binding constraint, not the signal.**
  - Free margin was only Rs 99041 at 13:14, so TITAN (163474) and ITC (106364) were skipped before any trade.
  - After NTPC took Rs 96795, free margin fell to Rs 2246. Every later signal was dead on arrival: HINDUNILVR, ITC, ADANIENT, TATASTEEL, TECHM x2, INFY, ADANIPORTS, POWERGRID and BEL.
  - In practice the strategy runs first-affordable-signal-wins, not best-signal-wins.
- **Only 3 names on today's list fit the wallet even when fully free:** NTPC (96795), TATASTEEL (90030) and INFY (94272). Most of the universe is untradeable at 1 lot on Rs 1 lakh.
- **The entry fill was Rs 2.00 above the signal price on NTPC (322.65 vs 320.65).** At 1500 qty that is about Rs 3000 of edge lost before the trade started, larger than the whole day target. The trade made only 0.60/share from fill to exit. Measured from the signal price it would have been 2.60/share. Either the signal fires on a stale bar close or the fill model is too pessimistic.
- **NTPC had no stop or target logged.** The stop and target columns are blank, so a position with about Rs 4.8 lakh notional was held for 85 minutes with no defined risk. It exited only on the 14:50 time-flat. The win came from drift, not from the target_x exit.
- **There are plumbing bugs.**
  - TCS29DEC26F (asked NFO 59132, got NSE 11536 TCS-EQ) and HDFCBANK29DEC26F (asked NFO 58979, got NSE 1333 HDFCBANK-EQ) resolved to equity tokens. The symbol-to-token map is wrong for these contracts.
  - BANKBEES is an ETF with no futures contract and should not be in this strategy's universe.
- **The PnL ledger is inconsistent.** The trade net is 615.16, the day net is 323, and the prior "day so far" is -344, with Rs 959 already missing from free margin at 13:14. Charges or a hidden prior debit are being booked somewhere the log does not show. This day's result cannot be trusted until that is fixed.

## Try next
- **Raise the bar so the one margin slot goes to stronger bursts.**
  - Set vol_x 4.0 and body_atr 1.5. This spends the single lot on a stronger burst instead of the first marginal one.
  - Add a pre-filter that drops any symbol whose 1-lot margin exceeds 95% of free margin, so unaffordable names are not even evaluated.
- **Make every entry carry real exits.** Every open must log a stop (e.g. 1.0 x ATR) and a target with target_x 1.5. Reject the entry if the fill is more than 0.3% from the signal price; NTPC's 0.62% gap would have been rejected.
- **Fix the plumbing before judging edge.**
  - Correct the NFO token mapping for TCS and HDFCBANK.
  - Remove ETFs like BANKBEES from the fut universe.
  - Reconcile the 615 vs 323 vs -344 PnL figures.
  - Keep enabled=1, side=0..2 unchanged and invert=0. One trade is not evidence for changing direction.

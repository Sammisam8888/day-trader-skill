## Learnings
- **The first setups were the only clean ones.** ICICIBANK P1610 (10:18 to 10:35, +1573) and ITC P305 (+394) both hit target.
- **Entries from 10:41 to 11:34 were the damage window.** HINDALCO, TRENT, TATASTEEL, TITAN, POWERGRID, NTPC P and the TATASTEEL re-entry netted Rs -4685. This happened after the target was already banked.
- **Correlated longs stopped together.** TATASTEEL C165 and HINDALCO C920 (both metals calls) stopped in the same second at 11:19 for -1378 combined. TITAN C4700 and POWERGRID C267.5, opened together at 11:10, both stopped for -2840. Up to four positions ran at once, so one false sweep in a chop became several losses.
- **Option payoff was uneven.** Average win was about Rs 1014 against an average loss of about Rs 838, with only 36% winners.
- **Exit labels do not match premium outcomes.** NTPC P322.5 shows "target" yet exited at 7.90, its entry price, for -40 after charges. The underlying likely reached target_r while the premium did not move (delta and spread). TRENT shows "stop" but made +177.
- **Re-entries and flips were mixed.**
  - HINDALCO re-entered at 13:57 after its 11:19 stop and won +2314.
  - TATASTEEL re-entered at 11:34 after its 11:19 stop and lost again (-605 at the 14:50 flat).
  - NTPC was traded as a put, then a call: -40 and -862.
- **Late entries carried into the flat lost.** TCS C2600 (opened 13:33) closed at -1092 and TATASTEEL at -605 at 14:50. HINDUNILVR (opened 12:46) was the only position that gained into the flat, at +611.
- **Infrastructure bug.** Four signals were skipped because NFO lookups returned the NSE equity token: POWERGRID 13:33, NTPC 13:46 and 14:27, SBIN 14:14. Today's skipped trades are unknown, so the results do not reflect all signals.

## Try next
- **Filter:** stop opening new positions once day net reaches the target, or after giving back 50% of the peak. Today that would have closed the day near +1147 to +2144 instead of -2474.
- **Filter:** cap open positions at 2, allow only one position per sector, and allow no same-day re-entry on a symbol after a stop. This would have avoided the 11:19 metals pair and the TATASTEEL repeat, at the cost of the HINDALCO re-entry win.
- **Knob changes:** raise reclaim_bars from the current value to 3 to cut fast false reclaims in chop. Set target_r to at least 2.0 so a delta of about 0.5 still clears charges on the premium. Fix the NFO token resolution and the day-summary calculation before trusting any stats.

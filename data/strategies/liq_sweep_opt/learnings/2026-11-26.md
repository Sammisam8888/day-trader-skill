# Reported "7 won, net Rs 185" and the 13:42 day-target banner are both false: the log shows 3 wins, 4 losses, and a late recovery to +410

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The log starts mid-session at 13:04. The strategy was already at -1061 before then. Two quick premium stops (BEL C405 -767, POWERGRID C267.5 -806) dragged it to a low of -2634 at 13:57. Three winners then pulled it back to a closing running total of +410: HINDALCO C920 +1650 (target), INFY C1160 +1178 (target) and SBIN P1150 +1468 (14:50 flat). Of the 7 closes in the log, 3 won and 4 lost.

The summary line ("7 won, net 185, best 54, worst 8") does not match the log. The "DAY TARGET reached at 13:42: net 1017" banner fired while the running total was between -1828 and -2634. The P&L/target accounting is broken and cannot be trusted for gating.

## Learnings
- **Accounting bug:** the 13:42 day-target event cannot be reconciled with the running "day so far" column. The banner reported +1017 while realized P&L was around -1828. It may be counting unrealized premium or a different ledger. Any logic that relaxes after the target would have acted on a phantom number.
- **Tight stops versus wider targets:**
  - Both stop-outs were tiny premium moves:
    - BEL 13.00 to 12.50 (-3.8%)
    - POWERGRID 7.90 to 7.50 (-5.1%)
  - Large lot sizes (1425 and 1900) turned these into about Rs 770-810 each.
  - Winners needed +6% to +8% in premium (INFY 50.70 to 53.80, HINDALCO 30.25 to 32.70).
  - With roughly 4% stops against 7% targets, the 4/7 loss rate leaves almost no edge.
- **Re-entry right after a target was a mistake:** the strategy re-bought HINDALCO C920 at 33.00 in the same second (14:20:16) it sold at 32.70 on target. It paid up 0.30 on the spread and was force-flattened at 14:50 for -381. With only 30 minutes left, the trade had no realistic room to reach a fresh target.
- **The 14:50 flat split both ways:** SBIN P1150 (entered 13:11) closed +1468, while KOTAKBANK P510 closed -871. The early-afternoon entry had time to work. The time stop decided the other two.
- **Instrument resolution bug:** 4 signals were lost to "asked for NFO, got NSE -EQ" lookups (HINDUNILVR 13:39, BHARTIARTL 13:45, JSWSTEEL 13:58, NTPC 14:20). NIFTYBEES (14:09) has no options and should not be in this strategy's universe.
- **Capital crowding:**
  - About 7 skips were "1 lot costs Rs X", including cheap ones such as LT Rs 14052 and SUNPHARMA Rs 14332.
  - Those were cheaper than trades that were taken, so the binding constraint was capital already tied up in 4-5 open positions, not contract price.
  - Signal selection is effectively first-come, not best-setup.

## Try next
- **Entry-time filter:** no new entries after 14:10 (keeps INFY at 14:03, drops HINDALCO at 14:20). Also no re-entry on the same contract within 15 minutes of a target exit.
- **target_r:** test 1.5 against the current value on this universe. Two of three winners came from target hits, but HINDALCO-type moves often stalled. A nearer target should convert more trades before the 14:50 flat while the stop distance stays unchanged.
- **Fixes that are not knobs:**
  - Repair the NFO token lookup so it never falls back to the -EQ symbol.
  - Remove NIFTYBEES from the universe.
  - Reconcile the day-target and summary calculations to realized net P&L before trusting any target-based behaviour.

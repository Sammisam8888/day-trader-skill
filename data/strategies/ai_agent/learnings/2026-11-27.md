# Chasing whichever model led the last hour, with stops wider than targets, cost more than any one model's edge

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The tape ground lower all day, and fades of failed moves mostly worked: by afternoon vwap_revert_inv was 39/44 and squeeze_break short was 4/4 in the last hour. The agent ended at about Rs -2985 net, against a Rs +1000 target. The supplied day summary (27/27 won, net Rs 81110, best 14020, worst 165) contradicts the log and is wrong. The log shows several losers, and 14020 is a MARUTI price, not a P&L. The log is also truncated between 10:31 and 13:16, so about Rs -1860 of the slide, from -1531 at 10:27 to roughly -3390 by 13:24, cannot be attributed to specific trades.

## Learnings
- Morning model churn locked in losses. The agent changed its model mix at 09:57, 10:12 and 10:27 (four decisions in 45 minutes). It dropped vwap_revert_inv at 10:27, when that model was -2000 on the day, and closed ADANIENT (-117) and JSWSTEEL (-72) at a loss. By 13:16 it was back on the same model at x1.25, calling it the "cleanest edge all day" at 31/36. The agent bought high and sold low on a model, using one-hour windows as evidence.
- vwap_revert_inv's payoff is heavily lopsided, so its losses dwarf its wins:
  - ULTRACEMCO 09:55 had a 33-point stop against a 13-point target and lost -621.
  - ITC 09:45 had a 1.24 stop against a 0.57 target and won only +179.
  - BANKBEES (-589) and HINDUNILVR (-556) each erased three or four typical winners.
  - Its high win rate (31/36) is what this structure needs to break even, not evidence of an edge.
- Stop fills slipped far past the planned stops:
  - RELIANCE 14:24 (squeeze_break): stop 1380.99, filled 1382.95, loss -739 versus about -330 planned.
  - ULTRACEMCO 09:58: stop 12795.13, filled 12798.05.
- The RELIANCE short alone undid most of the afternoon recovery. The day had climbed from -3394 at 13:24 to -2236 at 14:29.
- The late squeeze_break entry was a poor trade. RELIANCE was opened at 14:24, with about 11 minutes of entries left and 26 minutes to the 14:50 flat. It needed a 3.9-point move to hit target and had only 2 points of room to the stop.
- Tiny positions only paid charges:
  - MARUTI 1 share (+30)
  - BAJFINANCE 2 shares (+4)
  - POWERGRID 3 shares
  - ONGC 2 shares (+0.24)
  - ONGC 40 shares twice (+15, -3.5)
- The rsi2 x0.75 trades in the afternoon were the clean part of the day:
  - SUNPHARMA 13:24 +546
  - RELIANCE 13:34 +233
  - TITAN 14:24 +29
  - HDFCBANK 14:04 +38
  - MARUTI 14:14 +22

## Try next
- **Model switching:** require a model to be in or out for at least 2 consecutive decisions (30 min) before changing it, and do not close a dropped model's open positions at a loss. Leave them to their own stops and targets, as the 09:57 decision did.
- **vwap_revert_inv risk:** cap it at x0.5 whenever the signal's stop distance is more than 2x its target distance (for example ULTRACEMCO 33 vs 13, ITC 1.24 vs 0.57). Also skip any follow whose computed quantity is worth under about Rs 20000, such as 1-3 share trades or ONGC at 2 shares.
- **Late entries:** stop new squeeze_break entries after 14:15 (set its multiplier to 0 at the 14:12 decision). Keep rsi2 at x0.75, and only raise it to x1 if it again posts 5 or more wins in the last hour with a best win at least 1.5x its worst loss.

# Calls lost and puts won; ADANIENT ran 19% against us with no stop and erased the day

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy opened 5 option positions and ended the day at **net Rs -4346** after charges: 2 winners and 3 losers. Both puts made money: AXISBANK +1340 and BHARTIARTL +4065. All three calls lost: ADANIENT -7068, NTPC -1687 and POWERGRID -996. The pattern points to a weak afternoon for these names, though the log cannot confirm that.

Two system problems also showed up:
- **The summary line is wrong.** It reports "5 won, net Rs 198, best 96, worst 7", but the closes in the log add up to -4345.51 with 2 wins.
- **The day-target banner fired too early.** At 09:46 it claimed "net 1770" before any trade had closed, so it must be counting open positions at their marked price.

## Learnings
- **ADANIENT 09:46 call caused 163% of the day's loss.** It cost Rs 36585.60 (37% of the wallet), had no stop in the log, and was held from 118.40 to 95.80 until the 14:50 flatten, losing Rs 7068. NTPC was stopped out at -14% on premium, so some trades do get a stop. ADANIENT never hit one.
- **The premium cap is applied inconsistently.** ADANIPORTS was skipped at 09:46 because one lot cost Rs 27811. ADANIENT went through in the same minute at Rs 36586. Whatever the cap is, the largest position bypassed it.
- **The two late call entries were weaker than the 09:46 entries.** POWERGRID (10:31, -996) and NTPC (10:32, -1687) both lost. By then the opening drive was an hour old, and a "retrace" signal at that point is mostly noise. The 09:46 puts on AXISBANK and BHARTIARTL hit target within 11 and 48 minutes.
- **Target exits were fast and clean.** AXISBANK went 34.90 to 37.15 in 11 minutes, and BHARTIARTL went 42.30 to 51.00 in 48 minutes. The edge today came from quick exits at target, not from holding.
- **Two signals were lost to a quote-mapping bug.** SBIN at 09:55 and JSWSTEEL at 10:31 were skipped because the system asked for the NFO option token and got the NSE cash equity quote back. That is a symbol-resolution bug, not a strategy decision.
- **The day-target logic used unrealized P&L.** It declared "target reached" at 09:46 before any trade closed. If any logic relies on that flag (for example, size-down after target), it would be acting on fake numbers.

## Try next
- **Add a hard premium stop on every option position, for example -15% of the entry premium.** Today that would have held ADANIENT's loss to roughly Rs 5500 instead of Rs 7068. Also cap premium per trade at Rs 25000, and skip the trade if one lot costs more than that.
- **Stop taking new entries after 10:15.** Opening-drive fades placed an hour later lost on both POWERGRID and NTPC. Keep move_pct and retrace unchanged; one day is not enough evidence to retune them.
- **Fix the NFO token lookup and the reporting.**
  - Token lookup: SBIN29DEC26C1140 and JSWSTEEL29DEC26C1320 resolved to NSE equity quotes.
  - Day summary: it should report 2W/3L and Rs -4346, not 5W and Rs 198.
  - Day target: it should count realized P&L only.

  Until these are fixed, the daily numbers cannot be trusted for tuning.

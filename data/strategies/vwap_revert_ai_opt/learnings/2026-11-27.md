# Fading ADANIPORTS three times on the long side cost Rs 5166 on a day only the puts worked

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
From the underlying prices in the log, ADANIPORTS fell steadily (1916.70 at 09:39, 1910.00 at 09:40, 1903.30 at 10:39). The ICICIBANK and HDFCBANK SELL fades both paid, so the tape looked weak rather than mean-reverting. The strategy closed 8 trades: 3 won and 5 lost, for a net of Rs -5425 after charges. Puts went 2/2 for +1897; calls went 1/6 for -7321.

The summary line ("8 won, net Rs 315, best 59, worst 11") and the "DAY TARGET reached at 09:39: net 1408" banner are both wrong. No trade had closed by 09:39:03, and the closes in the log sum to -5424.51.

## Learnings
- **ADANIPORTS re-entries were the main loss.** The strategy re-bought calls three times into a falling stock:
  - 09:39 C1920 at 60.15, stopped in 32s for -601.
  - 09:40 C1920 at 56.70, re-entered 31s after that stop at a lower underlying, -2541.
  - 10:39 C1900 at 61.65, rolled down a strike on another BUY, -2025.
  - Total: -5166 on one name in one direction. Each new BUY signal came from a lower VWAP stretch, which points to a trend, not a reversion.
- **The SELL side worked.** ICICIBANK P1610 (09:39 to 10:25, +1296) and HDFCBANK P860 (10:09 to 11:20, +601) both hit target. On this day the downside fades lined up with the drift.
- **AXISBANK C1390 lost -2478 in 11 minutes.** It opened at 11:20:15, the same second JSWSTEEL C1320 was stopped (-510). Two long-call failures within a minute was a live warning that the strategy then reopened into.
- **Wins were smaller than losses.** Targets captured about +5.9% (ICICI) and +4.3% (HDFC) of premium. Stops gave up about -9.2% (ADANI 2nd), -8.8% (AXIS) and -6.7% (ADANI 3rd). Even a 50% hit rate loses money with that ratio.
- **COALINDIA C385 was a winner that never hit target.** It made +832 only through the 14:50 flat. It shows that slow, low-premium names revert, while high-beta names like ADANIPORTS and AXISBANK on a trending day do not.
- **Recurring skips, not losses but noise:**
  - NIFTYBEES and BANKBEES have no options and were skipped 5 times.
  - ADANIENT had a quote-mapping bug at 10:25 (NFO token 68763 resolved to NSE ADANIENT-EQ).
  - The stop and target columns are blank on every row, so the exit levels can't be audited from the log.

## Try next
- **Add a cooldown filter.** After a stop on an underlying, block new entries in the same direction on that underlying for the rest of the day, or at least 60 minutes. On today's log this would have skipped the 09:40 and 10:39 ADANIPORTS entries, saving Rs 4565.
- **Raise band_sd from the current value to 2.5,** so a fade needs a deeper stretch. Then check whether stop_sd should drop to about 1.0 to tighten the premium loss per stop and bring the loss/win ratio closer to 1.
- **Restrict the side knob to the SELL-signal/puts-only value when the index trades below VWAP at entry time,** or add that as an entry filter on long calls. Separately, drop the ETFs from the universe and fix the ADANIENT token mapping and the day-summary/target calculation.

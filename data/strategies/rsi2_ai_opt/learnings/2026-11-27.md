# Two stale option holds wiped out 3,626 at the 14:50 flat, and the day's stats are wrong

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy fired mixed signals. It bought puts on BHARTIARTL, SUNPHARMA, KOTAKBANK, SBIN, ICICIBANK, RELIANCE, BAJFINANCE and MARUTI, and calls on BEL, INFY, HINDUNILVR and HDFCBANK. Each position was one lot, with Rs 16-25k of premium. From the log there were 15 closes: 9 winners and 6 losers, for a net of Rs +1,653 after charges. The best trade was SUNPHARMA at +2,988 and the worst was HINDUNILVR at -1,862.

The supplied day numbers do not match the log. They claim 15/15 wins, a net of 899, a best of 406 and a worst of 11. The "DAY TARGET reached at 12:17: net 1131" banner also disagrees with "day so far 2068" on the row just before it. Both look like reporting bugs and should not be trusted for tuning.

## Learnings
- **One trade carried the day.** SUNPHARMA P1920 (11:29 -> 13:30) went from 47.25 to 55.95, an 18% premium move, for +2,988. Without it the day nets about -1,336.
- **The real damage came from positions that never hit stop or target.** KOTAKBANK P500 was held from 11:34 to 14:50 and fell from 11.80 to 10.95, losing 1,764. HINDUNILVR C2140 was held from 12:44 to 14:50 and fell from 54.45 to 48.40, an 11% premium loss, for -1,862. The ATR stop on the underlying was too wide to trigger. Meanwhile, theta and drift bled the premium for 2-3 hours.
- **Wins and losses are lopsided.** The average win was about 883 and the average loss about 1,049. Several "target" wins were only 0.25-0.50 of premium:
  - HDFCBANK at 14:13: +271
  - HDFCBANK at 14:26: +238
  - BEL at 12:46: +297
  - INFY at 13:39: +345

  The SBIN stop at 12:30 alone (-1,523) erased about 5 of those.
- **Two "target" exits lost money.** RELIANCE P1380 at 14:22 closed 34.40 -> 34.00 for -252, and MARUTI P13800 at 14:24 closed 408.85 -> 406.45 for -180. The underlying hit its RSI2 target, but the option premium did not follow. This happens because the target is measured on the stock, while the P&L is on the option.
- **Trading past the day target added nothing.** The ten trades opened after the 12:16 target hit netted about -116. All of the day's net came from positions opened before noon.
- **Data and plumbing problems:**
  - 14:34 RELIANCE: the quote lookup for an NFO token returned the NSE equity instrument instead of the option. This is a contract-mapping bug that could cause a mispriced fill.
  - BANKBEES and NIFTYBEES produced repeated signals even though they have no options. These should be filtered out before signal generation.
  - TATASTEEL, ADANIENT and TITAN were skipped because one lot was too expensive.

## Try next
- **Tighten the stop.** Lower stop_atr to 1.5 and add a premium-based guard: exit if the premium falls 8% below entry, or if a position has made no progress after 60 minutes. This targets the KOTAKBANK and HINDUNILVR type of loss.
- **Widen the target.** Raise target_atr to at least 2.5 and require the option premium to be above entry plus charges before a "target" exit fires. This removes the sub-Rs-300 wins and the losing "targets" on RELIANCE and MARUTI.
- **Add a time filter.** Allow no new entries after 13:45, and consider stopping new entries once the day target is hit. Fix the day-stats summary, the target banner and the NFO token mapping before using today's numbers for any knob tuning.

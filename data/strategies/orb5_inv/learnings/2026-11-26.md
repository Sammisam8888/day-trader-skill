# Hit the Rs 1000 day target by 10:31, peaked at +1567, then gave it all back to finish -189

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
Many opening-range breakouts failed early, which suits this inverted copy. 11 of the 16 positions opened by 09:42 hit target, and the day reached +1052 at 10:31 (POWERGRID) and peaked at +1567 at 10:53 (BHARTIARTL). After that it kept trading and holding. Two full stops (NTPC -531, RELIANCE -524) and seven 14:50 flat exits (-834 combined) took the day to -189 net after charges, with 13 wins and 9 losses out of 22 closed trades.

The supplied "Day numbers" line (22/22 won, Rs 56521 net, best 14064, worst 160) does not match the log and looks corrupted. Best 14064 and worst 160 are the MARUTI and TATASTEEL exit prices, not P&L. From the log: best trade +237.46 (TATASTEEL 09:34), worst -530.95 (NTPC 11:28).

## Learnings
- **The day target was not enforced.** "Day so far" crossed 1000 at 10:31:11 (POWERGRID), but RELIANCE had already opened at 10:31:00, ITC opened at 11:44, and 9 positions stayed open. Everything after the +1567 peak was a giveback of about 1756.
- **The edge was in the first 12 minutes only.**
  - Entries from 09:30 to 09:42 netted about +810.
  - Entries from 09:52 onward netted about -999: JSWSTEEL +123, BEL +9, NTPC -531, RELIANCE -524, ITC -77.
  - Once the early fades were done, later "breakouts" (NTPC short 09:53, RELIANCE short 10:31) were real moves that ran through the inverted stop.
- **The payoff is badly asymmetric.**
  - NTPC risked 2.81/sh to make 0.89/sh, and RELIANCE risked 23.85 to make 6.50.
  - Typical wins were Rs 100-240, while one stop cost about Rs 525, so a single stop erases 3-5 wins. RELIANCE also slipped 1.25/sh past its stop (1399.55 vs 1398.3).
- **Unfilled early trades bled slowly instead of resolving.**
  - Six positions opened at 09:30-09:37 never reached target and were dumped at 14:50 for -757 total: TITAN -224, HDFCBANK -172, TRENT -170, COALINDIA -122, SUNPHARMA -62, MARUTI -8.
  - If a fade has not worked within roughly an hour, it is not going to work.
- **Some positions are too small to matter.** BEL (7 qty) netted +9.44 and NIFTYBEES +10.45. Charges take a large share of the gross on these, and they add exposure without adding P&L.

## Try next
- **Add a day-target lock filter:** when day P&L is at or above Rs 1000, block new entries and flatten all open positions. Today that would have locked in about +1052 at 10:31 instead of finishing -189.
- **Add an entry cutoff and a time stop:** no new entries after 09:45, and exit any position that has not hit target by 11:00. Both would have avoided NTPC, RELIANCE and ITC and cut the drift on TITAN, HDFCBANK, TRENT and COALINDIA.
- **Set stop_frac to 0.5** (from the current wide stop) to roughly halve the loss per stopped trade toward Rs 260. Keep target_x unchanged so the fast early fades still pay. Add a minimum position-value filter (for example, skip if qty x price is under Rs 10000) to drop BEL-sized trades.

# Hitting the Rs 1000 target at 10:31 and then trading on turned +1251 into -1701

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The broad market faded through the session; NIFTYBEES slid from 294.01 at 10:31 to 292.38 by the 14:50 flatten, about -0.55%. orb5_inv opened 22 trades, 15 long and 7 short, and won only 9 of 22. Net was Rs -1701 after charges; the best trade was TECHM at +307 and the worst was AXISBANK at -523. The day target was reached at 10:31 (+1019, NTPC) and P&L peaked at +1251 at 11:20 (HDFCBANK). From there one stop-out and the 14:50 forced close of 11 open positions erased the gain and more.

Note: the summary line ("22 won, net 38055, best 12538, worst 223") does not match the trade log. It looks like it was built from price columns, not net Rs: 12538 is the ULTRACEMCO exit price. The figures here come from the log itself.

## Learnings
- **The target was not a stop.** The engine opened ULTRACEMCO (10:31), NIFTYBEES (10:31), BHARTIARTL (10:39), BAJFINANCE (10:53), ADANIENT (11:22) and SUNPHARMA (12:30) after or right around the target fill.
  - Every one of these lost.
  - Together with the morning longs still open, they cost Rs -1,410 of the day's damage. ULTRACEMCO alone lost -411 at 12:43.
- **The payoff is lopsided.** Targets sit at roughly 0.5-0.6x the stop distance. RELIANCE is an example: target +10.35, stop -18.05.
  - Winners clustered at Rs 107-307.
  - The AXISBANK stop (11:23, -523) and ULTRACEMCO stop (12:43, -411) each wiped out three or four winners.
  - At these ratios the strategy needs a win rate above about 65% to break even, and it got 41%.
- **Inverted shorts worked; inverted longs failed.** These are the original ORB's failed breakdowns.
  - The 7 shorts netted about +450. SBIN, ICICIBANK, BANKBEES, NTPC and HDFCBANK all hit target by 11:20.
  - The 15 longs netted about -2,151, because the market drifted lower all afternoon.
  - Only TECHM, HINDUNILVR, COALINDIA and ONGC hit target. The other 11 longs were stopped out or bled to the 14:50 flatten.
- **Wide stops left positions open into the flatten.** None of the 11 positions closed at 14:50 reached its stop (KOTAKBANK 501.5 vs 495.85, ITC 304.09 vs 303.0, RELIANCE 1375.65 vs 1365.0). Each lost Rs 32-374 anyway, because the stop was too far away to ever be hit or to cut the trade early.
- **Index exposure was conflicting.** The strategy was short BANKBEES (09:39) and long NIFTYBEES (10:31), alongside a long book in bank names (AXISBANK, KOTAKBANK). The net direction was essentially unmanaged.

## Try next
- **Filter: lock in the day target.** Once day P&L reaches Rs 1000, open no new trades and flatten all open positions. Today that would have banked about +1019 at 10:31 instead of -1701.
- **stop_frac = 0.5 (down from the current effective ~1.0).** This brings the stop close to the target distance, so stop losses like AXISBANK's -523 shrink to about -260, and stale positions get cut before 14:50.
- **Filter: no new entries after 10:30, measured as minutes past the opening range.** All six entries after 10:30 lost. If more days confirm the long-side weakness, test side = short-only for the inverted copy. That idea rests on one down-drifting day, so treat it as a hypothesis, not a rule.

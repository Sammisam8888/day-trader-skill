# Rs 270 round-trip charges plus a broken exit made the day lose Rs 2,323, not win Rs 2,493

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy took 3 trades on stock futures and lost on 2 of them, ending the day at Rs -2,323 net (-1,913.73, +145.34, -554.26). The "Day numbers" summary (3 won, +2,493, best 1166, worst 165) contradicts the log. It looks like absolute values or prices are being read as P&L, so that report is wrong. One lot used 90-93% of the Rs 100,000 wallet, so only one position could be open at a time. About 30 other signals were skipped, either for lack of margin or because the quote lookup failed.

## Learnings
- **Charges swallow small moves.** Every round trip cost about Rs 270:
  - INFY at 11:00: gross -1,640, net -1,914.
  - TATASTEEL at 13:22: gross +412, net +145.
  - INFY at 14:40: gross -280, net -554.
  
  A target worth only 0.15 points on TATASTEEL (2,750 qty) leaves almost nothing after charges.
- **The 14:29 INFY SELL closed as "target" at a loss.** It was bought back at 1165.70 against a 1165.00 entry. A short cannot hit its target above entry. The likely cause is that stop and target levels are computed on the cash price (signal at 1162.20) but checked against the futures price, or the other way round.
- **The spot-to-futures gap distorts levels.** The 10:54 INFY BUY signalled at 1158.70 on cash but filled at 1166.50 on futures, 7.8 points higher. The stop was hit only 6 minutes later at 1162.40, just 4.1 points below the fill. Levels derived from the cash price sit too close to, or on the wrong side of, the futures fill.
- **The quote lookup is unreliable.** NFO tokens came back as NSE equity quotes for ADANIPORTS, WIPRO, KOTAKBANK, SBIN, INFY, RELIANCE, ADANIENT and TITAN. At other times the same contracts (SBIN, RELIANCE) did get margin quotes. This is a data bug, not a market condition.
- **Most of the universe is untradeable at this wallet size.** Eleven names need more than Rs 100,000 per lot (HCLTECH, TECHM, BAJFINANCE, ICICIBANK and others). BANKBEES and NIFTYBEES have no futures at all. These names only generate skip noise.

## Try next
- Set target_atr to 3.0 and stop_atr to 2.0. The expected gross move per lot should then be at least 3x the roughly Rs 270 round-trip charge. Also add a filter that skips any entry where target distance x qty is under Rs 800.
- Compute stop and target from the actual futures fill price, offset by the observed basis, rather than from the cash signal price. Add a check that rejects a SELL whose target is at or above entry, which would have stopped the 14:29 INFY exit.
- Restrict the universe to names with lot margin under Rs 95,000 and with an existing futures contract (drop the BEES ETFs). Fix the NFO token resolution, and fix the day summary so it reads net P&L from the trade log.

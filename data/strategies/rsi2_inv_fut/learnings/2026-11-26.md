# One unstopped INFY short lost 5x what the NTPC win made, and the day summary wrongly reports a profit

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy traded only after 13:53. Two lots were affordable on the Rs 100000 wallet: NTPC and INFY, both inverted SELL signals. NTPC (13:53 to 14:02) made Rs 541 net. INFY (14:17 to 14:50) lost Rs 2917 net and was closed by the 14:50 flat rule. **The log's own running total ends at Rs -2375.** The "Day numbers" line (2 won, net Rs 1503, best 1182, worst 321) does not match the trades and looks like it is reading exit prices as P&L. Twenty-three other signals were skipped.

## Learnings
- **The losing trade had no stop.** The stop and target columns are blank for both opens.
  - INFY at 14:17 ran 6.60 points against the short (1175.00 to 1181.60) for 33 minutes. Nothing cut it until the 14:50 flat rule.
  - The NTPC win at 14:02 was only 0.55 points, which is about 0.17% of price.
  - With no stop, one loss erased the equivalent of five wins. Either stops are not being attached in fut mode, or the log does not record them. Either way it needs verifying.
- **Signals use spot prices but fills use futures prices, and the basis is large.**
  - NTPC: signal 320.20, fill 322.00 (+1.80).
  - INFY: signal 1166.90, fill 1175.00 (+8.10, about 0.7%).
  - If stop and target levels are computed from spot ATR and spot price but compared against futures fills, the exits are misplaced. The NTPC "target" exit at 321.45 sits above the spot signal price, which supports this.
- **Lot size, not the signal, decided what got traded.** 17 signals were skipped for margin. These include JSWSTEEL, TECHM, RELIANCE, SBIN, KOTAKBANK and others, needing Rs 90k to 220k against Rs 100k free. One open lot ties up roughly 94-97% of the wallet. So the INFY signal at 13:57 was skipped while NTPC was open, and WIPRO, ITC, POWERGRID and TATASTEEL were skipped while INFY was open. The trades taken were simply whichever cheap contracts fired first.
- **There is a token-mapping bug.** Requests for NFO futures tokens for WIPRO (13:29), BAJFINANCE (13:47), ITC (13:48) and HDFCBANK (14:27) came back as NSE cash-equity quotes. The skip guard caught them, which is correct. But WIPRO and ITC resolved fine later (14:18, 14:23), so the lookup is intermittent rather than always broken. NIFTYBEES and BANKBEES have no futures and should be removed from the fut universe.
- **The late INFY entry had little room.** Entering at 14:17 with a 14:50 forced flat gives about 30 minutes. That is not enough for a 2-period RSI reversion to play out, so the trade was effectively closed at whatever price the clock landed on.
- **The day summary is unreliable.** "2 won, net Rs 1503" contradicts the trade rows, which show 1 win, 1 loss and Rs -2375 net. Any tuning based on the summary line would be wrong.

## Try next
- **Set stop_atr = 1.0** and confirm the stop is written to the open row and computed on the futures price, not spot. Set target_atr = 1.5 so wins are larger than the NTPC 0.55-point scrape. Do not trade fut mode until the stop column is populated.
- **Add a filter: no new entries after 14:15.** Also skip any signal where the futures-to-spot basis exceeds 0.3% of price, unless levels are rebased to the futures price.
- **Fix the data issues before tuning low/high.**
  - Repair the intermittent NFO token lookup.
  - Drop the ETFs from the fut universe.
  - Fix the day-summary P&L calculation.
  - Optionally restrict the universe to contracts with lot margin under Rs 50000, so the wallet can hold more than one position.

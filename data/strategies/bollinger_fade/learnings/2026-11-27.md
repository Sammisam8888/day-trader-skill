# Buying the lower band again and again in a falling tape turned a +1048 morning into -2625

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The summary line supplied with the log is wrong and does not match the log. It says 35 won, net +68386, best 13838, worst 165. The trade-by-trade log actually shows 35 closed trades with 12 wins and 23 losses, and a net of -2625.37 after charges. Best trade was SUNPHARMA short at 10:58 (+1047.50); worst was SUNPHARMA long at 13:07 (-640.78). The strategy hit its Rs 1000 day target at 10:58 on a single SUNPHARMA short. With no loss limit, it kept trading through a midday slide (roughly 11:00-13:30) in which almost every long fade was stopped, and reached -4012 at 14:16. A partial afternoon bounce (KOTAKBANK 13:40, SUNPHARMA 14:30, INFY 14:40) clawed back about 1400.

## Learnings
- **Re-entering the same stock on the same side after a stop was the main leak.**
  - ADANIENT was bought four times at successively lower prices (2657.95, 2653.05, 2642.05, 2632.25) for a combined -1088.30.
  - BAJFINANCE was bought four times (1093.05 down to 1084.25) for -689.05.
  - ADANIPORTS was bought three times for -259.15, and ONGC was shorted three times for -320.38.
  - In each case the band was walking in the trend direction, and the fade signal just fired again lower.
- **Wins came only where price actually mean-reverted.** SUNPHARMA short 10:49 (+1047.50) and KOTAKBANK long 10:59 to 13:40 (+912.36) both reverted. So did INFY's third short at 14:14 (+872.86), but only after the 13:49 and 14:09 shorts were stopped for -851.76. INFY netted +21 over three tries, so persistence only broke even.
- **Stops were tight relative to how the market moved.** Stops sat at about half the target distance, e.g. ICICIBANK risked 3.55 to make 7.52. Most losers were stopped within 2-20 minutes (ADANIENT 13:24 lasted under 1 minute; SUNPHARMA 13:04 lasted 3 minutes). With a 34% hit rate that payoff does not survive.
- **Stop fills slipped past the stop price on fast moves.** Examples: INFY 14:13 filled at 1164.95 against a stop of 1163.94, ONGC 13:45 at 224.15 against 224.0, and COALINDIA 12:42 at 387.41 against 387.28. The real cost per loser is larger than stop_sd implies.
- **Position sizing was erratic.**
  - JSWSTEEL (1 share), ITC (2-3 shares), TATASTEEL (2) and POWERGRID (1) made or lost under Rs 4 each. They add charges and noise for no signal.
  - BAJFINANCE jumped from 9 to 161 shares on its fourth consecutive long at 13:09. That one lost -588.73.
  - At 10:59 open notional was about 5x the 100000 wallet: ICICI, KOTAK, ULTRACEMCO and NIFTYBEES were opened in the same minute, all long. That is one correlated bet, and three of the four were stopped.
- **Continuing after the day target cost Rs 3673.** Equity went from +1048 to -2625 because there is no give-back rule.

## Try next
- **Filter:** after a stop-out, block that stock on that side for the rest of the session, or until price closes back above or below the band middle. Today this alone would have removed about 2000 of losses across ADANIENT, BAJFINANCE, ADANIPORTS and ONGC re-entries.
- **sd 2.5 and stop_sd 1.5** (from tighter settings): fewer, more stretched entries with room to breathe. Also skip any entry where the computed qty gives risk under Rs 50 (the 1-3 share trades).
- **Filter:** after the day target is hit, stop opening new trades once day net falls back below 50% of target (+500). Today that would have halted at about 11:03 and ended near +671. Also cap concurrent same-direction positions at 2.

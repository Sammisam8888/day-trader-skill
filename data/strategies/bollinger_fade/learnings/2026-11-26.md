# Lower-band BUY fades kept buying a falling market from 13:03 to 13:59; nine stop-outs turned Rs +1910 into Rs -638

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The header numbers (34 won, net Rs 58267, best 12714, worst 163) do not match the trade log and should not be trusted. The log shows 34 closes: 14 winners, 20 losers, net Rs 1736. Best was ADANIENT at +1329.26 and worst was ICICIBANK at -655.21.

The morning favoured shorts. Upper-band SELL fades in ADANIENT, BAJFINANCE, HDFCBANK, TRENT, BHARTIARTL and ONGC all reached target, and realized P&L peaked at Rs 1910 at 13:12. From 13:03 to 13:59 the market kept sliding. The strategy kept buying lower-band touches and gave back about Rs 3150, reaching Rs -638 at 14:04. A bounce after 14:00 recovered it to Rs 1736 at the 14:50 flat.

The "DAY TARGET reached at 11:25: net 1032" banner is also inconsistent: realized P&L at that time was Rs 106. It probably includes open marks, so it should not be treated as a realized milestone.

## Learnings
- The big wins were SELL fades with wide stops held for hours:
  - ADANIENT 10:51 -> 12:50, +1329
  - BAJFINANCE 10:55 -> 13:12, +997
  - HDFCBANK 10:50 -> 14:10, +962
  - Their stop distances were 4-17 points against targets of 8-46 points, so the trades had room to breathe.
- BUY fades during the 13:00-14:00 down-leg failed because the band was walking down, not mean-reverting. Each was stopped out:
  - ICICIBANK 13:15, -655
  - BANKBEES 13:14, -518
  - BANKBEES 13:51, -650
  - HCLTECH 12:35, -477
  - RELIANCE 13:15, -296
  - NIFTYBEES 13:35, -270
  - ULTRACEMCO twice, -117 and -159
- At the same time, the short-side positions (BAJFINANCE, HDFCBANK) were hitting targets. The market direction was readable and the BUY side ignored it.
- Stops on the BUY entries were too tight for the noise:
  - BANKBEES 13:14 had a stop 0.40 below entry on a Rs 661 ETF (0.06%) and was hit in 42 seconds.
  - NIFTYBEES 13:35 had a stop 0.38 below entry.
  - Each of these losses cost about the same as a full winning target.
- Re-entering the same symbol right after a stop compounded the damage:
  - ULTRACEMCO was re-bought at 13:11, 3 minutes after the 13:08 stop, and was stopped again at 13:14.
  - BANKBEES was entered three times in 42 minutes. Two entries lost Rs 1168 combined before the third (13:56) finally worked, +898 at flat.
- Many trades were qty 1-7 (HINDALCO, ICICIBANK 11:20, TECHM 12:55, KOTAKBANK, POWERGRID, BEL, INFY, HDFCBANK 14:12, ONGC 11:10 and 12:10, TATASTEEL, WIPRO, NTPC, HCLTECH 13:46). They moved P&L by single rupees and only add clutter and charges. The sizing logic seems to collapse when capital is tied up.

## Try next
- Raise stop_sd to about 1.5 (or at least widen it from today's value) so BUY stops are not under about 0.15% of price. Accept fewer, larger-risk trades rather than the 0.06% stops seen on BANKBEES and NIFTYBEES.
- Add a trend filter: skip BUY fades when NIFTYBEES is below its own lower band or making lower 15-min lows, and skip SELL fades in the mirror case. Today that would have blocked the 13:11-13:51 BUYs. Use the side knob to restrict to shorts on such days if a per-regime setting is not possible.
- Add a 30-minute cooldown on a symbol after a stop-out (this would have blocked ULTRACEMCO 13:11 and BANKBEES 13:51). Also add a minimum position value of about Rs 20000 so qty-1 entries are skipped.

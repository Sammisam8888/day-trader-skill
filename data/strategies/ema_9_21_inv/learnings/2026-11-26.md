# Targets at about half the stop distance meant a 14-13 win/loss day still lost Rs 2134

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy fully reversed its morning gains. From 11:40 to 12:03 five longs hit target (BHARTIARTL, KOTAKBANK, BEL, INFY, JSWSTEEL), and the day peaked at +780. It never reached the Rs 1000 day target. From 12:11 to 14:07 a run of long stops hit as stocks sagged into early afternoon (MARUTI short, TRENT, WIPRO, ONGC, ITC, RELIANCE, BAJFINANCE, BANKBEES, TECHM short). The 14:50 flatten then took four more small losses.

Per the log, the day had 27 closed trades: 14 won and 13 lost. Net was about Rs -2134, the best trade was +235.74 (TATASTEEL 14:18) and the worst was -593.41 (ITC 13:24).

The supplied summary line ("27 won, net Rs 57659, best 14080, worst 163") does not match the log. It appears to report exit prices (MARUTI 14080.05) instead of P&L, so the reporting code needs a fix.

## Learnings
- **Payoff structure was the main cause.** Target distance was roughly half the stop distance on every trade:
  - ITC: risk 2.17, reward 1.07
  - BANKBEES: risk 3.00, reward 1.43
  - TECHM: risk 11.51, reward 5.68
  - The result was an average win of about Rs 142 against an average loss of about Rs 317.
  - That shape needs roughly a 67% hit rate to break even. Today's 52% lost money even though there were more winners than losers.
- **Fading the crosses worked only in one regime.** The morning signals were mostly bearish crosses, which this copy bought. That paid while stocks ground up into 12:00. The same long bias was stopped out 7 times between 12:49 and 13:53 (-2,748 combined) once the tape turned lower. The inverted logic had no regime check.
- **Stock-level whipsaw piled up costs and losses.**
  - JSWSTEEL was traded four times in alternating directions: BUY 11:25, SELL 12:05, BUY 13:05, SELL 14:22.
  - TRENT went long at 11:45 (stopped -320), then short twice.
  - MARUTI went short at 12:05 (stopped -509), then long at 13:25.
  - Re-crossing EMAs on the same name within an hour was noise, not signal.
- **Tiny position sizes produced trades that cannot move the day.** These four trades together made Rs 18.77:
  - INFY qty 1 (+2.17)
  - JSWSTEEL qty 1 (+2.12)
  - NTPC qty 3 (+1.16)
  - INFY qty 5 (+13.32)
- **Stops slipped beyond their levels.**
  - MARUTI: stop 14079.40, filled at 14080.05.
  - TECHM: stop 1834.06, filled at 1834.85.
  - NTPC: stop 322.48, filled at 322.51 at the flat.
  - On top of an already unfavorable payoff, this slippage made losses worse.
- **Late entries were wasted.** WIPRO (14:08), TRENT (14:12), JSWSTEEL (14:22) and NTPC (14:33) were all opened under 40 minutes before the 14:50 flat. None reached target, and all four closed red (-295 combined).

## Try next
- **Fix the payoff ratio.** Set stop_atr 1.5 and target_atr 2.0, so reward is at least risk, instead of the current roughly 0.5:1. Keep invert 1 so the change is tested in isolation.
- **Add filters for re-entries, tiny trades and late entries:**
  - Allow at most one entry per stock per session. This would have blocked JSWSTEEL #2-4, TRENT #2-3 and MARUTI #2.
  - Skip any trade with notional below Rs 20,000.
  - Block new entries after 14:15.
- **Test the slower EMA setting (fast 13, slow 34)** to cut cross frequency on choppy midday tape. Compare it against the current 9/21 over at least 5 sessions before adopting it.

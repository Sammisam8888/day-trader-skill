# Targets at half the stop distance made a 9-of-15 win day lose Rs 450

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The trade log does not match the summary line. The summary says 15 of 15 won, net Rs 19278, best 4123, worst 164. Those figures look like prices, not P&L. The log itself shows 15 closed trades, 9 wins and 6 losses, net -Rs 450 after charges. The best trade was +263 (SUNPHARMA short, 14:45) and the worst was -650 (TATASTEEL long, 12:23). The day was choppy: names bounced inside a few tenths of a percent, so tight targets were hit often and stops were also hit regularly. Average win was about Rs 193 and average loss about Rs 364. At that ratio the strategy needs a win rate of roughly 65% to break even, and it got 60%.

## Learnings
- The payoff shape is upside down. The target sits at about 0.5x the stop distance on nearly every trade. Examples: TATASTEEL stop -0.88 vs target +0.43; SUNPHARMA long stop -7.07 vs target +3.46; JSWSTEEL stop +4.73 vs target -2.29. So the 6 losers totalled Rs 2183, outweighing 9 winners at Rs 1733.
- Three trades decided the day: TATASTEEL long (-650), SUNPHARMA long (-591) and JSWSTEEL short (-567). Each lost about 3x a typical winner. Stop fills also slipped past the stop level, adding to the losses: TATASTEEL filled at 164.24 vs a 164.43 stop, and JSWSTEEL at 1321.55 vs 1319.98.
- Shorts outperformed longs on this one day. Shorts went 6 for 8, net about +Rs 455 (LT, HCLTECH, WIPRO, POWERGRID, SUNPHARMA 14:34 all hit target). Longs went 3 for 7, net about -Rs 905. This is a single-day sample.
- The 14:50 flat exit cost Rs 218 on KOTAKBANK (-147) and BEL short (-71). Entries after 14:00 had little time to reach target.
- Sizing glitch: RELIANCE short at 12:14 opened with qty 1. It hit target for Rs 3.54, a wasted slot. Other trades were sized at Rs 25k-95k notional.
- The SUNPHARMA long stopped out at 12:59, and the inverted SUNPHARMA short at 14:34 then won. The signal flipped direction within 90 minutes, which is consistent with a range-bound tape where EMA 9/21 crosses are mostly noise.

## Try next
- Fix the reward-to-risk ratio so the target is at least 1.5x the stop: stop_atr 1.5, target_atr 3.0 (or at minimum make target_atr >= stop_atr).
- Block new entries after 14:15 so trades are not force-closed at 14:50, and enforce a minimum position notional (for example Rs 20k) to stop qty-1 trades like RELIANCE.
- Test side = short-only for a few sessions to see whether today's short edge holds before keeping it. Separately, fix the day summary generator, which reports prices instead of net P&L.

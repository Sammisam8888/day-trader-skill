# Calls lost 0/3 and entries after 10:15 lost Rs 5825; the reported day summary is wrong (real net is -5459, not +185)

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The trade rows show 8 closed trades: 3 won and 5 lost, for a net of Rs -5459 after charges. That is far off the Rs 1000 target. The printed summary line ("8 won, net Rs 185, best 49, worst 4") and the 11:22 "DAY TARGET reached, net 1195" banner both contradict the rows: realized P&L at 11:22 was Rs -3345. The tape leaned down. Puts on ITC, AXISBANK and SUNPHARMA paid, while all three call entries (NTPC, HDFCBANK, HINDUNILVR) were stopped or bled out. Six positions were opened between 09:47 and 10:30, tying up about Rs 97,200 of the Rs 100,000 wallet.

## Learnings
- **The log's numbers can't be trusted.** The summary line and the 11:22 target banner disagree with the per-trade nets, which sum to -5459.
  - If the banner used mark-to-market on open positions, it should say so.
  - Either way, "target reached, keeps trading" fired while realized P&L was -3345. Any target or stop logic built on that number is acting on the wrong value.
- **Call side failed completely; put side was roughly flat.**
  - Calls: NTPC 10:31 stop -1990, HDFCBANK 11:20 stop -1355, HINDUNILVR flat at 14:50 -2132. Total -5477.
  - Puts: AXISBANK +3145, ITC +566, SUNPHARMA +444, COALINDIA -2753, ONGC -1384. Total about +18.
  - The upside breakouts had no follow-through on a soft day.
- **Late entries hurt.**
  - Entries before 10:15 (ITC, NTPC, AXISBANK, HDFCBANK) netted +366.
  - Entries from 10:20 onward (COALINDIA, ONGC, HINDUNILVR at 12:00, SUNPHARMA at 12:30) netted -5825.
  - The 12:00 and 12:30 "breakouts" of a 30-minute range are 2.5+ hours stale. Option premium decay then works against a slow move: HINDUNILVR lost 12.5% of premium with no stop hit.
- **Position size followed premium cost, not risk.** COALINDIA got 2 lots (2700 qty, Rs 19,575) because the premium was cheap. A 1-rupee premium drop became the day's worst loss (-2753), larger than either 1-lot call stop.
- **The wallet was nearly maxed by 10:30.** RELIANCE was skipped at 10:33 on cost (Rs 16,975). Allocation was simply first-come-first-served, with no ranking of signals.
- **Data bug:** the LT29DEC26P4100 lookup at 09:53 requested NFO token 135946 but received the NSE LT-EQ quote (11483). The trade was correctly skipped, but the token mapping needs fixing before it ever fills a trade at an equity price.

# Long-side runs and same-stock flips gave back the day; the short side carried it

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The tape drifted lower through the session. Short breaks that held paid well: BHARTIARTL +920, AXISBANK +907 and +578, MARUTI +316, RELIANCE +221. Per the log, liq_run closed 51 trades: 17 won and 34 lost, for a net of about Rs +321. Realized P&L peaked at Rs +1017 at 13:26, swung to Rs -862 at 14:43, and recovered only through the 14:50 flatten.

The supplied "Day numbers" line does not match the log and should be treated as a reporting bug:
- It claims 51/51 won and Rs 77044 net.
- Its "best 13775" and "worst 165" are prices, not P&L.
- The "DAY TARGET reached 10:36, net 1004" note fired with zero closed trades, so it was counting open mark-to-market.

## Learnings
- **Shorts made the day, longs lost it.** The 32 SELLs netted roughly Rs +1100. The 19 BUYs netted roughly Rs -785 and won only 3 times: INFY +4, ONGC +16, TATASTEEL +10.
  - Two longs did almost all of the long-side damage: TECHM 104 qty at 13:47 (-631) and ICICIBANK 25 qty at 13:41 (-133).
  - Upside breaks did not hold in a falling tape.
- **Immediate reversals on the same stock bled out.** After a stop, the strategy opened the opposite side in the same second:
  - ITC: stopped SELL at 12:02:36, then BUY, which was also stopped.
  - NTPC: SELL, BUY, SELL, BUY between 12:35 and 13:50, all stopped.
  - HCLTECH: five round-trips, four of them stopped.
  - Each loss was small, but together they are about 20 trades of pure chop plus charges. A stopped break means there is no pool to run, so it should not trigger a new signal on the other side.
- **Full-size losers came from very tight stops on low-priced, high-quantity names.** Stops around 0.21-0.26% of price were hit by ordinary noise:
  - POWERGRID 718 qty at 10:17: stop 0.70 away, -633.
  - NTPC 603 qty at 11:24: stop 0.76 away, -545.
  - HINDALCO 263 qty at 10:19: stop 1.90 away, -689.
  - Each risked about Rs 500, and those three alone cost more than the day's final net.
- **Holding paid where the trend agreed.** The 13:22 AXISBANK short and the 12:37 RELIANCE short never hit their targets but were still worth +578 and +221 at the 14:50 flatten. The edge is in breaks that hold, not in quick re-tests.
- **Many trades were too small to matter.** Lots of entries were 1-5 qty (TATASTEEL 1, SBIN 1, HCLTECH 1, WIPRO 1), each worth a few rupees. They add churn and charges without adding real exposure.

## Try next
- **Confirm the break before entering.** Set hold_bars 3 (from the apparent 1) and break_pct 0.12, so price has to genuinely hold beyond the pool. This should cut the 0.2%-stop whipsaws seen on POWERGRID, NTPC and HINDALCO.
- **Stop the flips.** Add a filter that blocks entries on a stock for 30 minutes after a stop on it. Also cap entries at 2 per stock per day. Together these remove the ITC, NTPC and HCLTECH flip chains.
- **Fix sizing and the day-target logic.**
  - Add a minimum stop distance of 0.35% of price, keeping the same Rs 500 risk so quantity shrinks on tight stops.
  - Skip entries under Rs 5000 notional.
  - Base the day-target check on realized P&L, not open mark-to-market.

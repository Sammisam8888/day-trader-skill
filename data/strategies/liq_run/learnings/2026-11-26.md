# Target hit by 11:09, then 28 losers gave it all back: failed breaks that reversed were the real edge

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The day had three phases. A mild up-move ran until about 11:00, a broad sell-off followed from 11:25 to 12:30, and a recovery came after 14:00 (RELIANCE, HCLTECH, LT and BHARTIARTL longs all paid late). liq_run closed 49 trades with 21 wins and 28 losses and finished at net +Rs 18 after charges. Its best trade was RELIANCE +1119.75 (14:25) and its worst was RELIANCE -787.42 (14:08). The system logged "day target reached at 11:09, net 1098", but realized P&L at that time was only +343. The strategy kept trading, fell to -2302 at 12:28, and spent the afternoon climbing back to flat. The summary footer ("49 won, net 98956, best 14020, worst 163") contradicts the log and looks like it is reading prices or wallet values, so the reporting needs fixing.

## Learnings
- **Breaks that failed, then reversed, paid more than the breaks themselves.**
  - TRENT long stopped at 11:43 (-584), then TRENT short won +914 at 12:50.
  - SBIN long stopped at 13:29 (-198), then SBIN short won +326 at 13:51.
  - RELIANCE short stopped at 14:08 (-787), then RELIANCE long won +1120 at 14:25.
  - On this day, the first stop-out on a name was a better signal than the original break.
- **Re-entering the same direction after a stop lost twice.**
  - MARUTI short was stopped at 10:29 (-601), and the second MARUTI short was stopped at 11:56 (-492). That is Rs 1093 on a name that kept rising.
  - RELIANCE long was re-entered at 11:05 at 1384.55, just above the 10:59 target exit, and stopped for -73.
- **Stops of about 0.25-0.35% got run within minutes, and fills slipped past the stop.**
  - KOTAKBANK short stopped 12 minutes after entry (-503).
  - MARUTI filled at 14020.05 against a stop of 14018.34.
  - WIPRO lost -601 on 710 shares.
  - TATASTEEL lost -581 on 1040 shares.
  - Holding the breakout for 1-2 bars is not enough to confirm it.
- **Many trades were sized at 1-5 shares** (INFY, NTPC, KOTAKBANK, ONGC, WIPRO, BEL, COALINDIA, POWERGRID, NIFTYBEES, TCS, HCLTECH). Each was worth only plus or minus Rs 1-30. They added noise and charges without moving the day, which shows capital was exhausted by too many concurrent positions.
- **The day-target check used unrealized P&L.** Because of that, the "keeps trading for more" decision rested on profit the strategy had not actually banked, and it then gave back about Rs 2600 at the worst point.

## Try next
- **hold_bars 3 (up from what was effectively 1-2) and break_pct 0.15.** This should filter out the quick fake breaks that hit KOTAKBANK, WIPRO, MARUTI and TATASTEEL.
- **Add a filter: no same-direction re-entry on a stock after a stop on that stock the same day.** Also test allowing only the opposite side for the next 30 minutes, which acts as a per-stock invert after a stop. This covers MARUTI, TRENT, SBIN and RELIANCE today.
- **Add a filter: skip entries whose risk is below Rs 50 (qty x stop distance), and cap open positions at 5.** Also base the day-target check on realized P&L only, and stop opening new trades once realized P&L is at or above Rs 1000.

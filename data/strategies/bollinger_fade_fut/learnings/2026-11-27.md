# One TATASTEEL lot made Rs 89 net, and charges took about 75% of the gross because the target was too close in futures-price terms

Date: 2026-11-27

## What happened
The strategy made one trade: a BUY on TATASTEEL29DEC26F, 2750 qty, at 11:24:07 for 165.74. It closed on target at 14:20:52 at 165.87. That is Rs 357.50 gross and Rs 88.82 net, so charges were about Rs 269. The day summary says net Rs 166, but the close row says 88.82 and "day so far 89", so the two numbers conflict and the reporting needs checking. The other 31 events were skips: 25 for margin above the free cash, 4 for wrong-exchange quotes and 3 for ETFs that have no futures contract.

## Learnings
- The 11:24 TATASTEEL signal was priced at 165.07 on the cash stock, but the futures fill was 165.74. That 0.67 gap (about 0.4%) is the futures premium, not slippage. Because the entry and target are computed on spot but filled on futures, the exit at 165.87 captured only 0.13 points of the reversion.
- A 0.13-point move on 2750 qty grosses Rs 357, and a round trip costs about Rs 269. Any target worth less than about 3x the charges, roughly Rs 800 here, is not worth taking on a single lot.
- The stop and target columns are blank on both TATASTEEL rows, so the log does not show the protective stop at all. For about 3 hours a Rs 91157 position, 91% of the wallet, ran without a stop visible in the log.
- Holding TATASTEEL locked out every other signal from 11:34 to 14:19. But only INFY (Rs 93336 to 93496 margin, at 14:09 and 14:14) would have fit in Rs 100000 anyway. SUNPHARMA, ICICIBANK, SBIN, RELIANCE, HDFCBANK, BAJFINANCE, ADANI*, HINDALCO, ONGC, POWERGRID, ITC, COALINDIA and MARUTI all need Rs 101k to 225k. Most of the universe cannot be traded on this wallet.
- The quote lookup failed for BHARTIARTL (12:24), ADANIENT (13:24), ADANIPORTS (13:24, 13:29) and INFY (13:49). Each time it asked for an NFO contract and got back the NSE-EQ token. This is a bug in the instrument mapping, not a market problem. NIFTYBEES and BANKBEES (10:54, 10:59, 11:04) are in the universe but have no futures contracts.

## Try next
- Add a filter that drops NIFTYBEES, BANKBEES and any symbol whose 1-lot futures margin is above about Rs 95000. Today that leaves roughly TATASTEEL and INFY, so scans and skips stop cluttering the log.
- Compute the bands, entry, stop and target on the futures price instead of spot. Add a filter that requires the expected reversion times the lot size to be at least 3x the round-trip charges (about Rs 800 on TATASTEEL).
- Set sd 2.5 (from the default) so entries happen further from the mean and the reversion leg is wide enough to cover charges. Set stop_sd 1.0 and confirm the stop actually appears in the open row.

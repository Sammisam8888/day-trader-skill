# Fades were signalled on cash prices but filled on futures, so the basis wiped out the edge and the summary line misreports a -2490 day as +1503

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The log does not support the day-numbers line. That line claims 2 wins and Rs +1503 net. The log shows 2 closed trades, both losers: NTPC -733.16 and INFY -1757.24, for a day total of Rs -2490 against a Rs 1000 target. The strategy didn't act until 13:09. Of 22 log rows, 20 were skips, and only NTPC and INFY passed the margin check on a Rs 100000 wallet. Once INFY was open, free margin fell to Rs 5027, and every later signal (POWERGRID, TECHM, BEL) was blocked until the 14:50 flatten.

## Learnings
- **The summary line is wrong.** "2 won, +1503, best 1182, worst 321" looks like it was built from exit prices (1181.70, 321.20), not from net Rs. The day was -2490. Fix the reporting before trusting any aggregate stats from this strategy.
- **Signal price and fill price come from different instruments.**
  - NTPC 14:03: BUY signalled at 319.30 but filled at 321.50, 2.20 higher.
  - INFY 14:07: SELL signalled at 1169.90 but filled at 1178.00, 8.10 away. Here the futures premium happened to favour the short, but the fade level was still computed on cash, not on the instrument traded.
  - The bands are computed on the underlying, while fills come from the future, so the band edge no longer means a stretched price on what we actually trade.
- **NTPC 14:03 -> 14:04 was stopped out in 96 seconds on a 0.30 move.** On 1500 qty that is Rs 450 gross. The other ~Rs 283 is charges and slippage, so friction was almost 40% of the loss. A stop this tight on a 1-lot, high-qty future is mostly noise.
- **INFY 14:07 -> 14:50 never reverted.** It rose from 1178.00 to 1181.70 over 43 minutes and was closed by the time flatten, not a stop. The log shows no stop or target recorded for either trade, so the protective levels can't be audited.
- **Most of the universe was untradeable:**
  - Margin: ULTRACEMCO, RELIANCE, ITC x2, HCLTECH, SBIN, KOTAKBANK, POWERGRID, TECHM and BEL all needed Rs 101935-221232 against Rs 100000 free.
  - No futures contract: NIFTYBEES x3 and BANKBEES x2.
  - Bad quote mapping: for ICICIBANK, TATASTEEL x2 and HDFCBANK, the NFO token request returned the NSE-EQ quote.
- **Each trade took about 95% of the wallet**, so the strategy could hold only one position at a time. After the INFY entry it was blind for the rest of the session.

## Try next
- **Filter the universe and fix the data.**
  - Before scanning, drop names whose 1-lot margin exceeds free capital, and drop names with no futures contract (NIFTYBEES, BANKBEES).
  - Fix the NFO token lookup for ICICIBANK, HDFCBANK and TATASTEEL.
  - Correct the day-summary calculation to use net Rs.
- **Compute the Bollinger bands and stops on the futures series itself**, not on the cash underlying, so signal, stop and fill all refer to one price.
- **Change the knobs:**
  - Raise stop_sd to 1.5 so a 0.30 tick can't stop a trade out within 2 minutes.
  - Raise sd to 2.5 so only larger stretches qualify, which lets the expected reversion cover roughly Rs 280 per round trip in charges.
  - Log the actual stop and target values on every open.

# Day summary misreported a Rs 10,079 loss as a Rs 255 profit, and the strategy's tiny targets and wide stops lost money

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
The strategy opened 7 option buys between 13:13 and 14:24: six puts on SELL signals (ITC twice, TECHM, ICICIBANK, LT, AXISBANK) and one call on HCLTECH. The underlyings drifted up in the afternoon, so the long puts lost value. The day closed at **-Rs 10,079 net with 2 wins and 5 losses**. The footer ("7 won, net Rs 255, best 71, worst 6") is wrong, and so is the 13:56 banner "DAY TARGET reached, net 1652": the running total at 13:56 was +96. Losses were driven by TECHM (-4,730), AXISBANK (-3,309) and LT (-2,536). Only HCLTECH (+1,271) and ITC (+132) made money.

## Learnings
- **The reporting is broken and cannot be trusted.** The footer stats and the day-target banner both disagree with the trade-by-trade "day so far" column. The false "target reached" at 13:56 also let the strategy keep trading. It then took TECHM's stop and HCLTECH's entry, and carried 4 positions into the 14:50 flatten.
- **Gains are tiny and losses are large.** The ITC 13:24 "target" was 6.90 to 7.00 (+1.4% premium, +132). The ITC 13:55 "target" exited at the entry price, 6.10, for -37 after charges, so the target is not even covering costs. The TECHM 14:03 stop was 54.65 to 46.90 (-14% premium, -4,730). One stop erased about 35 target-sized wins.
- **Premium decay hurt the trades flattened at 14:50.** LT fell 85.00 to 70.75 (-17%), AXISBANK 36.00 to 30.80 (-14%) and ICICIBANK 34.10 to 32.95. None hit a stop or target. Buying puts from 13:13 to 13:56 and holding into the afternoon on a day the market did not fall paid both theta and direction.
- **The strategy was one-sided against the tape.** Six of seven entries were bearish puts. The only bullish trade (HCLTECH call, 14:24) was the best trade at +1,271. Mean-reversion SELL signals kept firing while the broader move was up.
- **Position size is set by lot size, not risk.** TECHM's single lot cost Rs 32,790, a third of the wallet, so its stop dominated the day. ITC at Rs 11,900 carried far less risk.
- **The instrument lookup has bugs.** ONGC at 13:09 and ITC P307.5 at 13:25 requested an NFO token but got the NSE equity quote. BANKBEES has no options yet was checked 5 times (13:31 to 13:59).

## Try next
- Fix the P&L and day-target accounting so the footer and banner match the summed net (-10,079). Also make the option target clear charges plus a margin, at least about 5% of premium rather than 1-2%. No exit labeled "target" should lose money.
- Set stop_sd to 0.75 (tighter, so a stop costs closer to a target) and band_sd to 2.5 (fewer, more stretched entries). Also add a filter that skips any trade whose 1-lot premium exceeds Rs 20,000, which would have excluded TECHM, AXISBANK and ICICIBANK.
- Remove BANKBEES and any non-F&O symbols from the universe. Add an entry cutoff around 13:45 so option buys are not held through afternoon decay to the 14:50 flatten.

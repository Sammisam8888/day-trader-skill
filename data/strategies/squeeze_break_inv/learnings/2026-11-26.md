# Short side made +1666 and long side lost -1452; five full stops on longs, each about Rs 540, erased a Rs 1749 peak

Date: 2026-11-26

## What happened
Most of today's gains came from the short side. The inverted squeeze signals shorted large caps from 10:56 to 12:15, and all 10 of those shorts that reached a target won about Rs 155-230 each. That carried the day to +1110 by 11:56 and to a peak of +1749 at 12:32. From 12:49 to 13:55, five long entries hit full stops (TRENT, BAJFINANCE, BANKBEES, AXISBANK, ULTRACEMCO), each losing about Rs 530-560. The day bottomed at -599 at 13:55. Late long targets brought it back, and the day closed at **+213 net over 28 trades: 20 won, 8 lost**. The best trade was +237.51 (KOTAKBANK) and the worst was -560.53 (ULTRACEMCO).

Note: the summary line "28 won, net Rs 60468, best 14027, worst 163" does not match the log. It looks like it reads prices instead of P&L. The "DAY TARGET reached ... net 1028" banner also disagrees with the log's running total of 1110 at 11:56. The reporting code needs checking.

## Learnings
- **Win and loss sizes do not balance.** Stops are set about twice as far from entry as targets (TCS: stop 33.5 points away, target 16.7). So a win pays about Rs 210 and a loss costs about Rs 540. The strategy needs to win about 72% of trades just to break even. It won 71% (20/28), so the net was close to zero despite the high hit rate.
- **Shorts worked and longs did not.**
  - Shorts netted about +1666. TCS, COALINDIA, BHARTIARTL, TRENT, ONGC, LT, SBIN and MARUTI all hit target.
  - Longs netted about -1452. All five full stops were longs.
  - TRENT shows the gap clearly. The 11:10 short made +230.85. The 11:45 long, opened 25 minutes later at 3286.95, was stopped for -536.43.
- **Financial-sector longs lost together.** The long book held AXISBANK, ICICIBANK, BAJFINANCE, BANKBEES and later KOTAKBANK. BAJFINANCE (13:37), BANKBEES (13:49) and AXISBANK (13:49) were stopped within 12 minutes of each other, for -1642 combined. Effectively this was one leveraged bet on banks, not three independent trades.
- **Nothing protected gains after the day target.** The target was hit at 11:56 and trading continued with full size. The day gave back 1536 of the 1749 peak, and the 13:14 BANKBEES and 13:30-13:37 long entries were made after the peak.
- **Very small positions only add charges and noise.** TATASTEEL x2 made +0.39, POWERGRID x1 made +0.04, BEL x1 lost -1.04, and TATASTEEL x55 made only +15.77. Positions this small cannot materially affect the day.
- **The 14:50 forced exit was roughly neutral (-17 combined).** SUNPHARMA (-105) and HINDALCO (-58) were offset by ADANIENT (+197).

## Try next
- **Make wins and losses comparable in size.** Lower `stop_atr` by about 0.5 and raise `target_atr` by about 1.0 from today's values, so the stop distance is no more than the target distance. Today one stop erased about 2.6 wins.
- **Test a short-only filter.** Today's split was +1666 short versus -1452 long. Run with the short-only setting of `side` (check which of 0..2 means short-only in the code). Before keeping it, confirm over several days that longs keep losing.
- **Add a sector cap and a giveback lock.**
  - Allow at most 2 open positions in banks or financials (AXISBANK, ICICIBANK, KOTAKBANK, SBIN, BAJFINANCE, BANKBEES).
  - After the day target is hit, stop opening new trades if the day's net falls 50% below its peak. Today that would have triggered near +875 around 12:49.
  - Skip any entry with quantity under 10 shares or notional under Rs 5000.

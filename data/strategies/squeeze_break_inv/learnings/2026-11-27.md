# Winning 71% of trades still netted only Rs 432 because stops sit about 2x farther than targets

Date: 2026-11-27

Note: the summary line ("17 won, net Rs 29405, best 4127, worst 166") does not match the trade log. The log shows 17 closed trades: 12 won and 5 lost. Net is Rs +432.14, best is +278.31 (BHARTIARTL), and worst is -585.44 (ADANIPORTS). Everything below uses the log.

## What happened
Prices rose until about noon, then weakened from about 13:00 into the close. The four BUYs opened between 11:04 and 11:24 hit target before 12:00. Late longs were stopped out between 13:09 and 13:58, while every afternoon SELL reached target or closed green. The strategy crossed the Rs 1000 day target at 12:36 (Rs 1058). It then fell to Rs -920 at 13:58 and recovered to Rs +432 at the 14:50 flat. The 12 winners made Rs 2414, but the 4 real stop-outs gave back Rs 1977.

## Learnings
- The payoff is lopsided. On BAJFINANCE (10:54) the stop was 8.97 below entry and the target only 4.41 above, a reward-to-risk of about 0.49. Every trade used the same roughly 2:1 risk-to-reward. Average win was Rs 201 and average stop loss was Rs 494, so the 71% win rate only barely covered losses.
- Most of the giveback came from positions already open before 12:36. BAJFINANCE (opened 10:54, stopped 13:09, -550) and ADANIENT (opened 11:24, stopped 13:23, -537) sat near entry for about 2 hours, then broke when the market turned. New entries after the day target was hit netted only Rs -83. A "stop opening trades at day target" rule alone would not have saved the day.
- The ADANIPORTS re-entry was the worst decision of the day. At 13:29:23 the first long was stopped (-305), and in the same second a new long opened at 2.25x the size (72 vs 32 shares). It was stopped at 13:58 for -585. That is Rs 890 lost on one stock in 30 minutes, buying against an obvious afternoon slide.
- Longs netted Rs -922 (5 wins, 5 losses). Shorts netted Rs +1354 (7 of 7: RELIANCE, TRENT, TCS, BHARTIARTL, HINDUNILVR, HDFCBANK, KOTAKBANK). The inverted signals worked when they agreed with the afternoon drift and failed when they fought it.
- Morning entries clustered at 11:24: ADANIENT, TATASTEEL, HDFCBANK and LT were all bought at once. That is one correlated bet; it worked in the morning, but ADANIENT from the same batch became the second-largest loss.
- Stop fills slipped a little past the stop price (BAJFINANCE 1083.65 vs 1084.08, ADANIPORTS 1880.95 vs 1881.38). With targets this tight, even small slippage matters.

## Try next
- Fix the reward-to-risk ratio. The current settings look like stop_atr 3.0 with target_atr 1.5 (inferred from the 2:1 distances). Test stop_atr 1.5 with target_atr 1.5, or stop_atr 2.0 with target_atr 2.5, so one stop costs about one target instead of about 2.5.
- Add filters: no re-entry on a symbol for 30 minutes after a stop, never at a larger size (this would have avoided the -585 ADANIPORTS trade). Add a 60-minute time stop for trades that have not reached half their target, which would have cut BAJFINANCE and ADANIENT before the 13:00 breakdown.
- Keep side at 0 (both directions) for now; one day of 7/7 shorts is not enough evidence. Do log a market-direction tag at entry (for example, NIFTY above or below VWAP), so we can test after 5 or more days whether blocking trades against that direction improves results.
